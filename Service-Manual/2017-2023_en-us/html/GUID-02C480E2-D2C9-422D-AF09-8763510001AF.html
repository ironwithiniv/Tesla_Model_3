EPB Harness - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 3301021102 0.18 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. Remove

  1. Use a floor jack and a jack stand to raise and support the vehicle. See Raise Vehicle - Floor Jack (Mobile Service).
  2. Remove the LH rear wheel. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
  3. Remove the clips (x3) and push nut that attach the LH rear wheel liner to the vehicle, and then lift the liner for access to the harness.
  4. Release the connector lock, and then disconnect the LH electronic parking brake caliper electrical connector.
  5. Remove the bolt that attaches the LH rear EPB harness bracket to the LH rear caliper.
    5 Nm (3.7 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  6. Remove the bolt that attaches the LH rear EPB harness bracket to the body.
    5 Nm (3.7 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  7. Release the connector lock, and then disconnect the LH rear electronic parking brake harness from the rear main body harness.

Install

Installation procedure is the reverse of removal.
