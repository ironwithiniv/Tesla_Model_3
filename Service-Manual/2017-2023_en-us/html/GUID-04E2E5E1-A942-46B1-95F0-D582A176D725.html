Trunk (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 10107002 0.90 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-03-03: Added Clear Camera Calibration routine.
  • 2024-10-23: Added a reminder for technicians to install all badges that are equipped with the working vehicle.
Equipment:
  • 1134703-00-A Fixture, Rear Badge, Model 3

Remove

  1. Remove the rear license plate.
  2. Remove the LH and RH rear fog/reverse lights. See Light - Fog / Reverse - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the trunk exterior release switch. See Switch and Camera Assembly - Exterior Release - Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the LH and RH license plate lights. See Light - License Plate (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the trunk latch. See Latch - Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the bolts (x2) that attach the trunk electrical harness ground straps to the trunk lid.
    Note
    Self-tapping tri-lobular bolt (1128269-00-A) is non-reusable. Normal bolt (1447438-00-A) is reusable.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  7. Release the clips that attach the trunk electrical harness to the trunk lid.
  8. Release the electrical harness boot, and then move the trunk electrical harness through the trunk lid bulkhead and out of the trunk lid.
  9. Release the clips that attach the LH and RH bump stops to the trunk lid, and then remove the stops from the trunk lid.
  10. Unscrew the LH and RH over-slam bumpers from the trunk lid.
  11. With assistance, remove the bolts (x4) that attach the trunk lid to the trunk hinges, and then remove the trunk from the hinges.
    CAUTION
    Remove the bolts furthest from the body first, lift up on the trunk lid, and while doing so, remove the bolts closest to the body.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm socket

Install

CAUTION
If using this procedure to install an unpainted part, make sure to apply seam sealer to all seams prior to installing and painting the part. Refer to the Collision Repair manual for more information.
Tip
For instructions on how to align the trunk, refer to Trunk Alignment.
  1. With assistance, install the trunk to the trunk hinges, and then install the bolts that attach the trunk lid to the trunk hinges.
    CAUTION
    Install the bolts closest to the body first, and then install the bolts furthest from the body.
    Note
    Hand tighten, but do not torque the bolts at this time.
  2. Screw the LH and RH over-slam bumpers into the trunk lid.
  3. Install the LH and RH bump stops into the trunk lid, and then fasten the clips that attach the stops to the lid.
  4. Move the electrical harness in through the trunk lid bulkhead, and attach the harness boot to the trunk lid.
  5. Fasten the clips that attach the trunk electrical harness to the trunk lid.
  6. Install the bolts (x2) to attach the trunk electrical harness ground straps to the trunk lid.
    8 Nm (5.9 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Self-tapping tri-lobular bolt (1128269-00-A) is non-reusable. Normal bolt (1447438-00-A) is reusable.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  7. Install the trunk latch to the trunk lid, and then install the bolts that attach the latch to the trunk lid.
    Note
    Hand tighten, but do not torque the bolts at this time.
  8. Connect the electrical connector to the trunk latch.
  9. Install the LH and RH license plate lights. See Light - License Plate (Remove and Replace).
  10. Install the trunk exterior release switch. See Switch and Camera Assembly - Exterior Release - Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  11. Install the LH and RH rear fog/reverse lights. See Light - Fog / Reverse - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
  12. Carefully close the trunk and check the trunk to body gap and fitment.
    Location Gap Flush
    Trunk lid lower edge to rear lower fascia 4.5 mm +2.5/-2.0 mm 3.0 mm +4.5/-3.5 mm
    Trunk lid side edge to rear side fascia N/A 3.0 mm +1.5/-3.0 mm
    Fog/reverse light to taillight 3.5 mm +3.0/-2.5 mm 1.0 mm +1.7/-2.5 mm
  13. Open the trunk, loosen the hinge bolts, adjust the trunk lid position, and tighten the bolts.
  14. Repeat step 12 and step 13 until the gap and flush is minimal and uniform.
  15. Torque the hinge bolts to specification.
    24 Nm (17.7 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm socket
  16. Torque the trunk latch bolts to specification.
    8 Nm (5.9 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  17. Enable Service Mode. See Service Mode.
  18. Unlock the vehicle gateway. See Gateway (Unlock).
  19. On the touchscreen, tap the Service Mode "wrench" (at the bottom of the touchscreen UI), and then tap Driver Assist > Cameras > Clear Camera Calibration, select RearView in the Camera Selection drop list, click Run, and allow the routine to complete.
  20. Verify that the rear facing camera works properly.
  21. Install the trunk lid trim. See Trim - Lid - Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  22. Use IPA wipes to thoroughly clean these locations on the trunk lid:
    • Hinge bolts, washers and surrounding area
    • Area between the trunk lid lower lip and trunk latch
    • Trunk lid badge area above license plate location
  23. Vigorously shake the vehicle color touch-up paint applicator for several minutes.
  24. Apply a coat of touch-up paint to the discolored areas of the hinge bolts and washers, and allow at least 20 minutes to dry.
    Note
    Perform the remainder of this procedure while the touch-up paint dries.
  25. Affix a new emissions sticker to the trunk lid, in the gap between the trunk lid lower lip and the trunk latch.
  26. For China, Japan and Korea only: Install a new motor ID label on the trunk.
  27. Install the rear license plate.
    1.5 Nm (1.1 lbs-ft)
  28. Install the rear Tesla badge. See Tesla Badge - Rear (Remove and Replace).
    Note
    If the removed trunk/liftgate had additional badges due to vehicle configuration or regional badges, make sure that all badges are installed on the replacement trunk/liftgate.
