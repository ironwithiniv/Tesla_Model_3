Headliner (Heat Pump) (Remove and Install)

Correction code 15203111 0.66 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 1520 - Headliner

Remove

  1. Open all the doors and lower all the windows.
  2. Move the front seats fully forward.
  3. Release the clips (x4) and tethers (x2) that attach the RH side rail trim to the vehicle, and then remove the trim from the vehicle.
    Tip
    Release the upper corner of the trim first and work downward.
    Note
    Remove the tethers by twisting and pulling away from the housing.
    CAUTION
    DO NOT remove the tethers from the body side. The tethers must be replaced if removed from the body side.
  4. Push to open the RH rear coat hook.
  5. Use a small flat head screwdriver to pry open the RH rear coat hook screw cover.
  6. Remove the screw that attaches the RH rear coat hook to the body.
    2.5 Nm (1.8 lbs-ft)
  7. Release the clips that attach the RH upper B-pillar trim to the vehicle, and then move the upper B-pillar trim aside.
    Note
    It is not necessary to completely remove the upper B-pillar trim from the vehicle; only remove the trim with the seat belt still going though the upper B-pillar trim and set it aside.
  8. Repeat step 3 through step 7 for the LH side of the vehicle.
  9. Move the LH front seat fully rearward, and then move the seat back to the down position.
  10. Remove the LH instrument panel end cap. See End Cap - Instrument Panel - LH (Remove and Replace).
  11. Remove the LH middle A-pillar trim. See Trim - A-Pillar - Middle - LH (Remove and Replace).
  12. Remove the LH lower A-pillar trim. See Trim - A-Pillar - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  13. Remove the LH upper A-pillar trim. See Trim - A-Pillar - Upper - LH (Remove and Replace).
  14. Remove the driver sun visor. See Sun Visor - LH (Remove and Replace).
  15. Remove the lower quad camera cover. See Quad Camera Cover - Lower (Remove and Replace).
  16. Remove the rear view mirror. See Mirror - Rear View (Remove and Replace).
  17. Repeat step 9 through step 14 for the RH side of the vehicle.
  18. Release the clips that attach the headliner electrical harness to the LH A-pillar area.
  19. Disconnect the headliner electrical harness.
  20. Release the clips (x10) that attach the headliner to the body.
  21. With an assistant, remove the headliner from the vehicle.
    Note
    Remove the headliner through the front passenger door.

Install

Installation procedure is the reverse of removal.
