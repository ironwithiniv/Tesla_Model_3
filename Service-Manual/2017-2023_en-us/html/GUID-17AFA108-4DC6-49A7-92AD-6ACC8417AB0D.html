Vent Assembly - HV Battery - Pair (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 16106486 0.72 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Warning
Only technicians who have completed all required certification courses are permitted to perform this procedure. Tesla recommends third party service provider technicians undergo equivalent training before performing this procedure. For more information on Tesla Technician requirements, or descriptions of the subject matter for third parties, see HV Certification Requirements. Proper personal protective equipment (PPE) and insulating HV gloves with a minimum rating of class 0 (1000V) must be worn at all times a high voltage cable, busbar, or fitting is handled. Refer to Tech Note TN-15-92-003, High Voltage Awareness Care Points for additional safety information.
Parent topic: 1610 - HV Battery Enclosure

Remove

  1. Raise and support the vehicle. See Raise Vehicle - 2 Post Lift.
  2. Enable Service Mode. See Service Mode.
  3. Disconnect the 12V power. See 12V/LV Power (Disconnect and Connect).
  4. Perform the vehicle electrical isolation procedure. See Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure.
  5. Remove the mid aero shield panel. See Panel - Aero Shield - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  6. Release the fir tree clips that attach the coolant hoses to the LH shear plate.
  7. Release the fir tree clips that attach the coolant hoses to the RH shear plate.
  8. Put on personal protective equipment.
    Warning
    HV insulating gloves and leather glove protectors must be worn when exposed to the interior of the HV battery.
  9. Remove the nuts (x5) that attach the RH vent assembly to the HV battery, and then remove the RH vent assembly from the battery.
    Note
    The number of nuts may vary depending on the date of manufacture.
  10. Remove the nuts (x5) that attach the LH vent assembly to the HV battery, and then remove the LH vent assembly from the battery.
    Note
    The number of nuts may vary depending on the date of manufacture.

Install

  1. Put on personal protective equipment.
    Warning
    HV insulating gloves and leather glove protectors must be worn when exposed to the interior of the HV battery.
  2. Install the LH vent assembly to the HV battery, and then install the nuts (x5) that attach the LH vent assembly to the battery.
    10 Nm (7.4 lbs-ft)
  3. Install the RH vent assembly to the HV battery, and then install the nuts (x5) that attach the vent assembly to the battery.
    10 Nm (7.4 lbs-ft)
  4. Perform an HV battery air leak test. See HV Battery Air Leak Test.
  5. If the new vent assembly does not have a breather valve preinstalled, install one now. See Breathers - HV Battery (Remove and Replace).
  6. Fasten the fir tree clips that attach the coolant hoses to the RH shear plate.
  7. Fasten the fir tree clips that attach the coolant hoses to the LH shear plate.
  8. Install the mid aero shield panel. See Panel - Aero Shield - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  9. Connect the 12V power. See 12V/LV Power (Disconnect and Connect).
  10. Exit Service Mode. See Service Mode.
  11. Lower the vehicle. See Raise Vehicle - 2 Post Lift.
