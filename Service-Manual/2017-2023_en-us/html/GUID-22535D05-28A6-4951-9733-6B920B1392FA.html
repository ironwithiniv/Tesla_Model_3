Brake Pad Kit - Rear (Sport Brakes) (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 33010812 0.54 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-02-19: Added a warning.
  • 2025-05-07: Updated torque specifications for the bolts that attach the caliper to the bracket.
  • 2024-05-28: Updated references and cautions.
Warning
During installation of the brake caliper, make sure that the flexible brake hose is not twisted and can bend forward. The flexible brake hose must not make contact with the wheel arch liner when the vehicle is lowered.
Note
For detailed brake specifications, see Brakes.
Parent topic: 3301 - Brake Discs and Calipers

Torque Specifications

Table 1. Torque Specifications: Brake Pad Kit - Rear (Sport Brakes) (Remove and Replace)
Description Torque Value Reuse/Replace Notes
LH/RH rear caliper to the LH/RH rear caliper bracket, lower bolt
71 Nm (52.4 lbs-ft)
Reuse
LH/RH rear caliper to the LH/RH rear caliper bracket, upper bolt
34 Nm (25.1 lbs-ft)
Reuse

Remove

  1. Raise and support the vehicle. See Raise Vehicle - 2 Post Lift.
  2. Remove the LH and RH rear wheels. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
  3. Place the vehicle into EPB Service Mode. See Parking Brake - Caliper - Rear - LH (Release).
  4. Using a flat head screwdriver, compress the brake piston by carefully pushing the caliper away from the brake rotor.
  5. Remove the rubber cap that covers the upper LH rear caliper to caliper bracket bolt.
  6. Remove the upper bolt that attaches the LH rear brake caliper to the LH rear caliper bracket.
  7. Remove the lower bolt that attaches the LH rear brake caliper to the LH rear caliper bracket.
  8. Use a brake caliper S-hook to hang the LH rear caliper on the body.
    CAUTION
    Make sure the brake caliper and hoses are not twisted when hanging the caliper or during reinstallation, as this can damage the brake hose.
  9. Remove the brake pad from the LH rear brake caliper.
  10. Remove the brake pad from the LH rear brake caliper bracket.
  11. Brush the LH rear brake caliper and LH rear brake caliper bracket anti rattle springs with soapy water.

Install

  1. Install the brake pad on the LH rear brake caliper bracket.
    Tip
    During installation, press the brake pad away from the brake rotor.
  2. Install the brake pad on the LH rear brake caliper.
    Note
    Mate the brake pad spring clips to the LH rear caliper cutouts.
  3. Position the LH rear caliper on the LH rear caliper bracket.
    Warning
    During installation of the brake caliper, make sure that the flexible brake hose is not twisted and can bend forward. The flexible brake hose must not make contact with the wheel arch liner when the vehicle is lowered.
  4. Install the lower bolt that attaches the LH rear caliper to the LH rear caliper bracket.
    71 Nm (52.4 lbs-ft)
  5. Install the upper bolt that attaches the LH rear caliper to the LH rear caliper bracket.
    34 Nm (25.1 lbs-ft)
  6. Install the rubber cap that covers the upper LH rear caliper to caliper bracket bolt.
  7. Repeat steps 4 to 6 for the RH rear brake pads.
  8. Install the LH and RH rear wheels. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
  9. Lower the vehicle, and then shift the vehicle into Park.
  10. Pump the brake pedal to seat the pads against the brake rotor until the brake pedal feels firm.
  11. Perform a road test and burnish the brake pads. See Brake Pads - Burnishing.
    Note
    Ensure proper brake pedal feel and brake performance.
    Note
    Add correction code 33013000 as a separate activity to the SV.
