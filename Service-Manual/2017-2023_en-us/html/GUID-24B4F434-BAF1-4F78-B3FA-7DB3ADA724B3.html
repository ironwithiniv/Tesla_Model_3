Fan- Radiator (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 18201502 1.02 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 1820 - Refrigerant System

Remove

  1. Remove the active grille shutter. See Active Grille Shutter (Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace).
  2. Release the upper and lower locking tabs that attach the radiator to the cooling fan and shroud assembly , and then remove the radiator from the cooling fan and shroud assembly.
  3. Remove the bolts and isolators from the cooling fan and shroud assembly.

Install

Installation procedure is the reverse of removal.
