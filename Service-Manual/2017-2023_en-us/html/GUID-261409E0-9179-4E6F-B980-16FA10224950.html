Bag Holder Hook - Underhood Storage Unit - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 15241112 0.06 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 1524 - Luggage Compartment Trim

Torque Specifications

Table 1. Torque Specifications: Bag Holder Hook - Underhood Storage Unit - LH (Remove and Replace)
Description Torque Value Recommended Tools Reuse/Replace Notes
Bolt that attaches the LH bag holder hook to the frunk assembly
5 Nm (3.7 lbs-ft)
  • 10 mm socket
 Reuse

Remove

  1. Open the LH front door and lower the LH front window.
  2. Open the hood.
  3. Remove the bolt that attaches the LH bag holder hook to the frunk assembly.
    Tip
    Press the lower part of the LH bag holder hook to access the bolt.
    Note
    Newer vehicles may not be equipped with underhood storage unit bag hook covers.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  4. Remove the LH bag holder hook from the frunk assembly.

Install

  1. Install the bolt that attaches the LH bag holder hook to the frunk assembly.
    5 Nm (3.7 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Newer vehicles may not be equipped with underhood storage unit bag hook covers.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  2. Close the hood.
  3. Raise the LH front window and close the LH front door.
