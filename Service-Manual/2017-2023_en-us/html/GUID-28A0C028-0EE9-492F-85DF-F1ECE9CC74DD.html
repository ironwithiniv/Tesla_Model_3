Latch - Door - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 11353702 0.60 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 1135 - Latches Strikers and Pinch Sensors

Remove

  1. Remove the LH rear window regulator. See Window Regulator - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
  2. Disconnect the LH rear door electrical harness from the LH rear door latch connector.
  3. Remove the bolts that attach the LH rear door latch to the rear of the LH rear door.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T25 socket
    • 4 in extension
  4. Move the LH rear door latch cable grommet in through the LH rear door bulkhead, while at the same time, move the latch cable in and remove the latch from the door.

Install

  1. Install the LH rear door latch into the LH rear door, while at the same time, move the latch cable out through the door, and then secure the latch cable grommet in the door.
  2. Install and hand-tighten the bolts that attach the LH rear door latch to the rear of the LH rear door.
  3. Connect the LH rear door electrical harness to the LH door latch connector.
  4. Connect the LH rear door electrical harness to the puddle light, and remove the shop towel from the LH rear door striker.
  5. Adjust LH rear door gap and flush at the LH rear door striker.
  6. Torque the bolts that attach the LH rear door latch to the rear of the LH rear door.
    10 Nm (7.4 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T25 socket
    • 4 in extension
  7. Disconnect the LH rear door electrical harness from the puddle light, and re-install the shop towel into the LH rear door striker.
  8. Remove the LH rear window regulator. See Window Regulator - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
