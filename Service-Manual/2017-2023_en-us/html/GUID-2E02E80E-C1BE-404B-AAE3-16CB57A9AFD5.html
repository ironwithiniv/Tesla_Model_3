Multimeter (LoZ Setting)

This procedure describes how to select the low impedance mode on Fluke multimeters without LoZ mode on the mode selector.
  1. Turn the dial to OFF to switch off the multimeter.
  2. Press and hold the RANGE button.
  3. Turn the dial to the DIODE/CAPACITANCE test mode.
  4. Wait until LCAP shows on the display, and then release the RANGE button.
  5. Press the yellow button to activate the LoZ (low impedance) mode.
    Note
    LoZ will show at the top right corner of the display.
