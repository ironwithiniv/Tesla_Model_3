Comprehensive Vehicle Inspection

Correction code 00030410 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Note
This procedure details how to perform the Comprehensive Vehicle Inspectionk. Make sure to fill out SC-25-00-016, "Comprehensive Vehicle Inspection Checklist". Attach the filled out document to the Service Activity in SCA and provide a printed version to the customer if required.
Parent topic: 0001 - Maintenance Service

Procedure

  1. Inspect the front external lights.
    Note
    Look for cosmetic damages and humidity ingress. It is acceptable for headlights to have some condensation. See TN-19-17-001 for more information and examples.
  2. Inspect the rear external lights.
    Note
    Look for cosmetic damages and humidity ingress. It is acceptable for rear lights to have some condensation. See TN-19-17-001 for more information and examples.
  3. Inspect the aiming of the headlights. See Headlights (Check Aiming) (Inspection).
  4. Inspect the windshield for rock chips and cracks.
    Note
    No damage in the driver’s viewing area, airbag impact area, forward camera viewing area, or within 50 mm of the edge allowed. No more than 3 cracks or chips to the glass are allowed.
  5. Check all emergency release cables and switches.
    Note
    Use caution with front door releases as door glass will not drop and could damage seals or bright molding.
  6. Check the charge port door operation (5 cycles).
    Note
    Inspect and note functionality full range of motion, touch to open, and indicator light. Look for interference with other components, noises such as creaks, groans and clicks, pauses or incomplete actions.
  7. Open the liftgate/trunk to verify proper latch operation.
  8. Close the liftgate/trunk.
  9. Open the LH front door using the exterior handle.
    Note
    Inspect and note functionality for the door handle operation and full range of motion of the door. From outside look for interference with brightwork and door to body. Note noises such as creaks, groans and clicks, pauses or incomplete actions.
  10. Open the LH front door using the interior handle.
    Note
    Inspect and note functionality for the interior door release, puddle lamps, and manual release cable. From inside look for interference with other components, noises such as creaks, groans and clicks, pauses or incomplete actions.
  11. Function check all windows from the master switch.
    Note
    Document any abnormal operation/noises.
  12. Repeat the door handle and window checks on the LH rear door, the RH rear door and the RH front door.
  13. Check all driver controls.
    Note
    Shift stalk, turn signals, steering wheel scroll wheels, horns.
  14. On the touchscreen, touch Controls > Glovebox to open the glove box.
  15. Close the glove box.
  16. Function check the power folding side mirrors.
    Note
    Fold mirrors in/out. Check adjustment of glass.
  17. Function check LH front seat.
    Note
    Check full range of motion of seat. Test lumbar and seat heat/cooling.
  18. Function check LH front seat belt.
    Note
    Latch and unlatch buckle. Quickly pull retractor to check locking function.
  19. Fold the 60 seat in down position.
  20. Fold 60 seat into vertical position.
    Note
    Remove seat protection.
  21. Fold the 40 seat in down position.
  22. Fold the 40 seat into vertical position.
  23. Function check the rear seat belts.
    Note
    Latch and unlatch buckles. Quickly pull retractors to check locking function.
  24. Function check the RH front seat belt.
    Note
    Latch and unlatch buckle. Quickly pull retractor to check locking function.
  25. Function check RH front seat.
    Note
    Check full range of motion of seat.
  26. Verify operation of windshield wipers and nozzles.
    Note
    Ensure hood and doors are closed prior to activating wipers. Inspect and note functionality for wipers and wiper jets. Check for full range of motion, speed of motion, interference with other components, noises such as creaks, groans and clicks, pauses or incomplete actions.
  27. Inspect the upper steering column grub screws. See Grub Screws - Upper Steering Column (Inspect).
  28. Open the hood.
    Note
    Press "Open" button on touchscreen to release latch, Lift lid manually.
  29. Fill the washer reservoir with fluid.
  30. Remove the underhood storage unit. See Underhood Storage Unit (Remove and Replace).
  31. Perform inspection of under hood area.
    Note
    Perform inspection of vehicle under hood area. Check for signs of abnormal damage, wear, heavy rust, wiring damage, or impact damage. Pay attention to safety related component and note any items of significance.
  32. Visually inspect brake fluid level.
  33. Inspect coolant level and top off as necessary.
    Note
    Ensure that fluid level is at Max line.
  34. On the touchscreen, tap the Service Mode "wrench" (at the bottom of the touchscreen UI), and then tap Thermal > Actions > Test Thermal Performance, click Run, and allow the routine to complete.
  35. Inspect the radiator inlet at the front fascia for debris.
    Note
    Clear debris if necessary.
  36. Check the LH and RH rear wheels for rear suspension link play.
    Note
    If rear suspension link play is greater than the "Accepted upper limit play" shown in the video, perform the more thorough rear knuckle inspection (make sure to add correction code 31030110 or 31030130 as needed).
  37. Remove all wheels. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
  38. Measure and record LH rear brake pad thickness.
    Note
    Recommend replacement if 2mm or less. Check both inboard and outboard pads.
  39. Inspect LH rear brake rotor condition.
    Note
    Check for excessive rust or wear. See Brakes for Service limits.
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended: 1082759-00-A - Brake Disc Thickness Gauge Caliper
  40. Repeat the previous steps on the RH rear, RH front and LH front brake rotors and pads.
  41. Perform visual inspection of all brake pipes and hoses.
    Note
    Pay special attention to brake junction points.
  42. Record tread depth for all tires.
    Note
    Measure and record the outer, center, and inner tread depths. Measure the circumferential tire grooves only. Do not place the tire tread gauge probe on the molded treadwear indicators. Recommend replacement if tread depth is 4/32in (3mm) or less.
  43. Check all four tire pressures manually.
    Note
    4x tires, 4x caps, Set to specification if necessary, Refer to the "Tire and Loading Information" label located on the driver side B-pillar, Inspect wheels and tires for any bulges or damage while checking/setting pressures.
  44. Raise the vehicle to a comfortable working height, and lower the lift onto locks.
    CAUTION

    Make sure there is an audible click of the locks on both sides before lowering, otherwise the vehicle may tilt to one side.

    Make sure that the doors are clear of surrounding objects.

  45. Perform inspection of steering components.
    Note
    Check steering gear joints and electrical connections. Verify components are not bent or damaged.
  46. Perform visual inspection of HV battery enclosure.
    Note
    Check for any dents, scrapes, or other damage.
  47. Remove the front aero shield. See Panel - Aero Shield - Front (Remove and Replace).
  48. Remove the rear aero shield. See Panel - Aero Shield - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  49. Perform visual inspection of underside of vehicle for any coolant leaks.
    Note
    Pay special attention to hose connection points.
  50. Perform inspection of vehicle underside components front and rear.
    Note
    Perform inspection of vehicle underside front and rear. Check for signs of abnormal damage, wear, heavy rust, wiring damage, or impact damage. Pay attention to safety related component and note any items of significance.
  51. Perform inspection of suspension components.
    Note
    Check conditions of ball joints and bushings, etc. Inspect for any loose fasteners. Carefully apply pressure with a pry bar as needed.
  52. Inspect all rear lateral and compliance link's rubber bushing.
    Note
    Look for cracks on the rubber part compare the location according to the images and follow the guide:
    • Damage is contained to the green zone: Do not replace the part.
    • Damage is in the yellow zone on one side of the bushing: Do not replace the part.
    • Damage is in the yellow zone on both sides of the bushing: Part needs replacement.
    • Damage is in the red zone: Part needs replacement.
  53. Install the rear aero shield. See Panel - Aero Shield - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  54. Install the front aero shield. See Panel - Aero Shield - Front (Remove and Replace).
  55. Install all wheels. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
  56. Install the underhood storage unit. See Underhood Storage Unit (Remove and Replace).
  57. Remove the vehicle from the lift.
  58. Run ODIN routine PROC_ICE_X_ENTER-SERVICE-MODEvia Toolbox:(link)
  59. Check for active alerts.
    Note
    Check alert using UI via service mode > active alerts" to "Check alerts using UI via Service Mode > Service Alerts > Active Alerts.
  60. Update vehicle firmware if available.
    Note
    Select the clock on the center display, or go to the Software tab, then select install now to start firmware update. Select Install Now, Tap count down timer until clock runs down to 0, Check the center display and ensure the firmware package is installing.
  61. Run ODIN routine PROC_ICE_X_EXIT-SERVICE-MODEvia Toolbox:(link)
  62. Perform a road test using a series of ABS-active stops and ensure proper brake pedal feel and performance
    Note
    The vehicle must stop completely within 25 feet (7.6 meters) at 20 mph (32 kph), Confirm that no excessive vibration or noise is present
  63. Inspect vehicle alignment
    Note
    Confirm steering wheel is straight and no vibrations or noises are felt or heard from the suspension, On a flat road, at 35 mph (56 kph), the vehicle does not pull in either direction
