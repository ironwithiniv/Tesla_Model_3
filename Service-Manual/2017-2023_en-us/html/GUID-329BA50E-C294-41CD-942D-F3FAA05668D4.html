0006 - Periodic Roadworthiness Inspections

Depending on local regulations, Tesla vehicles periodically may need to undergo inspection or be taken to a third-party test centre to have their test evaluation and certification. This website contains Tesla-specific details aimed to facilitate the inspection such as VIN locations, jacking points, settings for free wheeling and how to complete the brake tests. This website is not intended, and should not be relied upon, as a comprehensive overview of period inspection requirements.
