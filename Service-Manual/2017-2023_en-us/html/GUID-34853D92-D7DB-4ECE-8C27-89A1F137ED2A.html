Spoke Cover - Steering Wheel - Lower (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 32051822 0.24 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-11-03: Updated the specification of the bolts (x4) that attach the bright surround to the steering wheel.
Parent topic: 3205 - Upper Column, Steering Wheel

Torque Specifications

Table 1. Torque Specifications: Spoke Cover - Steering Wheel - Lower (Remove and Replace) / Surround - Steering Wheel (Remove and Replace)
Description Torque Value Recommended Tools Reuse/Replace Notes
Bolts (x4) that attach the bright surround to the steering wheel
2.5 Nm (1.8 lbs-ft)
  • Torx T25 bit
Reuse
Bolts that attach the LH/RH steering wheel switch/steering controller/lower spoke cover to the bright surround
0.9 Nm (8 lbs-in)
  • Torx T10 bit
Reuse

Remove

  1. Disconnect LV power. See 12V/LV Power (Disconnect and Connect).
  2. Remove the driver airbag. See Airbag - Driver (Remove and Replace).
  3. Disconnect the steering wheel electrical wiring harness from the connector.
  4. Disconnect the electrical harness from the LH steering wheel switch connector.
  5. Disconnect the electrical harness from the RH steering wheel switch connector.
  6. Release the clips that attach the horn assembly, and then move the horn assembly to the side.
  7. Disconnect the electrical harness from the lower connector, and then remove the electrical harness from the steering wheel.
  8. Remove the bolts that attach the steering wheel surround to the steering wheel.
    Note
    Pry the lower portion of the bright surround assembly away from the steering wheel, and then work your way around.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T25 bit
  9. Remove the bolts (x2) that attach the steering wheel controller to the steering wheel surround, and then remove the controller.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T10 bit
  10. Remove the bolts that attach the lower spoke cover to the steering wheel surround.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T10 bit

Install

  1. Position the lower spoke cover onto the steering wheel surround, and then install the bolts.
    0.9 Nm (8 lbs-in)
    Note
    Insert the lower edge of the lower spoke.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T10 bit
  2. Install the bolts that attach the steering wheel controller to the steering wheel surround.
    0.9 Nm (8 lbs-in)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T10 bit
  3. Position the steering wheel surround onto the steering wheel, and then install the bolts.
    2.5 Nm (1.8 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T25 bit
  4. Install the electrical harness into the steering wheel, and then connect the electrical harness to the lower connector.
  5. Move the horn assembly to the center, and then fasten the clips that attach the horn assembly.
  6. Connect the electrical harness to the RH steering wheel switch connector.
  7. Connect the electrical harness to the LH steering wheel switch connector.
  8. Connect the steering wheel electrical harness to the connector.
  9. Install the driver airbag. See Airbag - Driver (Remove and Replace).
  10. Connect LV power. See 12V/LV Power (Disconnect and Connect).
