Suspension - Front (Inspection)

Correction code 31010700314 FRT 0.30 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Inspection Summary

Component Inspection Description Comment
Front Upper Control Arms (FUCA) With wheels on the ground, turn the steering wheel left/right to check for FUCA creaking noise See video FUCA Creaking Noise (step 1)
Front lateral links With wheels on the ground, turn the steering wheel full lock left/right to check for lateral link clicking noise See video Lateral Link Clicking Noise (step 1)
Front lateral links While raising the vehicle, listen for creaks in the suspension See video Lateral Link Creaking Noise (step 4)
Front aeroshield panel Inspect for contact between lateral link and front aeroshield panel See step 6
Front ball joints (condition) Visually inspect all front ball joints See steps 8, 9, 11, 12, 14, 15
Front ball joints (play) Inspect for excessive play on all front ball joints See steps 11, 12, 14, 15
Front compliance links Visually inspect compliance link bushings See steps 10 and 13
Front stabilizer bar bushings and links Visually inspect stabilizer bar bushings and links See steps 17 and 18
Front dampers and springs Visually inspect for signs of oil leakage, broken springs or any material broken off See step 19
Front lateral link bushes Turn steering wheel and inspect lateral link bushings See steps 20 through 30

Inspection Details

  1. Test for front suspension noise.
    Note
    While parked on the two post lift, before jacking up the vehicle, close all windows, release the brake pedal, and then turn the steering wheel to the left and right.
    • While slightly turning the steering wheel to the left and right, listen for creaking noises, indicating FUCA issues. See Toolbox article #54817.
    • While fully turning the steering wheel, listen for clicking noises near the end of the turning cycle, indicating front lateral link issues. See Toolbox article #6960000.
  2. Lower the LH front window.
  3. Prepare the vehicle for lifting. See Raise Vehicle - 2 Post Lift.
  4. Inspect the front lateral links for noise.
    Note
    During raising the vehicle, pay attention to creaking noises while the vehicle is being raised, before the front wheels leave the floor. This is an indication of front lateral link wear. Confirm their condition in later steps.
  5. Fully raise the vehicle.
  6. Inspect the front aero shield for contact with the lateral link.
    Note
    Check whether the front aero shield is touching the LH and/or RH front lateral link. Perform rework as per SB-24-12-009 when this is not yet done.
  7. Remove the front aero shield panel. See Panel - Aero Shield - Front (Remove and Replace).
  8. Inspect the LH front ball joints.
    Note
    Visually Inspect the ball joint for cracks, grease leakage, unseated boot and/or boot puncture.
  9. Inspect the RH front ball joints.
    Note
    Visually Inspect ball joints for cracks, grease leakage, unseated boot and/or boot puncture.
  10. Inspect the inner bushing of the LH compliance link.
    Note
    Inspect for unusual cracks. Pry to test for excessive movement. If cracking is found in the red highlighted area, then proceed to replacement.
  11. Inspect LH compliance link to knuckle ball joint
    Note
    Inspect the ball joint for cracks, grease leakage, unseated boot, boot puncture, and ball joint free play. Replace any damaged component
  12. Inspect the LH lateral link to knuckle ball joint.
    Note
    Inspect the ball joint for cracks, grease leakage, unseated boot, boot puncture, and ball joint free play. Replace any damaged component
  13. Inspect the inner bushing of the RH compliance link.
    Note
    Inspect for unusual cracks. Pry to test for excessive movement. If cracking is found in the red highlighted area, then proceed to replacement.
  14. Inspect the RH compliance link to knuckle ball joint.
    Note
    Inspect ball joint for cracks, grease leakage, unseated boot, boot puncture, and ball joint free play. Replace any damaged component.
  15. Inspect the RH lateral link to knuckle ball joint.
    Note
    Inspect ball joint for cracks, grease leakage, unseated boot, boot puncture, and ball joint free play. Replace any damaged component.
  16. Inspect the LH and RH front stabilizer bar bushings for unusual cracks. Use a pry bar to confirm there is no exessive movement.
  17. Lower the vehicle to a comfortable working height.
  18. Inspect the LH and RH stabilizer bar links.
  19. Visually inspect the LH and RH front dampers and springs for signs of oil leakage, craks or material that is broken off.
  20. Lower the vehicle until the tires are touching the ground.
  21. Turn the steering wheel fully to the left.
  22. Lock the steering wheel into position.
  23. Fully raise the vehicle.
  24. Inspect the inner bushing of the RH lateral link.
    Note
    Inspect for unusual cracks. Pry to test for excessive movement. If cracking is found in the red highlighted area, then proceed to replacement.
  25. Lower the vehicle until the tires are touching the ground.
  26. Remove the steering wheel holder.
  27. Turn steering wheel fully to the right.
  28. Lock the steering wheel into position.
  29. Fully raise the vehicle.
  30. Inspect the inner bushing of the LH lateral link.
    Note
    Inspect for unusual cracks. Pry to test for excessive movement. If cracking is found in the red highlighted area, then proceed to replacement.
  31. Install the front aero shield panel. See Panel - Aero Shield - Front (Remove and Replace)
  32. Fully lower the vehicle.
  33. Remove the steering wheel holder.
