Trim - Side - Trunk - RH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 15245102 0.30 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 1524 - Luggage Compartment Trim

Remove

  1. Remove the RH 2nd row seat back. See Seat Back - 2nd Row - RH (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the RH side trunk carpet clips (x2) from the body studs located under the 2nd row seat cushion.
  3. Raise the trunk carpet over the ISOFIX bars on the RH side.
  4. Remove the trunk sill trim panel. See Trim - Sill - Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the trunk garnish. See Garnish - Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  6. Release the clips that attach the puddle light to the RH trunk side trim, disconnect the electrical harness from the puddle light electrical connector, and then remove the puddle light from the vehicle.
  7. Remove the push clips that attach the RH trunk side trim to the body, and remove the side trim from the vehicle.

Install

Installation procedure is the reverse of removal.
