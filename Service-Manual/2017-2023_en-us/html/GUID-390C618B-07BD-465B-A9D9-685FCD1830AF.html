Warning

Only technicians who have completed all required certification courses are permitted to perform this procedure. Tesla recommends third party service provider technicians undergo equivalent training before performing this procedure. For more information on Tesla Technician requirements, or descriptions of the subject matter for third parties, see HV Certification Requirements. Proper personal protective equipment (PPE) and insulating HV gloves with a minimum rating of class 0 (1000V) must be worn at all times a high voltage cable, busbar, or fitting is handled. Refer to Tech Note TN-15-92-003, High Voltage Awareness Care Points for additional safety information.

Discharge

WARNING: To conform with local laws, High Voltage Lithium Batteries must be discharged before packing for shipment. Additionally, HV batteries must be discharged before being put into storage. HV batteries to be shipped must have a State of Charge (SoC) of less than 25% or 45%, depending on the region. If the method of shipment is unknown or uncertain, or the battery is to be stored, it is recommended to discharge the HV battery to less than 25%.

WARNING: After discharge, wait 20 minutes and verify that the SoC is less than the targeted value. This waiting period allows the open circuit voltage (OCV) to settle, which is critical for accurate measurements. Continue discharging if the SoC does not meet the required level.

Make sure to collect all HV battery shipping data using the HV Battery Communication Tool, preferably before removing the HV battery from the vehicle.

WARNING: Do not ship or store an HV battery that has a loss of high voltage isolation. Verify HV battery isolation before packing for shipment or storage. Use the HV Battery Communication Tool, described in Toolbox article #41977 or Customer Document CD-21-16-003, to determine if the HV battery parameters are within safe limits for shipment or storage.
Note
If possible, perform the discharge procedure and collect HV battery shipping data before HV battery removal. This method requires less time and user interaction than to use the Battery Discharge Tool after the battery has been removed. The Battery Discharge Tool can only be used by certified technicians..
Note
The steps of the discharge procedure are mostly touch time, and can be performed by the Service Advisors/Front of House team as well as Service Technicians..
  1. Enable Service Mode Plus. See Service Mode Plus.
  2. Identify the vehicle's current SOC Max: In the Service Mode Plus menu, touch High Voltage > HV Battery, and then touch the large HV battery diagram to display the HV Battery information window.
    • If the SOC Max is less than the SOC shipping threshold, skip to step 6 of this sub-procedure.
    • If the SOC Max is greater than the SOC shipping threshold, continue to the next step.
  3. Move the vehicle to a location where the battery can be safely discharged unattended.
  4. In the Service Mode Plus menu, touch High Voltage > HV Battery.
  5. In the Tools section, touch Start HV Discharge, and then in the window that opens, touch Run.
    Note
    The discharge routine starts and will automatically stop when the SOC Max value for surface transport has been attained.
  6. After discharge is complete, use the HV Battery Communication Tool to collect HV battery shipping data, and then move the vehicle to the service bay.

  38. Inspect the LH carpet for perforations. If perforations are present, separate the carpet and inspect for a fastener below the LH front seat.
    Note
    This step may vary depending on the build date of the vehicle.
  39. Remove the HV battery bolt below the rear end of the LH front seat.
  40. Move LH front seat to center location and seat back to upright position
    Note
    This step only applies if HV battery is equipped with bolts below LH front seat. Vehicle will be powered down and the seat needs to be in this position to allow both bolts to be installed hand tight.

    2. Make sure the thread stop washer is installed and tightened to the bottom of the lift adapter. If the thread stop washer is not installed, do not continue using the lift adapter.

      Warning
      Make sure the adapter is install in the correct orientation.
    4. Position the lift arms so that the lift adapters align with the datum hole on the vehicle.
    5. Lift the lift arms to fully seat the lift pads into the vehicle.
    6. Make sure all 4 adapters are fully seated into the vehicle.

  82. Perform a platter enclosure coolant drain. See Platter Enclosure Coolant (Drain and Refill).

  97. Remove the HV battery rear drive unit connector from the HV battery header.
  98. Support front portion of rear subframe
  99. Remove large bolt securing RH shear plate and subframe to body and remove shear plate
    Note
    1x bolt, 21mm, 125 Nm, Discard after removal
  100. Hand tighten the bolt that attaches the RH side of the subframe to the body.
  101. Remove the large bolt that attaches the LH shear plate and subframe to the body and remove the shear plate.
  102. Hand tighten the bolt that attaches the LH side of the subframe to the body.
  103. Remove the support stand from under the vehicle.
  104. Inspect the replacement HV battery assembly.
    Note
    If installing a new battery, then ski removal from the old HV battery is not necessary. If installing a remanufactured battery, then remove battery center ski from the old HV battery.
  105. Disconnect the ancillary harness connector from the RH rear of the HV battery.
  106. Release the clips that attach the PTC heater and A/C compressor electrical harness bracket to the front of the HV battery, and then separate the bracket from the battery.
    Note
    The PTC heater HV connector is only for vehicles without a heat pump.
  107. With an assistant, remove the end bolts that attach the center rail ski to the HV battery, and then remove the rail ski from the battery.
  108. Place HV battery table into position and lower vehicle to support HV battery
    Note
    Line up the table so the center 4 bolts are accessible through the opening in the table. Adjust the final battery table position using the adjustable caster jacks on the table.
  109. Remove the bolts (x8) that attach the HV battery LH and RH support brackets to the vehicle.
  110. Remove the front center bolt that attaches the HV battery to the vehicle.
  111. Remove the front bolts that attach the HV battery to the vehicle.
    Note
    The number of fasteners on newer vehicles may vary.
  112. Remove the bolts (x16) that attach the HV battery to the vehicle.
    Note

  135. Remove the pyro disconnect from the ancillary bay.

Install


  2. Open the crate top housing of the new HV battery pack.
    Note
    Confirm part number specific to vehicles configuration. See https://epcadmin.teslamotors.com/epcadmin.
  3. Put on HV insulating gloves and leather over gloves.
  4. Remove the bolts that attach the ancillary bay cover to the HVC.
    Note
    Fastener count may vary on newer vehicles. Inspect the bolts for plastisol rubber residue and replace bolts if found. Newer bolts are not equipped with plastisol.
  5. Release the ancillary bay cover bolts from the old HV battery.
  6. Remove the ancillary bay cover from the HV battery.
  7. Inspect the DC input busbar of the new HV battery pack. If new pack is different from the old pack, rework the DC input. See Busbar DC Input to Harness DC Input (Retrofit).
    Note
    Add correction code 16302806 as a separate correction to the Service Visit.
  8. Compare the ancillary bay cover from the new to the old HV battery. If the new pack has different mounting points for the second row inner rails, replace the ancillary bay cover from the old to the new pack. See Cover - Ancillary Bay (Swap) (Battery On Table)
    Note
    Add correction code 16300107 as a separate correction to the Service Visit.
  9. Install the HVC logic cap.
  10. Inspect the ancillary bay for any missing insulators.
    Note
    Install service insulator kit before continuing to the next step. The number of bolts on newer vehicles may vary.
  11. Lower the HV controller back into place.
  12. Remove the battery pyro and shunt insulators.
    Note
    Make sure to wear proper PPE from this step onwards until the ancillary bay cover has been reinstalled.
  13. Remove the bolts that attach the pyro disconnect to the ancillary bay.
    Note
    Discard the bolts after removal
  14. Remove the pyro disconnect from the ancillary bay.
    Note
    1x connector from the bottom. Lift pyro disconnect straight up and out of the ancillary bay.
  15. Remove the HVC logic cap.
  16. Remove the ancillary bay Service Insulator Kit.
  17. Position ancillary bay cover to THE HV battery for installation.
    Note
    Make sure that all accessible insulators are present. Wearing Electrical Protective Gloves is not necessary once all HV components are covered. If the new part does not have the volcano bracket bolt, remove it from the old part and install it on the new part.
  18. Install the ancillary bay cover. See Cover - Ancillary Bay (Remove and Install).
  19. Install the old Pyro Disconnect onto the old HV battery.
    Note
    Attach pyro to ancillary bay cover volcano brackets with zip ties. If ancillary bay cover is not equipped with volcano brackets, then attach pyro to RH rear corner of HV Battery with zip ties and add tape as notification as shown in illustration. Install a piece of paper roll under the pyro disconnect.
  20. Remove the nuts that attach the new HV battery to the crate.
  21. Position the gantry over the new HV battery crate and attach the H-frame to the HV battery.
  22. Lift the new HV battery out of the crate.
  23. Move the gantry away from the HV battery crate and over the second HV battery table.
  24. Lower the HV battery onto the second battery table.
    Note
    Make sure to align the HV battery onto the center ski. If the original pack is RWD and supplied pack is DM, center ski transfer will be required.
  25. Release the H-frame from the HV battery and raise the gantry.
  26. Remove the HV battery from under the gantry.
  27. Position the gantry over the old HV battery.
  28. Lower the H-frame onto the old HV battery and attach the H-frame to the HV battery.
    Note
    8x bolts, 8mm Allen
  29. Raise the old HV battery above the height of the HV battery crate.
  30. Position the HV battery crate underneath the old HV battery.
  31. Lower the old HV battery into the crate.
    Note
    Align the 4 studs and do not damage the battery.
  32. Remove the H-frame from the old HV battery.
    Note
    8x bolts, 8mm Allen, Hand tight
  33. Raise the gantry and H-frame from the old HV battery.
  34. Lower the gantry and remove the HV battery H-frame from the gantry.
  35. Install the nuts (x4) that attach the HV battery to crate hand tight.
  36. Close the crate top housing of the old HV battery.
  37. Install the logic cap onto the new HV battery pack.
  38. Remove the lower bolts (x6) that attach the rear battery enclosure skid plate.
  39. Remove the upper nut that attaches the HV battery rear skid plate.
    Note
    Fastener count may vary on older built vehicles.
  40. Remove the rear battery enclosure skid plate from the HV battery pack.
  41. Verify if the PTC fuse is installed inside the ancillary bay. If the fuse is not installed, proceed with Fuse - Cabin Heater - HV Battery (Battery On Table) (Install) and add correction code 16304026 as a separate correction to the Service Visit.
    Note
    Measure resistance at the rear HV header for the ancillary harness between PTC B+ and PTC B-, the 2 pins facing the rear of the car are +/- for the PTC. If resistance is OL, the fuse is not installed. If resistance is anything higher than 50 kOhm, then the fuse is installed. Be extra careful when measuring to not widen the female pins on the HV ancillary header during measurement.
  42. Remove the nuts (x2) that attach the RDU HV cable bracket to the HV battery and remove the RDU HV cable.
    Note
    Number of fasteners may vary on newer vehicles.
  43. Disconnect the RDU HV cable from the HV battery.
  44. Install the powertrain supply hose to the upper clips on the RH side of the HV battery, and then fasten the clips (x5) to attach the hose to the HV battery.
  45. Connect the powertrain supply hose fitting to the RH rear underside of the ancillary bay, and then fasten the clip.
  46. Install the powertrain return hose to the lower clips on the RH side of the HV battery, and then fasten the clips (x5) to attach the hose to the HV battery.
  47. Verify if the new HV battery front coolant passthrough fittings (x4) are male fittings or female fittings.
    1. If the HV battery front coolant passthrough fittings (x4) are male fittings, no further action is required. Continue to the next step in this procedure.
    2. If the HV battery front coolant passthrough fittings (x4) are female fittings, perform the HV battery front manifold coolant passthrough hose retrofit procedure. See Hose - Passthrough - Front Manifold - HV Battery (Male Coupler) (Retrofit).
  48. Install the front hose assembly onto the HV battery and attach the RH outer coolant hose connection.
