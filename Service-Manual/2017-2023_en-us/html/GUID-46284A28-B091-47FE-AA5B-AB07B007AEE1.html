Keeping the Vehicle in Neutral

As a standard safety feature, Tesla vehicles automatically engage the parking brake when it detects that the driver is leaving the vehicle, even if Neutral has been selected. Enable Tow Mode to make sure that the wheels roll freely without an occupant in the vehicle. Refer to Activate Tow Mode in the Owner's Manual.
Warning
Before entering Tow mode, make sure that the vehicle is on flat ground and the wheels are chocked to avoid movement. Do not work on a vehicle that does not have its wheels chocked. Doing so can cause serious damage, bodily injury, or death.
