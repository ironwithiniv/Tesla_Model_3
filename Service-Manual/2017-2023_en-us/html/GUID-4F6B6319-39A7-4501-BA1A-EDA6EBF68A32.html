Actuator - Lower Mode (Non-Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 18102502 3.54 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 1810 - Cabin HVAC

Remove

  1. Remove the center console. See Center Console (Remove and Install).
  2. Release the IP harness clips (x2) and carpet clip from the RH cross car beam.
  3. Remove the bolt that attaches the RH cross car beam to the HVAC assembly.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
    • 2 in extension
    • 10 mm 12-point deep socket
  4. Remove the bolt that attaches the RH cross car beam bracket to the cross car beam.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
    • 13mm socket
    • 3 in extension
  5. Remove the nut that attaches the RH cross car beam bracket to the body.
    Note
    Slightly pull the RH carpet towards the RH side of vehicle. Take note of the direction of the cross car beam bracket during removal.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
    • 13mm socket
    • 3 in extension
  6. Remove the RH cross car beam bracket from the vehicle.
  7. Insert the inflatable air bag up to the bottom of the lower mode actuator, then inflate it between the RH cross car beam bracket and lower HVAC assembly.
  8. Remove the bolts(x2) that attach the lower mode actuator to the HVAC assembly.
    Note
    A mini ratchet bit can be inserted behind the harness to reach the forward fastener.
  9. Disconnect the lower mode actuator electrical connector, and then remove the actuator from the HVAC assembly.

Install

  1. Connect the lower mode actuator electrical connector.
  2. Position the lower mode actuator on the HVAC assembly, align the guide, and then push the lower mode actuator towards the left to install.
  3. Install the bolts(x2) that attach the lower mode actuator to the HVAC assembly.
    1.2 Nm (.9 lbs-ft)
    Note
    A mini ratchet bit can be inserted behind the harness to reach the forward fastener.
  4. Deflate and remove the inflatable air bag between the RH cross car beam bracket and lower HVAC assembly.
  5. Position the RH cross car beam bracket to the body.
  6. Loosely install the nut that attaches the RH cross car beam bracket to the body.
  7. Loosely install the bolt that attaches the RH cross car beam bracket to the cross car beam.
  8. Loosely install the bolt that attaches the cross car beam to the HVAC assembly.
  9. Tighten the nut that attaches the RH cross car beam bracket to the body.
    25 Nm (18.4 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
    • 13mm socket
    • 3 in extension
  10. Tighten the bolt that attaches the RH cross car beam bracket to the cross car beam.
    25 Nm (18.4 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
    • 13mm socket
    • 3 in extension
  11. Tighten the bolt that attaches the RH cross car beam to the HVAC assembly.
    5.5 Nm (4.1 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
    • 10 mm 12-point deep socket
  12. Install the IP harness clips (x2) and carpet clip to the RH cross car beam.
  13. Install the center console. See Center Console (Remove and Install).
