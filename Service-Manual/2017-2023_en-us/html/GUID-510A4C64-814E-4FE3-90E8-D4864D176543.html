Brake Rotor - Front - LH (Measure Runout)

Correction code 33010100 0.35 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 3301 - Brake Discs and Calipers

Procedure

  1. Open the LH front door and lower the LH front window.
  2. Apply the brake pedal depressor.
    Note
    Verify brake pedal is fully pressed to avoid wheels from spinning.
  3. Raise and support the vehicle. See Raise Vehicle - 2 Post Lift.
  4. Remove the LH front wheel. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
  5. Remove the bolt that attaches the LH front rotor to the knuckle.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  6. Install the brake rotor run out clamping plate onto the rotor.
    100 Nm (73.7 lbs-ft)
    Note
    This simulates the wheel being installed, thus preventing inaccurate measurements.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 21 mm deep socket
  7. Lower the vehicle to suitable height, and then remove the brake pedal depressor.
  8. Install the brake rotor run out flex bar onto the front tie rod nut and setup for reading.
  9. Measure 10 mm inward from the front rotor end and align digital indicator. Press indicator rod and zero measurement.
    Note
    Make sure the indicator rod is pressed to allow travel in both directions and does not bottom out.
  10. Mark the top center of the front rotor to identify the starting point.
  11. Slowly rotate the front rotor 1 full revolution.
    Note
    Record the highest positive and lowest negative values as shown on the digital indicator as you complete the rotation.
    Note
    The total runout is the difference between the highest positive and lowest negative values and must not exceed 0.050 mm. If the total runout exceeds 0.050 mm, the brake rotor must be replaced due to excessive runout.
  12. Measure 25 mm inward from the front rotor end and align digital indicator. Press indicator rod and zero measurement.
    Note
    Make sure indicator rod is pressed to allow travel in both directions and does not bottom out.
  13. Slowly rotate the front rotor 1 full revolution.
    Note
    Record the highest positive and lowest negative values as shown on the digital indicator as you complete the rotation.
    Note
    The total runout is the difference between the highest positive and lowest negative values and must not exceed 0.050 mm. If the total runout exceeds 0.050 mm, the brake rotor must be replaced due to excessive runout.
  14. Measure 40 mm inward from the front rotor end and align digital indicator. Press indicator rod and zero measurement.
    Note
    Make sure indicator rod is pressed to allow travel in both directions and does not bottom out.
  15. Slowly rotate the front rotor 1 full revolution.
    Note
    Record the highest positive and lowest negative values as shown on the digital indicator as you complete the rotation.
    Note
    The total runout is the difference between the highest positive and lowest negative values and must not exceed 0.050 mm. If the total runout exceeds 0.050 mm, the brake rotor must be replaced due to excessive runout.
  16. Remove the brake rotor run out flex bar from the front tie rod nut.
  17. Lower the vehicle to suitable height and install the brake pedal depressor.
  18. Remove the brake rotor runout clamping plate from the front rotor.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 21 mm deep socket
  19. Install the bolt that attaches the front rotor to the knuckle.
    5 Nm (3.7 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  20. Install the LH front wheel. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
  21. Remove the brake pedal depressor.
