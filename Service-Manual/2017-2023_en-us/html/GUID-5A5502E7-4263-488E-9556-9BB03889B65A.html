Horn Assembly (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 17531202 0.12 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

    Parent topic: 1753 - Horns

    Torque Specifications

    Table 1. Torque Specifications: Horn Assembly (Remove and Replace)
    Description Torque Value Recommended Tools Reuse/Replace Notes
    Bolt that attaches the horn assembly to the front subframe
    12 Nm (8.8 lbs-ft)
    • 10 mm socket
    		 Reuse Recommend flashlight for visibility

    Remove

    Illustrations show LH front wheel removed, but this is not required for the procedure.
    1. Turn the steering wheel fully to the left.
    2. Release the clips (x7) that attach the front portion of the LH front wheel arch liner to the vehicle, and then move the wheel arch liner towards the rear of the vehicle to perform the following steps.
    3. Carefully insert a inflatable air bag between the wheel liner and the front fascia assembly, and then inflate the air bag.
      Note
      This is to gain access to the horn assembly.
    4. Remove the bolt that attaches the horn assembly to the front subframe.
      Note
      Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
      • 10 mm socket
    5. Disconnect the electrical connectors (x2) from the horn assembly.
    6. Remove the horn assembly from the vehicle.

    Install

    1. Position the horn assembly for installation, and then connect the electrical connectors (x2) to the horn assembly.
    2. Install the bolt that attaches the horn assembly to the front subframe.
      12 Nm (8.8 lbs-ft)
      Note
      Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
      • 10 mm socket
    3. Deflate and remove the inflatable air bag between the wheel liner and the front fascia.
    4. Install the clips (x7) that attach the front portion of the LH front wheel arch liner to the vehicle.
    5. Center the steering wheel.
