Torque Specifications

Table 1. Torque Specifications: Door and Hinge Cover - Charge Port (Remove and Replace)
Description Torque Value Recommended Tools Reuse/Replace Notes
Screws (x2) that attach the charge port door to the hinge
1 Nm (.7 lbs-ft)
  • Torx T20 bit
 Reuse

Remove

  1. Open the charge port door.
  2. Using a small screwdriver to pry at the hinge cover mounting location, slightly twist and slide the hinge cover down.
  3. Remove the screws (x2) that attach the charge port door to the hinge, and then remove the charge port door from the vehicle.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T20 bit
  4. Adjust the position of the charge port door hinge appropriately as needed, and then slide up to remove the hinge cover.

Install

  1. Install the hinge cover by sliding it down from the top of the hinge.
  2. Slide the hinge cover down to access the mounting point for the charge port door.
  3. Lift the locking tab and push the cover further down to uncover the bolt holes.
    Note
    Use a hook to hold the hinge if needed. Do not overstress the cover.
  4. Position the charge port door onto the hinge, and then install the screws (x2) that attach the charge port door to the hinge.
    1 Nm (.7 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T20 bit
  5. Slide the hinge cover up towards the charge port door.
  6. Close the charge port door.
    CAUTION
    Do not try to manually close the charge port door.
