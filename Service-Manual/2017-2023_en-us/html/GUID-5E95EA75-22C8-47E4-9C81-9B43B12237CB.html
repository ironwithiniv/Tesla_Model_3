Horn - Steering Wheel (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 32050012 0.18 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-11-03: Updated the specification of the bolts (x4) that attach the bright surround to the steering wheel.
Parent topic: 3205 - Upper Column, Steering Wheel

Torque Specifications

Table 1. Torque Specifications: Horn - Steering Wheel (Remove and Replace)
Description Torque Value Recommended Tools Reuse/Replace Notes
Bolts (x4) that attach the bright surround to the steering wheel
2.5 Nm (1.8 lbs-ft)
  • Torx T25 bit
Reuse

Remove

  1. Disconnect 12V power. See 12V/LV Power (Disconnect and Connect).
  2. Remove the driver airbag. See Airbag - Driver (Remove and Replace).
  3. Disconnect the steering wheel wiring harness electrical connector.
  4. Disconnect the LH steering wheel switch electrical connector. Repeat this step for the RH steering wheel switch electrical connector.
  5. Release clips (x3) that attach the horn to the steering wheel assembly.
    Note
    Pull the horn assembly straight out to release it.
  6. Remove the bolts (x4) that attach the steering wheel bright surround to the steering wheel.
    Note
    Pry the lower portion of the bright surround assembly away from the steering wheel, and then work your way around.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T25 bit
  7. Disconnect the lower connection from the steering wheel controller, and then remove the steering wheel horn assembly from the steering wheel.
  8. Disconnect the steering wheel horn assembly harness electrical connectors.
  9. Release the clip that attaches the steering wheel harness to the horn assembly.

Install

  1. Fasten the clip that attaches the steering wheel harness to the horn assembly.
  2. Connect the steering wheel horn assembly harness electrical connectors.
  3. Position the steering wheel surround onto the steering wheel, and then install the bolts.
    2.5 Nm (1.8 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T25 bit
  4. Position the horn assembly to the steering wheel, and then fasten the clips.
  5. Connect the LH steering wheel switch electrical connector. Repeat this step for the RH steering wheel switch electrical connector.
  6. Connect the lower electrical connector on the steering wheel controller.
  7. Connect the steering wheel electrical wiring harness connector.
  8. Install the driver airbag. See Airbag - Driver (Remove and Replace).
  9. Connect 12V power. See 12V/LV Power (Disconnect and Connect).
