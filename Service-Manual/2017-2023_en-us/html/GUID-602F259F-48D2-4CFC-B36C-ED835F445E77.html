Trim - Light - Side Repeater - Front - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 17403022 0.12 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Note
If the vehicle is being retrofit with the aero rib trim, remove the current LH and RH trim pieces, and replace them with the aero rib trim.
Note
When replacing the side repeater and the vehicle is originally equipped with a chrome aero rib trim, replace the black aero rib trim with the new chrome aero rib trim on the new side repeater.
Parent topic: 1740 - Exterior Lights

Remove

  1. Apply protective tape to the LH fender around the LH front side repeater light.
  2. Carefully insert a trim tool under the rear edge of the LH front side repeater trim, and then separate it from the LH front repeater light assembly.
    CAUTION
    Use caution and avoid contact with the body panel.
    Note
    Discard the cover after removal.
  3. Use IPA wipes to clean the surface area on the LH front side repeater light.
    Note
    Allow at least one minute of dry time.

Install

  1. Remove the adhesive on the LH front side repeater light trim.
  2. Align the top of the LH front side repeater light trim onto the LH front side repeater light assembly, and then apply equal and firm pressure to fully attach the trim onto the assembly.
  3. Remove the protective wrap from the LH front side repeater light trim.
  4. Remove the protective tape from the LH fender around the LH front side repeater light.
