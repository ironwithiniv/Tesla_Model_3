Hose - Passthrough - Front Manifold - HV Battery (Male Coupler) (Retrofit)

Correction code 18309906 FRT 0.06 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Warning
This procedure is a DRAFT. Although it has been validated, Warnings and Cautions might be missing. Follow safety requirements and use extreme caution when working on or near High Voltage systems and components.
  1. Position front manifold coolant hose onto HV battery
  2. Install front hose assembly onto HV battery and secure RH outer coolant hose connection
    Note
    Perform a push-pull-push test to make sure the hose is fully seated. Do not wiggle the hose at the connection.
  3. Connect RH inner coolant hose to front of HV battery
    Note
    Perform a push-pull-push test to make sure the hose is fully seated. Do not wiggle the hose at the connection.
  4. Connect LH inner coolant hose to front of HV battery
    Note
    Perform a push-pull-push test to make sure the hose is fully seated. Do not wiggle the hose at the connection.
  5. Connect LH outer coolant hose to front of HV battery
    Note
    Perform a push-pull-push test to make sure the hose is fully seated. Do not wiggle the hose at the connection.
  6. Remove plug then connect HV battery front manifold coolant hose
    Note
    Perform a push-pull-push test to make sure the hose is fully seated. Do not wiggle the hose at the connection.
