On-Board Diagnostics (OBD) Connector

If the vehicle is equipped with an OBD connector, it is located on the underside of the dashboard in the driver’s footwell. Some Model 3 and Model Y might not have an OBD connector installed, as this was not mandatory for electric vehicles until 2024.

For more information, refer to Diagnostic Cables.
