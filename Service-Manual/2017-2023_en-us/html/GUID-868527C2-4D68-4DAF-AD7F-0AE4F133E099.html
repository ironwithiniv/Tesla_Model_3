Seal - Body - Side - Front - Primary - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 11700302 0.18 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 1170 - Seals Body Closures

Remove

  1. Remove the LH B-pillar applique. See Applique - B-Pillar - LH (Remove and Replace).
  2. Release the clips (x3) that attach the front LH primary body seal to the mid B-pillar, the upper B-pillar, and the mid A-pillar.
  3. Pull on the seal to remove it from the body.

Install

  1. Loosely install the front LH primary body seal to the body.
  2. If the vehicle has markings on the B-pillar, skip the next step and proceed with alignment and installation.
  3. Apply tape on the B-pillar and mark a line on the tape 33 mm (1.3 in) below and parallel to the top of the B-pillar body opening.
  4. Align the rear LH primary seal joint line to the marking on the B-pillar.
  5. Fasten the clips (x3) that attach the front LH primary body seal to the mid A-pillar, the upper B-pillar, and the mid B-pillar.
  6. Pull the seal strings from the new seal.
  7. Tuck the seal into the bright molding, and make sure that it fits properly to the trim panels and the headliner.
  8. Press the seal into the lower corners, and then install the rest of the seal to the body.
    Note
    Stretch and tuck the seal so that it is evenly distributed around the perimeter.
  9. Install the LH B-pillar applique. See Applique - B-Pillar - LH (Remove and Replace).
  10. Calibrate the windows. See Window Regulator (Recalibrate).
  11. Confirm that the window operates correctly and without any abnormal noise.
