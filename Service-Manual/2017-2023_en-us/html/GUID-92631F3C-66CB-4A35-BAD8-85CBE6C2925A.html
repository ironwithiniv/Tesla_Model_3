Subframe Assembly - Front (Dual Motor) (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 30010312 3.90 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-04-21: Add steps of removing/installing the LH/RH suspension assemblies.
  • 2026-03-31: Added the rivet nut check/installation step.
CAUTION
If performing this procedure as a result of a collision, see the Collision Repair Manual to verify whether the steering gear must be replaced as well.
Equipment:
  • 1498673-00-A KIT, CABLE AXLE REMOVER, MS/MX/M3
  • 1099645-00-C Fixture, Subframe, Model 3
  • 1066521-00-A OTC Lift, 1,650LB Powertrain Table
  • 1130279-00-A Lifting Sling, Drive Unit, Model 3 (NA, APAC)
  • 1130279-01-A Lifting Sling, Drive Unit, Model 3 (EMEA)
CAUTION
Vehicles built before March 25th, 2019 have 1st generation front subframes. Vehicles built on March 25th, 2019 and after have 2nd generation front subframes. When a 1st generation subframe is replaced with a 2nd generation subframe, the 1st generation (discontinued) steering rack, if installed, must be replaced with a 2nd generation (currently available) steering rack. Refer to the Electronic Parts Catalog for the latest information.
Parent topic: 3001 - Chassis and Subframes

Remove

  1. Remove the front subframe assembly from the vehicle. See Subframe Assembly - Front (Dual Motor) (Remove and Install).
  2. Remove the steering rack. See Steering Rack (Dual Motor) (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the bolt that attaches the horn assembly to the front subframe.
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  4. With an assistant, remove and discard the nuts that attach the front stabilizer bar to the front subframe, and then remove the stabilizer bar from the subframe.
  5. Remove the bolts that attach LH and RH crash can brackets to the front subframe, and then set the crash can brackets aside.
  6. Release the clip that attaches the inverter hose to the subframe.
  7. Position the axle remover cable around the inner joint of the LH front drive unit halfshaft, and then use a cable tie to hold the axle remover cable in position.
  8. Hook the axle remover slide hammer on the 2 axle remover cable loops, and then use the slide hammer to remove the halfshaft from the drive unit.
  9. Repeat steps 7 to 8 on the RH front drive unit halfshaft.
  10. Release the locking tab, and then push the handle downward to disconnect the front drive unit inverter logic connector.
  11. Release the red locking tab, and then press down on the tab to disconnect the resolver logic connector.
  12. Release the grey locking tab, and then press down on the tab to disconnect the oil pump connector.
  13. Release the clip that attaches the front drive unit harness to the front drive unit motor.
  14. Release the clips that attach the front drive unit harness to the front subframe.
  15. Release the clips that attach the front drive unit harness to the front drive unit inverter.
  16. Remove the front drive unit harness from the front subframe.
  17. Position the subframe assembly under the gantry.
  18. Install the drive unit sling onto the gantry hook.
  19. Install the drive unit sling hooks onto the front drive unit.
  20. Slightly raise the gantry hook until the drive unit sling is taut.
  21. Remove the bolt that attaches the front drive unit to the front subframe.
  22. Lift the front drive unit up and away from the front subframe.
    Note
    Make sure not to move the rear front drive unit support posts.
  23. Lower the front drive unit onto a protected surface.
  24. With assistance, place a ½ Ton economy transmission jack under the LH suspension assembly.
    Note
    Avoid the suspension assembly falling.
  25. Remove the bolt and nut that attach the front LH lower compliance link to the front subframe, and then move the link aside.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Combination Wrench 12pt 21mm
  26. Remove and discard the bolts (x2) that attach the LH front lower lateral link to the subframe.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
  27. With assistance, remove the LH suspension assembly from the front subframe assembly.
    Note
    Protect the inner shaft of the halfshaft to avoid any dirt and stains.
  28. Repeat step 24 through step 27 on the RH side of the front subframe assembly.
  29. Release the straps that attach the front subframe to the subframe lifting tool.
  30. Remove the front subframe from the subframe lifting tool.

Install

  1. Position the subframe lifting tool against the front subframe, and then attach the straps (x3) to the subframe.
    Note
    Make sure to align the two subframe lifting tool guides with the front subframe.
  2. Verify the attachment point for the triumph horn on the new front subframe assembly. If the M6 rivet nut is missing, install a rivet nut (1129125-00-B) onto the front subframe assembly.
    Note
    Recommended Tool: 1054712-00-A Rivet Nut Setter Kit
  3. Lower the front drive unit onto the front subframe.
    Note
    Make sure not to move the rear front drive unit support posts from their previous positions.
  4. Install the bolt that attaches the front drive unit to the front subframe.
    80 Nm (59.0 lbs-ft)
  5. Lower the gantry hook until the drive unit sling is slack.
  6. Remove the drive unit sling hooks from the front drive unit.
  7. Remove the drive unit sling from the gantry hook.
  8. Move the subframe lifting tool to a working area.
  9. Position the front drive unit harness on the front subframe, and then install the clips that attach the front drive unit harness to the subframe.
  10. Install the clips that attach the front drive unit harness to the front drive unit inverter.
  11. Install the clip that attaches the front drive unit harness to the front drive unit motor.
  12. Connect the oil pump connector, and then engage the locking tab.
    Important
    Perform a push-pull test to verify that the connector is fully attached.
  13. Connect the resolver logic connector, and then engage the locking tab.
    Important
    Perform a push-pull test to verify that the connector is fully attached.
  14. Connect the front drive unit inverter logic connector, close the handle, and then engage the locking tab.
    Important
    Perform a push-pull test to verify that the connector is fully attached.
  15. Install the LH and RH front drive unit halfshafts into the front drive unit.
    CAUTION
    Take care not to damage or displace the oil seals.
    CAUTION
    Make sure that the opening of the snap rings are facing towards the bottom of the drive unit.
  16. Verify that the halfshafts are fully seated:
    • Carefully push the halfshaft into the drive unit until there is an audible "click" from the halfshaft stub contacting the pinion shaft.
    • There will be a slight pulling sensation on the halfshaft as the halfshaft circlip locks into place.
    • Pull on the inner halfshaft cup to confirm that the circlip is locked into place. If the halfshaft detaches from the drive unit then reinstall the halfshaft and then test that it is fully seated.
  17. Install the clip that attaches the inverter hose to the subframe.
  18. Hand-tighten the bolts that attach the LH and RH front subframe crash can brackets to the front subframe.
  19. Position the front stabilizer bar on the front subframe.
  20. Install new nuts to attach the front stabilizer bar to the front subframe, and then mark each nut with a paint pen.
    35 Nm (25.8 lbs-ft)
  21. Install the bolt that attaches the horn assembly to the front subframe.
    12 Nm (8.8 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  22. Install the steering rack. See Steering Rack (Dual Motor) (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
    CAUTION
    2nd generation front subframes cannot accommodate 1st generation (discontinued) steering racks. A 2nd generation (currently available) steering rack must be installed.
  23. With assistance, position the LH suspension assembly onto the front subframe assembly.
    Note
    Align the compliance link to its installation position.
  24. With assistance, hand-tighten new bolts (x2) that attach the LH front lower lateral link to the front subframe.
    Note

    EMEA/APAC: Use 2123635-00-A. If 2123635-00-A is unavailable, use 1109912-00-B.

    NA: Use 1109912-00-B.

    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
  25. Hand-tighten the bolt and nut that attach the LH front compliance link to the front subframe.
  26. With assistance, torque the bolt and nut that attach the LH front compliance link to the front subframe.
    115 Nm (84.8 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Mark with a paint pen.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Combination Wrench 12pt 21mm
  27. Remove the ½ Ton economy transmission jack from under the LH suspension assembly.
  28. Repeat step 23 through step 27 on the RH side of the front subframe assembly.
  29. Torque the bolts (x4) that attach the LH and RH front subframe crush cans to the front subframe.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
  30. Install the front subframe assembly into the vehicle and stop this step after the bolts and nuts securing the front lateral links to the struts are torqued. See Subframe Assembly - Front (Dual Motor) (Remove and Install).
  31. Tighten the bolts that attach the LH and RH front lower lateral links to the front subframe.
    Note

    EMEA/APAC: Use 2123635-00-A and torque to 190 Nm. If 2123635-00-A is unavailable, use 1109912-00-B and torque to 135 Nm.

    NA: Use 1109912-00-B and torque to 135 Nm.

    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
  32. Continue to perform the rest of steps listed in the front subframe assembly installation. See Subframe Assembly - Front (Dual Motor) (Remove and Install).
  33. Refer to the Alignment Requirement tables to determine whether an EPAS alignment check (EC) or four wheel alignment check (AC) is necessary. If performed, add the alignment check/adjust correction code as a separate activity to the SV. See Alignment Requirement - Suspension.
