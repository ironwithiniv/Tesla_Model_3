Contactor - Fast Charge - HV Battery (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 16302602 1.98 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Warning

Only technicians who have completed all required certification courses are permitted to perform this procedure. Tesla recommends third party service provider technicians undergo equivalent training before performing this procedure. For more information on Tesla Technician requirements, or descriptions of the subject matter for third parties, see HV Certification Requirements. Proper personal protective equipment (PPE) and insulating HV gloves with a minimum rating of class 0 (1000V) must be worn at all times a high voltage cable, busbar, or fitting is handled. Refer to Tech Note TN-15-92-003, High Voltage Awareness Care Points for additional safety information.

Parent topic: 1630 - HV Battery Electrical Components

Remove

  1. Remove the ancillary cover. See Cover - Ancillary Bay (Remove and Install).
  2. Remove the pyrotechnic battery disconnect. See Pyrotechnic Battery Disconnect (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the clip that attaches the HV battery ancillary bay harness to the HV battery fast charge contactor.
    Note
    On newer packs, this harness clip is located further from the contactor and therefore does not need to be removed for access.
  4. Remove the HV battery probing guide. See Guide - Probing - HV Battery (Remove and Replace).
  5. Disconnect the electrical harness from the HV battery fast charge contactor connector.
    Note
    Squeeze the tabs on either side of the harness connector, to release the tabs from the contactor connector.
    CAUTION
    Do not pry the connectors apart, as this action breaks the tabs and connectors, and necessitates harness replacement.
  6. Disconnect the HV battery AC inlet harness from the power conversion system connector.
    Note
    Release the locking tab by pulling upward. A plastic trim tool may be needed to help lift the connector upward.
  7. Release the clips that attach the HV battery AC inlet harness cover to the power conversion system.
    Note
    The 1PH and 3PH configurations differ. The 1PH configuration includes a wiring harness fixing bracket secured by 2 clips; the 3PH configuration does not have this bracket. For 3PH vehicles, skip this step.
  8. Remove the HV battery DC input assembly. See DC Input Assembly - HV Battery (Harness Type) (Remove and Replace) or DC Input Assembly - HV Battery (Busbar Type) (Remove and Replace).
  9. Remove and discard the bolts that attach the negative and positive busbars to the DC links and DC input.
  10. Remove the clips that attach the HV battery ancillary bay harness to HV battery fast charge contactor cover.
  11. Remove the nut and bolts that attach the fast charge contactor to the ancillary bay, and remove the contactor with the harness from the vehicle.
  12. Release the clips (x3) and remove the negative AC inlet terminal cover.
  13. Remove and discard the bolts that secures the negative and positive AC inlet terminals to negative and positive inlet sides of the fast charge busbar.
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Insulated Ratchet/torque wrench
    • Insulated 4 in extension
    • Insulated 8 mm socket
  14. Release the clips (x3) and remove the positive AC inlet terminal cover.
  15. Release the clips (x8) that attach the HV battery fast charge contactor cover to the fast charge contactor, and then remove the cover with the AC inlet harness from the contactor.
    CAUTION
    The fast charge contactor cover is a one-time use as the clips are fragile and break easily.
  16. Remove and discard the bolts that attach the busbars to the fast charge contactor, and then remove the busbars from the contactor.
  17. Remove the bolts that attach the fast charge contactor to the insulator, and remove the contactor from the insulator.

Install

  1. Perform a zero adjust of the Hioki resistance meter in preparation to measure resistances later in this procedure. See Resistance Meter (Zero Adjust).
  2. Use IPA wipes to clean the high voltage mating surfaces of the fast charge contactor and the positive and negative DC link and DC input busbars.
  3. Install the HV battery fast charge contactor to the insulator.
    Note
    Align the 2 guides as the fast charge contactor is installed on the insulator.
  4. Install the bolts that attach the fast charge contactor to the insulator, and then mark the bolts with a paint pen after they are torqued.
    5.5 Nm (4.1 lbs-ft)
  5. Install the positive and negative inlet and DC link busbars to the fast charge contactor, and install new bolts (x4) to attach the busbars to the contactor.
  6. Precondition the bolts by torquing to 10 Nm, then back off one half turn, retorque to 10 Nm, and then mark the bolts with a paint pen.
    10 Nm (7.4 lbs-ft) -180 deg
    10 Nm (7.4 lbs-ft)
  7. Use the Hioki resistance meter to measure the resistance at the positive HV joints of the fast charge contactor on the DC link busbar and inlet busbar.
    Note
    The maximum acceptable resistance is 0.030 mΩ (30 μΩ).
  8. Use the Hioki resistance meter to measure the resistance at the negative HV joints of the fast charge contactor on the DC link busbar and the inlet busbar.
    Note
    The maximum acceptable resistance is 0.040 mΩ (40 μΩ) per joint. If the resistance is above this value, escalate a Toolbox session, as appropriate.
  9. Position the HV battery fast charge contactor cover with the AC inlet harness on the fast charge contactor, and then engage the clips (x8) that attach the cover and harness to the fast charge contactor.
    CAUTION
    The fast charge contactor cover is a one-time use as the clips are fragile and break easily.
  10. Install new bolts that secures the negative and positive AC inlet terminals to negative and positive inlet sides of the fast charge busbar, and then mark them with a paint pen.
    4.4 Nm (3.2 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Insulated Ratchet/torque wrench
    • Insulated 4 in extension
    • Insulated 8 mm socket
  11. Position the AC inlet terminal covers on the negative and positive ring terminals, and then engage the clips that attach the terminal covers to the negative and positive ring terminals.
  12. Position the fast charge assembly to the ancillary bay for installation.
    Note
    2 guide pins.
  13. Install the bolts and nut that attach the fast charge assembly to the ancillary bay, and then mark the bolts and nut with a paint pen after they are torqued.
    5.5 Nm (4.1 lbs-ft)
    8 Nm (5.9 lbs-ft)
  14. Install the clips that attach the HV battery ancillary bay harness to HV battery fast charge contactor cover.
  15. Install the HV battery DC input assembly. See DC Input Assembly - HV Battery (Harness Type) (Remove and Replace) or DC Input Assembly - HV Battery (Busbar Type) (Remove and Replace).
  16. Install new bolts (x4) to attach the negative and positive busbars to the DC links and DC input.
  17. Precondition the bolts by torquing to 15 Nm, then loosen the bolt by half a turn, then retorque the bolts to 5 Nm + 60 degrees.
    15 Nm (11.1 lbs-ft) - 180 deg
    5 Nm (3.7 lbs-ft) + 60 deg
    Note
    Mark the bolts with a paint pen after torquing.
  18. Use the Hioki resistance meter to measure the resistance at both HV joints of the DC input assembly.
    Note
    The maximum acceptable resistance is 0.060 mΩ (60 μΩ).
  19. Use the Hioki resistance meter to measure the resistance at the positive HV joint both of the fast charge busbar DC link side.
    Note
    The maximum acceptable resistance is 0.025 mΩ (25 μΩ).
  20. Use the Hioki resistance meter to measure the resistance at the negative HV joint both of the fast charge busbar DC link side.
    Note
    The maximum acceptable resistance is 0.040 mΩ (40 μΩ).
  21. Fasten the clips that attach the HV battery AC inlet harness cover to the power conversion system.
    Note
    The 1PH and 3PH configurations differ. The 1PH configuration includes a wiring harness fixing bracket secured by 2 clips; the 3PH configuration does not have this bracket. For 3PH vehicles, skip this step.
  22. Connect the electrical harness to the HV battery fast charge contactor connector.
  23. Install the HV battery probing guide. See Guide - Probing - HV Battery (Remove and Replace).
  24. Install the clip that attaches the HV battery ancillary bay harness to the HV battery fast charge contactor.
  25. Measure the voltage across the pyrotechnic battery disconnect mount points, and then install the pyrotechnic battery disconnect. See Pyrotechnic Battery Disconnect (Remove and Replace).
  26. Install the ancillary cover. See Cover - Ancillary Bay (Remove and Install).
