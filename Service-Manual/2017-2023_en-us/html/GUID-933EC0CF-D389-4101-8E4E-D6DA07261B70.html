Knuckle - Suspension - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 31030102 0.96 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-10-07: Updated the torque figure for the LH halfshaft nut from 245 Nm to 300 Nm.
  • 2025-05-01: Added note about ball joint replacement.
  • 2024-12-11: Made correction to Note that mentions one/both rear lower fore links.
  • 2023-11-17: Mentioned correction code 31030312 for scenarios where both rear fore links are replaced as well.
  • 2023-11-14: Mentioned correction code 31030302, which is to be added when one rear fore link is replaced as well.
Equipment:
  • 1135103-00-A Tool, Spring Compressor, Hook, Model 3
  • 1062500-00-A Card, Magnetic Field Viewer
  • 1137855-00-A TOOL, REAR RIDE HEIGHT TORQUE, MODEL 3
  • 1081765-00-A 3/4 TON UNDERHOIST STAND
  • 1459409-00-A ADAPTER, HUB JACK, MODEL 3
Warning
During installation of the brake caliper, make sure that the flexible brake hose is not twisted and can bend forward. The flexible brake hose must not make contact with the wheel arch liner when the vehicle is lowered.
Note
Refer to the Parts Catalog for information on the various rear lower fore link PNs that should be paired with the replacement rear knuckle.
Note
If play on the upper knuckle ball joint is the only reason for rear knuckle replacement, refer to Ball Joint - Rear - Upper - Rear Knuckle - LH (Remove and Replace) and check for markings on the upper ball joint. If no markings are present, replace the rear upper ball joint instead of the rear knuckle.
Note
If rear fore link(s) must be replaced, add correction code 31030302 (for one link) or 31030312 (for two links) to the activity. Refer to Service Manual procedure 31031412 for instructions on removing and installing the rear lower fore link, and/or refer to Service Manual procedure 31031402 for instructions on removing and installing the rear upper fore link.
Parent topic: 3103 - Rear Suspension (Including Hubs)

Remove

  1. Inspect the tires for abnormal wear.
    Note
    Note any abnormal tire wear that could indicate the need for an alignment.
  2. Open the LH front door.
  3. Place the vehicle into "Towing" mode.
  4. Raise and support the vehicle. See Raise Vehicle - 2 Post Lift.
  5. Loosen the LH rear wheel lug nuts, but do not remove the wheel at this time. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
  6. Loosen the LH rear halfshaft nut.
  7. Remove the LH rear wheel. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
  8. Disconnect the electrical wiring harness connector from the parking brake.
  9. Remove and discard the bolts (x2) that attach the LH rear caliper to the LH rear knuckle, remove the caliper from the knuckle, and allow the caliper to hang from an S-hook.
    83 Nm (61.2 lbs-ft)
    CAUTION
    Make sure the brake caliper and hoses are not twisted when hanging the caliper or during reinstallation, as this can damage the brake hose.
    Note
    Hand-tighten the lug nut to secure the rotor on early production vehicles.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • External Torx E18
  10. Remove and discard the nut, and remove the washer that attach the LH halfshaft to the hub assembly.
  11. Remove the bolt that attaches the brake rotor to the hub.
    Note
    Early production vehicles may not be equipped with a bolt securing the rotor.
  12. Install the hub puller onto the LH rear rotor, and then install and hand-tighten the puller washers (x2) and the lug nuts (x2) onto the rotor studs.
  13. Use the hub puller to free the halfshaft from the hub splines.
    Note
    The halfshaft is removed at a later step.
  14. Remove the lug nuts (x2) and the puller washers (x2) from the LH rear rotor studs, and then remove the hub puller from the rotor.
  15. Remove the LH rear brake rotor from the vehicle.
    CAUTION
    Place the rotor hat down to prevent damage to the rotor face.
  16. Remove the bolts (x3) that attach the LH rear hub to the knuckle, and then remove the hub from the axle.
  17. Remove the bolts (x3) that attach the LH rear brake dust shield to the knuckle, and then remove the dust shield from the vehicle.
  18. Remove the bolt that attaches the rear LH ABS wheel speed sensor to the knuckle.
  19. Release the clip and remove the grommet that attach the rear LH ABS wheel speed sensor cable to the rear knuckle and bracket.
  20. Remove the LH rear suspension cover. See Cover - Rear Suspension - LH (Remove and Replace).
  21. Install the spring compressor onto the LH rear coil spring.
  22. Remove and discard the nut that attaches the LH stabilizer bar link to the LH rear suspension knuckle, and then remove the link from the knuckle.
    Note
    Counter-hold the ball joint with a 5 mm hex wrench.
  23. Remove the bolt and nut that attach the LH rear upper aft link to the LH rear suspension knuckle.
    134 Nm (98.8 lbs-ft)
    134 Nm (98.8 lbs-ft)
  24. Remove the bolt and nut that attach the LH rear upper fore link to the LH rear suspension knuckle.
    76 Nm (56.0 lbs-ft)
    76 Nm (56.0 lbs-ft)
  25. Remove the bolt and nut that attach the LH toe link to the LH rear suspension knuckle.
    76 Nm (56.0 lbs-ft)
    76 Nm (56.0 lbs-ft)
  26. Remove the bolt and nut that attach the LH rear lower fore link to the LH rear suspension knuckle.
    76 Nm (56.0 lbs-ft)
    76 Nm (56.0 lbs-ft)
  27. Remove the bolt and nut that attach the LH rear aft lower link to the LH rear suspension knuckle.
    115 Nm (84.8 lbs-ft)
    115 Nm (84.8 lbs-ft)
  28. Remove the LH rear suspension knuckle from the vehicle.

Install

Warning
During installation of the brake caliper, make sure that the flexible brake hose is not twisted and can bend forward. The flexible brake hose must not make contact with the wheel arch liner when the vehicle is lowered.
  1. Apply approximately 1 gram of Molykote M-77 Lubricant Paste only to the hub mating face on the outboard side of the LH rear drive unit halfshaft.
    CAUTION
    Do not apply any lubricant to the halfshaft splines. Lubrication on the splines can prevent the hub from fully seating and can introduce contamination. If lubricant is mistakenly applied, wipe the splines clean with a shop towel.
  2. Position the LH rear suspension knuckle on the rear suspension.
  3. Install and hand-tighten the bolt and nut that attach the LH rear upper aft link to the LH rear suspension knuckle.
  4. Install and hand-tighten the bolt and nut that attach the LH rear upper fore link to the LH rear suspension knuckle.
  5. Install and hand-tighten the bolt and nut that attach the LH toe link to the LH rear suspension knuckle.
  6. Install and hand-tighten the bolt and nut that attach the LH rear lower fore link to the LH rear suspension knuckle.
  7. Install and hand-tighten the bolt and nut that attach the LH rear aft lower link to the LH rear suspension knuckle.
  8. Install and hand-tighten the nut that attaches the LH stabilizer bar link to the LH rear suspension knuckle.
    Note
    Counter-hold the ball joint with a 5 mm hex wrench.
  9. Remove the support stand from underneath the LH rear suspension.
  10. Install the clip and grommet that attach the rear LH ABS wheel speed sensor cable to the rear knuckle and bracket.
  11. Install the bolt that attaches the rear LH ABS wheel speed sensor to the knuckle.
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
    • 4 in extension
  12. Position the LH rear brake dust shield on the knuckle, and then install the bolts that attach the dust shield to the knuckle.
    8 Nm (5.9 lbs-ft)
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T30 socket
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  13. Inspect the tone ring on the LH rear hub prior to installation.
    CAUTION
    Replace the hub if tone ring shows signs damage.
  14. Position the LH rear hub into the knuckle, and then install the bolts that attach the hub to the vehicle.
    85 Nm (62.7 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless 3/8in Dr Impact
    • 18 mm socket
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
  15. Install only the axle nut onto the halfshaft, and then use a ratchet and socket to manually tighten the nut to pull the halfshaft into the hub until it seats.
    Note
    Do not use power tools to tighten the nut as it might strip the threads.
  16. Remove the axle nut from the halfshaft, install a new washer onto the halfshaft, and then reinstall the axle nut hand tightened.
    Note
    The axle nut is torqued in a later step.
  17. Position the LH rear brake rotor onto the vehicle.
  18. Install new bolts that attach the LH rear caliper bracket to the knuckle.
    83 Nm (61.2 lbs-ft)
  19. Connect the electrical harness to the LH rear brake caliper connector.
  20. Install the hub jack adapter onto the LH rear hub and hand-tighten the lug nuts.
  21. Use an underhoist stand to support the hub jack adapter.
  22. Locate the rear ride height torque tool insertion point in the subframe, and then insert the rear ride height torque gauge to verify that the rear suspension is set to ride height specifications. Adjust the support stand or spring compressor tool, if necessary.
    Note
    The suspension is at the simulated ride height when the top of the tool touches the subframe and the lower part of the tool interfaces with the lower control arm.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 1137855-00-A TOOL, REAR RIDE HEIGHT TORQUE, MODEL 3
  23. Measure the distance between the bottom of the quarter panel to the center of the rear axle to make sure that the rear suspension is set to ride height: The distance should measure 378 mm.
  24. Tighten the bolt and nut that attach the LH rear aft lower link to the LH rear suspension knuckle. Mark the bolt with a paint pen.
    115 Nm (84.8 lbs-ft)
    115 Nm (84.8 lbs-ft)
  25. Tighten the bolt and nut that attach the LH rear lower fore link to the LH rear suspension knuckle. Mark the bolt with a paint pen.
    76 Nm (56.0 lbs-ft)
    76 Nm (56.0 lbs-ft)
  26. Tighten the bolt and nut that attach the LH toe link to the LH rear suspension knuckle. Mark the bolt with a paint pen.
    76 Nm (56.0 lbs-ft)
    76 Nm (56.0 lbs-ft)
  27. Tighten the bolt and nut that attach the LH rear upper fore link to the LH rear suspension knuckle. Mark the bolt with a paint pen.
    76 Nm (56.0 lbs-ft)
    76 Nm (56.0 lbs-ft)
  28. Tighten the bolt and nut that attach the LH rear upper aft link to the LH rear suspension knuckle. Mark the bolt with a paint pen.
    134 Nm (98.8 lbs-ft)
    134 Nm (98.8 lbs-ft)
  29. Tighten the nut that attaches the LH stabilizer bar link to the LH rear suspension knuckle. Mark the bolt with a paint pen.
    55 Nm (40.6 lbs-ft)
  30. Remove the underhoist stand from underneath the LH rear suspension.
  31. Remove the lug nuts that attach the hub jack adapter, and then remove the hub jack adapter from the vehicle.
  32. Install the bolt that attaches the brake rotor to the hub.
    5 Nm (3.7 lbs-ft)
  33. Install the LH rear suspension cover. See Cover - Rear Suspension - LH (Remove and Replace).
  34. Install the LH rear wheel. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
  35. Torque the LH rear axle nut.
    300 Nm (221.2 lbs-ft)
  36. Apply parking brake by pressing the PARK button.
  37. Remove the vehicle from the lift.
  38. Refer to the Alignment Requirement tables to determine whether an EPAS alignment check (EC) or four wheel alignment check (AC) is necessary. If performed, add the alignment check/adjust correction code as a separate activity to the SV. See Alignment Requirement - Suspension.
