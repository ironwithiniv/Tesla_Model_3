Windshield Scratch Repair

Correction code 10200118

    CAUTION
    The following procedure is applicable to windshield scratch repairs only.
    1. Inspect the windshield for repair eligibility. See Windshield and Roof Glass Repair and Replacement Guidelines.
    2. Use IPA wipes to clean the windshield thoroughly.
    3. Inspect the glass scratch removal kit, ensure all tools are present, and then gather shop tools and PPE.
      Tip
      Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
      • Fender cover
      • Glass scratch removal kit
      • Step stool
    4. Fill the water feed bottle.
      Note
      Stop at the fill line.
    5. Connect the water feed tube, and then pressurize the water feed bottle.
    6. Install the polishing disk on the backer pad.
      Note
      Align the polishing disk hole with the backer pad hole.
    7. Install the backer pad on the Gforce Max tool.
    8. Use a wire brush to open the polishing disk pores, position finger over the backer pad water dispensing hole, and then activate the water feed button to moisten the polishing disk.
      Tip
      Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
      • Wire brush
    9. Apply a small amount of polishing compound to the moistened polishing disk, and then scrape the compound into the disk thoroughly.
      Note
      Do not leave compound on the surface of the disk.
    10. Align the scratch in the center of the splash guard, and then install the splash guard on the windshield.
    11. Use the suction cup holder to install the water feed bottle on the vehicle.
      Note
      Position the water feed bottle vertically near the repair area.
    12. Place the fender cover on the hood, and then position the step stool near the vehicle as necessary to maintain a comfortable position when applying direct downward pressure.
      CAUTION
      Keep the disk flat on the glass and battle fatigue during polishing.
      Tip
      Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
      • Fender cover
      • Step stool
    13. Polish the windshield in the direction of the scratch as follows:
      1. Grip the Gforce Max tool with both hands.
      2. Place the polishing disk flat on the glass.
      3. Pull the trigger fully, and then apply moderate downward pressure to create friction and heat.
      4. Move briskly over the damaged area in a circular motion.
      5. Press the water feed button to release more compound as needed.
    14. Stop halfway through the repair and check the glass temperature.
      Note
      Laminated glass temperature should be between 115 to 150 degrees F.
    15. Re-pressurize the water feed bottle, and then repeat step 13 to finish polishing the windshield in the direction of the scratch.
    16. Use water and a paper towel to clean the glass, inspect the inside of the glass for any clouding caused by residual polishing compound, and then inspect the outside of the glass for full scratch repair.
      Note
      Do not use IPA wipes to clean the glass.
      Tip
      Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
      • Flashlight LED
    17. Remove the polishing disk and backer pad from the Gforce Max tool.
      CAUTION
      Keep items clean and stored in a clean container to avoid contamination and damage to glass during next use.
      Note
      If necessary, counter hold the backer pad with the glass scratch removal kit wrench.
    18. Disconnect the water feed tube, and then drain the water from the feed bottle.
      Note
      Store all items in the glass scratch removal kit.
