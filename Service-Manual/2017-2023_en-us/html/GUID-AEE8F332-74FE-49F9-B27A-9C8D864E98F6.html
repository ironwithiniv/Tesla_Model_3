Switch - Steering Wheel - RH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 17300802 0.24 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-11-03: Updated the specification of screws (x4) that attach the bright surround to the steering wheel.
Parent topic: 1730 - Interior Switches

Remove

  1. Remove the driver airbag. See Airbag - Driver (Remove and Replace).
  2. Disconnect the steering wheel electrical wiring harness from the connector.
  3. Disconnect the electrical harness from the LH steering wheel switch connector.
  4. Disconnect the electrical harness from the RH steering wheel switch connector.
  5. Release the clips that attach the horn assembly, and then move the horn assembly to the side.
  6. Disconnect the electrical harness from the lower connector, and then remove the electrical harness from the steering wheel.
  7. Remove the screws (x4) that attach the bright surround to the steering wheel, and then remove the surround.
    2.5 Nm (1.8 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T25 bit
  8. Remove the screws (x3) that attach the RH steering wheel switch to the bright surround, and then remove the switch from the surround.
    0.9 Nm (8 lbs-in)

Install

  1. Install the RH steering wheel switch into the bright surround, and then install the screws (x3) that attach the switch to the surround.
    0.9 Nm (8 lbs-in)
  2. Install the bright surround into the steering wheel, and then install the screws (x4) that attach the surround to the steering wheel.
    2.5 Nm (1.8 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T25 bit
  3. Install the electrical harness into the steering wheel, and then connect the electrical harness to the lower connector.
  4. Move the horn assembly to the center, and then fasten the clips that attach the horn assembly.
  5. Connect the electrical harness to the RH steering wheel switch connector.
  6. Connect the electrical harness to the LH steering wheel switch connector.
  7. Connect the steering wheel electrical harness to the connector.
  8. Install the driver airbag. See Airbag - Driver (Remove and Replace).
  9. Reinstall the vehicle firmware. See Software Reinstall - Touchscreen.
  10. Connect a laptop with Toolbox 3 to the vehicle.
  11. In Toolbox, click the Actions tab, type "VCLEFT" in the search field, click onTEST-SELF_VCLEFT_X_SWCvia Toolbox:(link), and then select Run.
  12. Click the Actions tab, type "VCLEFT" in the search field, click on TEST-RESET_VCLEFTvia Service Mode Plus:Chassis ➜ SCCM ➜ Reset VCLeftvia Toolbox:(link), and then select Run.
  13. Disconnect the laptop from the vehicle.
