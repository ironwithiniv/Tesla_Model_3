Amplifier - Antenna - FM (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 21213502 0.36 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 2130 - Antenna - FM HD Radio

Remove

  1. Remove the LH side rail trim. See Trim - Side Rail - LH (Remove and Install).
  2. Disconnect the electrical connector from the FM antenna amplifier.
  3. Remove the bolt and release the clip that attach the FM antenna amplifier to the C-pillar, and then remove the amplifier from the C-pillar.
    4 Nm (2.9 lbs-ft)
    Note
    The FM antenna amplifier is still connected to the backlight harness.
  4. Release the tabs that attach the FM antenna amplifier cover to the FM antenna amplifier, and then remove the cover.
  5. Disconnect the backlight electrical connector from the FM antenna amplifier.

Install

Installation procedure is the reverse of removal.
