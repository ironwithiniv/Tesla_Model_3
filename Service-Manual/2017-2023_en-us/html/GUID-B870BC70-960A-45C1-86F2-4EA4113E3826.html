Remove

  1. Remove the LH front floormat.
  2. Remove the center console. See Center Console (Remove and Install)
  3. Raise the center console armrest for access.
  4. Remove the LH and RH center console decor trim. See Decor Trim - Center Console - LH (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the screws that attach the LH bright strip to the center console.
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
  6. Release the 6 tabs that attach the bright strip to the center console, and then remove the bright strip.
  7. Remove the screws that attach the RH bright strip to the center console.
  8. Release the 6 tabs that attach the bright strip to the center console, and then remove the bright strip.
  9. Release the clips that attach the cup holder decor to the center console.
  10. Disconnect the electrical connector from security controller, and then remove the cup holder decor.
  11. Disconnect the connectors from the 12V outlet and the puddle light.
  12. Remove the screws that attach the cup holder assembly to the center console.
    1.1 Nm (.8 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T20 socket
  13. Release the clips that attach the cup holder assembly to the center console, and then remove the cup holder assembly from the center console.
  14. Disconnect the connectors from the RH side of the center console.
  15. Disconnect the connectors from the USB controller.
  16. Release the clips that attach the cables to the center console front bin insert.
  17. Remove the screws that attach the center console front bin insert to the center console.
    1.1 Nm (.8 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T20 socket
  18. Pull up to release the clips (x4) that attach the center console front bin insert to the center console, and then remove the bin.

Install

Installation procedure is the reverse of removal.
