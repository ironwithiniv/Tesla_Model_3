Sensor - Temperature - RH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code

Parent topic: 1810 - Cabin HVAC

Remove

  1. Remove the RH center console side panel carpet. See Side Panel - Center Console - LH (Remove and Replace).
  2. Disconnect the electrical wiring harness connector from the RH temperature sensor.
  3. Release the tabs that attach the RH temperature sensor to the HVAC module, and then remove the LH temperature sensor.

Install

Installation procedure is the reverse of removal.
