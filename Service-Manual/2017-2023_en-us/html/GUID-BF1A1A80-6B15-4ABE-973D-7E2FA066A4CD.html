Sliding Lid - Center Console (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 15191832 0.48 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 1519 - Center Console

Remove

Note
This procedure is performed with the center console installed in the vehicle. Some images may show the center console outside of the vehicle, but that is only to provide more clarity on the fasteners and components.
  1. Open the LH and RH front doors and lower the LH and RH front windows.
  2. Move the driver and front passenger seats to the most rearward and lowest position.
  3. Remove the rear underhood apron. See Underhood Apron - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  4. Disconnect LV power. See 12V/LV Power (Disconnect and Connect).
  5. Open the armrest.
  6. Release the clips (x17) that attach the center console decor to the center console, then disconnect the electrical connector, and then remove the decor from the vehicle.
    Note
    Release all surrounding clips, and then release front upper portion of the trim.
  7. Remove the bolts (x14) that attach the bright strip to the center console.
    1.6 Nm (1.2 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T20 bit
  8. Release the locating tabs (x12) that attach the bright strip to the center console, and then remove the bright strip from the vehicle.
  9. Release the clips (x6) that attach the cup holder to the center console, and then disconnect the security body controller module electrical connector.
  10. Disconnect the cup holder electrical connectors (x2).
  11. Remove the screws (x4) that attach the cup holder to the center console.
    1.1 Nm (.8 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T20 bit
  12. Release the clips (x2) that attach the cup holder to the center console, and then remove cup holder from the vehicle.
    Note
    Feed the electrical harness through the access hole.
  13. Disconnect the sliding lid electrical connector, and release the tabs (x2) that attach sliding lid to the center console.
    CAUTION
    Do not pull on the electrical connector wires.
  14. Remove the screws (x9) that attach the sliding lid to the center console.
    1.1 Nm (.8 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T20 bit
  15. Lift straight up to remove the sliding lid from the center console.

Install

  1. Position the sliding lid onto the center console.
    Note
    Align with the locating pins (x2) on the center console side.
  2. Install the screws (x9) that attach the sliding lid onto the center console.
    1.1 Nm (.8 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T20 bit
  3. Connect the sliding lid electrical connector, and then fasten the tabs that attach the electrical connector to the center console.
  4. Install the cup holder into position, and then secure it with clips (x2).
    Note
    Feed the electrical harness through the access hole.
  5. Install the screws (x4) that attach the cup holder to the center console.
    1.1 Nm (.8 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T20 bit
  6. Connect the cup holder electrical connectors (x2).
  7. Connect the security body controller module electrical connector, and then fasten the clips (x6) that attach the cup holder decor to the center console.
  8. Position the center console bright strip onto the center console, and then fasten it with locating tabs (x12).
    Note
    Verify that there are no gaps present along the strip.
  9. Install the bolts (x14) that attach the bright strip to the center console.
    1.6 Nm (1.2 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T20 bit
  10. Connect the electrical connector, and then fasten then clips (x17) that attach the center console decor to the center console.
  11. Close the center console armrest.
  12. Reconnect LV power. See 12V/LV Power (Disconnect and Connect).
  13. Install the rear underhood apron. See Underhood Apron - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  14. Restore the driver and front passenger seats back to their original position.
