Basic Vehicle Inspection (Inspection)

Correction code 00030700 FRT 0.90 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Warning
This procedure is a DRAFT. Although it has been validated, Warnings and Cautions might be missing. Follow safety requirements and use extreme caution when working on or near High Voltage systems and components.
  • 2026-06-18: Released new procedure.
Note
This procedure details how to perform the Basic Vehicle Inspection. Fill out SC-26-00-006 while performing this procedure.
Parent topic: 0001 - Maintenance Service

Procedure

  1. Function check LH front door (exterior)
    Note
    Inspect and note functionality for the door handle operation and full range of motion of the door, From outside look for interference with brightwork and door to body, Note noises such as creaks, groans and clicks, pauses or incomplete actions
  2. Function check all windows from the master switch
    Note
    Document any abnormal operation/noises
  3. Function check LH rear door (exterior)
    Note
    Inspect and note functionality for the door handle operation and full range of motion of the door, From outside look for interference with brightwork and door to body, Note noises such as creaks, groans and clicks, pauses or incomplete actions
  4. Function check RH rear door (exterior)
    Note
    Inspect and note functionality for the door handle operation and full range of motion of the door, From outside look for interference with brightwork and door to body, Note noises such as creaks, groans and clicks, pauses or incomplete actions
  5. Function check RH front door (exterior)
    Note
    Inspect and note functionality for the door handle operation and full range of motion of the door, From outside look for interference with brightwork and door to body, Note noises such as creaks, groans and clicks, pauses or incomplete actions
  6. Function check the exterior lights
    Note
    Recommend assistance, inspect and note functionality of Headlight (LH and RH), Side Repeater (LH and RH), Taillight (LH and RH), CHMSL, Fog Light (LH and RH), Reverse Light (LH and RH), Front Turn Signal (LH and RH), Rear Turn Signal (LH and RH)
  7. Verify operation of windshield wipers and nozzles
    Note
    Ensure hood and doors are closed prior to activating wipers, Inspect and note functionality for wipers and wiper jets, Check for full range of motion, speed of motion, interference with other components, noises such as creaks, groans and clicks, pauses or incomplete actions
  8. Verify operation of horn
    Note
    Press button on steering wheel and verify high and low tones both sound
  9. Perform a road test using a series of ABS-active stops and ensure proper brake pedal feel and performance
    Note
    The vehicle must stop completely within 25 feet (7.6 meters) at 20 mph (32 kph), Confirm that no excessive vibration or noise is present
  10. Inspect vehicles alignment
    Note
    Confirm steering wheel is straight and no vibrations or noises are felt or heard from the suspension, On a flat road, at 35 mph (56 kph), the vehicle does not pull in either direction
  11. Open hood
    Note
    Press "Open" button on touchscreen to release latch, Lift lid manually
  12. Remove rear apron
    Note
    12x clips, Only store rear apron visible face upwards
  13. Remove cabin intake upper duct assembly
    Note
    3x clips, Some configurations may come with 4x clips
  14. Remove closeout panel from frunk assembly
    Note
    4x clips, 1x connector, Frunk safety switch, 3x guide tabs, Grab closeout panel from bottom then pull it upward to release clips, Disconnect switch
  15. Remove bolts securing frunk assembly
    Note
    7x bolts, 10mm, 5 Nm
  16. Remove frunk assembly from vehicle
    Note
    12x clips, Replace any clips if broken or missing, Number of clips may vary on older vehicles
  17. Perform inspection of under hood area
    Note
    Perform inspection of vehicle under hood area, Check for signs of abnormal damage, wear, heavy rust, wiring damage, or impact damage, Pay attention to safety related component and note any items of significance
  18. Visually inspect brake fluid level
  19. Fill washer reservoir with fluid
  20. Inspect coolant level and top off as necessary
    Note
    Ensure that fluid level is at Max line
  21. Raise and support vehicle
    Note
    remove charging equipment
  22. Raise vehicle fully and lower lift onto locks
    Note
    Set vehicle to comfortable working height, Ensure an audible click of locks on both sides is heard before lowering, Vehicle may tilt to the side if both locks are not engaged
  23. Remove outer fasteners securing front aero shield
    Note
    9x bolts, 10mm, 5 Nm
  24. Remove front aero shield
    Note
    2x nuts, 15mm, 8 Nm
  25. Remove rear aero shield
    Note
    13x bolts, 10mm, 5 Nm
  26. Remove LH rear suspension cover
    Note
    1x bolt, 10mm, 6 Nm, 2x tabs
  27. Remove RH rear suspension cover
    Note
    1x bolt, 10mm, 6 Nm, 2x tabs
  28. Check all four tire pressures manually
    Note
    4x tires, Set to specification if necessary, Refer to the "Tire and Loading Information" label located on the driver side B-pillar, Inspect wheels and tires for any bulges or damage while checking/setting pressures
  29. Record tread depth for LH front tire
    Note
    Measure and record the outer, center, and inner tread depths, Measure the circumferential tire grooves only, Do not place the tire tread gauge probe on the molded treadwear indicators, Recommend replacement if tread depth is 4/32in (3mm) or less
  30. Record tread depth for LH rear tire
    Note
    Measure and record the outer, center, and inner tread depths, Measure the circumferential tire grooves only, Do not place the tire tread gauge probe on the molded treadwear indicators, Recommend replacement if tread depth is 4/32in (3mm) or less
  31. Record tread depth for RH rear tire
    Note
    Measure and record the outer, center, and inner tread depths, Measure the circumferential tire grooves only, Do not place the tire tread gauge probe on the molded treadwear indicators, Recommend replacement if tread depth is 4/32in (3mm) or less
  32. Record tread depth for RH front tire
    Note
    Measure and record the outer, center, and inner tread depths, Measure the circumferential tire grooves only, Do not place the tire tread gauge probe on the molded treadwear indicators, Recommend replacement if tread depth is 4/32in (3mm) or less
  33. Inspect underside of vehicle for fluid leaks
  34. Perform inspection of steering components
    Note
    Check steering gear joints, fasteners, and electrical connections, Verify components are not bent or damaged
  35. Perform inspection of front suspension components
    Note
    Check conditions of ball joints and bushings, etc, Inspect for any loose fasteners, Carefully apply pressure with a pry bar as needed
  36. Perform inspection of rear suspension components
    Note
    Check conditions of ball joints and bushings, etc, Inspect for any loose fasteners, Carefully apply pressure with a pry bar as needed
  37. Perform visual inspection of HV battery enclosure
    Note
    Check for any dents, scrapes, or other damage
  38. Perform visual inspection of LH front brake pads
    Note
    Perform visual inspection, Recommend replacement if 2mm or less, Check both inboard and outboard pads
  39. Measure and record LH front brake pad thickness
    Note
    Recommend replacement if 2mm or less, Check both inboard and outboard pads
  40. Inspect LH front brake rotor condition
    Note
    Check for excessive rust or wear, Minimum spec: See technical data
  41. Perform visual inspection of LH rear brake pads
    Note
    Perform visual inspection, Recommend replacement if 2mm or less, Check both inboard and outboard pads
  42. Measure and record LH rear brake pad thickness
    Note
    Recommend replacement if 2mm or less, Check both inboard and outboard pads
  43. Inspect LH rear brake rotor condition
    Note
    Check for excessive rust or wear, Minimum spec: See technical data
  44. Perform visual inspection of RH rear brake pads
    Note
    Perform visual inspection, Recommend replacement if 2mm or less, Check both inboard and outboard pads
  45. Measure and record RH rear brake pad thickness
    Note
    Recommend replacement if 2 mm or less, Check both inboard and outboard pads
  46. Inspect RH rear brake rotor condition
    Note
    Check for excessive rust or wear, Minimum spec : See technical data
  47. Perform visual inspection of RH front brake pads
    Note
    Perform visual inspection, Recommend replacement if 2mm or less, Check both inboard and outboard pads
  48. Measure and record RH front brake pad thickness
    Note
    Recommend replacement if 2mm or less, Check both inboard and outboard pads
  49. Inspect RH front brake rotor condition
    Note
    Check for excessive rust or wear, Minimum spec: See technical data
  50. Install RH rear suspension cover
    Note
    1x bolt, 10mm, 6 Nm, 2x tabs
  51. Install LH rear suspension cover
    Note
    1x bolt, 10mm, 6 Nm, 2x tabs
  52. Install rear aero shield
    Note
    13x bolts, 10mm, 5 Nm, Apply Loctite 222 to bolts before installing
  53. Position front aero shield to vehicle
    Note
    2x nuts, 15mm, 8 Nm, Hand-tighten the nuts to temporarily hold the panel in place
  54. Apply Loctite 222 onto front aero shield bolts and install outer fasteners to front aero shield
    Note
    9x bolts, 10mm, 5 Nm
  55. Lower vehicle fully
    Note
    Raise lift off locks, then hold lock release lever to keep locks free while vehicle is lowered
  56. Remove vehicle from lift
    Note
    Lower rack arms fully and remove from under vehicle
  57. Install frunk assembly to vehicle
    Note
    12x clips, Make sure frunk assembly is fully seated onto clips, Number of clips may vary on older vehicles, Ensure A/C compressor HVIL harness is not pinched between frunk assembly and thermal beam, replace any damaged clips
  58. Install bolts securing frunk assembly
    Note
    7x bolts, 10mm, 5 Nm
  59. Install frunk close out panel
    Note
    4x clips, 1x connector, Frunk safety switch, 3x guide tabs, Align 3x tabs then push downward to secure clips
  60. Install cabin intake upper duct assembly
    Note
    3x clips, Some configurations may come with 4x clips
  61. Install rear apron
    Note
    12x clips
  62. Close hood
