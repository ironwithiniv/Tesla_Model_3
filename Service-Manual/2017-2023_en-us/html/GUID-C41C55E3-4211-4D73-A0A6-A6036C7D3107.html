Wheel Disposal (Rework)

Correction Code See below FRT 0.06 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Correction codes:
  • 34015007 Wheel Disposal - Front - LH (Rework)
  • 34015017 Wheel Disposal - Front - RH (Rework)
  • 34015027 Wheel Disposal - Rear - LH (Rework)
  • 34015037 Wheel Disposal - Rear - RH (Rework)
  • 2026-07-10: Added individual correction codes for front and rear LH/RH wheel disposal.
Note
This procedure is performed to make sure that a wheel that was replaced for any reason cannot be reused.
Warning
Wear appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE) including cut-resistant gloves and safety glasses when performing this procedure.
Parent topic: Wheels and Tires

Procedure

  1. Put the wheel on a bench with the valve hole facing you.
  2. Measure approximately 3 cm (1.2 in) from the center of the valve hole, and then make a mark with a paint pen.
  3. Use a 10 mm drill bit to drill a hole at the marked position.
  4. Dispose of the wheel at the appropriate waste location.
