Module - Body Controller - Front (Dual Motor) (Non-Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 17152912 1.32 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-10-28: Added steps to inspect and update (if necessary) the coolant pump type to ensure it keeps consistent before and after the replacement.
  • 2025-04-28: Corrected torque figures for the bolts that attach the shock tower brace to the vehicle.
  • 2025-03-10: Updated post-replacement routine.
  • 2024-03-18: Added instructions to check the integrity of the controller module cover seal and to replace the cover if any damage is found.
Parent topic: 1715 - Electronic Control Modules

Remove

  1. Enable Service Mode Plus, then touch Thermal > Actions > Thermal Configurations > coolantPumpType to inspect the coolant pump type.
    Note
    Two configurations for M3 non-heat pump type: DUAL or DUAL_MIX. Ensure that the configuration remains consistent before and after the coolant pump replacement.
  2. Remove the 2nd row lower seat cushion. See Seat Cushion - Lower - 2nd Row (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the rear underhood apron. See Underhood Apron - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the cabin intake duct. See Duct - Upper - Cabin Intake (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the underhood storage unit. See Underhood Storage Unit (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the LH and RH wiper arms. See Wiper Arms (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the LH and RH shock tower covers. See Cover - Shock Tower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the cowl screen panel. See Panel - Cowl Screen (Remove and Replace).
  9. Disconnect 12V power. See 12V/LV Power (Disconnect and Connect).
  10. Remove the 12V auxiliary battery. See 12V/LV Battery (Remove and Replace).
  11. Remove the bolt that attaches the 12V battery rear hook tie down to the body, and then remove the tie down from the body.
  12. Remove the nuts (x2) that attach the superbottle to the thermal beam.
  13. Remove the upper bolts (x2) that attach the battery bracket to the vehicle.
  14. Release the clip that attaches the coolant hose to the 12V battery bracket.
  15. Release the clip that attaches the 12V battery vent hose to the 12V battery bracket.
  16. Loosen the inner bolts that attach the shock tower brace to the RH shock tower.
  17. Loosen the outer bolt that attaches the shock tower brace to the RH shock tower.
  18. Remove the inner bolts that attach the shock tower brace to the LH shock tower.
  19. Remove the outer bolt that attaches the shock tower brace to the LH shock tower.
  20. Shift the LH side of the shock tower brace towards the front of the vehicle to increase working space.
  21. Remove the bolt that attaches the thermal harness cover to the front body controller module, and then remove the cover from the module.
  22. Disconnect the thermal harness from the front body controller module connector.
  23. Release the clip that attaches the suction/liquid lines to the body near the TXV, and then move the electrical harness aside to gain access to the front body controller module.
  24. Remove the bolts (x9) that attach the front body controller module cover to the front body controller module, and then remove the cover.
    Note
    Check the condition of the cover seal; if any damage is found, order a new front body controller module cover.
  25. Disconnect the electrical harness from the front body controller module connectors (x4).
  26. Remove the nuts (x6) and bolt that attach the power and ground cables to the front body controller module.
    Note
    Reuse the bolt and discard the nylon nuts (x6).
  27. Remove the bolts (x3) that attach the front body controller module to the body.
  28. Release the clips (x2) that attach the bottom of the front body controller module to the body, and then remove the lower busbar from the stud.
  29. Remove the bolt that attaches the ground strap to the upper rear of the front body controller module, and then remove the ground strap from the module.
  30. Remove the bolt that attaches the ground strap to the lower right side of the front body controller module, and then remove the ground strap from the module.
  31. Remove the bolt that attaches the ground strap to the lower rear of the front body controller module, and then remove the ground strap from the module.
  32. Remove the front body controller module from the vehicle.

Install

Note
Modules that are revision -K and later do not have the ground busbar and stud in the top corner. These modules are not faulty and the ground busbar and stud do not need to be installed onto the new part.
  1. Set the front body controller module where it installs into the vehicle, and then tilt the module forward
  2. Install the ground strap to the lower rear of the front body controller module, and then install the bolt that attaches the ground strap to the module.
    9 Nm (6.6 lbs-ft)
  3. Install the ground strap to the lower right side of the front body controller module, and then install the bolt that attaches the ground strap to the module.
    9 Nm (6.6 lbs-ft)
  4. Install the ground strap to the upper rear of the front body controller module, and then install the bolt that attaches the ground strap to the module.
    9 Nm (6.6 lbs-ft)
  5. Slide the busbar onto the lower stud, and then fasten the electrical harness clips (x2) to the bottom of the front body controller module.
  6. Install the bolts (x3) that attach the front body controller module to the body.
    10 Nm (7.4 lbs-ft)
    10 Nm (7.4 lbs-ft)
  7. Install new nuts (x6) and bolt to attach the power and ground cables to the front body controller module.
    8.5 Nm (6.3 lbs-ft)
    8.5 Nm (6.3 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Make sure that the terminal lugs fit in their channels neatly.
  8. Connect the electrical harness to the front body controller module connectors (x4).
  9. Visually inspect that the power and the ground cables exit the front body controller module neatly in their respective channels, and parallel to each other.
    CAUTION
    Make sure that no cables or wires will be pinched when the front body controller module cover is installed.
  10. Install the front body controller module cover to the front body controller module, and then install the bolts (x9) that attach the cover to the module.
    6 Nm (4.4 lbs-ft)
    CAUTION
    Make sure that the cover seal has no damage. If any damage is found, a new front body controller module cover must be installed. Failure to do so may result in water ingress into the front body controller module.
  11. Connect the thermal harness to the front body controller module connector.
  12. Fasten the clip that attaches the suction/liquid lines to the body near the TXV.
  13. Install the thermal harness cover to the front body controller module, and then install the bolt that attaches the cover to the module.
    6 Nm (4.4 lbs-ft)
  14. Shift the LH side of the shock tower brace rearward so that the bolt holes in the brace and shock tower align.
  15. Install the outer bolt that attaches the shock tower brace to the LH shock tower.
    62 Nm (45.7 lbs-ft)
  16. Install the inner bolts (x2) that attach the shock tower brace to the LH shock tower.
    55 Nm (40.6 lbs-ft)
  17. Tighten the outer bolt that attaches the shock tower brace to the RH shock tower.
    62 Nm (45.7 lbs-ft)
  18. Tighten the inner bolts (x2) that attach the shock tower brace to the RH shock tower.
    55 Nm (40.6 lbs-ft)
  19. Fasten the clip that attaches the 12V battery vent hose to the 12V battery bracket.
  20. Fasten the clip that attaches the coolant hose to the 12V battery bracket.
  21. Install the upper bolts (x2) that attach the battery bracket to the vehicle.
    15 Nm (11.1 lbs-ft)
  22. Install the nuts (x2) that attach the superbottle to the thermal beam.
    8 Nm (5.9 lbs-ft)
  23. Install the 12V battery rear hook tie down to the body, and then install the bolt that attaches the tie down to the body.
    9 Nm (6.6 lbs-ft)
  24. Install the 12V auxiliary battery. See 12V/LV Battery (Remove and Replace).
  25. Connect 12V power. See 12V/LV Power (Disconnect and Connect).
  26. Enable Service Mode Plus. See Service Mode Plus.
  27. On the vehicle touchscreen, touch Thermal > Actions > Thermal Configurations > coolantPumpType, and inspect whether the coolant pump type remains consistent before and after the coolant pump replacement. If not, update the coolant pump type to be consistent.
    Note
    Two configurations for M3 non-heat pump type: DUAL or DUAL_MIX.
  28. Install the cowl screen panel. See Panel - Cowl Screen (Remove and Replace).
  29. Install the LH and RH shock tower covers. See Cover - Shock Tower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  30. Install the LH and RH wiper arms. See Wiper Arms (Remove and Replace).
  31. Install the underhood storage unit. See Underhood Storage Unit (Remove and Replace).
  32. Install the cabin intake duct. See Duct - Upper - Cabin Intake (Remove and Replace).
  33. Install the rear underhood apron. See Underhood Apron - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  34. Install the 2nd row lower seat cushion. See Seat Cushion - Lower - 2nd Row (Remove and Replace).
  35. Reinstall the vehicle firmware. See Software Reinstall - Touchscreen.
  36. Perform the following routine using Service Mode or Toolbox (see 0005 - Service Modes): PROC_VCFRONT_X_POST-REPLACEMENT-PROCEDUREvia Service Mode Plus:low-voltage ➜ Front Controller Post-Replacement ➜ Front Controller Post-Replacement Procedurevia Toolbox:(link)
  37. Perform the headlights adjust procedure. See:
    • Headlights - Adjust - NA (Wall Method)
    • Headlights - Adjust - NA (Tool Method)
    • Headlights - Adjust - EMEA (Wall Method)
    • Headlights - Adjust - EMEA (Tool Method)
