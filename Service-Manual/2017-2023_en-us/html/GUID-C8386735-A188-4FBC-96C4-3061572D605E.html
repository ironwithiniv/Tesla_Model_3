Torque Specifications

Description Torque Value Recommended Tools Reuse/Replace Notes
Bolt that attaches the RH and center buckles to the body
40 Nm (29.5 lbs-ft)
  • 15 mm socket
 Replace

Remove

  1. Remove the 2nd row seat cushion. See Seat Cushion - Lower - 2nd Row (Remove and Replace).
  2. Disconnect the RH and center seat buckle electrical connectors.
  3. Release the bracket that attaches the female side of the electrical connectors to the body studs.
  4. Remove and discard the bolt, and then remove the RH and center seat buckles together.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 15 mm socket
    Note
    If the bolt is hard to remove, use an inductive heating tool to melt the adhesive patch material. Position the inductive heating tool coil around the bolt, and then heat the bolt for 20 seconds. Allow the bolt to cool down for 2 minutes, and then remove and discard the bolt.

Install

  1. Install new bolt that attaches the RH and center seat buckles to the body.
    40 Nm (29.5 lbs-ft)
    CAUTION
    Ensure the fastener is going through both the RH and center buckle anchor plates.
    CAUTION
    Ensure the guide slot on the center buckle anchor plate is aligned with the pin on the RH buckle anchor plate.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 15 mm socket
  2. Install the bracket that attaches the female side of the connectors to the body studs.
  3. Connect the RH and center seat buckle electrical connectors.
  4. Install the 2nd row seat cushion. See Seat Cushion - Lower - 2nd Row (Remove and Replace).
  5. Raise all windows and close all doors
