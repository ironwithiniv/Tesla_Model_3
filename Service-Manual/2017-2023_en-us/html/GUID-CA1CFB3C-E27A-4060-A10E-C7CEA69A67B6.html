Tuner - FM / HD / DAB (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 21220102 0.36 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-09-21: Added the steps to disconnect and connect the LV power.
Parent topic: 2122 - Audio System - AM, FM and HD Radio

Remove

  1. Disconnect the LV power. See 12V/LV Power (Disconnect and Connect).
  2. Remove the RH side rail trim. See Trim - Side Rail - LH (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the RH rear C-pillar trim. See Trim - C-Pillar - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
  4. Disconnect the backlight electrical connector from the tuner.
  5. Disconnect the electrical connectors from the front of the tuner.
  6. Disconnect the electrical connector from the rear of the tuner.
  7. Release the clips that attach the wiring harnesses to the tuner.
  8. Remove the bolts that attach the tuner to the body, and then remove the tuner from the vehicle.

Install

  1. Install the bolts that attach the tuner onto the body.
    10.1 Nm (7.4 lbs-ft)
  2. Install the clips that attach the wiring harnesses onto the tuner.
  3. Connect the electrical connector into the rear of the tuner.
  4. Connect the electrical connectors into the front of the tuner.
  5. Connect the backlight electrical connector into the tuner.
  6. Install the RH rear C-pillar trim. See Trim - C-Pillar - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
  7. Install the RH side rail trim. See Trim - Side Rail - LH (Remove and Replace).
  8. Connect the LV power. See 12V/LV Power (Disconnect and Connect).
  9. Connect a laptop with Toolbox 3 to the vehicle.
  10. In Toolbox, type Tuner in the search field.
    Note
    Make sure that Actions is selected, if not already.
  11. In Toolbox, click the play button next to the PING-BASH_ TUNER, and then select Run.
  12. Disconnect the laptop from the vehicle.
