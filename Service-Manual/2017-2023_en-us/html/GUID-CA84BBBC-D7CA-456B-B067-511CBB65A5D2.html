Harness - Fascia - Front (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 17101002 0.66 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 1710 - Harnesses

Remove

  1. Remove the front fascia. See Fascia - Front (Remove and Install).
  2. If present, disconnect the electrical harness from the RH and LH front turn signal/fog light connectors.
  3. Release the clips that attach the electrical harness to the front fascia and RH and LH front turn signal/fog lights.
  4. Disconnect the electrical harness from the front parking distance sensors (x6).
  5. Disconnect the electrical harness from the ambient air temperature sensor connector.
  6. Carefully cut the cable tie that attaches the front fascia harness to the tow hook cover.
  7. Release the clips that attach the electrical harness to the center of front fascia, and then remove the electrical harness from the fascia.

Install

  1. Position the new front fascia harness in the front fascia.
  2. Install the clips that attach the front fascia harness to the RH and LH front turn signal/fog lights.
  3. Install the clips that attach the electrical harness to the center of front fascia.
  4. If present, connect the electrical harness from the RH and LH front turn signal/fog light connectors.
  5. Connect the electrical harness to the front parking distance sensors (x6).
  6. Connect the electrical harness to the ambient air temperature sensor connector.
  7. Insert a cable tie on the back side of the tow hook cover, and then attach the front fascia harness electrical contact to the cable tie.
    Note
    After locking the cable tie, cut off the excess cable.
  8. Install the front fascia. See Fascia - Front (Remove and Install).
