Decor Trim - Center Console - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 15193602 0.06 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 1519 - Center Console

Remove

  1. Open the LH front door.
  2. Move the LH front seat to the lowest and most rearward position.
  3. Release the clips (x9) that attach the LH center console decor trim to the center console, and then remove the decor trim.
  4. Repeat step 1 through step 3 to remove the RH center console decor trim, if necessary.

Install

Installation procedure is the reverse of removal.
