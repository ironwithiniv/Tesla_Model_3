Frame - Seat Cushion - Driver Seat (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 13012202 1.02 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-07-07: Reorder the steps for seat calibration and seat configuration update.
  • 2026-01-26: Added steps to remove and install side shield carrier.
  • 2024-12-09: Added mini-tilt motor configuration information.
  • 2023-12-15: Added steps to remove and install pre-tensioner.
  • 2023-03-23: Added seat track position sensor remove/install steps.
  • 2024-05-22: Removed the steps about the seatback shell and the headrest according to the latest validation.
Warning
Seat base edges are sharp and can cause personal injury. Wear cut resistant gloves when removing the seat from the vehicle, and while performing procedures that involve work on or near the seat base.
Equipment:
  • 1133787-00-A Key, Seat Belt Release, Model 3
Parent topic: 1301 - Front Seat Tracks and Motors

Remove

  1. Remove the driver seat from the vehicle. See Seat Assembly - 1st Row - LH (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the driver seat outboard side shield. See Side Shield - Outboard - Driver Seat (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the clips (x2) that attach the side shield carrier to the driver seat.
  4. Disconnect the electrical harness from the pre-tensioner connector.
  5. Remove and discard the bolt that attaches the pre-tensioner to the cushion frame.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T50 socket
  6. Remove the driver seat inboard side shield. See Side Shield - Inboard - Driver Seat (Remove and Replace).
  7. Unhook the straps that secure the lower section of the seatback, and move the seatback flap away from the underside of seat.
  8. Remove and discard the patch bolt that attaches the seat belt buckle to the seat cushion frame, and then remove the buckle.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T50 socket
  9. Disconnect the seat belt buckle.
  10. Remove seat belt buckle harness clip from seat frame.
  11. Released the clip that attaches the seat belt buckle connector to the seat assembly.
    Note
    Pull the connector backward to release it.
  12. Remove the clips (x2) that attach the side shield carrier.
  13. Partially lift the side shield carrier upper shroud.
    CAUTION
    Take care of not damaging the carrier and the trim.
  14. Remove the inner side cover carrier from the LH front seat by sliding it backwards.
    Note
    2 positioning clips. Pull the carrier backward to release the clips.
  15. Rotate the seat to gain access to the underside of seat.
  16. Disconnect the driver seat seatback heater electrical connector.
  17. Release driver seat seatback harness connector from the seat bottom.
  18. Disconnect the heat pad electrical connector.
  19. Release the clip that attaches the heat pad connector to the seat bottom, slide the connector forward and remove it from the seat bottom.
  20. Disconnect the electrical harness from the seat occupied sensor connector.
  21. Release the clip that attaches the seat occupied sensor to the seat underside, and then separate the sensor from the seat underside.
  22. Release the clips (x2) that attach the seat cushion foam to the seat suspension.
  23. Disconnect the lift motor connector.
  24. Disconnect the electrical harness from the seat tilt motor connector.
  25. Remove the clips (x2) that attach the track motor harness to the track motor housing.
  26. Disconnect the electrical harness from the seat track motor connector.
  27. Remove the clip that attaches the LH front seat harness to the outboard seat track.
  28. Release the clips (x3) that attach the electrical harness along the seat cushion frame.
    Note
    Older harnesses are clipped directly to the seat frame, while newer harnesses are attached to a harness guide that is clipped to the seat rail. See images.
  29. Remove the clips (x4) that attach the driver seat harness onto the under side of the seat frame.
  30. Remove the clips (x3) that attach the driver seat harness onto the inboard side of the seat frame.
  31. Remove the harness from the seat frame.
  32. Return the seat to an upright position.
  33. Remove and discard the bolts (x2) that attach the LH side of the driver seat seatback frame to the seat cushion frame.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T50 socket
  34. Release the seat belt buckle harness clip from the seat frame.
  35. Disconnect the seat belt buckle electrical connector, and then set the seat belt buckle aside.
  36. Remove and discard the bolts (x2) that attach the RH side of the driver seat seatback frame to the seat cushion frame.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T50 socket
  37. Rotate the seatback assembly and lift it straight up from the cushion frame. Carefully organize the surrounding seat harness.
  38. Turn the seat bottom over for access.
    CAUTION
    Protect the seat cushion from damage.
  39. Remove the front retaining clip that attaches the front of the seat trim to the frame from the seat cushion frame.
    Note
    May need to start with a hook tool. Pull the clip away from the frame to release.
  40. Remove the inboard retaining clip that attaches the side of the seat trim to the frame from the seat cushion frame.
    Note
    May need to start with a hook tool. Pull the clip away from the frame to release.
  41. Remove the outboard retaining clip that attaches the side of the seat trim to the frame from the seat cushion frame.
    Note
    May need to start with a hook tool. Pull the clip away from the frame to release.
  42. Remove the cushion pad assembly from the seat frame assembly.
    Note
    Lift the cushion straight off. Ensure the heating pad and seat occupied sensor harnesses are clear of the seat frame.
  43. Remove the cushion suspension from the cushion frame.
  44. Remove the front and rear seat track covers (x4). See Cover - Front - Seat Track - 1st Row Seat (Remove and Replace) and Cover - Rear - Seat Track - 1st Row Seat (Remove and Replace).

Install

  1. Install the front and rear seat track covers (x4). See Cover - Front - Seat Track - 1st Row Seat (Remove and Replace) and Cover - Rear - Seat Track - 1st Row Seat (Remove and Replace).
  2. Install LH front seat cushion suspension from power seat cushion frame.
  3. Place the cushion pad assembly onto the seat cushion frame for installation. Insert the rear of the seat cushion to the seat bottom frame, and then lower the rest. Ensure the seat harness is routed through the back of the seat frame.
  4. Install the inboard seat trim retaining clip onto the seat frame.
    Note
    Fold the clip over towards the frame. The open end should engage the seat frame. If not, the trim will need to be pulled until the correct amount of tension is achieved. Line up one end of the clip strip opening with the seat frame and press down until the clip is fully seated onto the frame.
  5. Install the front seat trim retaining clip onto the seat frame.
    Note
    Fold the clip over towards the frame. The open end should engage the seat frame. If not, the trim will need to be pulled until the correct amount of tension is achieved. Line up one end of the clip strip opening with the seat frame and press down until the clip is fully seated onto the frame.
  6. Install the outboard seat trim retaining clip onto the seat frame.
    Note
    Fold the clip over towards the frame. The open end should engage the seat frame. If not, the trim will need to be pulled until the correct amount of tension is achieved. Line up one end of the clip strip opening with the seat frame and press down until the clip is fully seated onto the frame.
  7. Return the seat to upright position.
  8. Place the seatback frame onto the cushion frame for installation.
    Note
    The seatback frame can rest on the cushion frame without fasteners.
  9. Install new bolts (x4) to attach the LH and RH sides of the driver seat seatback frame to the seat cushion frame.
    43 Nm (31.7 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T50 socket
  10. Install the inner side cover carrier to the LH front seat.
  11. Install the clips (x2) that attach the side shield carrier.
  12. Install a new patch bolt to attach the seat belt buckle to the seat cushion frame.
    40 Nm (29.5 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T50 socket
  13. Install the seat belt buckle harness clip.
  14. Install the clip that attaches the seat belt buckle connector to the seat assembly.
    Note
    Push the connector forward to install it.
  15. Install the clips (x2) that attach the side shield carrier to the driver seat.
  16. Connect the seat belt buckle connector.
  17. Install a new bolt to attach the pre-tensioner to the cushion frame.
    40 Nm (29.5 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T50 socket
  18. Rotate the seat to gain access to the underside of the seat.
    Note
    Protect seat cushion from damage.
  19. Position the driver seat harness to the seat frame for installation.
    Note
    Make sure the harness is routed properly. The main branch of the harness should line up with the center cut out for the center clip. Route the inboard and outboard legs of the harness between the track and the frame. Both inboard and outboard legs should wrap around the front of the frame.
  20. Install the clips (x4) that attach the driver seat harness to the seat cushion frame.
  21. Install the clips (x3) that attach the driver seat harness to the inboard side of the seat cushion frame.
  22. Install the clips (x3) that attach the driver seat harness to the outboard side of the seat cushion frame.
  23. Install the clip that attaches the LH front seat harness to the outboard seat track.
  24. Connect the driver pre-tensioner connector.
    Note
    The pre-tensioner is a two-stage connector. Ensure the lock is engaged after the connector is fully seated.
  25. Connect the electrical harness from the seat track motor connector.
  26. Install the clips (x2) that attach the track motor harness to the track motor housing.
  27. Connect the tilt motor connector.
  28. Connect the lift motor connector.
  29. Slide the heat pad connector onto the seat frame clip to secure it.
  30. Connect the heat pad connector.
  31. Install the seat occupied sensor onto the seat frame.
  32. Connect the seat occupied sensor connector.
  33. Install the seatback harness connector to the seat bottom.
  34. Connect the seat heater harness.
  35. Install the seat cushion retaining clips (x2) onto the seat suspension.
    Note
    If necessary, pull the seat cushion and trim tight for correct placement on the seat suspension.
  36. Hook the straps that attach the lower section of the seatback to the seat suspension.
    Note
    Ensure the straps are tight for proper fit and installation.
  37. Rotate the seatback to the upright position.
  38. Install the driver seat inboard side shield. See Side Shield - Inboard - Driver Seat (Remove and Replace).
  39. Install the driver seat outboard side shield. See Side Shield - Outboard - Driver Seat (Remove and Replace).
  40. Install the driver seat into the vehicle. See Seat Assembly - 1st Row - LH (Remove and Replace).
    Note
    Do not perform the seat calibration routine at this time.
  41. Replacement front seat assemblies and seat cushion frame assemblies are equipped with a newly-designed "mini-tilt" motor, and replacing these parts (or the tilt motor itself) might require a configuration change. Review the configuration information in the table and perform the following substeps if a configuration update is needed.
    1. Access the Vehicle Configuration section of the Infotainment Dashboard. See Gateway Configuration (Display and Change).
    2. Update cfg_frontSeattype according to the combination of tilt motors described in the table.
  42. If servicing the driver seat:
    1. Use the vehicle touchscreen to place the vehicle into Service Mode. See Service Mode.
    2. Reinstall the vehicle software. See Software Reinstall - Touchscreen.
    3. On the vehicle touchscreen, touch Controls > Service > Driver Seat, Steering & Mirrors Calibration > Start.
    4. Exit Service Mode. See Service Mode.
  43. If servicing the front passenger seat in a North American or Korean vehicle:
    1. Consult the parts catalog to see if a configuration change is needed.
    2. Reinstall the vehicle software. See Software Reinstall - Touchscreen.
    3. Remove all objects from the front passenger seat before beginning calibration.
    4. Locally connect a laptop with Toolbox 3 to the vehicle. See Toolbox (Connect and Disconnect).
    5. Unlock the vehicle gateway. See Gateway (Unlock).
    6. In Toolbox, click on the Actions tab, and then search for "OCS".
    7. Run the PROC_OCS1P_SELF-CALIBRATIONvia Service Mode Plus:Safety & Restraints ➜ Seats ➜ OCS1P Self Capacitance Calibrationvia Toolbox:(link) routine.
    8. Click on the Actions tab, and then search for "Seat calibration".
    9. Run the PROC_VCRIGHT_SEAT-CALIBRATEvia Service Mode:Low Voltage ➜ Seats ➜ Calibrate Seatvia Toolbox:(link) routine.
    10. Disconnect Toolbox and remove the laptop from the vehicle.
  44. If servicing the front passenger seat in a European vehicle built before May 2019:
    1. Consult the parts catalog to see if a configuration change is needed.
      Note
      Early European vehicles came with OCS module but only trace wires connected and no sandwich pad. From ~ May 2019 and forward, vehicles are configured for Resistive pad and without OCS module installed on seat. When replacing the passenger seat on an early Model 3 the new seats will come with Resistive pad only, and the vehicle needs to have configuration corrected.
    2. Reinstall the vehicle software. See Software Reinstall - Touchscreen.
    3. Remove all objects from the front passenger seat before beginning calibration.
    4. Locally connect a laptop with Toolbox 3 to the vehicle. See Toolbox (Connect and Disconnect).
    5. Unlock the vehicle gateway. See Gateway (Unlock).
    6. In Toolbox, click on the Actions tab, and then search for "Occupancy".
    7. Run the PROC_ICE_X_SET-PASSENGER-OCCUPANCY-SENSORvia Toolbox:(link) routine.
    8. Click on the Actions tab, and then search for "Seat calibration".
    9. Run the PROC_VCRIGHT_SEAT-CALIBRATEvia Service Mode:Low Voltage ➜ Seats ➜ Calibrate Seatvia Toolbox:(link) routine.
    10. Disconnect Toolbox and remove the laptop from the vehicle.
