Ski - Center Rail - HV Battery (Dual Motor) (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 16102612 2.10 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-01-23: Updated the torque of the center ski cover bolts.
  • 2023-10-24: Updated with center rail ski orientation steps.
Equipment:
  • 1140311-00-A Lever Lock, HV Connector, Model 3
Warning

Only technicians who have completed all required certification courses are permitted to perform this procedure. Tesla recommends third party service provider technicians undergo equivalent training before performing this procedure. For more information on Tesla Technician requirements, or descriptions of the subject matter for third parties, see HV Certification Requirements. Proper personal protective equipment (PPE) and insulating HV gloves with a minimum rating of class 0 (1000V) must be worn at all times a high voltage cable, busbar, or fitting is handled. Refer to Tech Note TN-15-92-003, High Voltage Awareness Care Points for additional safety information.

Parent topic: 1610 - HV Battery Enclosure

Remove

  1. Perform the vehicle electrical isolation procedure. See Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure.
  2. Remove the front aero shield panel. See Panel - Aero Shield - Front (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the bolt that attaches the front drive unit HV harness to the front drive unit.
  4. Slide the release to unlock the front drive unit HV connector handle of the front drive unit to HV battery harness from the secured position.
  5. Fully raise the handle on the front drive unit HV connector.
  6. Remove the front drive unit HV connector from the front drive unit HV header.
  7. Remove the front HV battery skid plate. See Skid Plate - HV Battery - Front (Remove and Replace).
  8. Disconnect the PTC heater HV electrical harness, and then remove the PTC heater HV electrical harness from the connector bracket.
  9. Disconnect the A/C compressor HV electrical harness, and then remove the A/C compressor HV electrical harness from the connector bracket.
  10. Position a coolant drain under the LH front of the HV battery.
  11. Release the clip, disconnect the HV battery RH supply manifold from the HV battery LH supply manifold, and then immediately plug the male and female fittings.
  12. Release the clips that attach the PTC heater and A/C compressor electrical harness bracket to the front of the HV battery, and then separate the bracket from the battery.
  13. Remove the rear HV battery skid plate. See Skid Plate - HV Battery - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  14. Release the clips that attach the RH inner HV battery return hose to the HV battery.
  15. Position the coolant drain under the RH rear of the HV battery.
  16. Release the clip, and then disconnect the RH inner HV battery return hose fitting from the RH end of the HV battery platter enclosure.
  17. Quickly plug both the male and female fittings.
  18. Remove the coolant drain from under the vehicle.
  19. Slide the release to unlock the HV battery front drive unit connector handle of the front drive unit to HV battery harness from the secured position.
  20. Fully raise the handle on the HV battery front drive unit connector.
  21. Remove the HV battery front drive unit connector from the HV battery header.
  22. Remove the nuts that attach the rear drive unit HV electrical harness bracket to the HV battery.
  23. Slide the release to unlock the HV battery rear drive unit connector handle of the rear drive unit to HV battery harness from the secured position.
  24. Fully raise the handle on the HV battery rear drive unit connector.
  25. Remove the HV battery rear drive unit connector from the HV battery header.
  26. Slide the release to unlock the HV battery PTC heater and A/C compressor connector handle of the PTC heater and A/C compressor to HV battery harness from the secured position.
  27. Fully raise the handle on the HV battery PTC heater and A/C compressor connector.
  28. Remove the HV battery PTC heater and A/C compressor connector from the PTC heater and A/C compressor header.
  29. Release the clips that attach the PTC heater and A/C compressor electrical harness cover to the rear of the HV battery.
  30. If the center rail ski is being removed to replace the HV harness, use a paint pen to mark an "F" at the front end of the ski.
    Note
    Some center rail skis have an arrow stamped into the extrusion to indicate the front end.
  31. With an assistant, remove the bolts (x6) that attach the center rail ski to the HV battery.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • External Torx Plus EP14
  32. Remove the center rail ski from the vehicle.
  33. If the center rail ski is being replaced, set the replacement ski next to the ski just removed, and align the bolt through holes (x6) side by side.
    Note
    The ski appears to be symmetrical, but it is not.
    Note
    Make sure that the front pointing arrows stamped into skis are side by side as well.
  34. Remove the cap cover at the front of the HV battery center rail ski.
  35. Remove the cap cover at the rear of the HV battery center rail ski.
  36. Remove the HV battery to front drive unit harness from the center of the HV battery center rail ski.
  37. Remove the PTC heater and A/C compressor electrical harnesses from the sides of the HV battery center rail ski.

Install

  1. Install the PTC heater and A/C compressor electrical harness into the sides of the HV battery center rail ski.
    Note
    The end of the harness with 2 connectors goes to the front end of the center rail ski.
    Note
    Some center rail skis have an arrow stamped into the extrusion to indicate the front end.
  2. Install the HV battery to front drive unit harness into the center of the HV battery center rail ski.
    Note
    The end of the harness with the plastic bracket goes to the front end of the center rail ski.
  3. Install the cap cover at the rear of the HV battery center rail ski.
  4. Install the cap cover at the front of the HV battery center rail ski.
  5. With an assistant, install the center rail ski to the HV battery.
    Note
    The end of the center rail ski with 3 connectors goes to the front of the vehicle.
  6. Install the bolts (x6) that attach the center rail ski to the HV battery.
    35 Nm (25.8 lbs-ft)
    32 Nm (23.6 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Torque the middle 4 long bolts to 35 Nm, and the side 2 short bolts to 32 Nm.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • External Torx Plus EP14
  7. If not already done so, use a paint pen to mark an "F" at the front end of the center rail ski.
  8. Fasten the clips that attach the PTC heater and A/C compressor electrical harness cover to the rear of the HV battery.
  9. Fully raise the handle on the HV battery PTC heater and A/C compressor connector of the PTC heater and A/C compressor to HV battery harness.
  10. Use both hands to firmly connect the HV battery PTC heater and A/C compressor connector of the PTC heater and A/C compressor to HV battery harness to the HV battery header.
    CAUTION
    Make sure that the connector fits the header squarely and tightly, and that both retention pins enter the handle.
  11. While pressing the HV battery PTC heater and A/C compressor connector onto the HV battery header, fully lower the handle.
    CAUTION
    Make sure that the handle does not bind as it is lowered.
  12. Slide the release to lock the HV battery PTC heater and A/C compressor connector handle in the secured position.
  13. Verify that the HV battery PTC heater and A/C compressor connector is fully seated, and compare both sides of the connector that it is properly secured in place.
    Note
    An improperly seated connector might lead to connector damage and PTC heater or A/C compressor problems later on.
  14. Fully raise the handle on the HV battery rear drive unit connector of the rear drive unit to HV battery harness.
  15. Install the HV connector special tool onto the HV battery rear drive unit connector.
  16. Use both hands to firmly connect the HV battery rear drive unit connector of the rear drive unit to HV battery harness to the HV battery header.
    CAUTION
    Make sure that the connector fits the header squarely and tightly, and that both retention pins enter the handle.
  17. Remove the HV connector special tool from the HV battery rear drive unit connector.
  18. While pressing the HV battery rear drive unit connector onto the HV battery header, fully lower the handle.
    CAUTION
    Make sure that the handle does not bind as it is lowered.
  19. Slide the release to lock the HV battery rear drive unit connector handle in the secured position.
  20. Verify that the HV battery rear drive unit connector is fully seated, and compare both sides of the connector that it is properly secured in place.
    Note
    An improperly seated connector might lead to connector damage and rear drive unit problems later on.
  21. Install the nuts that attach the rear drive unit HV electrical harness bracket to the HV battery.
    10 Nm (7.4 lbs-ft)
  22. Fully raise the handle on the HV battery front drive unit connector of the front drive unit to HV battery harness.
  23. Install the HV connector special tool onto the HV battery front drive unit connector.
  24. Use both hands to firmly connect the HV battery front drive unit connector of the front drive unit to HV battery harness to the HV battery header.
    CAUTION
    Make sure that the connector fits the header squarely and tightly, and that both retention pins enter the handle.
  25. Remove the HV connector special tool from the HV battery front drive unit connector.
  26. While pressing the HV battery front drive unit connector onto the HV battery header, fully lower the handle.
    CAUTION
    Make sure that the handle does not bind as it is lowered.
  27. Slide the release to lock the HV battery front drive unit connector handle in the secured position.
  28. Verify that the HV battery front drive unit connector is fully seated, and compare both sides of the connector that it is properly secured in place.
    Note
    An improperly seated connector might lead to connector damage and front drive unit problems later on.
  29. Position the coolant drain under the RH rear of the HV battery.
  30. Remove the plugs from the male and female fittings, quickly connect the RH inner HV battery return hose to the HV battery platter enclosure, and then fasten the clip.
  31. Remove the coolant drain from under the RH rear of the HV battery.
  32. Fasten the clips that attach the RH inner HV battery return hose to the HV battery.
  33. Install the rear HV battery skid plate. See Skid Plate - HV Battery - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  34. Position the coolant drain under the LH front of the HV battery.
  35. Install the PTC heater and A/C compressor electrical harness bracket to the front of the HV battery, and then fasten the clips that attach the bracket to the HV battery.
  36. Remove the plugs from the HV battery RH supply manifold and the HV battery LH supply manifold, immediately connect the male and female fittings, and then fasten the clip.
  37. Install the A/C HV electrical harness into the connector bracket, and then connect the A/C HV electrical harness.
  38. Install the PTC heater HV electrical harness into the connector bracket, and then connect the PTC heater HV electrical harness.
  39. Install the front HV battery skid plate. See Skid Plate - HV Battery - Front (Remove and Replace).
  40. Fully raise the handle on the front drive unit HV connector of the front drive unit to HV battery harness.
  41. Install the HV connector special tool onto the front drive unit HV connector.
  42. Use both hands to firmly connect the front drive unit HV connector of the front drive unit to HV battery harness to the front drive unit HV header.
    CAUTION
    Make sure that the connector fits the header squarely and tightly, and that both retention pins enter the handle.
  43. Remove the HV connector special tool from the front drive unit HV connector.
  44. While pressing the front drive unit HV connector onto the front drive unit HV header, fully lower the handle.
    CAUTION
    Make sure that the handle does not bind as it is lowered.
  45. Slide the release to lock the front drive unit HV connector handle in the secured position.
  46. Verify that the front drive unit HV connector is fully seated, and compare both sides of the connector that it is properly secured in place.
    Note
    An improperly seated connector might lead to connector damage and front drive unit problems later on.
  47. Install the bolt that attaches the front drive unit HV harness to the front drive unit.
    10 Nm (7.4 lbs-ft)
  48. Install the front aero shield panel. See Panel - Aero Shield - Front (Remove and Replace).
  49. Connect 12V power. See 12V/LV Power (Disconnect and Connect).
  50. Perform a vacuum refill of the cooling system. See Cooling System (Vacuum Refill).
