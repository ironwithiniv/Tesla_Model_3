Car Computer (Info-Ryzen) (Remove and Install)

Correction code 21151011 1.50 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-04-30: Added the coolant vacuum refill step to avoid overheating.
  • 2026-02-02: Added video and images to show computer removal technique. Reordered steps for easier removal.

Parent topic: 2115 - Car Computer

Remove

  1. Open the LH front door.
  2. Lower all four windows.
  3. Unlock the vehicle gateway. See Gateway (Unlock).
  4. Perform the following routine using Service Mode or Toolbox (see 0005 - Service Modes): PROC_VCFRONT_X_START-THERMAL-FILL-DRAIN-COOLANTvia Toolbox:(link)
    Note
    This routine has a time limit of 5 hours. If it takes longer than 5 hours, perform this step again.
  5. Disconnect LV power. See 12V/LV Power (Disconnect and Connect).
  6. Remove the cabin intake upper duct assembly. See Duct - Upper - Cabin Intake (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the fresh air intake assembly. See Duct - Fresh Intake (Remove and Replace)
  8. Open RH front door
  9. Move RH front seat backward
  10. Remove the glovebox, front passenger knee airbag, front passenger footwell air duct, and front passenger IP air wave duct. See Duct - Air Wave - RH (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  11. Place absorbent material beneath car computer to catch any spilled coolant.
  12. Place absorbent material below car computer coolant hose connection points to collect any spilled coolant.
  13. Disconnect inner coolant hose to car computer mount
    Note
    1x spring clip, Plug hose
  14. Disconnect outer coolant hose to car computer mount and remove coolant from car computer
    Note
    1x spring clip, Plug hose
  15. Install the car computer coolant plug.
    Note
    1x coolant plug
  16. Remove the nuts that secure the car computer to the bulkhead.
    Note
    4x nyloc nuts (newer vehicles have 6x nyloc nuts), 13mm, 7.1 Nm. If necessary, keep two nuts threaded to hold car computer in place. Discard nuts after removal
  17. Perform the following steps for vehicles with a larger car computer visor:
    Note
    The larger visor interferes with the front passenger airbag.
    1. Disconnect the electrical connectors (x3 for North America, x2 for other regions) for the front passenger airbag (Figure A).
    2. Remove and discard the patch bolts (x4) that attach the front passenger airbag to the cross car beam (Figure B).
      8 Nm (5.9 lbs-ft)
      Tip
      Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
      • Torx T30 socket or External Torx E10
  18. Wear an ESD wrist strap and connect the strap to vehicle ground.
  19. Release the ground strap from the car computer
    Note
    1x bolt, 10mm, 7 Nm.
  20. Disconnect the connectors on the front side of the car computer.
    Note
    3x connectors, If equipped, release locking tab, then remove connector body
    Note
    Connector location might be different depending on car computer version
  21. Release the harness guide clip.
    Note
    1x clip, Pinch release tabs with needle nose pliers.
  22. Release the harness clips from the top of the car computer.
    Note
    2x clips
  23. Remove the car computer from the bulkhead.
    Tip
    x6 studs. With one hand placed inboard of the car computer and the other hand under the computer, release the inboard studs from the bulkhead, then lift the bottom of the car computer upwards to release the remaining studs around the coolant inlets.
    CAUTION
    Do not damage the air bag connections or wiring harnesses.
  24. Remove car computer visor
    Note
    4x clips
  25. Release the harness connectors (x10) on the LH side of the car computer.
    Note
    If equipped, release locking tab, then remove the connector body. Connector count might vary depending on car computer version
  26. Release the harness clips (x2) from the computer.
  27. Release the connectors (x7) from the RH side of the car computer, and then remove the car computer from the vehicle.
    Note
    Connector count might vary depending on the car computer version.
    Note
    If the original car computer uses a removable SIM card, transfer it to the new unit.

Install

  1. Install the connectors (x7) into the RH side of car computer.
    Note
    Connector count might vary depending on car computer version.
  2. Install the harness connectors (x10) into the LH side of car computer.
    Note
    If equipped, secure the red locking tab. Connector count might vary depending on car computer version
  3. Secure harness to car computer
    Note
    2x clips
  4. Install the car computer visor onto car computer
    Note
    4x clips, Make sure all clips are fully seated and visor is properly aligned
  5. Slide the car computer into position on the bulkhead.
    Tip
    Push the passenger air bag up and towards the rear of the vehicle to provide clearance for the visor. Position the outboard studs near the coolant ports into place. Reach around the inboard side of the car computer to align the remaining studs on the bracket into the bulkhead.
  6. Install the ground strap to the car computer.
    Note
    1x bolt, 10mm, 7 Nm.
  7. Connect the glove box USB cable to the car computer.
    Note
    1x connector
  8. Install new nyloc nuts that attach the car computer to the bulkhead.
    Note
    4x nyloc nuts (newer vehicles have 6x nyloc nuts), 13mm, 7.1 Nm
  9. Remove the coolant plug from the car computer.
  10. Connect the outer coolant hose to the car computer mount.
    Note
    1x spring clip, Perform push, pull and push to ensure the hose is fully seated
  11. Connect the inner coolant hose to the car computer mount.
    Note
    1x spring clip, Perform push, pull and push to ensure the hose is fully seated
  12. Remove the absorbent material from below the car computer coolant hose connection points.
  13. Remove the absorbent material from beneath the car computer.
  14. Perform a cooling system vacuum refill. See Cooling System (Vacuum Refill).
  15. Perform the following steps if the front passenger airbag was released earlier.
    1. Install new patch bolts (x4) that attach the front passenger airbag to the cross car beam (Figure C).
      8 Nm (5.9 lbs-ft)
      Tip
      Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
      • Torx T30 socket or External Torx E10
    2. Connect the electrical connectors (x3 for North America, x2 for other regions) for the front passenger airbag (Figure D).
  16. Install the front passenger IP air wave duct, front passenger footwell air duct, glovebox, front passenger knee airbag, and all interior trim panels. See Duct - Air Wave - RH (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  17. Move RH front seat to original position
  18. Install the underhood storage unit and all other underhood ducting and panels. See Underhood Storage Unit (Remove and Replace).
