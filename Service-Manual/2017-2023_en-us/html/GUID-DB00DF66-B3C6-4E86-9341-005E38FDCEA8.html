 Reuse

Remove

Warning
The video(s) included in this procedure are meant as an overview for supplemental purposes only. Follow all of the steps listed in the procedure to avoid damage to components and/or personal injury.
  1. Release the clips (x4) that attach the sun visor bracket cover to the vehicle.
  2. Remove the fastener that attaches the sun visor to the vehicle.
    3.5 Nm (2.6 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T25 socket
  3. Disconnect the electrical connector from the sun visor.
  4. Remove the sun visor from the vehicle.

Install

Installation procedure is the reverse of removal.

CAUTION
Make sure the sun visor electrical harness is routed so that it isn't damaged during installation.
