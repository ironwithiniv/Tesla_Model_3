Antenna - MIMO - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 21331102 0.36 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Note
On vehicles built on or after October 5th, 2020, the eCall antenna makes use of the cellular antenna that is located in the LH MIMO antenna unit. For eCall antenna replacement instructions for vehicles built before October 5th, 2020, see Antenna - eCall (LHD) (Remove and Replace) or Antenna - eCall (RHD) (Remove and Replace).
Parent topic: 2133 - Antenna - Wi-Fi

Remove

  1. Open all the doors and lower all the windows.
    Note
    If vehicle is being powered down, latch the rear doors to prevent accidental closure.
  2. Move the front seats forward.
  3. Remove the LH side rail assembly. See Trim - Side Rail - LH (Remove and Install).
  4. Push to open the LH rear coat hook.
  5. Pry open the LH rear coat hook screw cover with a small flat head screwdriver.
  6. Remove the screw that attaches the LH rear coat hook to the body.
  7. Release the clips that attach the LH upper B-pillar trim to the vehicle, and then move the upper B-pillar trim aside. See Trim - B-Pillar - Upper - LH (Remove and Replace).
    Note
    Do not remove the trim from the seat belt.
  8. Repeat step3 through step 7 for the RH side of the vehicle.
  9. Release the clips (x4) that attach the rear of the headliner to the vehicle, and then use an air bladder to separate the rear portion of the headliner from the vehicle.
    CAUTION
    Be careful not to damage headliner.
  10. Release the locking tabs, and then disconnect the LH MIMO antenna connectors (x2).
  11. Remove the bolt that attaches the LH MIMO antenna to the vehicle.
    10 Nm (7.4 lbs-ft)
    Note
    The bolt is captive in antenna housing and cannot be removed from the antenna.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T25 socket
  12. Release the clips (x2) that attach the LH MIMO antenna to the vehicle, and then remove the antenna from the vehicle.

Install

  1. Install the LH MIMO antenna into the vehicle.
  2. Install the bolt that attaches the LH MIMO antenna to the vehicle.
    10 Nm (7.4 lbs-ft)
    Note
    The bolt is captive in antenna housing and cannot be removed from the antenna.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T25 socket
  3. Connect the LH MIMO antenna connectors (x2), and then engage the locking tabs.
  4. Position the rear of the headliner to vehicle, and then secure the clips (x4) that attach the headliner to the vehicle.
    Note
    Make sure all the clips are fully engaged.
  5. Install the LH upper B-pillar trim, and then fasten the clips that attach the trim to the vehicle. See Trim - B-Pillar - Upper - LH (Remove and Replace).
  6. Install the screw that attaches the LH rear coat hook to the body.
    2.5 Nm (1.8 lbs-ft)
  7. Close the LH rear coat hook screw cover.
  8. Close the LH rear coat hook.
  9. Install the LH side rail trim. See Trim - Side Rail - LH (Remove and Replace).
  10. Repeat step 5 through step 9 for the RH side of the vehicle.
  11. Move the front seats to original positions.
  12. Verify BT, WiFi, and LTE are functioning properly.
  13. Raise all the windows and close all the doors.
