Speaker - Parcel Shelf - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 21213802 0.48 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 2121 - Audio System - Speakers Subwoofer Amplifier

Remove

  1. Remove the 2nd row lower seat cushion. See Seat Cushion - Lower - 2nd Row (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the LH and RH 2nd row seat side bolsters. See Bolster - Side - Seat - 2nd Row - LH (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the LH and RH side rail trims. See Trim - Side Rail - LH (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the LH and RH upper C-pillar trims. See Trim - C-Pillar - Upper - LH (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the package tray trim. See Trim - Package Tray (Remove and Replace).
  6. Release the tabs that attach the parcel shelf speaker grille to the vehicle.
  7. Remove the screws that attach the LH parcel shelf speaker to the vehicle.
    2.3 Nm (1.7 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Support and then remove the speaker bracket underneath.
  8. Lift the LH parcel shelf speaker from the vehicle, and then disconnect the electrical connector from the speaker.
  9. Remove the LH parcel shelf speaker from the vehicle.

Install

Installation procedure is the reverse of removal.
