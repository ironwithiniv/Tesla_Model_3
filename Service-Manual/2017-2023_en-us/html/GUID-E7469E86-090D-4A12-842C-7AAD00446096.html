CAUTION
The fluids provided in the table below represent the fluids used and approved for use. Using alternative fluids that are not approved by Tesla is done at the risk of the individual repairer and any damage that occurs as a result is not covered under the New Vehicle Limited Warranty or Used Vehicle Limited Warranty.

Air Conditioning

Depending on its intended sales region, the air conditioning system is filled with R134a or R1234yf refrigerant.

  • 1012403-00-B, REFRIGERANT, R134a
  • 2006643-00-A, REFRIGERANT, R1234yf

For China only, please use the following refrigerant:

  • 1065472-10-A, REFRIGERANT, R134A 13.5KG CYLINDER

If oil needs to be added to the air conditioning system, use the appropriate oil as specified below.

CAUTION
Select the corresponding refrigerant oil type according to different types of compressors. Do not mix different types of A/C system oil.
  • Hanon or Sanden compressor type: use RB100EV A/C oil (part number 1994534-00-A or 1031093-01-A)
  • Denso compressor type: use ND-11 A/C oil (part number 1031093-00-A or 1108849-00-A).
Note
For calculations, both ND-11 and RB100EV compressor oils have a density of 0.98 grams/ml at 15°C.
  • Given a value in grams, divide by 0.98 to find the value in ml (milliliters or cc).
  • Given a value in ml (milliliters or cc), multiply by 0.98 to find the value in grams.
If ultraviolet dye needs to be added to the system to troubleshoot a leak, the following leak detector injection kit and refill leak detector dye are Tesla-approved for all Tesla refrigerant systems.

  • 2036976-00-A Tracer line TP9815EV Injection Kit

    TRACERLINE A/C LEAK DETECTION KIT TP9814EV-BX (STARTER KIT W/TOOL)

  • 2010495-00-A TP9815EV A/C Dye, Leak Detector

    TRACERLINE A/C LEAK DYE REPLENISHMENT TP9815EV-P12 (12 DYE PACKS)

Brake Fluid

DOT 4 BASF Hydraulan 404 (part number 1012041-10-C) is the approved brake fluid.

Note
The brake fluid filler cap is marked 'DOT 3'. This is the minimum requirement and does not state to only use DOT 3 brake fluid.
CAUTION
Only use new fluid from sealed containers.

Coolant

The cooling system contains an ethylene glycol (phosphate and nitrate free) based coolant.

  • 1029320-00-A, ETHYLENE GLYCOL COOLANT - 50/50 MIX - 1 Gallon
  • 1061466-00-A, ETHYLENE GLYCOL COOLANT - 50/50 MIX - 55 Gallon Drum

For China only, please use the following coolant:

  • 2143051-05-A, LC Heat Transfer Fluid_4L
For EMEA only, please use the following coolant:
  • 1119779-00-A, G48 Coolant – 50/50 MIX 1 Liter

The overall concentration of ethylene glycol should not fall below 50% by volume to make sure that the anti-corrosion properties of the coolant are maintained.

Concentrations of ethylene glycol greater than 60% are not recommended as cooling efficiency will be impaired.

G-48 coolant is a pre-mixed 50/50 solution of ethylene glycol and water. The coolant is ready to use. Do not add water.

CAUTION
Tesla cannot provide warranties for any type of coolant other than Tesla G-48 coolant. Use of other coolants is at your own risk.

Gearbox Fluid

 To reduce gearbox fluid variances and improve drive unit efficiency, service centers are to transition to KAF1/KAF1 (V1.2) gearbox fluid for all vehicle drive units. When refilling or topping up drive unit gearboxes, first deplete supplies of the specified SK 212-B, ATF-9, and EDF2 gearbox fluids, and only then substitute with KAF1/KAF1 (V1.2). KAF1 and KAF1 (V1.2) gearbox fluids are fully backwards compatible with the SK 212-B, ATF-9, and EDF2 gearbox fluids, and incidental mixing of the specified fluid with KAF1 or KAF1 (V1.2) is permitted. Eventually, service centers will only stock KAF1/KAF1 (V1.2) fluid.
CAUTION
SK 212-B gearbox fluid is reddish, ATF-9 and EDF2 fluids are dark purple, and KAF1/KAF1 (V1.2) fluid is amber colored. If you are unable to determine what fluid is in the drive unit, refill or top up with only KAF1 or KAF1 (V1.2). Do not mix SK 212, ATF-9, or EDF2 gearbox fluids with each other.
  • For all 3DU drive units up to and including Model Year 2023 use ATF-9 (part number 1135241-00-A) or EDF2 (part number 1616951-00-A), or ultimately KAF1 (V1.2) (part number 1646886-02-B or 1646886-04-B)
  • For all 3DU drive units Model Year 2024 and later use KAF1 (part number 1646886-02-A or 1646886-04-A)
  • For all 4DU drive units use KAF1 (V1.2) (part number 1646886-02-B or 1646886-04-B)
