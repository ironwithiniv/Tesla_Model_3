Clean Forward Camera View (Precision Cleaning of Inboard Glass)

Correction code 10200348 FRT 0.24 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Procedure

  1. Gently lift the wiper arms away from the windshield to access the wiper blades, and then clean the wiper blades (x2) with isopropyl (rubbing) alcohol, especially the edge of the wiper blades.
    CAUTION
    If wiper blades show degradation and need to be replaced, recommend customers to replace the wiper blades to prevent reduced camera visibility performance.
    CAUTION
    Do not lift a wiper arm beyond its intended position.
    Note
    Remind customers to periodically clean the edge of the wiper blades as instructed in the Owner's Manual.
  2. Apply glass cleaner onto a microfiber towel to clean the outside surface of the front windshield, then use a second dry microfiber towel to wipe the windshield in a circular motion until the windshield is basically dry. Verify the windshield has no stains or residue, then allow 1 minute to fully dry.
  3. Use an LED flashlight to illuminate the outside surface of the windshield in the forward facing camera area, inspect for presence of any haze, fogginess, stains and/or residue on the inside of the windshield. Shine the flashlight towards you as shown in the images.
    Note
    Perform the inspection indoors.
    Note
    Flashlight should be pointing at the windshield towards you, see diagram for proper inspection method.
    Note
    If the presence of haze, smudges is confirmed with flashlight based inspection method, continue to the next step.
  4. Open the LH front door and lower the LH front window.
  5. Turn off the climate system.
  6. Remove the lower quad camera cover. See Quad Camera Cover - Lower (Remove and Replace).
    CAUTION
    Be careful not to damage interior trim or the interior of the windshield when removing/installing components. Use only non-marring trim tools and hand tools. Do not use power tools.
  7. Remove the rear view mirror. See Mirror - Rear View (Remove and Replace).
    CAUTION
    Be careful not to damage interior trim or the interior of the windshield when removing/installing components. Use only non-marring trim tools and hand tools. Do not use power tools.
  8. Release the clips that attach the upper quad camera cover to the vehicle, and then allow the assembly to hang from the harness.
    CAUTION
    Do not remove the upper camera cover completely, as the electrical harness is still attached to the interior camera.
    CAUTION
    Be careful not to damage interior trim or the interior of the windshield when removing/installing components. Use only non-marring trim tools and hand tools. Do not use power tools.
  9. Remove and discard the patch screws (x2) that attach the front camera glare shield to the vehicle, and then all the assembly to hang from the harness.
    CAUTION
    Be careful not to damage interior trim or the interior of the windshield when removing/installing components. Use only non-marring trim tools and hand tools. Do not use power tools.
  10. Place the vehicle into Service Mode. See Service Mode.
  11. Unlock the vehicle gateway. See Gateway (Unlock).
  12. Clean the inside surface of the windshield in the forward facing camera area as follows:
    1. Put on nitrile gloves.
    2. Use pre-moistened clean wipes to thoroughly wipe the area. Use a plastic trim tool to help apply upward pressure and clean the crevices between the forward facing camera and windshield. See images for reference.
      CAUTION
      Do not use unapproved IPA wipes. Using unapproved IPA wipes may damage the hydrophobic coating on the windshield.
      CAUTION
      Be careful not to touch or damage the forward facing camera lens while using the trim tool.
    3. Use cleanroom (dry) wipes to thoroughly wipe the area. Make sure the wipe is laid flat onto the surface to prevent glass from streaking. Use a plastic trim tool to help apply upward pressure and clean the crevices between the forward facing camera and windshield. See images for reference.
      CAUTION
      Be careful not to touch or damage the forward facing camera lens while using the trim tool.
    4. Use the LED flashlight to inspect that the area is clean and no streaks or residue remains. Use additional cleanroom (dry) wipes to wipe the area, if necessary.
    5. Use a cleanroom (dry) wipe folded into a square for a final wipe. Wiping in a sweeping motion will help to remove remaining dust on glass.
    Note
    Clean the entire area thoroughly, including the edges and corners.
    Note
    For HW4 vehicles, pay special attention to ensure the notch is also clean (see picture for notch location).
  13. On the touchscreen, tap the Service Mode "wrench" (at the bottom of the touchscreen UI), and then tap Driver Assist > Forward Camera View Cleaning, select Main/Wide tabs to preview all forward facing cameras.
  14. Preview all forward facing cameras and assess the visual clarity.
    • If no residue or dirt is visible across all camera lenses, continue to the next step to install all components that were removed for access.
    • If the image is blurry or the visuals are impeded, use Zeiss Pre-moistened Clean Wipes and then cleanroom (dry) wipes to clean the camera lenses as needed.
  15. Install the forward facing camera hood. See Glare Shield (Remove and Replace).
    CAUTION
    Be careful not to damage interior trim or the interior of the windshield when removing/installing components. Use only non-marring trim tools and hand tools (no power tools) to avoid damaging the interior surface of the windshield and interior trim.
  16. On the vehicle touchscreen, perform the following steps:
    1. Tap Service > Driver Assist > Forward Camera View Cleaning > Windshield clean complete, and then tap Run and allow the routine to complete.
      Note
      On some vehicle configurations, the Windshield clean complete button may not be present if there is no existing alert.
    2. Tap Reset DAS, and then tap Run and allow the routine to complete.
  17. Exit Service Mode.
  18. Raise the LH front window and close the LH front door.
