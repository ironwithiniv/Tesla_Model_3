Antenna - GPS (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 21320102 0.30 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2024-09-12: Added note that it is unnecessary to disconnect the harness from triple camera assembly.
Parent topic: 2132 - Antenna - GPS

Remove

  1. Remove the forward facing camera. See Camera - Triple - Forward Facing (Remove and Replace).
    Note
    It is not necessary to disconnect the electrical harness from the triple camera assembly. After removing the fasteners that attach the triple camera assembly to the vehicle, allow the assembly to hang from the harness.
  2. Disconnect the electrical harness from the humidity and temperature sensor connector.
  3. Disconnect the electrical harness from the GPS antenna connector.
  4. Remove the screw that attaches the electrical harness to the windshield bracket, and then move the electrical harness aside.
    1 Nm (.7 lbs-ft)
  5. Remove the screw that attaches the GPS antenna to the bracket, and then remove the GPS antenna from the vehicle.
    1 Nm (.7 lbs-ft)

Install

Installation procedure is the reverse of removal, except for the following:

  • After the installation of the new GPS antenna, perform the forward facing camera target calibration. See Camera - Forward Facing (Pitch Verification).
