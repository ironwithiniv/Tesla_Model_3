Harness - USB - Car Computer to Glove Box (RHD) (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 17106852 0.36 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 1710 - Harnesses

Remove

CAUTION
This procedure applies to models without knee airbags.
  1. Open the front doors.
  2. Remove the LH and RH instrument panel end caps. See End Cap - Instrument Panel - LH (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the LH and RH air wave bezel caps. See End Cap - Air Wave - LH (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the main instrument panel decor trim. See Decor Trim - Instrument Panel - Main (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the LH middle A-Pillar trim. See Trim - A-Pillar - Middle - LH (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the LH lower A-Pillar trim. See Trim - A-Pillar - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the glove box. See Glove Box - Without Knee Airbags (Remove and Replace).
  8. Disconnect the LH air wave duct thermistor connector.
  9. Remove the clips (x3) that attach the LH air wave duct, and then remove the duct.
  10. Disconnect the glove box USB cable from the car computer.
  11. Release the glove box USB harness clips (x2) and remove the harness from the vehicle.

Installation procedure is the reverse of removal.
