Inverter - Rear Drive Unit (3DU) (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 40200602 2.94 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-11-18: Added recommended tools for the phase out cover bolts, as 14 Nm cannot be reached if a cardan joint is used to apply torque. And added a note to not place the inverter face down on HV lugs.
  • 2026-07-07: Updated steps to connect/disconnect the LV maintainer.
  • 2025-06-12: Updated the mount bolts and inverter bolts from "reuse" to "replace".
  • 2024-12-04: Updated inverter fastener torque sequence.
  • 2024-04-05: Updated gearbox fluid information with link.
  • 2024-02-27: Updated gearbox fluid to KAF 1.
  • 2023-10-10: Added note to make sure that correct ground strap is available.
  • 2023-07-25: Removed resolver error learn substeps.
  • 2023-07-17: Removed photos showing bare hands.
CAUTION
Do not service the drive unit inverter and the car computer concurrently. First, service the drive unit inverter. After installation of the drive unit inverter has completed successfully, then service the car computer.
Note
Check the electronic parts catalog and make sure to have the correct ground strap for the inverter that is being installed.
Equipment:
  • 1130479-00-A Skt, 3/8in Dr, 10EP Torx Plus External

  • 1450875-00-A Kit, Inverter Leak Test Adapter, M3
  • 1026636-00-A Pack Enclosure Leak Tester, HV Battery
  • 1140311-00-A Lever Lock, HV Connector, Model 3

Remove

  1. While performing the courtesy inspection, note any abnormal tire wear that could indicate the need for an alignment.
  2. Move the vehicle to a 2 post lift. See Raise Vehicle - 2 Post Lift.
  3. Place stanchions around the rear of the vehicle to setup a safety perimeter.
  4. Verify that the vehicle firmware is version 2022.20.6 or later. Update the vehicle firmware if this is not the case. See Software Update.
  5. Connect a laptop with Toolbox 3 to the vehicle. See Toolbox (Connect and Disconnect).
  6. In Toolbox 3, click the Dashboards tab, and type ‘Rear Drive Inverter Replacement' into the search field, and click [Model 3/Y] REAR Drive INVERTER - Replacement.
  7. From the BEFORE INVERTER SWAP Action List, click Store Drive Inverter Bootloader Data before Replacement, click Run, and allow the routine to complete.
  8. From the BEFORE INVERTER SWAP Action List, click Store Drive Inverter Application Data before Replacement, click Run, and allow the routine to complete.
  9. From the BEFORE INVERTER SWAP Action List, click Start Thermal Fluid Fill/Drain (Coolant only), click Run, and allow the routine to complete.
  10. Disconnect the laptop with Toolbox 3 from the vehicle. See Toolbox (Connect and Disconnect).
    Note
    Do not close this instance of Toolbox 3 as it is needed later on in the procedure.
  11. Remove the rear underhood apron. See Underhood Apron - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  12. Remove the cabin intake duct. See Duct - Upper - Cabin Intake (Remove and Replace).
  13. Remove the outer HVAC plenum duct. See Plenum - Inlet - HVAC (Remove and Replace).
  14. Disconnect LV power. See 12V/LV Power (Disconnect and Connect).
  15. Perform Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure. See Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure.
  16. Remove the LH and RH rear wheels. See Wheel (Remove and Replace).
  17. Remove the LH and RH rear suspension covers. See Cover - Rear Suspension - LH (Remove and Replace).
  18. Remove the rear aero shield panel. See Panel - Aero Shield - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  19. Remove the rear diffuser. See Diffuser - Rear Fascia (Remove and Replace).
  20. Release the red locking tab and then disconnect the electrical harness from the rear subframe harness RH connector.
  21. Release the clip that attaches the rear subframe harness to the body on the RH side.
  22. Disconnect the electrical harness from the rear subframe harness LH connector.
  23. Release the clip that attaches the rear subframe harness to the body on the LH side.
  24. Remove the nut that attaches the rear drive unit ground strap to the body, and then remove the ground strap from the body.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm 12-point deep socket
  25. Remove and discard the nut that attaches the LH rear stabilizer bar end link to the rear stabilizer bar.
    Note
    Use a 5 mm hex tool to prevent the ball joint stud from turning and damaging the ball joint.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 15 mm socket
    • 5 mm hex
  26. Remove LH rear stabilizer bar end link from the rear stabilizer bar.
    Note
    Move the stabilizer bar up and down to ease removal of the end link.
  27. Remove and discard the nut that attaches the RH rear stabilizer bar end link to the rear stabilizer bar.
    Note
    Use a 5 mm hex tool to prevent the ball joint stud from turning and damaging the ball joint.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 15 mm socket
    • 5 mm hex
  28. Remove RH rear stabilizer bar end link from the rear stabilizer bar.
    Note
    Move the stabilizer bar up and down to ease removal of the end link.
  29. Remove and discard the bolts (x4) that attach the rear stabilizer bar to the rear subframe, and then remove the stabilizer bar from the subframe.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 11 mm socket
  30. Release the clips (x2) that attach the coolant hoses to LH shear plate.
  31. Release the clips (x4) that secure coolant hoses to the rear skid plate.
    Note
    Clip quantity varies with older vehicles.
  32. Release the clips (x2) that attach the coolant hoses to RH shear plate.
  33. Remove the HV battery rear skid plate. See Skid Plate - HV Battery - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  34. Position a coolant drain collector underneath the LH rear of the HV battery.
  35. Release the clip, disconnect the rear drive unit inverter inlet hose from the HV battery, and then immediately plug both fittings.
  36. Release the clip, disconnect the rear drive unit inverter inlet hose from the rear drive unit inverter, and then immediately plug both fittings.
  37. Release the clip that attaches the rear drive unit inverter inlet tube from rear drive unit HV harness.
  38. Remove the rear drive unit inverter inlet tube down and out from between the rear subframe and the HV battery.
  39. Remove the coolant drain collector from under the vehicle.
  40. Release the locking tab, push the handle downward, and then disconnect the rear subframe electrical harness from the rear drive unit inverter logic connector.
    CAUTION
    Once disconnected, make sure that coolant does not get into the connectors.
  41. Release the clip that attaches the rear subframe electrical harness to the rear drive unit inverter.
  42. Put on HV gloves and leather over gloves.
  43. Remove the bolt that attaches the rear drive unit HV harness bracket to the rear drive unit inverter.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  44. Slide the red locking tab, raise the black handle, disconnect the rear drive unit HV harness from the rear drive unit inverter HV header,and then set the HV harness aside.
  45. Remove the nut(s) that attach(es) the rear drive unit HV harness bracket to the HV battery.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  46. Slide the red locking tab, raise the black handle, and then disconnect the rear drive unit HV harness from the HV battery header.
  47. Remove the rear drive unit HV harness down and out from between the rear subframe and the HV battery.
    Note
    Rotate the harness as it is removed to allow access for the bracket.
  48. Remove HV gloves and leather over gloves.
  49. Position the subframe lifting tool under, and up against the rear subframe.
  50. With the help of an assistant, fully secure the straps (x3) that attach the rear subframe to the subframe lifting tool.
  51. Use a paint pen to trace around the LH and RH rear subframe mounting bolts, so that the bolts can be realigned later during the subframe installation.
  52. Use a paint pen to apply a witness mark where the rear subframe contacts the body at the LH and RH rear subframe mounting bolts, so that the subframe can be realigned to the body later during subframe installation.
  53. Remove the LH and RH shear plate bolts (x6). See Shear Plate - Rear Subframe - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  54. Remove and discard the LH rear subframe bolt that attaches the subframe to the body.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 21 mm socket
  55. Remove and discard the RH rear subframe bolt that attaches the subframe to the body.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 21 mm socket
  56. Carefully lower the subframe and rear drive unit assembly approximately 8.25 cm (3.25 in) from the vehicle.
    Warning
    Do not lower the subframe any more than 8.25 cm (3.25 in) as suspension and brake components are still attached to both the subframe and the body.
  57. Tilt the subframe fixture forward in order to access motor mount fasteners.
  58. Insert a rubber or wood block between the rear drive unit and the subframe fixture so as to support the rear drive unit while the LH mount is removed.
  59. Remove the LH mount bolt that attaches the rear drive unit LH mount to the rear subframe.
    Note
    Use a pry bar between the block and rear drive unit to unload the bolt so that it can be removed.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 18 mm socket
  60. Release the clip that attaches the rear drive unit ground strap to the rear subframe.
  61. Release the clip, disconnect the rear drive unit inverter to heat exchanger hose from the rear drive unit inverter outlet, and then immediately plug both fittings.
  62. Release the clip that attaches the rear drive unit inverter to heat exchanger hose to the rear drive unit LH mount.
  63. Remove and discard the bolts (x3) that attach the rear drive unit LH mount to the rear drive unit.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • External Torx E14
  64. Remove the rear drive unit LH mount up, over, and out between the rear subframe and the body.
  65. Put on the ESD wrist strap and attach it to the rear drive unit inverter housing.
  66. Remove and discard the rear drive unit phase out cover label to access the bolts behind.
  67. Remove the bolts (x2) that attach the phase out cover to the rear drive unit gearbox, and then use a plastic trim tool to remove the cover from the gearbox.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • External Torx Plus EP10, low profile
    • 4 in extension
  68. Inspect the condition of the phase out cover and O-rings. If the cover has less than 6 O-rings, or if the O-rings or cover are damaged, discard the cover and use a new cover, part 1096984-00-C or newer revision, for installation.
  69. Remove the bolts (x3) that attach the rear drive unit motor 3-phase terminals to the rear drive unit LH inverter 3-phase terminals.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm hex
    • 4 in extension
  70. Remove and discard the bolts (x12) that attach the rear drive unit inverter to the rear drive unit gearbox.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • External Torx Plus EP10, low profile
    • 4 in extension
  71. Carefully remove the rear drive unit inverter from the rear drive unit gearbox, and move the inverter down and out between the rear subframe and drive unit.
    CAUTION
    Take care not to damage the inverter circuit board when removing the inverter.
    CAUTION
    Do not damage or place face down on the HV lugs.
    Note
    The gasket is a one-time use part.
  72. Remove the plugs from the inverter and drain the coolant.
  73. Set the rear drive unit inverter onto an ESD mat, and then remove the ESD wrist strap clip from the inverter housing.
    CAUTION
    Do not damage or place face down on the HV lugs.
  74. Inspect the rear drive unit gearbox housing to make sure that there is no gap pad stuck there. If there is, remove the gap pad, clean the area with an alcohol wipe, and allow at least one minute to dry.
    Note
    Also inspect the gearbox housing and remove any dropped fasteners or loose debris.
  75. If the pyrotechnic fuse of the drive unit inverter has not been actuated, dispose of the drive unit inverter as hazardous material. If the pyrotechnic fuse has been actuated, follow the normal regulations for waste disposal.
    Note
    If Autodiag falsely indicated that the pyrotechnic fuse was actuated, but visual check confirms that it was not activated, list the serial number of the drive unit inverter in Autodiag using 'Report an Issue'.

Install

  1. Prepare the new rear drive unit inverter and attach the ESD strap to the new inverter housing
  2. Use an alcohol wipe to clean the phase terminals of the rear drive unit motor and the new rear drive unit inverter, and allow at least one minute to dry.
  3. Inspect the condition of the gap pad on the new rear drive unit inverter PCB. Replace the gap pad if it does not fulfill these criteria:
    • New and undamaged condition.
    • Fully covers the discharge resistor array and no resistors are visible.
    • Clean and free of debris.
    • Evenly adhered to the inverter PCB.
  4. Make sure that a new gasket is installed onto the new rear drive unit inverter.
  5. Carefully install the new rear drive unit inverter in and up between the rear subframe and rear drive unit. Align the inverter to the 2 pins in the rear drive unit gearbox, and then install the inverter to the gearbox.
    CAUTION
    Take care not to damage the inverter circuit board when installing the inverter.
    Note
    Make sure that the phase terminals align during installation.
  6. Install and hand-tighten new bolts (x12) that attach the new rear drive unit inverter to the rear drive unit LH gearbox.
  7. Tighten the 2 bolts nearest the alignment dowels first, and then tighten the remaining bolts (x10) that attach the new rear drive unit inverter to the rear drive unit gearbox.
    12.5 Nm (9.2 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • External Torx Plus EP10, low profile
    • 4 in extension
  8. Install and hand-tighten the bolts (x3) that attach the rear drive unit motor 3-phase terminals to the new rear drive unit inverter 3-phase terminals.
  9. Tighten the bolts (x3) that attach the rear drive unit motor 3-phase terminals to the new rear drive unit inverter 3-phase terminals.
    11.5 Nm (8.5 lbs-ft)
    CAUTION
    If any bolts are dropped into the gearbox housing, they must be retrieved.
    Note
    If installing a new bolt, tighten to 12.5 Nm, back off 180 degrees, then torque to 5 Nm +40 degrees.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm hex
    • 4 in extension
  10. Lightly lubricate the rear drive unit gearbox phase out cover bores with clean gearbox fluid, and if a new phase out cover is to be installed, lightly lubricate the O-rings of the cover.
    Note
    Refer to Fluids and Capacities to determine which gearbox fluid is applicable.
  11. Install the phase out cover into the rear drive unit gearbox, and then install the bolts (x2) that attach the cover to the gearbox.
    14 Nm (10.3 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • External Torx Plus EP10, low profile
    • 4 in extension
  12. Install a new phase out cover label.
  13. Remove the ESD wrist strap from the new inverter, attach it to the old inverter, and prepare the old inverter for shipment to MRB.
  14. Perform an inverter air leak test. See Inverter Air Leak Test.
  15. Install the rear drive unit LH mount in, over, and down between the rear subframe and the body.
  16. Install the rear drive unit LH mount to the rear drive unit, and then install and hand-tighten new bolts (x3) that attach the mount to the drive unit.
    Note
    Mount Bolt PN: 1111864-00-B
  17. Tighten the bolts (x3) that attach the rear drive unit LH mount to the rear drive unit in the sequence shown.
    35 Nm (25.8 lbs-ft) + 55 degrees
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • External Torx E14
  18. Install the LH mount bolt that attaches the rear drive unit LH mount to the rear subframe.
    80 Nm (59.0 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Use a pry bar between the block and rear drive unit to position the mount so that the bolt can be installed.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 18 mm socket
  19. Remove the block from between the rear drive unit and the subframe fixture.
  20. Remove the plugs from the fittings, immediately connect the rear drive unit inverter to heat exchanger hose to the rear drive unit inverter outlet, fasten the clip, and then perform a Push-Pull-Push check of the fitting.
  21. Fasten the clip that attaches the rear drive unit inverter to heat exchanger hose to the rear drive unit LH mount.
  22. Fasten the clip that attaches the rear drive unit ground strap to the rear subframe.
  23. Tilt the subframe fixture backward to its original position.
  24. Carefully raise the subframe and rear drive unit assembly until they are up against the body.
    CAUTION
    Be mindful of the suspension and brake components that are still attached to both the subframe and the body.
  25. Install and hand-tighten a new RH rear subframe bolt to attach the rear subframe to the body.
  26. Install and hand-tighten a new LH rear subframe bolt to attach the rear subframe to the body.
  27. Install the LH and RH shear plate bolts (x6). See Shear Plate - Rear Subframe - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  28. Use a pry bar to shimmy the rear subframe with respect to the body, so that the subframe mounting bolts are centered in the tracings, and the witness marks align where the subframe contacts the body.
  29. Tighten the RH rear subframe bolt that attaches the rear subframe to the body, and then mark the bolt after tightening.
    160 Nm (118.0 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 21 mm socket
  30. Tighten the LH rear subframe bolt that attaches the rear subframe to the body, and then mark the bolt after tightening.
    160 Nm (118.0 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 21 mm socket
  31. Release the straps (x3) that attach the rear subframe to the subframe lifting tool.
  32. Lower the subframe lifting tool, and move it out from under the vehicle.
  33. Put on HV gloves and leather over gloves.
  34. Install the rear drive unit HV harness in and up between the rear subframe and the HV battery.
    Note
    Rotate the harness as it is installed to allow access for the bracket.
  35. Verify that the black release lever of the rear drive unit HV harness connector is in the open position, install the connector flat and square to the HV battery header, secure the release lever to the closed position, and then slide the red connector locking tab.
  36. Install the nut(s) that attach(es) the rear drive unit HV harness bracket to the HV battery.
    10 Nm (7.4 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  37. Verify that the black release lever of the rear drive unit HV harness connector is in the open position, install the connector flat and square to the rear drive unit inverter HV header, secure the release lever to the closed position, and then slide the red connector locking tab.
  38. Install the bolt that attaches the rear drive unit HV harness bracket to the rear drive unit inverter.
    6 Nm (4.4 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  39. Remove the HV gloves and leather over gloves.
  40. Inspect the rear subframe electrical harness logic connector and the rear drive unit inverter logic connector for coolant, and use shop air and a clean dry shop towel to remove any coolant from the connectors.
  41. Connect the rear subframe electrical harness to the rear drive unit inverter logic connector, raise the handle upward, and fasten the locking tab.
  42. Fasten the clip that attaches the rear subframe electrical harness to the rear drive unit inverter.
  43. Position a coolant drain collector underneath the LH rear of the HV battery.
  44. Install the rear drive unit inverter inlet tube in and up between the rear subframe and the HV battery.
  45. Fasten the clip that attaches the rear drive unit inverter inlet tube to rear drive unit HV harness.
  46. Remove the plugs from the fittings, immediately connect the rear drive unit inverter inlet hose to the HV battery, fasten the clip, and then perform a Push-Pull-Push check of the fitting.
  47. Remove the plugs from the fittings, immediately connect the rear drive unit inverter inlet hose to the rear drive unit inverter, fasten the clip, and then perform a Push-Pull-Push check of the fitting.
  48. Remove the coolant drain collector from under the vehicle.
  49. Fasten the barrel clip that attaches the rear drive unit inverter inlet hose to the HV battery.
  50. Install the HV battery rear skid plate. See Skid Plate - HV Battery - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  51. Fasten the clips (x2) that attach the coolant hoses to RH shear plate.
  52. Fasten the clips (x4) that secure coolant hoses to the rear skid plate.
    Note
    Clip quantity varies with older vehicles.
  53. Fasten the clips (x2) that attach the coolant hoses to LH shear plate.
  54. Install the rear stabilizer bar to the rear subframe, and then install new bolts (x4) to attach the stabilizer bar to the subframe.
    30 Nm (22.1 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 11 mm socket
  55. Install the LH rear stabilizer bar end link into the rear stabilizer bar.
    Note
    Move the stabilizer bar up and down to ease installation of the end link.
  56. Install a new nut to attach the LH rear stabilizer bar end link to the rear stabilizer bar.
    55 Nm (40.6 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Use a 5 mm hex tool to prevent the ball joint stud from turning and damaging the ball joint.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 15 mm socket
    • 5 mm hex
  57. Install the RH rear stabilizer bar end link into the rear stabilizer bar.
    Note
    Move the stabilizer bar up and down to ease installation of the end link.
  58. Install a new nut to attach the RH rear stabilizer bar end link to the rear stabilizer bar.
    55 Nm (40.6 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Use a 5 mm hex tool to prevent the ball joint stud from turning and damaging the ball joint.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 15 mm socket
    • 5 mm hex
  59. Install the rear drive unit ground strap to the body, and then install the nut that attaches the ground strap to the body.
    10 Nm (7.4 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm 12-point deep socket
  60. Fasten the clip that attaches the rear subframe harness to the body on the LH side.
  61. Connect electrical harness to the rear subframe harness LH connector.
  62. Fasten the clip that attaches the rear subframe harness to the body on the RH side.
  63. Connect electrical harness to the rear subframe harness RH connector.
  64. Install the rear diffuser. See Diffuser - Rear Fascia (Remove and Replace).
  65. Install the mid aero shield panel. See Panel - Aero Shield - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  66. Install the LH and RH rear suspension covers. See Cover - Rear Suspension - LH (Remove and Replace).
  67. Perform a cooling system vacuum refill. See Cooling System (Vacuum Refill).
  68. Connect LV power. See 12V/LV Power (Disconnect and Connect).
  69. Connect a LV maintainer to the vehicle. See LV Maintainer (Connect and Disconnect).
  70. Reconnect the laptop with Toolbox 3 to the vehicle.
  71. In Toolbox 3, click the Dashboards tab, and type ‘Rear Drive Inverter Replacement' into the search field, and click [Model 3/Y] REAR Drive INVERTER - Replacement.
  72. From the AFTER INVERTER SWAP Action List, click Stop Thermal Fluid Fill/Drain, click Run, and allow the routine to complete.
  73. From the AFTER INVERTER SWAP Action List, click Restore REAR Drive INVERTER Bootloader Data after Replacement and Usage ID Configuration, click Run, and allow the routine to complete.
  74. From the AFTER INVERTER SWAP Action List, click Service CAN Redeploy, click Run, and allow the update to finish.
  75. Disconnect the LV maintainer. See LV Maintainer (Connect and Disconnect).
  76. From the AFTER INVERTER SWAP Action List, click Restore Drive Inverter Application Data after Replacement, click Run, and allow the routine to complete.
  77. From the AFTER INVERTER SWAP Action List, click Secure pairing (immobilizer pairing) between the Security Controller and Drive Inverter and Vehicle Odometer Pairing to UI, click Run, and allow the routine to complete.
  78. From the AFTER INVERTER SWAP Action List, click Coolant Air Purge, click Run, and allow the routine to complete.
  79. From the AFTER INVERTER SWAP Action List, click Thermal System Performance Test, click Run, and allow the routine to complete.
  80. Add coolant to the reservoir as necessary, and install the reservoir cap.
  81. Manually engage the hood latch.
    Note
    If the hood is open, or if the hood latch is not manually engaged, the vehicle speed will be limited and the resolver learn routine will fail.
  82. Touch the brake pedal to make sure the drive rails are on.
  83. From the RESOLVER LEARN Action List, click Rear Drive Unit Resolver Error Learning, click Run, and follow the displayed instructions.
  84. Disconnect the laptop with Toolbox 3 from the vehicle.
  85. Install the LH and RH rear wheels. See Wheel (Remove and Replace).
  86. Remove the stanchions from the rear of the vehicle.
  87. On the vehicle touchscreen, open the hood to disengage the hood latch.
  88. Install the outer HVAC plenum duct. See Plenum - Inlet - HVAC (Remove and Replace).
  89. Install the cabin intake duct. See Duct - Upper - Cabin Intake (Remove and Replace).
  90. Install the rear underhood apron. See Underhood Apron - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  91. Remove the vehicle from the 2 post lift. See Raise Vehicle - 2 Post Lift.
  92. In this procedure, the front or rear subframe was removed and reinstalled. Therefore, refer to the Alignment Requirement tables to determine whether an EPAS alignment check (EC) or four wheel alignment check (AC) is necessary. If performed, add the alignment check/adjust as a separate activity. See Alignment Requirement - Suspension.
