Actuator - 5-Way Valve (Dual Motor) (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 18306312 0.54 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-11-05: Added steps for the coolant valve test routine.
  • 2025-04-29: Corrected torque figures for the bolts that attach the shock tower brace to the vehicle.
Equipment:

  • 1108272-00-B Cap, Logic Conn, Inv, 3DU

Remove

  1. Remove the 2nd row lower seat cushion. See Seat Cushion - Lower - 2nd Row (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the underhood storage unit. See Underhood Storage Unit (Remove and Replace).
  3. Disconnect the 12V power. See 12V/LV Power (Disconnect and Connect).
  4. Remove the nuts (x2) that attach the superbottle to the brace.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  5. Remove the bolts (x2) that attach the battery bracket to the shock tower brace.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm socket
  6. Remove the inner bolts (x2) that attach the shock tower brace to the RH shock tower.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 15 mm socket
  7. Remove the outer bolt that attaches the shock tower brace to the RH shock tower.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 15 mm socket
  8. Remove the inner bolts that attach the shock tower brace to the LH shock tower.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 15 mm socket
  9. Remove the outer bolt that attaches the shock tower brace to the LH shock tower.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 15 mm socket
  10. Release the shock tower brace using the inflatable air bag.
    Tip
    Use a flat head screwdrive to lift the the shock tower brace, and then place the inflatable air bag on both ends of the shock tower brace.
  11. Release the clip that attaches the battery return hose to the superbottle.
  12. Release the clip that attaches the electrical harness to the lower LH side of the superbottle.
    Tip
    Lift the superbottle for better access.
  13. Disconnect the electrical harness from the 5-way valve actuator connector.
    Tip
    Lift the superbottle for better access.
  14. Remove the screws (x3) that attach the 5-way valve actuator to the 5-way valve cover.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T20 bit
  15. Remove the 5-way valve actuator from the 5-way valve cover.

Install

  1. Use the diverter valve wrench to align the larger stemshell shaft spline with the larger recess on the 5-way valve actuator, and then position the 5-way valve actuator on the 5-way valve cover.
    CAUTION
    Very little force is needed to mate the actuator to the cover. If the actuator does not mate to the cover easily, remove the actuator and check the stemshell shaft alignment.
  2. Install the screws that attach the 5-way valve actuator to the 5-way valve cover.
    2 Nm (1.5 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T20 bit
  3. Connect the electrical harness to the 5-way valve actuator connector.
  4. Install the clip that attaches the electrical harness to the lower LH side of the superbottle.
  5. Install the clip that attaches the battery return hose to the superbottle.
  6. Deflate and remove the air bag, and then replace the shock tower brace back to the original position.
  7. Install the outer bolt that attaches the shock tower brace to the LH shock tower.
    62 Nm (45.7 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 15 mm socket
  8. Install the inner bolts (x2) that attach the shock tower brace to the LH shock tower.
    55 Nm (40.6 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 15 mm socket
  9. Tighten the outer bolt that attaches the shock tower brace to the RH shock tower.
    62 Nm (45.7 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 15 mm socket
  10. Tighten the inner bolts (x2) that attach the shock tower brace to the RH shock tower.
    55 Nm (40.6 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 15 mm socket
  11. Install the bolts (x2) that attach the battery bracket to the shock tower brace.
    15 Nm (11.1 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm socket
  12. Install the nuts (x2) that attach the superbottle to the shock tower brace.
    8 Nm (5.9 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  13. Connect 12V power. See 12V/LV Power (Disconnect and Connect).
  14. Place the vehicle in Service Mode. See Service Mode.
  15. Unlock the vehicle gateway. See Gateway (Unlock).
  16. On the touchscreen, tap the Service Mode "wrench" (at the bottom of the touchscreen UI), and then tap Thermal > Coolant System > Coolant Valve Test > Run, and allow the routine to complete.
  17. Exit Service Mode. See Service Mode.
  18. Install the underhood storage unit. See Underhood Storage Unit (Remove and Replace).
  19. Install the 2nd row lower seat cushion. See Seat Cushion - Lower - 2nd Row (Remove and Replace).
