About the Mobile Service Indicator

Some Service Manual procedures can be performed by Mobile Service technicians in a remote setting away from the Service Center (e.g., the customer's home or workplace). To determine whether a Service procedure can be performed by Mobile Service, various factors are considered, such as:
  • The ability to access and service the part in a remote setting
  • The safety of the customer, technician, vehicle, and parts in a remote setting
  • Whether the replacement part can be safely transported in a Tesla Mobile Service vehicle
  • Whether the tools required for the repair are available in a Tesla Mobile Service vehicle
The Mobile Service Indicator, located directly above the Service procedure correction code, defines Mobile eligibility.
CAUTION
The Mobile Service Indicator in the Service Manual only states whether it's physically possible to perform a procedure in a remote setting, however, this Mobile Service indicator does not take into account any local restrictions or laws. Observe all local regulations and legal requirements in your state, country, or region to determine if Mobile Service can be performed.
Note
The Mobile Service Indicator takes into account the region that was selected on service.tesla.com. To ensure the Mobile Service Indicator provides accurate information, make sure the selected region matches the current location of the vehicle undergoing service.
Note
For Tesla Employees: If a Service Manual procedure does not contain a Mobile Service Indicator, refer to the Service Center App to determine Mobile Service eligibility. If the Mobile Service Indicator in the Service Manual conflicts with the Mobile Service Indicator in the Service Center App, the guidance in the Service Center App takes precedence.
