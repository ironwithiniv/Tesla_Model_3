Harness - Front End Module (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 17101402 0.60 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-03-08: Added steps to disconnect and connect the LV power.
  • 2023-03-22: Deleted unnecessary hyperlink at the end of step 11.
Parent topic: 1710 - Harnesses

Remove

  1. Open all four doors and lower all four windows.
  2. Disconnect the LV power. See 12V/LV Power (Disconnect and Connect).
  3. Remove the underhood storage unit. See Underhood Storage Unit (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the front fascia. See Fascia - Front (Remove and Install).
  5. Release the connector locks, and then disconnect the LH and RH front end carrier electrical harness connectors.
  6. Release the clip that attaches the front radar sensor wiring harness to the front end carrier.
  7. Disconnect the electrical harness connector from the radar sensor.
  8. Disconnect the electrical connector from the HomeLink transmitter module, if applicable.
  9. Release the clip that attaches the BLE assembly wiring harness to the front end carrier, and then disconnect the electrical harness connector from the front BLE assembly.
    Note
    The BLE assembly is only available on cars produced before December 2017.
  10. Disconnect the electrical connectors (x2) from the horn assembly.
  11. Release the connector lock, and then disconnect the active grille shutter electrical connector.
  12. Release the clips (x11) that attach the FEM electrical harness to the front end carrier, and then remove the harness from the front end carrier.

Install

Installation procedure is the reverse of removal.

CAUTION
Replace any broken clips.
