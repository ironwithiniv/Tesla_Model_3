Service Kit - Check Valves - Supermanifold (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 18402902 FRT 1.32 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-06-04: Added the step of checking the refrigerant system pressure, and removed the first responder loop steps and Thermal/HVAC performance test steps.
Note
This procedure requires the A/C Refrigerant (Recovery and Recharge) procedure to be performed. If not already automatically added to the Service Visit, add correction code 18200102 as a separate correction within the same activity to the Service Visit.
Parent topic: 1840 - Supermanifold

Remove

  1. Set up the A/C machine.
  2. Remove the underhood storage unit. See Underhood Storage Unit (Remove and Replace).
  3. Perform the following routine using Service Mode or Toolbox (see 0005 - Service Modes): PROC_VCFRONT_X_START-THERMAL-FILL-DRAINvia Service Mode:
    • Thermal ➜ Actions ➜ Start Thermal Fill/Drain
    • Thermal ➜ Coolant System ➜ Start Coolant Fill/Drain
    • Thermal ➜ Refrigerant System ➜ Start Refrigerant Fill/Drain
    via Service Mode Plus:
    • Drive Unit ➜ Front Drive Unit Replacement ➜ Start Coolant Fill/Drain
    • Drive Unit ➜ Rear Drive Unit Replacement ➜ Start Coolant Fill/Drain
    via Toolbox:(link)
    Note
    Select "X" at top right of window to close once complete, Thermal Fluid Fill/Drain routine has a 5 hour time limit, After 5 hours EXV's will close and routine must be performed again, Verify EXV positions are open VIA Garage >Thermal > Refrigerant loop EXV, Verify LCC, CC Left, Evap, CC Right, Chiller are all at ~100%. Recirc is at ~20%, LCC Solenoid is OPENED
  4. Recover the A/C refrigerant. See A/C Refrigerant (Recovery and Recharge) (Restore).
    Warning
    Make sure there is no overpressure in the A/C system before performing the following steps. Failure to do so may result in damage to components and/or personal injury.
    Warning
    HFO-R1234yf and R134a are considered hazardous and, if handled incorrectly, can cause serious injury. Suitable personal protective equipment, consisting of face and eye protection, safety glasses, face shield, heat-proof gloves, chemical resistant gloves, rubber boots and apron or waterproof overall must be worn when carrying out operations on the air conditioning system.
  5. On the touchscreen, make sure that the refrigerant system pressure is at atmospheric level (approx. 1 bar).
    Note
    On the touchscreen, touch Service Mode > Thermal > Refrigerant System.
  6. On the touchscreen, tap Safety > Power Off to power off the vehicle.
  7. Remove A/C hoses from the vehicle.
  8. Carefully depress the Schrader valves (x2) to level out any pressure difference between the A/C system pressure and the actual ambient atmospheric pressure.
    CAUTION
    Make sure to depress both Schrader valves. Failure to do so may result in injury.
  9. Remove the supermanifold to compressor A/C line. See Assembly - A/C Line - Supermanifold to Compressor (Remove and Replace).
  10. Release the locking tab, and then disconnect the electrical connector from the high pressure and temperature sensor.
    CAUTION
    DO NOT push down on the red locking tab. Pull the tab away from the connector until the connector is unlocked, and then continue pulling the main body of the connector to fully disconnect it.
  11. Release the locking tab, and then disconnect the electrical connector from the recirculation EXV.
    CAUTION
    DO NOT push down on the red locking tab. Pull the tab away from the connector until the connector is unlocked, and then continue pulling the main body of the connector to fully disconnect it.
  12. Release the locking tab, and then disconnect the electrical connector from the liquid cooled condenser EXV.
    CAUTION
    DO NOT push down on the red locking tab. Pull the tab away from the connector until the connector is unlocked, and then continue pulling the main body of the connector to fully disconnect it.
  13. Release the locking tab, and then disconnect the electrical connector from the liquid cooled condenser shut off valve.
    CAUTION
    DO NOT push down on the red locking tab. Pull the tab away from the connector until the connector is unlocked, and then continue pulling the main body of the connector to fully disconnect it.
  14. Release the locking tab, and then disconnect the electrical connector from the right side cabin condenser EXV.
    CAUTION
    DO NOT push down on the red locking tab. Pull the tab away from the connector until the connector is unlocked, and then continue pulling the main body of the connector to fully disconnect it.
  15. Release the locking tab, and then disconnect the electrical connector from the high pressure and temperature subcool EXV.
    CAUTION
    DO NOT push down on the red locking tab. Pull the tab away from the connector until the connector is unlocked, and then continue pulling the main body of the connector to fully disconnect it.
  16. Release the locking tab, and then disconnect the electrical connector from the left side cabin condenser EXV.
    CAUTION
    DO NOT push down on the red locking tab. Pull the tab away from the connector until the connector is unlocked, and then continue pulling the main body of the connector to fully disconnect it.
  17. Release the locking tab, and then disconnect the electrical connector from the chiller EXV.
    CAUTION
    DO NOT push down on the red locking tab. Pull the tab away from the connector until the connector is unlocked, and then continue pulling the main body of the connector to fully disconnect it.
  18. Release the locking tab, and then disconnect the electrical connector from the evap EXV.
    CAUTION
    DO NOT push down on the red locking tab. Pull the tab away from the connector until the connector is unlocked, and then continue pulling the main body of the connector to fully disconnect it.
  19. Move thermal harness from supermanifold to side.
  20. Remove and discard the liquid cooled condenser check valve snap ring from the supermanifold.
    Tip
    Use an inspection mirror as needed.
  21. Remove the liquid cooled condenser check valve from the supermanifold.
    CAUTION
    If the check valve separates from the aluminum cap during removal, do not use metal tools. Metal tools may scrape the supermanifold housing causing internal damage. Use fingers or wrap electrical tape around a 90 degree pick.
  22. Remove and discard the evaporator check valve snap ring from the supermanifold.
    Warning
    Wear the Face Shield throughout the entire process to protect against foreign objects flying out under pressure.
  23. Remove the evaporator check valve from the supermanifold.
    CAUTION
    If the check valve separates from the aluminum cap during removal, do not use metal tools. Metal tools may scrape the supermanifold housing causing internal damage. Use fingers or wrap electrical tape around a 90 degree pick instead.
  24. Remove and discard the cabin condenser check valve snap ring from the supermanifold.
    Warning
    Wear the Face Shield throughout the entire process to protect against foreign objects flying out under pressure.
  25. Remove the cabin condenser check valve from the supermanifold.
    CAUTION
    If the check valve separates from the aluminum cap during removal, DO NOT use metal tools. Metal tools may scrape the supermanifold housing causing internal damage. Use fingers or wrap electrical tape around a 90 degree pick instead.

Install

  1. Use the appropriate oil to lubricate the O-rings on the cabin condenser check valve. See for A/C oil specifications. Then install the cabin condenser check valve into the supermanifold.
  2. Install a new cabin condenser check valve snap ring into the supermanifold.
    Warning
    Wear the Face Shield throughout the entire process to protect against foreign objects flying out under pressure.
    Note
    Verify the snap ring is properly seated inside the groove on the supermanifold.
  3. Lubricate O-rings on the evaporator check valve with the appropriate A/C oil. See Fluids and Capacities for A/C oil specifications. Then install the check valve into the supermanifold.
  4. Install a new evaporator check valve snap ring onto the supermanifold.
    Note
    Make sure the snap ring is fully seated inside the groove in the supermanifold.
    Tip
    Use an inspection mirror as needed.
  5. Lubricate O-rings on the liquid cooled condenser check valve with the appropriate A/C oil. See Fluids and Capacities for A/C oil specifications. Then install the check valve into the supermanifold.
    Tip
    Use an inspection mirror as needed.
  6. Install a new liquid cooled condenser check valve snap ring onto the supermanifold.
    Note
    Make sure the snap ring is fully seated inside the groove in the supermanifold.
    Tip
    Use an inspection mirror as needed.
  7. Route thermal harness back to supermanifold.
  8. Connect the electrical connector of the evap EXV.
    CAUTION
    Push the red locking tab towards the connector to engage the locking mechanism. DO NOT push down or pull up on the red locking tab.
  9. Connect the electrical connector of the chiller EXV.
    CAUTION
    Push the red locking tab towards the connector to engage the locking mechanism. DO NOT push down or pull up on the red locking tab.
  10. Connect the electrical connector of the left side cabin condenser EXV.
    CAUTION
    Push the red locking tab towards the connector to engage the locking mechanism. DO NOT push down or pull up on the red locking tab.
  11. Connect the electrical connector of the high pressure and temperature subcool EXV.
    CAUTION
    Push the red locking tab towards the connector to engage the locking mechanism. DO NOT push down or pull up on the red locking tab.
  12. Connect the electrical connector on the right side cabin condenser EXV.
    CAUTION
    Push the red locking tab towards the connector to engage the locking mechanism. DO NOT push down or pull up on the red locking tab.
  13. Connect the electrical connector on the liquid cooled condenser shut off valve.
    CAUTION
    Push the red locking tab towards the connector to engage the locking mechanism. DO NOT push down or pull up on the red locking tab.
  14. Connect the electrical connector on the liquid cooled condenser EXV.
    CAUTION
    Push the red locking tab towards the connector to engage the locking mechanism. DO NOT push down or pull up on the red locking tab.
  15. Connect the electrical connector on the recirc EXV.
    CAUTION
    Push the red locking tab towards the connector to engage the locking mechanism. DO NOT push down or pull up on the red locking tab.
  16. Connect the electrical connector on the high pressure and temperature sensor.
    CAUTION
    Push the red locking tab towards the connector to engage the locking mechanism. DO NOT push down or pull up on the red locking tab.
  17. Install the supermanifold to compressor A/C line. See Assembly - A/C Line - Supermanifold to Compressor (Remove and Replace).
    Note
    Before installation, install new seal washers on the supermanifold to compressor A/C line.
  18. Perform the cooling system vacuum refill procedure. See Cooling System - Vacuum Refill (Test/Adjust).
  19. Start the vacuum leak test and refrigerant recharge procedure. See A/C Refrigerant (Recovery and Recharge) (Restore).
  20. Unlock the vehicle gateway. See Gateway Unlock.
  21. Perform the following routine using Service Mode or Toolbox (see 0005 - Service Modes): TEST-SELF_VCFRONT_X_HEAT-PUMP-COMMISSIONINGvia Service Mode:Thermal ➜ Refrigerant System ➜ Commission Heat Pumpvia Service Mode Plus:Drive Inverter ➜ Front Drive Inverter Replacement ➜ Heatpump commissioningvia Toolbox:(link)
  22. Exit Service Mode. See Service Mode.
  23. Install the underhood storage unit. See Underhood Storage Unit (Remove and Replace).
