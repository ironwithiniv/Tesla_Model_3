Axle Lifting - 4-Post Lift

Correction code 10000505 FRT 0.10 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-05-04: Released new procedure.
Parent topic: 0006 - Periodic Roadworthiness Inspections

Procedure

Warning
To reduce the risk of personal injury, use appropriate equipment while performing this operation.
Warning
Do not work on an incorrectly supported vehicle.
  1. Drive the vehicle on the 4-post lift.
  2. Put wheel chocks (x2) at the rear wheels.
  3. Fully lift the vehicle.
  4. Lift the vehicle's front axle.
    Note
    Position the axle jack under the front suspension. Extend the jack pads and position them under the front lower lateral link and align the pads with the fasteners that support the front damper assembly.
  5. Lower the vehicle's front axle.
  6. Remove the wheel chocks (x2) from the rear wheels and put wheel chocks (x2) at the front wheels.
  7. Lift the vehicle's rear axle.
    Note
    Position the axle jack under the rear suspension. Extend the jack pads and position them under the rear lower aft link and align the pads with the fasteners that connect the rear wheel hubs.
  8. Lower the vehicle's rear axle. and then remove the wheel chocks from the front wheels.
  9. Remove the vehicle from the 4-post lift.
