LV Battery Recover

Note
The LV battery won't recover unless the HV system is healthy and supporting the vehicle. Make sure that the HV system is functioning correctly before attempting to recover the LV battery.
  1. Enable Service Mode. See Service Mode.
  2. If required, power up the vehicle using external power. See LV Maintainer (Connect and Disconnect) (Maintenance)
  3. Make sure that the high voltage system is supporting the low voltage system. On the touchscreen, touch Service Mode > Low Voltage > Power Distribution and make sure that the PCS DCDC Support Status indicates Active.
  4. Make sure that no external power source is connected to the jump post.
  5. Make sure that the LV battery is properly connected. On the touchscreen, touch Service Mode > Low Voltage > Power Distribution and make sure that the Li-ion LV Battery ECPA (Electrical Connector Position Assurance) status indicates Closed.
  6. Confirm that the Li-ion LV battery MOSFET status is Closed. On the touchscreen, touch Service Mode > Low Voltage > Power Distribution and make sure that the Li-ion LV Battery MOSFET status indicates Closed (Green).
  7. If the MOSFET status is Open (Orange), the LV battery has not auto-recovered and the following alert(s) will be present:
    • VCFRONT_a478_LVBMS_MOSFET_Open or VCBATT1_a478_LVBMS_MOSFET_Open
  8. On the touchscreen, touch Close MOSFET > Run, and allow the routine to complete.
  9. If the previous step does not successfully recover the battery, proceed with using the Tesla LV Li-ion battery charger to recover the LV battery, and then repeat this procedure.
  10. Exit Service Mode. See Service Mode.
