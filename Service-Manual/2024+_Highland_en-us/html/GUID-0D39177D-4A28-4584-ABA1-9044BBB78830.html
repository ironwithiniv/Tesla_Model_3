Tweeter - Front Door - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1513010032 FRT 0.06 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2024-12-16: Deleted steps to remove/install Blind Spot Collision Warning device from/to tweeter.
Parent topic: 1513 - Door Trim

Remove

  1. Open the LH front door and lower the LH front window.
  2. Pull straight up to release the clips (x2) that attach the LH front door tweeter assembly to the door panel.
    CAUTION
    Do not pull the tweeter assembly too far from the door panel, as the tweeter electrical connector is still attached.
    Warning
    The video(s) included in this procedure are meant as an overview for supplemental purposes only. Follow all of the steps listed in the procedure to avoid damage to components and/or personal injury.
  3. Disconnect the LH tweeter connector and the blind spot collision warning device connector.
  4. Use a small flat head screwdriver to release the clip, and then remove the LH tweeter from the LH front door panel.

Install

  1. Connect the LH tweeter connector and the blind spot collision warning device connector.
  2. Secure the clip to install the LH tweeter to the LH front door panel.
  3. Pull straight down to install the clips (x2) that attach the LH front door tweeter assembly to the door.
  4. Raise the LH front window and close the LH front door.
