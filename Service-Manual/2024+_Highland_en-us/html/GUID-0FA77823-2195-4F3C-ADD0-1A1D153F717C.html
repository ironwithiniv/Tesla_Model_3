Gas Strut - Hood (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1133030062 FRT 0.06 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Equipment:
  • 1080562-00-A Telescoping Hood Prop
Parent topic: 1133 - Closure Assist Mechanisms and Hinges

Remove

  1. Open the hood.
  2. Use a prop to attach the hood assembly in the open position.
  3. Release the clip that attaches the hood gas strut to the hood mount.
  4. Release the clip that attaches the hood gas strut to the body mount, and then remove the hood gas strut from the vehicle.

Install

  1. Lubricate the pivot points for the hood gas strut.
  2. The rod end of the hood gas strut mounts to the body and the body side of the hood gas strut mounts to the hood assembly.
  3. Install the clip that attaches the hood gas strut to the body mount, and then install the hood gas strut to the vehicle.
  4. Install the clip that attaches the hood gas strut to the hood mount.
  5. Remove the hood assembly prop from the vehicle.
  6. Close the hood.
