Bump Stop - Hood - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1010010202 FRT 0.06 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-11-18: Released new procedure.
Parent topic: 1010 - Body Panels

Remove

  1. Open the LH front door and lower the LH front window.
  2. Open the hood.
  3. Turn counter-clockwise to remove the LH hood bump stop from the hood.

Replace

  1. Turn clockwise to install the LH hood bump stop to the hood.
  2. Close the hood.
  3. Check that hood fitment meets specifications per CVIS.
  4. Open the hood and adjust the LH hood bump stop.
    Note
    Rotate the hood bump stop clockwise to lower the hood. Rotate the hood bump counter-clockwise to raise the hood when adjusting.
  5. Close the hood.
  6. Raise the LH front window and close the LH front door.
