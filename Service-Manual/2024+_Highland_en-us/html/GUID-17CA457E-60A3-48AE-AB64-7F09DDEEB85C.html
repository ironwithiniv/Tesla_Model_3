Harness - Center Console (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1710010052 FRT 0.78 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 1710 - Harnesses

Remove

  1. Remove the center console structure. See Structure - Center Console (Remove and Replace).
  2. Release the clip that attaches the center console harness to the rear of the center console structure.
  3. Release the tabs (x2) that attach the lamp switch connector to the center console structure.
  4. Disconnect the lamp switch connector.
  5. Disconnect the USB module connector.
  6. Release the clips (x5) that attach the center console harness to the front of the center console structure.
  7. Disconnect the upper puddle lamp connectors (x2).
  8. Flip the center console structure over.
  9. Remove the center console air wave. See Air Wave - Center Console (Remove and Replace).
  10. Release the clips (x2) that attach the center console harness to the back of the center console structure.
  11. Disconnect the LV power outlet connector.
  12. Release the clips (x7) that attach the center console harness to the back of the center console structure, and then remove the harness from the vehicle.

Install

  1. Position the center console harness to the center console structure, and then fasten the clips (x7) that attach the center console harness to the back of the center console structure.
  2. Connect the LV power outlet connector.
  3. Fasten the clips (x2) that attach the center console harness to the back of the center console structure.
  4. Install the center console air wave. See Air Wave - Center Console (Remove and Replace).
  5. Flip the center console structure over.
  6. Connect the upper puddle lamp connectors (x2).
  7. Fasten the clips (x5) that attach the center console harness to the front of the center console structure.
  8. Connect the USB module connector.
  9. Connect the lamp switch connector.
  10. Fasten the tabs (x2) that attach the lamp switch connector to the center console structure.
  11. Fasten the clip that attaches the center console harness to the rear of the center console structure.
  12. Install the center console structure. See Structure - Center Console (Remove and Replace).
