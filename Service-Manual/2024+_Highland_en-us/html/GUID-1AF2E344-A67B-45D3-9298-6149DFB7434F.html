Harness - Headliner (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1710100022 FRT 1.32 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-07-02: Added steps to connect/disconnect LV power.
Parent topic: 1710 - Harnesses

Remove

  1. Disconnect the LV power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
  2. Remove the headliner. See Headliner (Remove and Install).
  3. Disconnect the electrical connector from the overhead console.
  4. Disconnect the electrical connector from the LH front headliner microphone.
  5. Disconnect the electrical connector from the RH front headliner microphone.
  6. Disconnect the electrical connector from the LH map lamp.
  7. Disconnect the electrical connector from the RH map lamp.
  8. Disconnect the electrical connector from the LH headliner speaker.
  9. Disconnect the electrical connector from the RH headliner speaker.
  10. Remove and discard the black soundproofing cotton which covers the electrical harness.
  11. Separate the glue attaching points (x2) that attach the headliner harness to the headliner.
  12. Release the clips (x23) and tree clips (x2) that attach the headliner harness to the headliner, and then remove the harness from the headliner.

Install

  1. Fasten the clips (x23) and tree clips (x2) that attach the headliner harness to the headline.
  2. Use a glue gun to attach the headliner harness to the headliner. Allow time for the glut to fully dry.
  3. Connect the electrical connector to the RH headliner speaker.
  4. Connect the electrical connector to the LH headliner speaker.
  5. Connect the electrical connector to the LH front headliner microphone.
  6. Connect the electrical connector to the RH front headliner microphone.
  7. Connect the electrical connector to the LH map lamp.
  8. Connect the electrical connector to the RH map lamp.
  9. Connect the front overhead console electrical connector.
  10. Install new soundproofing cotton which covers the electrical harness.
    Note
    Before installation, clean any remaining soundproofing cotton.
  11. Install the headliner. See Headliner (Remove and Install).
  12. Connect the LV power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
