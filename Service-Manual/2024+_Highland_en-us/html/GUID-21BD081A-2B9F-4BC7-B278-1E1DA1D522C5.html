Panel - Door Trim - Rear - LH (Remove and Install)

Correction code 1513020011 FRT 0.12 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 1513 - Door Trim

Torque Specifications

Table 1. Torque Specifications: Panel - Door Trim - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace)
Description Torque Value Recommended Tools Reuse/Replace Notes
Screws (x2) that attach the LH rear door panel to the LH rear door
6 Nm (4.4 lbs-ft)
  • Torx T30 socket
 Reuse

Remove

  1. Open the LH rear door, and fully lower the window.
  2. Remove the screws (x2) that attach the LH rear door trim panel to the door.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T30 socket
  3. Remove the LH rear door puddle light. See Puddle Lamp - Rear Door (Remove and Replace).
  4. Pull out on the trim panel from the puddle light opening to release the clips that attach the trim panel to the door.
    CAUTION
    Do not remove the trim panel from the door at this time.
  5. Continue to pull and release all of the clips around the edge of the trim panel, working from the bottom up.
    CAUTION
    Do not remove the trim panel from the door at this time.
  6. Disconnect the electrical wiring harness from the door connector.
    CAUTION
    Do not remove the trim panel from the door at this time.
  7. Raise the trim panel up to unhook it from the door, and then remove the panel from the door.
  8. Check the LH rear door for any remaining push clip retainers, and if present, remove them from the door.

Install

  1. Install the LH rear door puddle light to the door trim panel. See Puddle Lamp - Rear Door (Remove and Replace).
  2. Bring the trim panel to the door, and rest it on the door edge.
  3. Connect the electrical harness to the LH rear door connector.
  4. Lift the trim panel, compress the LH rear door inner belt seal slightly, and hook the three trim panel tabs into the top edge of the door.
  5. Fasten the clips that attach the trim panel to the door.
  6. Make sure that the trim panel fits tightly, and that there are no gaps from misaligned or bent clips.
  7. Install the screws (x2) that attach the LH rear door trim panel to the LH rear door.
    6 Nm (4.4 lbs-ft)
    CAUTION
    Make sure that all fasteners are properly torqued and not cross-threaded.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T30 socket
  8. Raise the LH rear window and close the door.
