Pump - Windshield Washer (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1750020312 FRT 0.18 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Equipment:
  • Air Operated Fluid Evacuator
Parent topic: 1750 - Wipers and Washers

Remove

  1. Remove the underhood storage unit. See Underhood Storage Unit (Remove and Replace).
  2. Open the washer reservoir cap.
  3. Place a fluid evacuator near the front of the vehicle and connect it with a compressed air.
  4. Open the evacuator valve and position the evacuator hose into a corner of the washer reservoir, and then evacuate as much as washer as possible.
  5. Disconnect the compressed air and remove the fluid evacuator.
  6. Disconnect the windshield washer pump connector.
  7. Release the clip that attaches the windshield washer hose to the washer pump.
  8. Remove the windshield washer pump from the windshield washer reservoir.
  9. Inspect the grommet for damage and replace as necessary.
    Note
    The new washer pump does not come with a new grommet.

Install

  1. Install the windshield washer pump to the washer reservoir.
    Note
    Ensure the washer pump is aligned with the positioning slot.
  2. Fasten the clip that attaches the windshield washer hose to the washer pump.
  3. Connect the windshield washer pump connector.
  4. Install the underhood storage unit. See Underhood Storage Unit (Remove and Replace).
  5. Fill the windshield washer reservoir with fluid.
  6. Close the washer reservoir cap.
  7. Verify operation of the windshield wipers and washer.
