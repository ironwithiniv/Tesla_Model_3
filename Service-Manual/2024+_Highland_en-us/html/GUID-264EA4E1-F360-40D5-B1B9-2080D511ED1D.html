Basic Vehicle Inspection (Inspection)

Correction code 00030700 FRT 0.90 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Warning
This procedure is a DRAFT. Although it has been validated, Warnings and Cautions might be missing. Follow safety requirements and use extreme caution when working on or near High Voltage systems and components.
  • 2026-06-18: Released new procedure.
Note
This procedure details how to perform the Basic Vehicle Inspection. Fill out SC-26-00-006 while performing this procedure.
Parent topic: 0001 - Maintenance Service

Procedure

  1. Open all doors from the exterior. Verify that the door operates normally and does not do any of the following:
    • Collide with the body or brightwork
    • Produce unusual noises such as creaks, groans, or clicks
    • Pause or fail to fully open
  2. Function check LH front door (interior)
    Note
    Inspect and note functionality for the interior door release, puddle lamps, and manual release cable, From inside look for interference with other components, noises such as creaks, groans and clicks, pauses or incomplete actions
  3. Function check all windows from master switch
    Note
    Document any abnormal operation/noises
  4. Function check wipers and spray jets
    Note
    Ensure hood and doors are closed prior to activating wipers, Inspect and note functionality for wipers and wiper jets, Check for full range of motion, speed of motion, interference with other components, noises such as creaks, groans and clicks, pauses or incomplete actions
  5. Function check all driver controls
    Note
    Shift stalk, turn signals, steering wheel scroll wheels, horns
  6. Function check steering column through UI
    Note
    Move the column to its extreme position in each direction using steering wheel buttons, Return to original position
  7. Function check exterior lights. Verify that the following lights operate normally:
    • Headlights (LH and RH)
    • Fog lights (LH and RH)
    • Taillights (LH and RH)
    • Center high mount stopping light (third brake light)
    • Reverse lights (LH and RH)
    • Side repeaters (LH and RH)
    • Front turn signals (LH and RH)
    • Rear turn signals (LH and RH)
  8. Function check hazard light switch
  9. Function check LH rear door (exterior)
    Note
    Inspect and note functionality for the door handle operation and full range of motion of the door, From outside look for interference with brightwork and door to body, Note noises such as creaks, groans and clicks, pauses or incomplete actions
  10. Function check RH rear door (exterior)
    Note
    Inspect and note functionality for the door handle operation and full range of motion of the door, From outside look for interference with brightwork and door to body, Note noises such as creaks, groans and clicks, pauses or incomplete actions
  11. Function check RH front door (exterior)
    Note
    Inspect and note functionality for the door handle operation and full range of motion of the door, From outside look for interference with brightwork and door to body, Note noises such as creaks, groans and clicks, pauses or incomplete actions
  12. Open hood
  13. Remove rear apron
    Note
    14x clips
  14. Remove fresh air gutter assembly
    Note
    3x clips
  15. Check coolant level in reservoir
  16. Check brake fluid level
  17. Fill washer reservoir with fluid
  18. Install fresh air gutter assembly
    Note
    3x clips
  19. Install rear apron
    Note
    14x clips, Hold rear wall of frunk to keep from flexing while engaging front clips
  20. Close hood
  21. Raise and support vehicle
    Note
    remove charging equipment
  22. Raise vehicle partially and lower lift onto locks
    Note
    Set vehicle to comfortable working height, Make sure there's an audible click of the locks on both sides before lowering, Otherwise vehicle may tilt to the side
  23. Check all four tire pressures manually
    Note
    4x tires, 4x caps, Set to specification if necessary, Refer to the "Tire and Loading Information" label located on the driver side B-pillar, Inspect wheels and tires for any bulges or damage while checking/setting pressures
  24. Record tread depth for LH front tire
    Note
    Measure and record the outer, center, and inner tread depths, Measure the circumferential tire grooves only, Do not place the tire tread gauge probe on the molded treadwear indicators, Recommend replacement if tread depth is 4/32in (3mm) or less
  25. Record tread depth for RH front tire
    Note
    Measure and record the outer, center, and inner tread depths, Measure the circumferential tire grooves only, Do not place the tire tread gauge probe on the molded treadwear indicators, Recommend replacement if tread depth is 4/32in (3mm) or less
  26. Record tread depth for LH rear tire
    Note
    Measure and record the outer, center, and inner tread depths, Measure the circumferential tire grooves only, Do not place the tire tread gauge probe on the molded treadwear indicators, Recommend replacement if tread depth is 4/32in (3mm) or less
  27. Record tread depth for RH rear tire
    Note
    Measure and record the outer, center, and inner tread depths, Measure the circumferential tire grooves only, Do not place the tire tread gauge probe on the molded treadwear indicators, Recommend replacement if tread depth is 4/32in (3mm) or less
  28. Measure and record LH rear brake pad thickness
    Note
    Recommend replacement if 2mm or less, Check both inboard and outboard pads
  29. Inspect LH rear brake rotor condition
    Note
    Check for excessive rust or wear, Minimum spec 26mm
  30. Measure and record RH rear brake pad thickness
    Note
    Recommend replacement if 2 mm or less, Check both inboard and outboard pads
  31. Inspect RH rear brake rotor condition
    Note
    Check for excessive rust or wear, Minimum spec 26mm
  32. Measure and record RH front brake pad thickness
    Note
    Recommend replacement if 2mm or less, Check both inboard and outboard pads
  33. Inspect RH front brake rotor condition
    Note
    Check for excessive rust or wear, Minimum spec 30mm
  34. Measure and record LH front brake pad thickness
    Note
    Recommend replacement if 2mm or less, Check both inboard and outboard pads
  35. Inspect LH front brake rotor condition
    Note
    Check for excessive rust or wear, Minimum spec 30mm
  36. Raise vehicle fully and lower lift onto locks
    Note
    Set vehicle to comfortable working height, Ensure an audible click of locks on both sides is heard before lowering, Vehicle may tilt to the side if both locks are not engaged
  37. Remove the front aero shield. See Panel - Aero Shield - Front (Remove and Replace).
  38. Remove the rear aero shield. See Panel - Aero Shield - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  39. Remove the LH and RH rear suspension covers. See Cover - Rear Suspension - LH (Remove and Replace).
  40. Perform visual inspection of HV battery enclosure
    Note
    Check for any dents, scrapes, or other damage
  41. Inspect underside of vehicle for fluid leaks
  42. Perform inspection of steering components
    Note
    Check steering gear joints, fasteners, and electrical connections, Verify components are not bent or damaged
  43. Perform inspection of front suspension components
    Note
    Check conditions of ball joints and bushings, etc, Inspect for any loose fasteners, Carefully apply pressure with a pry bar as needed
  44. Perform inspection of rear suspension components
    Note
    Check conditions of ball joints and bushings, etc, Inspect for any loose fasteners, Carefully apply pressure with a pry bar as needed
  45. Install the LH and RH rear suspension covers. See Cover - Rear Suspension - LH (Remove and Replace).
  46. Install the rear aero shield. See Panel - Aero Shield - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  47. Install the front aero shield. See Panel - Aero Shield - Front (Remove and Replace).
  48. Lower vehicle until tires are touching ground
    Note
    Raise lift off locks, then hold lock release lever to keep locks free while vehicle is lowered
  49. Raise all four windows
  50. Close all four doors
  51. Perform a road test.
    1. Perform a series of ABS-active stops and ensure proper brake pedal feel and performance.
      Note
      The vehicle must stop completely within 25 feet (7.6 meters) at 20 mph (32 kph), Confirm that no excessive vibration or noise is present.
    2. Check the vehicle alignment.
      Note
      Confirm steering wheel is straight and no vibrations or noises are felt or heard from the suspension. On a flat road, at 35 mph (56 kph), the vehicle should not pull in either direction.
