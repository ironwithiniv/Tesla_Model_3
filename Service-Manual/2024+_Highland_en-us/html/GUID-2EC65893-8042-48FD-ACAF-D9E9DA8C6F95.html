Touchscreen - Front (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 2107123022 FRT 0.18 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Equipment:
  • 1773092-00-A Diagnostic Harness Adapter - All Models
  • 1930388-00-A OBD II TO RJ45 NETWORK PORT

Parent topic: 2105 - Touchscreen

Remove

  1. Open the LH front door and lower the LH front window.
  2. Disconnect the LV battery power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
  3. Remove the instrumental panel service panel. See Service Panel - Instrument Panel (Remove and Replace).
  4. Disconnect the temperature sensor connector.
    Warning
    The video(s) included in this procedure are meant as an overview for supplemental purposes only. Follow all of the steps listed in the procedure to avoid damage to components and/or personal injury.
  5. Remove and discard the patch bolts (x2) that attach the center display to the instrumental panel carrier.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 2 in extension
    • 10 mm socket
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  6. Disconnect the harness connectors (x2) from the touchscreen.
  7. Remove the touchscreen from the vehicle.

Install

  1. Position the touchscreen into the vehicle, and then connect the harness connectors (x2) to the touchscreen.
  2. Install the new patch bolts (x2) that attach the center display to the instrumental panel carrier.
    16 Nm (11.8 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 2 in extension
    • 10 mm socket
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  3. Connect the temperature sensor connector.
  4. Install the instrumental panel service panel. See Service Panel - Instrument Panel (Remove and Replace).
  5. Connect the LV battery power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
  6. Reinstall the software. See Software Reinstall - Touchscreen.
  7. Raise the LH front window and close the LH front door.
