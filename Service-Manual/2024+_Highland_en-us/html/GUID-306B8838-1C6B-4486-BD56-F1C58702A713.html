Oil Pump - Electric - Tesla - Rear Drive Unit (Remove and Replace)

Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. Parent topic: 4001 - Rear Drive Unit Assembly

Remove

  1. Raise and support the vehicle. See Raise Vehicle - 2 Post Lift.
  2. Open the LH front door and lower the LH front window.
  3. Disconnect the LV battery power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
  4. Remove the rear aero shield panel. See Panel - Aero Shield - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  5. Disconnect the oil pump connector.
  6. Position the oil drain under the oil filter area, and then remove the oil filter from the drive unit.
    Warning
    The video(s) included in this procedure are meant as an overview for supplemental purposes only. Follow all of the steps listed in the procedure to avoid damage to components and/or personal injury.
  7. Remove the bolts (x2) that secures the oil pump.
    Warning
    The video(s) included in this procedure are meant as an overview for supplemental purposes only. Follow all of the steps listed in the procedure to avoid damage to components and/or personal injury.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
    • 4 in extension
    • Socket 3/8in Dr EPL10
    • ¼ in to 3/8 in adapter
    • Torque wrench with angle measurement
  8. Remove the oil pump assembly and allow the fluid to drain.
    Note

    Use the rear drive unit casting as leverage to loosen the oil pump and then pull the oil pump away.

    Drain the oil until only slow drips exit the oil pump housing.

  9. Remove the fill plug from the rear drive unit, and wait for 10 minutes for the oil to naturally flow out until it reaches the droplet state.
  10. Inspect the 3 O-rings on the removed oil pump, the O-ring on the removed fill plug, and the seal on the removed oil filter. Make sure that no O-ring or seal is left in the housing or damaged.
    Note
    Replace all damaged components.

Install

  1. Install the new oil pump assembly.
    Note
    Lightly coat the o-rings with clean ATF-9.
  2. Install the bolts (x2) that secures the oil pump.
    5 Nm (3.7 lbs-ft)
    Warning
    The video(s) included in this procedure are meant as an overview for supplemental purposes only. Follow all of the steps listed in the procedure to avoid damage to components and/or personal injury.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
    • 4 in extension
    • Socket 3/8in Dr EPL10
    • ¼ in to 3/8 in adapter
    • Torque wrench with angle measurement
  3. Lightly lubricate the new oil filter seal, and then install the new oil filter to the rear drive unit.
    3 Nm (2.2 lbs-ft) + 135 degrees
  4. Set up the fill kit with KAF 1, fill the measuring container to 2300ml.
  5. Connect the 12V power supply to the oil pump.
  6. Install the fluid transfer pump.
    Note
    Trim both the input and output hoses to 26", insert the output hose into the fill hole until fully seated, and then pull back 5mm.
  7. Place the measured fluid container in the drain basket, fully submerge the input hose, then prime the pump with KAF 1 fluid.
  8. With assistance, pump KAF 1 fluid into the rear drive unit.
    Note
    The minimum refill capacity of the rear drive unit assembly is 2.1 liters. Using excess gearbox fluid in the fill container allows the oil pump to dispense the full 2.1L without drawing air. Make sure that there is less than 200mL left when completed. Pumping the full 2.3 liters is not required.
    Note
    The assistant will run the oil pump switch box using XP-10 Jump Pack and have the rear drive unit fill plug ready for installation.
    Note
    Do not run the pump dry. Pump 5 full strokes from fluid container while simultaneously running the oil pump switch box for entire fill process. Keep running the oil pump switch box until the drain plug is installed in the next step. If the pump is not running or the drain plug is not installed, fluid will drain from plug within 10 seconds.
  9. Install the fill plug into the rear drive unit.
    15 Nm (11.1 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
    • 8 mm socket
    • ¼ in to 3/8 in adapter
  10. Disconnect the 12V power supply tool from the oil pump.
  11. Connect the oil pump connector, and then remove the oil drain container from underneath the drive unit.
  12. Install the rear aero shield panel. See Panel - Aero Shield - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  13. Connect the LV battery power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
  14. Locally connect a laptop with Toolbox 3 to the vehicle. See Toolbox (Connect and Disconnect).
  15. Reinstall the software. See Software Reinstall - Touchscreen.
  16. On the laptop, select Actions tab, and search for "Oil Pump Low Pressure Test - Rear Drive Unit". Select via Toolbox:(link), click Run, and allow the routine to complete.
  17. Disconnect the laptop.
  18. Disable Service Mode. See Service Mode.
  19. Raise the LH front window and close the LH front door.
