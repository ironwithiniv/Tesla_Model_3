USB Module - Center Console (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1519010162 FRT 0.12 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 1519 - Center Console

Remove

  1. Remove the center console rear end panel. See Panel - Rear End - Center Console (Remove and Replace).
  2. Release the clips and tabs that attach the USB bezel to the center console.
    Note
    First release the clips from the side of the USB bezel, and then slide the tabs on the USB bezel out of the base. Take care not to damage the tabs.
  3. Disconnect the USB connector and then remove the USB bezel from the center console.

Install

  1. Connect the USB connector.
  2. Install the tabs and clips that attach the USB bezel to the center console.
    Note
    First slide the tabs into the base, and then secure the clips.
  3. Install the center console rear end panel. See Panel - Rear End - Center Console (Remove and Replace).
