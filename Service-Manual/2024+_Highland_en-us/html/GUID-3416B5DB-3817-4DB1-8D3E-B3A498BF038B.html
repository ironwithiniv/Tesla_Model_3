Tesla Badge - Rear (China) (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1205010022 FRT 0.18 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-10-28: Added information about the new jig installation tool 1967006-00-B.
  • 2024-09-04: Added a note to remind that the badge is not reusable due to the positioning problem.
Equipment:
  • 1453028-00-A Badge Removal Kit
  • 1465974-00-A Pressure Roller
  • 1967006-00-A/B Chinese Badge Alignment Tool
Note
The badge is not reusable due to the required positioning accuracy. If the badge is removed for any reason, it must be replaced with a new one.
Parent topic: 1205 - Badges and Films

Remove

  1. Prepare the badge removal tool (1453028-00-A) with approximately 24 inches/60 centimeters of monofilament line.
  2. Tie slip knots at both ends of the fishing line and attach them each to the center groove of a handle.
  3. Move the badge removal tool in a sawing motion to separate the Chinese Tesla badge from the trunk lid.
  4. Use IPA wipes to clean all adhesive residue and debris from the lower left trunk lid surface.

Install

  1. Adjust the trunk to an appropriate height, install the trunk badge alignment tool (1967006-00-A/B) into position on the trunk lid and secure the tool in place.
    Note
    1967006-00-A is only applicable to metallic color badges, while 1967006-00-B is applicable to both charcoal black and metallic color badges.
    Note
    Ensure that jig fits perfectly with the lower and left edge of the trunk.
  2. Peel the adhesive backing from the Chinese Tesla badge, then place the badge into the jig. Align the holes of the protective film to the pins of jig, then press the badge to the trunk.
  3. Remove the protective film from the badge, and then remove the jig from the vehicle.
    Note
    Do not discard the protective film, it will be used again in the next step.
  4. Replace the protective film over the badge, use a pressure roller (1465974-00-A) to fully adhere the badge, and then remove the protective film.
    CAUTION
    Be careful not to damage the badge during the rolling process.
  5. Use an IPA wipe to clean the badge and the area around the badge.
    Note
    Make sure that all edges of the badge are securely affixed to the trunk lid surface.
  6. Close the trunk.
