Lumbar Support - Backrest - Driver Seat (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1301020042 FRT 0.42 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2024-04-11: Add software reinstall step.
  • 2025-06-26: Added the seat calibration step, and the part number of the disposable lumbar pump clips.
Equipment:
  • 1132841-00-A - Headrest Removal Tool
  • 2077509-00-A - Lumbar Pump Clip (2)
Parent topic: 1301 - Front Seat Tracks and Motors

Torque Specifications

Table 1. Torque Specifications: Lumbar Support - Backrest - Driver Seat (Remove and Replace)
Description Torque Value Recommended Tools Reuse/Replace Notes
Bolt that attaches the driver seat lumbar support assembly to the driver seat frame
5 Nm (3.7 lbs-ft)
  • 10 mm socket
 Reuse

Remove

  1. Remove the driver seat back shell. See Shell - Seat Back - Front Seat - LH (Remove and Replace).
  2. Disconnect the LV power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
  3. Release LH front seat back trim retainers (X6) from seat back frame.
    CAUTION
    Wearing certain PPE to avoid hands cut.
  4. Remove the upper and lower flaps covering the outboard side of the seat back frame.
    Note
    Pull the flaps out of the metal retaining tabs to release them.
  5. Pull the seat upholstery away from the airbag to gain access to the electrical connector and then press and hold the black tabs on both sides and pull them down to disconnect the airbag electrical connector.
  6. Remove the harness clip securing the air bag pigtail to the seat back frame with a clip prytool.
  7. Disconnect the electrical harness from the recliner motor connector.
  8. Disconnect the electrical harness from the lumbar valve pack and ECU.
  9. Disconnect the electrical harness from the lumbar pump connector.
  10. Disconnect the electrical harness from the backrest ventilation fan connector.
  11. Use a trim tool to release the clips (x4) that attach the electrical harness to the seat frame.
  12. Remove the bolt that attaches the lumbar support assembly to the seat frame.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  13. Release lower portion of power lumbar assembly from seat back frame.
  14. Remove the upper and lower flaps covering the inboard side of the seat back frame.
    Note
    Pull the flaps out of the metal retaining tabs to release them.
  15. Pull the seat upholstery away from the FSAB to gain access to the electrical connector, and then press and hold the black tabs on both sides and pull them down to disconnect the connector.
  16. Remove and discard the clips (x2) holding the lumbar pump to the seat frame.
    Note
    Squeeze the three lobes of the retaining clips and push towards the pump to release.
  17. Slide the lumbar support assembly down, then press the bottom of the lumbar support assembly in, and at the same time, slide the lumbar support assembly up. Pivot the bottom of the lumbar support assembly out from the frame, and pull the lumbar support assembly down and out of the frame.
    Warning
    The video(s) included in this procedure are meant as an overview for supplemental purposes only. Follow all of the steps listed in the procedure to avoid damage to components and/or personal injury.

Install

  1. Slide the lumbar assembly posts into the top of the seat back frame and push the assembly in toward the foam. Then slide the assembly down towards the bottom of the seat frame and clip the bottom of the assembly to the seat frame.
    Warning
    The video(s) included in this procedure are meant as an overview for supplemental purposes only. Follow all of the steps listed in the procedure to avoid damage to components and/or personal injury.
  2. Install new clips (x2) (2077509-00-A) holding the lumbar pump to the seat frame.
  3. Connect the LH front seat FSAB connector.
  4. Install the lower and upper flaps covering the inboard corner of the seat back frame.
    Note
    Pull the flaps over the metal retaining tabs to secure them.
  5. Connect the lumbar pump connector.
  6. Connect the electrical harness of the lumbar valve pack and ECU.
  7. Connect the electrical harness of the recliner motor.
  8. Secure the lower portion of power lumbar assembly onto seat back frame.
  9. Install the bolt securing the power lumbar assembly to the seat back frame.
    5 Nm (3.7 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  10. Install the clips (x4) that attach the electrical harness to the seat frame.
  11. Install the electrical harness of the backrest ventilation fan.
  12. Install the harness clip securing the airbag pigtail to the seat back frame with a clip prytool.
  13. Connect the LH front seat side air bag connector.
  14. Install the lower and upper flaps covering the outboard corner of the seat back frame.
    Note
    Pull the flaps over the metal retaining tabs to secure them.
  15. Secure LH front seat back trim retainers (x6) to the seat back frame.
    CAUTION
    Wearing certain PPE to avoid hands cut.
  16. Install the driver seat back shell. Shell - Seat Back - Front Seat - LH (Remove and Replace).
  17. Connect the LV power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
  18. Reinstall the software. See Software Reinstall - Touchscreen.
  19. Perform the following routine using Service Mode or Toolbox (see 0005 - Service Modes): PROC_VCLEFT_SEAT-CALIBRATEvia Service Mode:Low Voltage ➜ Seats ➜ Calibrate Seatvia Toolbox:(link)
    CAUTION
    The seat will move automatically during this routine. Prior to execution, make sure there is nothing (including objects and aftermarket accessories) behind or underneath the related seat to prevent component damage.
  20. Exit Service Mode. See Service Mode.
  21. Raise all windows and close all doors.
