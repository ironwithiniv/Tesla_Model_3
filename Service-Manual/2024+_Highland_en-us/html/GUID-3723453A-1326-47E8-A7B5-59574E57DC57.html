License Plate Light (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1740020052 FRT 0.06 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-06-16: Simplified procedure, no trim removal required.
  • 2025-12-26: Added the trunk latch cover steps.
Parent topic: 1740 - Exterior Lights

Remove

  1. Open the trunk.
  2. Using a plastic tool trim, push the license plate light towards the LH side of the vehicle to compress the mounting clip, and then pull the license plate light down to remove it from the vehicle.
  3. Disconnect the electrical connector from the license plate light.

Install

Installation procedure is the reverse of removal.
