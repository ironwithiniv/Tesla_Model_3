Switch Window - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1513020072 FRT 0.12 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 1513 - Door Trim

Torque Specifications

Table 1. Torque Specifications: Switch Window - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace)
Description Torque Value Recommended Tools Reuse/Replace Notes
Bolt at the middle of the LH rear door armrest
6 Nm (4.4 lbs-ft)
  • Torx T30 socket
 Reuse

Remove

  1. Open the LH rear door and lower the LH rear window.
  2. Remove the bolt at the middle of the LH rear door armrest, as illustrated.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T30 socket
  3. Release the clips (x8) that attach the bolt cover to the middle of the LH rear door armrest, and then remove the bolt cover from the armrest.
  4. Disconnect the LH rear window switch electrical connector.
  5. Release the tabs (x3) that attach the LH rear window switch to the door trim, and then remove the switch from the vehicle.
    Warning
    The video(s) included in this procedure are meant as an overview for supplemental purposes only. Follow all of the steps listed in the procedure to avoid damage to components and/or personal injury.

Install

  1. Release the harness from the replacement LH rear window switch.
  2. Secure the tabs (x3) that attach the LH rear window switch to the door trim.
    Warning
    The video(s) included in this procedure are meant as an overview for supplemental purposes only. Follow all of the steps listed in the procedure to avoid damage to components and/or personal injury.
  3. Connect the LH rear window switch electrical connector.
  4. Fasten the clips (x8) that attach the bolt cover to the middle of the LH rear door armrest.
  5. Install the bolt at the middle of the LH rear door armrest.
    6 Nm (4.4 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T30 socket
  6. Raise the LH rear window and close the LH rear door.
