Harness - Fascia - Rear (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1710050022 FRT 0.42 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 1710 - Harnesses

Remove

  1. Remove the rear fascia. See Fascia Assembly - Rear (Remove and Install).
  2. Disconnect the electrical connector from the pedestrian warning speaker.
    Warning
    The video(s) included in this procedure are meant as an overview for supplemental purposes only. Follow all of the steps listed in the procedure to avoid damage to components and/or personal injury.
  3. Disconnect the electrical connectors from the LH and RH rear fascia lamps respectively.
  4. Remove the clips (x5) and locator hooks (x2) that attach the rear fascia harness to the vehicle.
  5. Remove the rear fascia harness.

Install

  1. Install the rear fascia harness.
  2. Install the clips (x5) and locator hooks (x2) that attach the rear fascia harness to the vehicle.
  3. Connect the electrical connectors to the LH and RH rear fascia lamps respectively.
  4. Connect the electrical connector to the pedestrian warning speaker.
  5. Install the rear fascia. See Fascia Assembly - Rear (Remove and Install).
