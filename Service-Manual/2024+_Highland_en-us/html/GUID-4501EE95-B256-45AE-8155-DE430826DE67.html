Link - Compliance - Lower - Front - Pair (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 3101021102 FRT 0.48 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-09-07: Added steps to hand-tighten the fasteners securing the compliance link to the vehicle before torquing them.
  • 2024-03-05: Updated the torque specification table and tool list.
CAUTION
If performing this procedure as a result of a collision, see the Collision Repair Manual to verify whether the steering gear must be replaced as well.
Parent topic: 3101 - Front Suspension (Including Hubs)

Torque Specifications

Table 1. Torque Specifications: Link - Compliance - Lower - Front - Pair (Remove and Replace)
Description Torque Value Recommended Tools Reuse/Replace Notes
Nut that attaches the LH and RH front lower compliance links to the knuckle
180 Nm (132.7 lbs-ft)
  • SKT, IMPACT, 3/4" DR, DEEP, 22MM
 Replace 1109263-00-B
Bolt and nut that attach the LH and RH front lower compliance links to the front subframe
115 Nm (84.8 lbs-ft)
  • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
  • 21 mm deep socket
  • Ratchet/torque wrench
 Reuse

Remove

  1. Inspect the vehicle for abnormal tire wear that might indicate the need for a wheel alignment. Note any findings.
  2. Raise and support the vehicle. See Raise Vehicle - 2 Post Lift.
  3. Remove the front wheels. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
  4. Remove the front aero shield. See Panel - Aero Shield - Front (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove and discard the nut that attaches the LH front lower compliance link to the knuckle.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • SKT, IMPACT, 3/4" DR, DEEP, 22MM
  6. Remove the bolt and nut that attach the LH front lower compliance link to the front subframe, and then remove the link from the vehicle.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 21 mm deep socket
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  7. Repeat step 5 and step 6 for the RH side of the vehicle.

Replace

  1. With assistance, position the LH front lower compliance link to the front subframe, and then hand-tighten the bolt and nut that attach the LH front lower compliance link to the front subframe.
    Note
    Torque at a later step.
  2. Hand-tighten a new nut that attaches the LH front lower compliance link to the knuckle.
    Note
    Torque at a later step.
  3. Torque the bolt and nut that attach the LH front lower compliance link to the front subframe.
    115 Nm (84.8 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 21 mm deep socket
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  4. Torque the nut that attaches the LH front lower compliance link to the knuckle.
    180 Nm (132.7 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • SKT, IMPACT, 3/4" DR, DEEP, 22MM
  5. Repeat step 1 to 4 for the RH side of the vehicle.
  6. Install the front aero shield. See Panel - Aero Shield - Front (Remove and Replace).
  7. Install the front wheels. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
  8. Refer to the Alignment Requirement tables to determine whether an EPAS alignment check (EC) or four wheel alignment check (AC) is necessary. If performed, add the alignment check/adjust as a separate activity. See Alignment Requirement - Suspension.
