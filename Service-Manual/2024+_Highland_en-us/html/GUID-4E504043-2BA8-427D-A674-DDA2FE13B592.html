Junction Block - Brake Line (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 3303010042 FRT 1.02 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

CAUTION
Brake fluid dissolves paint. Have clean towels and plenty of water standing by to wash spilled brake fluid off painted surfaces.
Note
Dispose of used brake fluid in a manner consistent with local environmental codes.
Parent topic: 3303 - Brake Pipes and Hoses

Torque Specifications

 Reuse
The LH rear brake line tube nut at the LH rear brake pipe junction block
16 Nm (11.8 lbs-ft)
  • 12 mm socket
 Reuse
Nut that attaches the LH rear brake pipe junction block to the vehicle
5.5 Nm (4.1 lbs-ft)
  • 10 mm socket
 Reuse

Remove

  1. Raise and support the vehicle. See Raise Vehicle - 2 Post Lift.
  2. Remove the rear underhood apron. See Underhood Apron - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove all four wheels. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
  4. Remove the LH rear wheel arch liner. See Wheel Arch Liner - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
  5. Position the oil drain under the rear brake line junction block.
  6. Remove the RH rear brake line tube nut from the LH rear brake pipe junction block.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 12 mm socket
  7. Remove the LH rear brake line tube nut from the LH rear brake pipe junction block.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 12 mm socket
  8. Remove the black clip from the LH rear brake pipe junction block.
  9. Remove the nut that attaches the LH rear brake pipe junction block to the vehicle, and then remove the block from the vehicle.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket

Install

  1. Position the LH rear brake pipe junction block to the vehicle, and then place the LH and RH rear brake pipes to the block.
  2. Install the nut that attaches the LH rear brake pipe junction block to the vehicle.
    5.5 Nm (4.1 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  3. Install the black clip to the LH rear brake pipe junction block.
  4. Install the RH rear brake line tube nut to the LH rear brake pipe junction block.
    16 Nm (11.8 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 12 mm socket
  5. Install the LH rear brake line tube nut to the LH rear brake pipe junction block.
    16 Nm (11.8 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 12 mm socket
  6. Remove the oil drain from under the vehicle.
  7. Install the LH rear wheel arch liner. See Wheel Arch Liner - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
  8. Perform a brake fluid flush on the brakes. See Brake Fluid Bleed/Flush (Maintenance).
