DC Harness - Power Conversion System (PCS2 Lite) (Model 3 Standard) (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 16300501002 FRT 1.38 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Equipment:
  • 1057602-00-A Ratchet, 1/4" Sq Dr, HV Insulated
  • 1057604-00-A Skt, 1/4" Sq Dr, 8mm, HV Insulated
  • 1131071-00-A Dummy Disconnect, Pyro, Safety
  • 1108272-00-B Cap, Logic Conn, Inv, 3DU
  • 1076927-00-A Resistance meter, microohm, Hioki RM 3548
Warning
Remove all jewelry (watches, bracelets, rings, necklaces, earrings, ID tags, piercings, etc.) from your person, and all objects (keys, coins, pens, pencils, tools, fasteners, etc.) from your pockets before performing any procedure that exposes you to high voltage.
Warning
If corrective eyewear is necessary to safely perform any procedure, make sure that the eyewear is securely restrained to the head and cannot fall off.
Warning

Only technicians who have completed all required certification courses are permitted to perform this procedure. Tesla recommends third party service provider technicians undergo equivalent training before performing this procedure. For more information on Tesla Technician requirements, or descriptions of the subject matter for third parties, see HV Certification Requirements. Proper personal protective equipment (PPE) and insulating HV gloves with a minimum rating of class 0 (1000V) must be worn at all times a high voltage cable, busbar, or fitting is handled. Refer to Tech Note TN-15-92-003, High Voltage Awareness Care Points for additional safety information.

Parent topic: 1630 - HV Battery Electrical Components

Remove

  1. Perform the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure. See Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure.
    Note
    After verifying there is no HV voltage, reinstall the removed parts but Do not reconnect the LV power.
  2. Remove the 2nd row lower seat cushion. See Seat Cushion - 2nd Row (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the ancillary bay cover. See Ancillary Bay Cover (Remove and Install).
    Warning
    HV insulating gloves and leather glove protectors must be worn throughout the remainder of this procedure. Do not remove gloves or protectors until otherwise noted.
  4. Install the logic connector cap to the high voltage controller connector.
  5. Inspect the ancillary bay for missing insulators.
    Note
    Install the service insulator kit before proceeding with the following steps.
  6. Remove the pyrotechnic battery disconnect. See Pyro Disconnect - HV Battery (Remove and Replace).
  7. Use rubber bands to secure the high voltage controller to the vehicle.
  8. Remove the insulators (x2) from the fuse and bolts.
  9. Remove and discard the nuts (x2) that attach PCS2 output fuse to the ancillary bay bus, and then remove the fuse.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Insulated 8 mm socket
  10. Remove and discard the nut that attaches the negative output harness to negative HV battery DC link busbar.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Insulated 8 mm socket
  11. Remove and discard the nuts (x2) that attach the A/C compressor fuse to the ancillary bay bus, and then remove the fuse.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Insulated 8 mm socket
  12. Remove and discard the nut that attaches the ancillary positive busbar bracket to the positive HV battery DC link busbar, and then remove the bracket.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Insulated 8 mm socket
  13. Disconnect the HV DC harness connector from the PCS2 Lite by pulling the locking tab upward.
    Note
    May need a plastic trim tool to help lift the connector upward.
  14. Remove the bolt that attaches the RH ancillary bay bus cover to the body.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Insulated Torx T25 bit
  15. With assistance, carefully lift up the DC link busbar cover.
    Note
    1x clip. Be careful not to damage cover when lifting.
  16. Remove the PCS2 lite HV DC harness from the ancillary bay.
    Note
    It is not allowed to damage the insulation layer of the harness. If it is damaged, replace the harness.
    1. Remove the harness terminals from the bolt studs(x2).
    2. Carefully lift up the DC link busbar cover, and then pull the harness outward to release it from the slots (x3).
    3. Release the harness from the hook, and then remove the harness from the vehicle.

Install

  1. Install the PCS2 lite HV DC harness to the ancillary bay.
    Note
    3x harness slots, 1x hook, 2x studs
    CAUTION
    It is not allowed to damage the insulation layer of the harness. If necessary, use a HV insulated 90 degree hook to assist.
    CAUTION
    Make sure that harness is not pinched.
    Note
    After the harness is properly positioned, install the harness terminals onto the bolt studs.
  2. Carefully lower the DC link busbar cover into position.
    Note
    1x clip
  3. Install the bolt that attaches the RH ancillary bay bus cover to the body.
    3 Nm (2.2 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Insulated Torx T25 bit
  4. Connect the HV DC harness connector to the PCS2 Lite by pushing it downward, then pressing the locking tab down to secure it.
  5. Install a new nut that attaches the negative output harness to negative HV battery DC link busbar.
    4.5 Nm (3.3 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Mark the nut with a paint pen after installation.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Insulated 8 mm socket
  6. Perform a zero adjust of the Hioki resistance meter in preparation to measure resistances later in this procedure.
    Warning
    HV insulating gloves and leather glove protectors must be worn throughout the remainder of this procedure. Do not remove gloves or protectors until otherwise noted.
  7. Use the Hioki resistance meter to measure the resistance at the negative output harness terminal and the negative HV battery DC link busbar.
    Note
    The maximum acceptable resistance is 55μΩ or less.
  8. Install a new nut that attaches the ancillary positive busbar bracket to the positive HV battery DC link busbar.
    4.5 Nm (3.3 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Mark the nut with a paint pen after installation.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Insulated 8 mm socket
  9. Use the Hioki resistance meter to measure the resistance at the ancillary positive busbar bracket to positive HV battery DC link busbar.
    Note
    The maximum acceptable resistance is 60μΩ or less.
  10. Install new nuts (x2) that attach PCS2 output fuse to the ancillary bay bus.
    4.5 Nm (3.3 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Mark the nut with a paint pen after installation.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Insulated 8 mm socket
  11. Use the Hioki resistance meter to measure the resistance at the PCS fuse input joint.
    Note
    The maximum acceptable resistance is 145μΩ or less.
  12. Use the Hioki resistance meter to measure the resistance at the PCS fuse output joint.
    Note
    The maximum acceptable resistance is 105μΩ or less.
  13. Install new nuts (x2) that attach the A/C compressor fuse to the ancillary bay bus.
    4.5 Nm (3.3 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Mark the nut with a paint pen after installation.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Insulated 8 mm socket
  14. Use the Hioki resistance meter to measure the resistance at the compressor fuse input joint.
    Note
    The maximum acceptable resistance is 145μΩ or less.
  15. Use the Hioki resistance meter to measure the resistance at the compressor fuse output joint.
    Note
    The maximum acceptable resistance is 105μΩ or less.
  16. Release the rubber bands, and then position the high voltage controller back to its original position.
  17. Install the insulators (x2) onto the fuse and bolts.
  18. Measure the voltage across the pyrotechninc battery disconnect mount points, and then install the pyrotechnic battery disconnect. See Pyro Disconnect - HV Battery (Remove and Replace).
  19. Remove the service insulator kit.
  20. Remove the logic connector cap from the high voltage controller connector.
  21. Install the ancillary bay cover. See Ancillary Bay Cover (Remove and Install).
    Note
    Before installing the ancillary bay cover, inspect the ancillary bay cover seal and confirm there is no visual damage. If the seal is torn and/or delaminated, replace the ancillary bay cover.
  22. Install the 2nd row lower seat cushion. See Seat Cushion - 2nd Row (Remove and Replace).
  23. Connect the LV power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
