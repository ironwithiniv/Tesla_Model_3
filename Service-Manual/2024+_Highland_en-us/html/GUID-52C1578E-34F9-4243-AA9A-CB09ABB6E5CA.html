Sliding Doors Assembly - Center Console (Model 3 Standard) (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 15190402602 15190402601 FRT 0.48 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Warning
This procedure is a DRAFT. Although it has been validated, Warnings and Cautions might be missing. Follow safety requirements and use extreme caution when working on or near High Voltage systems and components.
  • 2025-09-05: Released new procedure.
  1. Remove the LH and RH hockey sticks from the center console assembly. See Hockey Stick - Center Console - LH (Model 3 Standard) (Remove and Replace)
  2. Disconnect front lamp switch connector
    Note
    1x connector, 1x locking tab, Using a 90 degree pick, carefully unlock locking tab, gently disconnect the connector from the center console harness assembly
  3. Lift center console arm rest
  4. Remove the screws securing both sides of sliding door assembly and cup holder panel assembly
    Note
    12x screws, T20, 1 Nm
  5. Remove sliding lid and cup holder panel assembly from center console
    Note
    6x clips
  6. Separate sliding door assembly from cup holder panel assembly
    Note
    7x clips
  7. Install cup holder panel assembly into sliding door assembly
    Note
    7x clips
  8. Install sliding door assembly and cup holder panel assembly into center console
    Note
    x7 clips
  9. Install screws securing both sides of sliding door assembly and cup holder panel assembly
    Note
    12x screws, T20, 1 Nm
  10. Close center console arm rest
  11. Connect front lamp switch to center console harness assembly
    Note
    1x connector, 1x locking tab
  12. Install the LH and RH hockey sticks onto the center console assembly. See Hockey Stick - Center Console - LH (Model 3 Standard) (Remove and Replace)
