Door - Rear - LH (Remove and Install)

Correction code 1010025041 FRT 0.24 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2024-01-24: Added a note in case that the shouldered / tapered bolts are not allowing for successful adjustment.
Parent topic: 1010 - Body Panels

24 Nm (17.7 lbs-ft)

 Reuse

Remove

  1. Open the rear LH door, and fully lower the window.
  2. Release the clips (x4) of the rear LH door electrical harness seal at the B-pillar, and pull the wiring harness out of the B-pillar bulkhead.
  3. Disconnect the LH rear door harness connector from the LH body harness.
  4. Remove the bolt that attaches the LH rear door check strap to the body.
    Note
    Unbolting the door check allows the door to swing freely beyond opening limits.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T40 socket
  5. With assistance, remove the bolts (x4) that attach the LH rear door assembly to the vehicle.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket

Install

  1. With assistance, hand-tighten the bolts (x4) that attach the LH rear door assembly to the vehicle.
  2. Install the bolt that attaches the door check to the B-pillar.
    24 Nm (17.7 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T40 socket
  3. Connect the LH rear door harness connector from the LH body harness.
  4. Secure the tabs (x4) that attach the LH rear door harness seal to the B-pillar.
  5. Close the LH rear door and check for gap/flushness.
    Note
    If the shouldered / tapered bolts are not allowing for successful adjustment, replace the selected shouldered / tapered bolts with 1063260-00-C. Adjust the LH rear door, if necessary. Refer to CVIS for current specifications.
  6. Tighten the bolts (x4) that attach the LH rear door assembly to the vehicle.
    24 Nm (17.7 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  7. Raise the rear LH window and close LH rear door.
