Seatback - 2nd Row - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1305010032 FRT 0.24 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Equipment:
  • Torchless Heat Induction Tool - 2152873-00-A
Parent topic: 1305 - 2nd Row Seat Assemblies and Hardware

Remove

  1. Remove the LH 2nd row seat side bolster. See Bolster - Side - Seat - 2nd Row - LH (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the 2nd row lower seat cushion. See Seat Cushion - 2nd Row (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove and discard the bolt that attaches the center 2nd row seat belt fixed support and then remove the seat belt fixed support from the vehicle.
    Tip
    If the bolt is hard to remove, use an inductive heating tool to melt the adhesive patch material. Position the inductive heating tool coil around the bolt, and then heat the bolt for 20 seconds. Allow the bolt to cool down for 2 minutes, and then remove and discard the bolt. The inductive heater coil, bolt, and adjacent areas will be extremely hot.
    Warning
    Persons that have a pacemaker or any other kind of electronic or metal surgical implant must stay at least 1 m (3 ft) away from the inductive heater while it is in use.
    Warning
    Remove all metallic items (such as coins, keys, electronics, and tools) from pockets before using the inductive heater.
    Warning
    Ensure that there is a properly-maintained fire extinguisher in the work area before using the inductive heater.
    Warning
    Use the inductive heater in a well-ventilated area.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 15 mm socket
    • Torchless Heat Induction Tool - 2152873-00-A (for removal only)
  4. Disconnect the electrical harness from the heated seat connector for the LH 2nd row seat back assembly.
  5. Remove the nuts (x2) that attach the LH 2nd row seat back assembly hinge bracket to the body.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm socket
  6. Lift the left side of the LH seat back assembly up, move forward and to the left, and pull the inner pivot out of the RH seat back assembly.
  7. Remove the LH seat back assembly from the vehicle.
    Warning
    The video(s) included in this procedure are meant as an overview for supplemental purposes only. Follow all of the steps listed in the procedure to avoid damage to components and/or personal injury.

Install

  1. Move the LH seat back assembly into the 2nd row area.
  2. Insert the inner pivot of the LH seat back assembly into the receptacle of the RH seat back assembly, move the assembly rearward and to the right, and then lower the LH seat back assembly hinge bracket onto the body studs.
    Warning
    The video(s) included in this procedure are meant as an overview for supplemental purposes only. Follow all of the steps listed in the procedure to avoid damage to components and/or personal injury.
  3. Install the nuts (x2) that attach the LH seat back assembly hinge bracket to the body.
    18 Nm (13.3 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm socket
  4. Connect the electrical harness to the heated seat connector for the LH 2nd row seat back assembly.
  5. Install new bolt that attaches the center 2nd row seat belt fixed support to the vehicle.
    40 Nm (29.5 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Make sure the guide pin is aligned.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 15 mm socket
    • Torchless Heat Induction Tool - 2152873-00-A (for removal only)
  6. Install the 2nd row lower seat cushion. See Seat Cushion - 2nd Row (Remove and Replace).
  7. Install the LH 2nd row seat side bolster. See Bolster - Side - Seat - 2nd Row - LH (Remove and Replace).
