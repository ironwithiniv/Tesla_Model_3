Torque Specifications

Table 1. Torque Specifications: Sun Visor Assembly - LH (Remove and Replace)
Description Torque Value Recommended Tools Reuse/Replace Notes
Screw that attaches the sun visor to the vehicle
3.85 Nm (2.8 lbs-ft)
  • Torx T25 socket
 Reuse

Remove

  1. Open the LH front door and lower the LH front window.
  2. Release the clips (x4) that attach the sun visor bracket cover to the vehicle.
  3. Remove the screw that attaches the sun visor to the vehicle.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T25 bit
  4. Remove the LH sun visor from the left side from the headliner area.
    Warning
    The video(s) included in this procedure are meant as an overview for supplemental purposes only. Follow all of the steps listed in the procedure to avoid damage to components and/or personal injury.
  5. Disconnect the electrical connector from the sun visor.
  6. Remove the sun visor from the vehicle.

Install

  1. Connect the electrical connector to the sun visor.
    CAUTION
    Make sure the sun visor electrical harness is routed so that it isn't damaged during installation.
  2. Apply medium-strength Loctite 242 to the screw, then install the screw that attaches the sun visor to the vehicle.
    3.85 Nm (2.8 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T25 bit
  3. Install the clips (x4) that attach the sun visor bracket cover to the vehicle.
