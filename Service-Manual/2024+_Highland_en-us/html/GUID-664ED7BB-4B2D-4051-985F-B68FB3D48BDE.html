Windshield Washer Bottle and Pump - Connection Hose Assembly (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1750030102 FRT 0.24 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-04-22: Added steps to evacuate/refill the washer reservoir and connect/disconnect the front camera washer hose.
Parent topic: 1750 - Wipers and Washers

Remove

  1. Remove the underhood storage unit. See Underhood Storage Unit (Remove and Replace).
  2. Open the washer cap.
  3. Place the fluid evacuator in front of the vehicle.
    Note
    Connect the compressed air and open the valve.
  4. Evacuate washer fluid from the washer reservoir.
    Note
    Pump as much washer fluid as possible into the evacuator.
  5. Remove the fluid evacuator.
    Note
    Disconnect the compressed air and close the valve.
  6. Unlock the black tab, then disconnect the front camera washer hose if equipped.
  7. Disconnect the washer hose from the washer pump.
  8. Release the clip that attaches the washer hose to the washer reservoir.
  9. Release the clips (x4) that attach the washer hose to the body, then remove the hose.

Install

  1. Position the washer hose into place and secure the clips (x4) that attach the hose to the body.
  2. Secure the clip that attaches the washer hose to the washer reservoir.
  3. Connect the washer hose to the washer pump.
  4. Engage the black tab, then connect the front camera washer hose if equipped.
  5. Install the underhood storage unit. See Underhood Storage Unit (Remove and Replace).
  6. Fill the windshield washer reservoir with fluid.
  7. Close the washer reservoir cap.
  8. Verify operation of the windshield wipers and washer.
