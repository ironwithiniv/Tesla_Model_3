Module - Body Controller - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1715010042 FRT 0.54 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Torque Specifications

Table 1. Torque Specifications: Module - Body Controller - LH (Remove and Replace) / Module - Body Controller - RH (Remove and Replace)
Description Torque Value Recommended Tools Reuse/Replace Notes
Bolt that attaches the LH/RH body controller module to the vehicle
4.75 Nm (3.5 lbs-ft)
  • 10 mm 12-point deep socket
 Reuse

Remove

  1. Disconnect the LV power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
  2. Remove the LH lower A-pillar trim. See Trim - A-Pillar - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the LH footwell duct. See Duct - Footwell - LH (Remove and Replace).
  4. Disconnect the front door electrical connector J7 from the LH body controller.
  5. Disconnect the LH front seat harness electrical connector from the LH body controller.
  6. Disconnect the IP harness electrical connector J10 from the LH body controller.
  7. Disconnect the HVAC electrical connector from the LH body controller.
  8. Disconnect the LV power cable from the LH body controller.
  9. Disconnect the body 1 electrical connector J2 from the LH body controller.
  10. Disconnect the underhood storage unit electrical connector J6 from the LH body controller.
  11. Disconnect the body 3 electrical connector J5 from the LH body controller.
  12. Disconnect the body 2 electrical connector J4 from the LH body controller.
  13. Disconnect the front body controller harness electrical connector X909 from the LH body controller.
  14. Release the clips (x2) that attach the front body harness to the LH body controller.
  15. Release the clips (x2) that attach the LH body harness to the LH body controller and A-pillar, move it aside.
  16. Remove the bolt that attaches the LH body controller to the vehicle.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm 12-point deep socket
  17. Slide the LH body controller upward to release the W tab, maneuver the LH body controller out from under the instrument panel, then remove the LH body controller module from the vehicle.
    Warning
    The video(s) included in this procedure are meant as an overview for supplemental purposes only. Follow all of the steps listed in the procedure to avoid damage to components and/or personal injury.

Install

  1. Maneuver the LH body controller module into position under the IP carrier. Align the W-clip with the body cutout, and then slide down to attach the LH body controller module to the body.
    Warning
    The video(s) included in this procedure are meant as an overview for supplemental purposes only. Follow all of the steps listed in the procedure to avoid damage to components and/or personal injury.
  2. Install the bolt that attaches the LH body controller module onto the vehicle.
    4.75 Nm (3.5 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm 12-point deep socket
  3. Install the clips (x2) that attach the LH body harness to the LH body controller module and A-pillar.
  4. Install the clips (x2) that attach the front body harness to the LH body controller module.
  5. Connect the front body harness electrical connector X909 to the LH body controller module.
  6. Connect the body 2 electrical connector J4 to the LH body controller module.
  7. Connect the body 3 electrical connector J5 to the LH body controller module.
  8. Connect the underhood storage unit electrical connector J6 to the LH body controller module.
  9. Connect the body 1 electrical connector J2 to the LH body controller module.
    Note
    Ensure the electrical connector is fully seated, then engage the lock.
  10. Connect the LV power cable to the LH body controller module.
  11. Connect the HVAC electrical connector to the LH body controller module.
  12. Connect the IP harness electrical connector J10 to the LH body controller module.
    Note
    Ensure the electrical connector is fully seated, then engage the lock.
  13. Connect the LH front seat harness electrical connector to the LH body controller module, then engage the lock.
  14. Connect the LH front door electrical connector J7 to the LH body controller module.
    Note
    Ensure the electrical connector is fully seated, then engage the lock.
  15. Install the LH footwell duct. See Duct - Footwell - LH (Remove and Replace).
  16. Install the LH lower A-pillar trim. See Trim - A-Pillar - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  17. Connect the LV power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
  18. Connect the vehicle to a laptop with Toolbox.
  19. Reinstall the software. See Software Reinstall - Touchscreen.
  20. Close the LH front door, and sit in the front passenger seat.
    Note
    The routines listed in the following steps will adjust the LH front seat and window to calibrate their positions. Therefore, ensure that the LH front seat is clear of all items during the calibration process.
  21. Perform the following routine using Service Mode or Toolbox (see 0005 - Service Modes): PROC_CONTROLLER_LEFT_POST-REPLACEMENT-PROCEDUREvia Service Mode Plus:Left Controller ➜ Left Controller Post Replacement ➜ Left Controller Post Replacement Procedurevia Toolbox:(link)
    CAUTION
    The seat will move automatically during this routine. Prior to execution, make sure there is nothing (including objects and aftermarket accessories) behind or underneath the related seat to prevent component damage.
  22. Perform the following routine using Service Mode or Toolbox (see 0005 - Service Modes): PROC_VCSEC_X_POST-REPLACEMENT-PROCEDUREvia Service Mode:Low Voltage ➜ Keys ➜ VCSEC Post Replacementvia Service Mode Plus:low-voltage ➜ VCSEC Post Replacement ➜ VCSEC Post Replacement Procedurevia Toolbox:(link)
  23. Perform the following routine using Service Mode or Toolbox (see 0005 - Service Modes): PROC_VCSEC_C_PAIR-NFC-CARD-V2via Service Mode:Low Voltage ➜ Keys ➜ Pair Keyvia Toolbox:(link)
    Note
    Place a NFC card on the phone dock while the routine is running.
  24. Close the RH front door and open the LH front door.
  25. Disconnect the laptop from the vehicle.
  26. Disable Service Mode. See Service Mode.
  27. Move the LH front seat to original position.
  28. Raise all windows and close the LH front door.
