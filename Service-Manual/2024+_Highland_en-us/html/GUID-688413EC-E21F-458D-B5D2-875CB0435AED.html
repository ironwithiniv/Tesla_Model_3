Bushing - Front Drive Unit - RH (3DU) (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 3901010092 FRT 2.82 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 3901 - Front Drive Unit Assembly

Remove

  1. Place the vehicle on the 2-post lift.
    Note
    Ensure the vehicle is not charging.
  2. Remove the front subframe. See Subframe Assembly - Front (RWD) (Remove and Replace).
  3. Install the bushing removal cups and stud onto the RH bushing, and use cups K, B and I to drive the bushing out of the front drive unit.
    Note
    Always apply torque to the bearing side when removing or installing a bushing. Drive the bushing until the cup and bushing assembly is fully removed from the front drive unit.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Combination Wrench 12pt 22mm
    • Ratcheting Combination wrench Fixed head 22mm
    • Drive Unit Bushing Kit
  4. Remove cups and the old bushing from the threaded stud.

Install

  1. Install the bushing press cups with a new bushing into position.
  2. Use cups L, J and C to press the bushing into the front drive unit.
    Note
    When installing a new bushing, ensure the rubber tab is visible at the top of cup J. Always apply torque to the bearing side when removing or installing a bushing. Press the new bushing in until the flange on the bushing makes contact with the front drive unit case.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Combination Wrench 12pt 22mm
    • Ratcheting Combination wrench Fixed head 22mm
    • Drive Unit Bushing Kit
  3. Remove cups from the front drive unit, and inspect the alignment of the RH bushing.
  4. Install the front subframe. See Subframe Assembly - Front (RWD) (Remove and Replace).
