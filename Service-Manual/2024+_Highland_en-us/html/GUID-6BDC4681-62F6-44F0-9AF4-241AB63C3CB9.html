Bushing - Gearcase - Rear Drive Unit (4DU) (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 4001010072 FRT 3.90 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Equipment:
  • 1142559-20-A - M3 FDU Motor Stand Adapter
  • Drive Unit Bushing Kit
Parent topic: 4001 - Rear Drive Unit Assembly

Remove

  1. Raise the 4DU drive unit assembly out of the subframe. See Drive Unit - Rear (4DU) (Remove and Replace).
  2. Lower the rear drive unit onto an appropriate surface.
  3. Remove the 3 hooks of the drive unit sling tool from the drive unit.
  4. With assistance, hang-tighten the bolts (x3) and the nuts (x3) that attach the rear drive unit engine stand adapter to the rear drive unit.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 15 mm socket
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
    • Combination Wrench 12pt 15mm
  5. Attach the gantry hook to the rear drive unit adapter, and then use the gantry to lift the rear drive unit assembly onto the engine stand.
  6. Move the engine stand into position, and then install the bolts (x4) and the nuts (x4) that attach the rear drive unit adapter to the engine stand.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 18 mm socket
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
    • Combination Wrench 12pt 18mm
  7. Hand-tighten the nuts (x2) that attach the bushing removal tool to the gearcase bushing.
    Note
    Use cups "C", "A", and "G" to drive the bushing out. Always drive on the bearing side and hold the opposite.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Combination Wrench 12pt 22mm
  8. Press the gearcase bushing out from the casting.
  9. Separate the bushing removal tool and remove the gearcase bushing.

Install

  1. Hand-tighten the nuts (x2) to attach the bushing installation tool to the new gearcase bushing.
    Note
    Use cups "D" and "E" to install the bushing. Always drive on the bearing side and hold the opposite. Ensure the keyway is up and the lug on the bushing is in the center of the "D" cup opening.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Combination Wrench 12pt 22mm
  2. Spray lubricant to the bushing exterior.
  3. Press in the new gearcase bushing.
  4. Separate the bushing installation tool and remove the tool from the rear drive unit.
  5. Raise the gantry hook to apply tension and support the rear drive unit engine stand adapter.
  6. Remove the bolts (x4) and the nuts (x4) that attach the rear drive unit adapter to the engine stand, and then move the engine stand away.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 18 mm socket
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
    • Combination Wrench 12pt 18mm
  7. Lower the rear drive unit and the engine stand adapter onto crate.
  8. Remove the gantry hook from the rear drive unit adapter and move the hook aside.
  9. Remove the bolts (x3) and the nuts (x3) that attach the rear drive unit engine stand adapter to the rear drive unit.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 15 mm socket
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
    • Combination Wrench 12pt 15mm
  10. Install the 4DU drive unit assembly. See Drive Unit - Rear (4DU) (Remove and Replace).
    Note
    No need to reinstall the firmware.
