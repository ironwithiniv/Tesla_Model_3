Hinge - Door - Rear - Upper - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1133050062 FRT 0.36 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Bolts (x2) that attach the LH rear upper door hinge to body
27 Nm (19.9 lbs-ft)
  • 13 mm socket
 Reuse

Remove

  1. Open all four doors and lower all four windows.
  2. Apply masking tape to the area surrounding the LH rear door upper hinge on the body and door to aid in alignment during installation.
  3. Use a jack to support the LH rear door from the bottom.
    Note
    Make sure to use soft material to protect the painted surface.
  4. Remove the bolts (x2) that attach the LH rear door upper hinge to the door.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  5. Remove the bolts (x2) that attach the LH rear door upper hinge to the body.
    Note
    Support the door hinge to ensure it does not fall in the door jam.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm socket
  6. Carefully remove the LH rear door upper hinge from the vehicle.

Install

  1. Use the alignment tape to position the LH rear door upper hinge onto the body, and then hand tighten the bolts (x2) that attach the hinge to the body.
  2. Use the alignment tape to position the LH rear door upper hinge onto the door, and then hand tighten the bolts (x2) that attach the hinge to the door.
  3. Tighten the bolts (x2) that attach the LH rear door upper hinge to the body.
    27 Nm (19.9 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm socket
  4. Tighten the bolts (x2) that attach the LH rear door upper hinge to the door.
    27 Nm (19.9 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  5. Remove the jack that supports the LH rear door.
  6. Close the LH rear door and check for gap and flushness. Adjust as necessary.
    Note
    See Panel Alignment Specifications
  7. Remove the masking tape from the door and body.
  8. Clean the LH rear door side upper hinge bolts with IPA wipes.
    Note
    Allow 1 minute for the IPA to dry.
  9. Apply touch up paint on the LH rear door hinge bolts as needed.
    Note
    Shake the touch up paint before applying. Allow the first coat to dry for 20 minutes before applying and second coat.
  10. Raise all four windows and close all four doors.
