Carrier - Side Shield - Outboard - 1st Row (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1307010022 FRT 0.18 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 1307 - Front Seat Covers Pads and Trims

Remove

  1. Remove the driver seat outboard side shield assembly from the driver seat. See Side Shield - Outboard - LH (Remove and Replace).
    Note
    It is not necessary to remove the back rest and cushion adjust switch from the trim assembly after removing the trim from the seat.
  2. Use a flat head screwdriver to release the front clips (x2) that attach the driver seat outboard side shield carrier to the seat assembly.
    Warning
    The video(s) included in this procedure are meant as an overview for supplemental purposes only. Follow all of the steps listed in the procedure to avoid damage to components and/or personal injury.
  3. Partially lift the carrier and then pull it backwards to release the rear tabs (x2), and then rotate the carrier away to remove it from the seat frame.
    Note
    Don't use too much force to prevent damage to the carrier and trim.

Install

  1. Push the driver seat outboard side shield carrier forward to secure the rear tabs (x2) into the seat frame, and then rotate the carrier to install it to the seat assembly.
    Warning
    The video(s) included in this procedure are meant as an overview for supplemental purposes only. Follow all of the steps listed in the procedure to avoid damage to components and/or personal injury.
  2. Install the front clips (x2) that attach the driver seat outboard side shield carrier to the seat assembly.
  3. Install the driver seat outboard side shield assembly. See Side Shield - Outboard - LH (Remove and Replace).
