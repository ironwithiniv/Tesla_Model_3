Woofer - Parcel Shelf (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 2121010012 FRT 0.48 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Remove

  1. Open all doors and lower all windows.
  2. Move the LH and RH front seats forward.
  3. Release the clips (x4), locations (x2) and tether hooks (x2) that secure the LH side rail assembly, and then remove the assembly from the vehicle.
    Note
    Pull the upper corner of the side rail, then work your way to bottom. Pull the lower side rail toward RH to release the clips. Release the tethers from the bolted bungee tether.
  4. Use a small flat head screwdriver to remove and discard the tether hooks (x2) from the LH body.
  5. Remove the LH side bolster. See Bolster - Side - Seat - 2nd Row - LH (Remove and Replace).
  6. Release clips (x3) and tabs (x3) to release the LH C-pillar assembly, then set the trim aside.
    Note
    Pull the rear C-pillar trim inward then forward to remove.
  7. Repeat step 3 to 6 for the RH side.
  8. Remove the package tray harness clips (x2) around the C-pillar area.
  9. Disconnect the package tray harness connector from the RH body harness.
  10. Release tabs (x5), then remove the package tray.
    Note
    3 tabs are at the rear of the panel and 2 are on the sides. Slide towards the front of the vehicle to remove.
  11. Disconnect the electrical connector from the LH woofer.
  12. Remove the bolts (x4) that secure the LH woofer, and then remove the woofer from the vehicle.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • Ratchet/torque wrench

Install

  1. Install the bolts (x4) that secure the LH woofer.
    4 Nm (2.9 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Ensure that the two locating pins are inserted into the holes.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  2. Connect the electrical connector to the LH woofer.
  3. Secure the tabs (x5) to install the package tray.
    Note
    3 tabs are at the rear of panel and 2 are on the sides. Slide towards the rear of the vehicle to install.
  4. Connect the package tray harness connector to the RH body harness.
  5. Install the package tray harness clips (x2) around the C-pillar area.
  6. Secure the clips (x3) and tabs (x3) to install the LH C-pillar assembly.
    Note
    Slide the back tabs into the package tray, and then align the clips.
  7. Install the LH side bolster. See Bolster - Side - Seat - 2nd Row - LH (Remove and Replace).
  8. Install new tether hooks (x2) (1771862-00-A) to the LH side rail assembly.
  9. Install the clips (x4), locations (x2) and tether hooks (x2) that secure the LH side rail assembly, and then remove the assembly from the vehicle.
    Note
    Align the clips from bottom to top, then press the clips from top to bottom. Align the seal to sit on the top of the trim.
  10. Return the 2nd row 60 seatback to vertical position.
  11. Repeat step 6 to 10 for the RH side.
  12. Move the LH and RH front seats to original position.
  13. Raise all windows and close all doors.
