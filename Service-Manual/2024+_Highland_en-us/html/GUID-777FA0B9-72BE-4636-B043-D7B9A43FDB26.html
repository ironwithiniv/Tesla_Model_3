Connector - Pedestrian Warning Speaker (CN) (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 17100752 FRT 0.30 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 1710 - Harnesses

Remove

  1. Remove the RH front wheel. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
  2. Remove the clips (x9) that attach the front portion of the RH front wheel arch liners to the vehicle, and then move the wheel arch liner aside to perform the following steps.
  3. Disconnect the electrical connector from the pedestrian warning speaker.
    Note
    The connector access may vary depending on the version of the super horn audio speaker installed on the vehicle.
  4. Remove the clip that attaches the harness to the pedestrian warning speaker.
  5. Remove the connector locking cover, and then press down on the pins (x2) while pulling the harness downwards to remove the connector from the vehicle.

Install

  1. Insert the pins (x2) into the connector, and then install the connector locking cover.
    Note
    Perform a pull-push-pull test to verify the pins are fully seated.
  2. Install the clip that attaches the harness to the pedestrian warning speaker.
  3. Connect the electrical connector to the pedestrian warning speaker.
  4. Install the clips (x9) that attach the front portion of the RH front wheel arch liners to the vehicle.
  5. Install the RH front wheel. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
