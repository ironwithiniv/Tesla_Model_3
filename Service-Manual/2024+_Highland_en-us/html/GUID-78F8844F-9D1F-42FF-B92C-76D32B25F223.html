Establish an In-Vehicle Connection (OBD2 Port)

  1. Open the LH front door.
  2. Lower the LH front window.
  3. Connect the OBD2 diagnostics cable to the PCAN-USB adapter.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • OBD2 Diagnostics Cable - 2077826-00-A
    • PCAN-USB Adapter - 1016990-00-A
  4. Connect the OBD2 diagnostics cable to the vehicle's OBD2 port.
  5. Connect the PCAN-USB adapter to the laptop USB port.
  6. Open the Tesla EDR Retrieval Program. The program automatically attempts to connect to the RCM.
    Note
    When connected properly, a green circle and the message “Connected to RCM” displays on the bottom left corner of the window.
  7. Once connected to the EDR, click “Run EDR Retrieval” and follow the on-screen prompts to retrieve and save EDR data.
  8. The retrieved *.edr data file can be used to generate a Tesla EDR Report at https://edr.tesla.com.
  9. Close the Tesla EDR Retrieval Program.
  10. Disconnect the PCAN-USB adapter from the laptop USB port.
  11. Disconnect the OBD2 diagnostics cable from the vehicle's OBD2 port.
  12. Disconnect the OBD2 diagnostics cable from the PCAN-USB adapter.
  13. Raise the LH front window.
  14. Close the LH front door.
