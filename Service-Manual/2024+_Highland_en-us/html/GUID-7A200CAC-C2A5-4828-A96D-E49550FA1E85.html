Tire Rotation

Correction code 34010105 0.29 (Mobile Service Technicians: use the mobile tire rotation procedure) NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-08-19: Added step to record the tread depth of each tire.
  • 2026-08-12: Clarified that, if only installing 2 new tires, new tires must only be installed on rear of vehicle if all 4 wheels are the same size. Corrected terminology regarding staggered tire sizes.
  • 2025-11-06: Added steps for indirect TPMS calibration.
  • 2024-04-29: Added instructions on how to reset tire rotation through the vehicle touchscreen.

Vehicle ride quality and handling are maximized when all 4 tires are the same age, model, and tread depth. Rotating tires regularly allows them to wear at the same rate and increases the service life. Tesla recommends tire rotation every 6,250 miles (10,000 km) or if tread depth difference is 2/32 in (1.5mm) or greater, whichever comes first.

Note
Tesla recommends replacing tires in pairs unless the other tires are within 2/32 in (1.5 mm) of tread depth of the new tire.
Note
If only 2 new tires are installed and all 4 wheels are the same size, the new tires must be installed on the rear of the vehicle. In wet conditions, the tires that have less tread have less traction than the new tires. Reduced traction at the front of the vehicle leads to understeer, which most drivers are better able to react to than the oversteer that would result if the worn tires were on the rear of the vehicle.
Note
Tesla recommends replacing a tire if any of the tread depths are 4/32 in. (3 mm) or less.
Parent topic: 3402 - Tires

Procedure

  1. Record the tread depth of each tire.
    Note
    Measure and record the outer, center, and inner tread depths. Do not set the tire tread gauge probe on the molded tread wear indicators. Recommend replacement if tread depth is 4/32 in (3 mm) or less.
  2. Remove all 4 wheels. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
  3. Check if the tires are directional or non-directional.
    Note
    Directional tires are identified with an arrow indicating the direction of rotation on the sidewall.
  4. Check if the vehicle has different size tires in the front and rear.
    Note
    Performance vehicles have larger tires in the rear.
  5. Reinstall the wheels according to the type of tires.
  6. Install all 4 wheels. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
  7. If the vehicle is in Service Mode, exit Service Mode.
  8. Tap Controls > Service > Wheel & Tire > Tires, choose the tire season and click Reset. After resetting, check if the Tire Service Mileage in the upper right corner is displayed as Less than 500 km ago.
  9. If the vehicle is equipped with indirect TPMS, perform the following calibration steps:
    1. Manually check all 4 tire pressures, and if necessary, inflate all tires to specification.
      Note
      Refer to the "Tire and Loading Information" label located on the driver side B-pillar.
      Note
      Inspect wheels and tires for any bulges or damage while checking/setting pressures.
    2. On the vehicle touchscreen, navigate to Service > Calibrate Tire Pressure, and then tap Calibrate to calibrate tire pressures.
      Note
      Vehicles equipped with BLE TPMS will not have this calibration routine.
