Thermistor - HVAC - RH (Remove and Replace)

Remove

  1. Open the front doors and lower the front windows.
  2. Move the front seats forward.
  3. Remove the center console end panel. See Panel - Rear End - Center Console (Remove and Replace).
  4. Move the front seats backward.
  5. Remove the rear underhood apron. See Underhood Apron - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the cabin intake duct. See Assembly - Fresh Air Gutter - HVAC (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the LH and RH wiper arms and blades. See Wiper Arm - Driver Side (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the LH and RH shock tower covers. See Cover - Shock Tower - LH (Remove and Install).
  9. Remove the cowl screen panel. See Panel - Cowl Screen (Remove and Replace).
  10. Remove bolts (x2) that attach the steering column to the body.
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 13 mm socket
    • 3 in extension
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
  11. Disconnect the LV power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
  12. Remove the LH and RH upper A-pillar trims. See Trim - A-Pillar - Upper - LH (Remove and Replace).
  13. Remove the LH and RH lower A-pillar trims. See Trim - A-Pillar - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  14. Remove the LH and RH footwell ducts. See Duct - Footwell - LH (Remove and Replace) and Duct - Footwell - RH (Remove and Replace).
  15. Remove the LH and RH center console side panels. See Carpet - Side - Center Console - LH (Remove and Replace) and Carpet - Side - Center Console - RH (Remove and Replace).
  16. Remove the center console assembly from the vehicle. See Center Console (Remove and Replace).
  17. Remove the lower instrument panel. See Assembly - Instrument Panel - Lower (Remove and Replace).
  18. Remove the steering wheel. See Steering Wheel (Remove and Replace).
  19. Remove the steering column control module. See Module - Steering Column Control (Remove and Replace).
  20. Remove the touchscreen. See Touchscreen - Front (Remove and Replace).
  21. Remove the instrument panel speaker grille. See Speaker Grille - Instrument Panel (Remove and Replace).
  22. Remove the LH and RH dash speakers. See Speaker - Dash - LH (Remove and Replace).
  23. Remove the center dash speaker. See Speaker - Dash - Center (Remove and Replace).
  24. Remove the instrument panel upper assembly from the vehicle. See IP Carrier (Remove and Install).
  25. Remove the LH air wave duct. See Duct - Air Wave - LH (Remove and Replace).
  26. Disconnect the IP electrical harness connector from the steering column.
  27. Release the clips (x2) that attach the headliner electrical harness to the LH lower A-pillar trim.
  28. Disconnect the HVAC connector from the LH body controller.
  29. Disconnect the instrument panel harness X035 connector from the LH body controller.
    Note
    Release the lock and then remove the connector.
  30. Release the blower motor harness clips (x2) from the cross car beam.
  31. Disconnect the blower motor connector.
  32. Release the instrument panel harness clips (x4) from the body.
  33. Release the locking tab and then pivot the lever to disconnect the instrument panel harness connector from RCM.
  34. Disconnect the J10 instrument panel electrical connector from the RH body controller module.
  35. Disconnect the J11 HVAC electrical connector from the RH body controller module.
  36. Disconnect three connectors on the car computer.
  37. Release the HVAC harness from the instrument panel harness slots.
  38. Release the clips (x2) that attach the headliner electrical harness to the RH lower A-pillar trim.
  39. Remove the clips (x2) that attach the LH front main carpet to the footwell area.
  40. Remove the clip that attaches the RH front main carpet to the footwell area.
  41. Remove the nuts (x4) that attach the steering column to the cross car beam.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 13 mm socket
    • 3 in extension
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
  42. Lower the steering column and set it aside.
  43. Remove the LH and RH cross beam bar brackets. See Riser Bracket - Instrument Panel - RH (Remove and Replace).
  44. Remove the bolts (x2) that attach the LH corners of the cross car beam to the body.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 15 mm socket
    • 3 in extension
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  45. Remove the bolts (x2) that attach the RH corners of the cross car beam to the body.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 15 mm socket
    • 3 in extension
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  46. Remove the bolt that attaches the cross car beam to the HVAC assembly.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  47. Loosen the bolt that attaches the cross car beam to the upper side of the cowl.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13mm 12-point combination wrench
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
    • 13 mm socket
  48. Remove the bolts (x4) that attach the cross car beam to the HVAC assembly.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 10 mm socket
    • 3 in extension
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  49. Remove the cross car beam and wiring harness assembly from the vehicle.
    Note
    Recommend assistance.
    CAUTION
    Be careful not to damage the interior.
  50. Disconnect the RH lower duct thermistor connector.
  51. Release the tabs (x2) and remove the RH lower duct thermistor from the HVAC assembly.

Install

  1. Install the RH lower duct thermistor to the HVAC assembly.
  2. Connect the RH lower duct thermistor connector.
  3. Position the cross car beam and wiring harness assembly back to the vehicle.
    Note
    Recommend assistance.
    CAUTION
    Be careful not to damage the interior.
  4. Install the bolts (x2) that attach the LH corners of the cross car beam to the body.
    29 Nm (21.4 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 15 mm socket
    • 3 in extension
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  5. Install the bolts (x2) that attach the RH corners of the cross car beam to the body.
    29 Nm (21.4 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 15 mm socket
    • 3 in extension
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  6. Install the bolts (x2) that attach the steering column to the body.
    25 Nm (18.4 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm socket
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
  7. Install the bolt that attaches the cross car beam to the upper side of the cowl.
    25 Nm (18.4 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13mm 12-point combination wrench
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
    • 13 mm socket
  8. Install the bolt that attaches the cross car beam to the HVAC assembly.
    5.5 Nm (4.1 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  9. Install the bolts (x4) that attach the cross car beam to the HVAC assembly.
    5.5 Nm (4.1 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 10 mm socket
    • 3 in extension
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  10. Install the LH and RH cross beam bar brackets. See Riser Bracket - Instrument Panel - RH (Remove and Replace).
  11. Position the steering column for installation.
  12. Install the nuts (x4) that attach the steering column to the cross car beam.
    15 Nm (11.1 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 13 mm socket
    • 3 in extension
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
  13. Fasten the clips (x2) that attach the LH front main carpet to the footwell area.
  14. Fasten the clip that attaches the RH front main carpet to the footwell area.
  15. Fasten the clips (x2) that attach the headliner electrical harness to the LH lower A-pillar trim.
  16. Connect the HVAC connector to the LH body controller.
  17. Connect the instrument panel harness X035 connector to the LH body controller.
  18. Fasten the blower motor harness clips (x2) from the cross car beam.
  19. Connect the blower motor connector.
  20. Connect the IP electrical harness connector to the steering column.
  21. Fasten the instrument panel harness clips (x4) from the body.
  22. Connect the instrument panel harness connector to RCM.
  23. Fasten the clips (x2) that attach the headliner electrical harness to the RH lower A-pillar trim.
  24. Connect three connectors on the car computer.
  25. Secure the HVAC harness to the instrument panel harness slots.
  26. Connect the J11 HVAC electrical connector to the RH body controller module.
  27. Connect the J10 instrument panel electrical connector to the RH body controller module.
  28. Install the LH air wave duct. See Duct - Air Wave - LH (Remove and Replace).
  29. Install the instrument panel upper assembly to the vehicle. See IP Carrier (Remove and Install).
  30. Install the center dash speaker. See Speaker - Dash - Center (Remove and Replace).
  31. Install the LH and RH dash speakers. See Speaker - Dash - LH (Remove and Replace).
  32. Install the instrument panel speaker grille. See Speaker Grille - Instrument Panel (Remove and Replace).
  33. Install the touchscreen. See Touchscreen - Front (Remove and Replace).
  34. Install the steering column control module. See Module - Steering Column Control (Remove and Replace).
  35. Install the steering wheel. See Steering Wheel (Remove and Replace).
  36. Install the lower instrument panel. See Assembly - Instrument Panel - Lower (Remove and Replace).
  37. Install the center console assembly from the vehicle. See Center Console (Remove and Replace).
  38. Install the LH and RH center console side panels. See Carpet - Side - Center Console - LH (Remove and Replace) and Carpet - Side - Center Console - RH (Remove and Replace).
  39. Install the LH and RH footwell ducts. See Duct - Footwell - LH (Remove and Replace) and Duct - Footwell - RH (Remove and Replace).
  40. Install the LH and RH lower A-pillar trims. See Trim - A-Pillar - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  41. Install the LH and RH upper A-pillar trims. See Trim - A-Pillar - Upper - LH (Remove and Replace).
  42. Install the cowl screen panel. See Panel - Cowl Screen (Remove and Replace).
  43. Install the LH and RH shock tower covers. See Cover - Shock Tower - LH (Remove and Install).
  44. Install the LH and RH wiper arms and blades. See Wiper Arm - Driver Side (Remove and Replace).
  45. Connect the LV power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
  46. Install the cabin intake duct. See Assembly - Fresh Air Gutter - HVAC (Remove and Replace).
  47. Install the rear underhood apron. See Underhood Apron - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  48. Install the center console end panel. See Panel - Rear End - Center Console (Remove and Replace).
  49. Raise all the windows and close all the doors.
