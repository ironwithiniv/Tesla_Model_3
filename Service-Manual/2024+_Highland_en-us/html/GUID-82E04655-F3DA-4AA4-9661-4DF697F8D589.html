Blower - Cushion - Front Seat - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1301010312 FRT 0.06 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-04-24: Removed the steps to release/secure the driver seat.
Warning
Seat base edges are sharp and can cause personal injury. Wear cut resistant gloves when removing the seat from the vehicle, and while performing procedures that involve work on or near the seat base.
Parent topic: 1301 - Front Seat Tracks and Motors

Remove

  1. Open the LH front door and lower the LH front window.
  2. Move the driver seat rearward and adjust the seat cushion to the highest position.
  3. Disconnect the electrical connector from the driver seat cushion ventilation fan.
  4. Release the clips (x2) and guide tab that attach the driver seat cushion ventilation fan to the driver seat frame, and then remove the fan from the vehicle.

Install

  1. Fasten the clips (x2) and guide tab that attach the driver seat cushion ventilation fan to the driver seat frame.
  2. Connect the electrical connector to the driver seat cushion ventilation fan.
  3. Move the driver seat back to its original position.
  4. Raise the LH front window and close the LH front door.
