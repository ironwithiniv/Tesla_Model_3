Actuator - Lower Mode - HVAC (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1810050102 FRT 2.58 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-07-07: Released new procedure.
Parent topic: 1810 - Cabin HVAC

Remove

  1. Remove the HVAC lower mode actuator. See Actuator - Lower Mode - HVAC (Remove and Install).

Replace

  1. Remove the HVAC lower mode actuator. See Actuator - Lower Mode - HVAC (Remove and Install).
  2. Connect a laptop with Toolbox 3 to the vehicle. See Toolbox (Connect and Disconnect).
  3. In Toolbox, click the Actions tab, type "Actuator" into the search field, click TEST-SELF_VCRIGHT_X_HVAC-CASE-ACTUATORSvia Toolbox:(link), click Run, and allow the routine to complete.
  4. Disconnect the laptop from the vehicle and exit Service Mode Plus.
