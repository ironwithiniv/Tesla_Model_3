Controller - High Voltage (Backup and Restore)

Parent topic: 1630 - HV Battery Electrical Components

HVC Backup

  1. Open the LH front door and lower the LH front window.
  2. Connect a laptop with Toolbox to the vehicle. See Toolbox (Connect and Disconnect).
    Note
    Connecting the vehicle to Toolbox will also automatically activate Service Mode Plus.
  3. On the vehicle touchscreen, tap High Voltage > Procedures > High Voltage Controller (HVC) Replacement , and select Backup Old HVC, click Run and allow the routine to complete.
  4. Disconnect the laptop with Toolbox from the vehicle. See Toolbox (Connect and Disconnect).
  5. Return to the procedure that requested a backup of the high voltage controller data.

HVC Restore

  1. Connect the laptop with Toolbox to the vehicle. See Toolbox (Connect and Disconnect).
    Note
    Connecting the vehicle to Toolbox will also automatically activate Service Mode Plus.
  2. On the vehicle touchscreen, tap High Voltage > Procedures > High Voltage Controller (HVC) Replacement , and select Restore New HVC, click Run and allow the routine to complete.
  3. Exit Service Mode. See Service Mode.
  4. Disconnect the laptop with Toolbox from the vehicle. See Toolbox (Connect and Disconnect).
  5. Raise the LH front window and close the LH front door.
  6. Return to the procedure that requested a restore of the high voltage controller data.
