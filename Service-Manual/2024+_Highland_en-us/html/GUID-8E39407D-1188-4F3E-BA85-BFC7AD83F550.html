Service Panel - Instrument Panel (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1405010612 FRT 0.06 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 2105 - Touchscreen

Torque Specifications

Table 1. Torque Specifications: Service Panel - Instrument Panel (Remove and Replace)
Description Torque Value Recommended Tools Reuse/Replace Notes
2 Nm (1.5 lbs-ft)
  • Torx T20 socket
Reuse

Remove

  1. Open the RH front door, and lower the RH front window.
  2. Insert a plastic trim tool into the top center slot of the service panel, and then release the clips (x2) and tabs (x4) that attach the service panel to the instrument panel.
    CAUTION
    An electrical harness is still connected to the service panel. Do not pull the service panel too far from the instrument panel.
  3. Disconnect the electrical harness from the cabin temperature sensor.
  4. Remove the service panel from the touchscreen.
  5. Remove the bolts (x2) that attach the cabin temperature sensor to the service panel, and then remove the service panel from the vehicle.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T20 socket

Install

  1. Position the cabin temperature sensor to the instrument panel service panel, and then install the bolts (x2) that attach the sensor to the service panel.
    2 Nm (1.5 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T20 socket
  2. Connect the temperature sensor connector, and then secure the clips (x2) and tabs (x4) that attach the service panel to the vehicle.
  3. Raise the RH front window and close the RH front door.
