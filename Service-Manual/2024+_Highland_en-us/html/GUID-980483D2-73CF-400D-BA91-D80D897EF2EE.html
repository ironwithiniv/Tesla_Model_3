Seal - Inverter - Gearbox (3DU) (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 3920010022 FRT 3.72 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Equipment:
  • 1056154-00-A Anti Static Mat /w Wrist Grounding Cord and Wrist Strap
Parent topic: 3920 - Front Drive Inverter

Remove

  1. Remove the front drive unit inverter. See Inverter - Front Drive Unit (3DU) (Remove and Replace).
  2. Carefully place the inverter on an ESD safe mat (1056154-00-A), and then remove the front drive unit inverter gasket from the inverter.
    CAUTION
    Do not place the inverter facing downwards on the circuit board as it is fragile.

Install

  1. Use IPA wipes to clean the mating surfaces of the phase lugs and the inverter, and wait for 1 minute to allow the surfaces to dry.
    Note
    Make sure no debris is left on the mating surfaces.
  2. Install a new front drive unit inverter gasket onto the inverter.
    Note
    Make sure that the 3-phase cables and the guides are aligned during the installation.
  3. Install the front drive unit inverter. See Inverter - Front Drive Unit (3DU) (Remove and Replace).
