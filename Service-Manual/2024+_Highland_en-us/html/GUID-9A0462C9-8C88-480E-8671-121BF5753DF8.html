Stabilizer Bar - Rear (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 3103020032 FRT 0.36 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 3103 - Rear Suspension (Including Hubs)

55 Nm (40.6 lbs-ft)
  • 15 mm socket
  • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench

Bolts (x4) that attach the rear stabilizer bar to the rear subframe

 Replace 1111154-00-B

Remove

  1. Raise and support the vehicle. See Raise Vehicle - 2 Post Lift.
  2. Remove the rear aero shield panel. See Panel - Aero Shield - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove and discard the nuts (x2) that attach the stabilizer bar to the end links.
    Note
    Loosen the nut first, and then continue to remove the nut by counter-holding the ball joint with a hex wrench.
    Note
    Might need to move the rear stabilizer bar up/down to get the correct angle in order to remove the end links.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 15 mm socket
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
  4. Remove and discard the bolts (x4) that attach the rear stabilizer bar to the rear subframe.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 3 in extension
    • 11 mm deep socket
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
  5. With assistance, remove the rear stabilizer bar from the vehicle.

Install

  1. Place the rear stabilizer bar into position for installation onto the vehicle, and then raise the support stand to support the rear stabilizer bar.
  2. Install new bolts (x4) that attach the rear stabilizer bar to the rear subframe.
    30 Nm (22.1 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 3 in extension
    • 11 mm deep socket
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
  3. Align the LH end link with the rear stabilizer bar.
    Note
    Move the rear stabilizer bar up/down to get the correct angle to install the rear link.
  4. Install new nut that attaches the stabilizer bar to the LH end links. Mark the nut with a paint pen.
    Note
    Counter-hold the ball joint with a hex wrench.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 15 mm socket
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
  5. Repeat 3 and 4 for the right side of the vehicle.
  6. Install the rear aero shield panel. See Panel - Aero Shield - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the vehicle from the lift.
