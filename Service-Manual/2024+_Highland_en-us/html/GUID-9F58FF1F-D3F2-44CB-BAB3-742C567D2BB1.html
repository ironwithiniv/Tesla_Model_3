Carpet - Side - Center Console - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1519010052 FRT 0.06 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 1519 - Center Console

Remove

  1. Open the LH front door and lower the LH front window.
  2. Move the front LH front seat fully rearward.
  3. Remove the front LH floor mat from the vehicle.
  4. Release the clips (x11), locator clips (x2) and tab that attach the center console side panel to the center console, and then remove the side panel from the vehicle.
    Warning
    The video(s) included in this procedure are meant as an overview for supplemental purposes only. Follow all of the steps listed in the procedure to avoid damage to components and/or personal injury.
    Note
    Use a plastic trim tool to release the rearmost clips first and work toward the front of the vehicle. If the clips are too difficult to release, use a clip prytool.
    Note
    Replace the clips if they are damaged.

Install

  1. Install the clips (x11), locator clips (x2) and tab that attach the center console side panel to the center console, starting from the front of the side panel and work rearward.
    Note
    Align the tab in the front of the LH side panel carpet before attaching the clips.
    Note
    Replace the clips if they are damaged.
  2. Install the front LH floormat to the vehicle.
  3. Move the front LH front seat to its original position.
  4. Raise the LH front window and close the LH front door.
