Harness - Front Subframe (Dual Motor) (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1710050012 FRT 0.36 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Equipment:
  • 1060071-00-A Torque Wrench ¼ in Dr Hexagon
Parent topic: 1710 - Harnesses

Remove

  1. Place the vehicle on the 2-post lift.
    Note
    Ensure the vehicle is not charging.
  2. Open the LH front door and lower the LH front window.
  3. Remove the front aero shield panel. See Panel - Aero Shield - Front (Remove and Replace).
  4. Disconnect the LV battery power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
  5. Release the locking tab and push the handle downward to disconnect the front drive unit inverter logic connector.
  6. Release the front subframe harness clips (x2) from the inverter housing.
  7. Remove the front subframe harness clips (x3) from the left side of the front subframe.
  8. Release the grey locking tab, and then press on the tab to release the front subframe harness connector.
  9. Remove the front subframe harness connector clip from the front subframe.
  10. Remove the front subframe harness clips from the front and the right side of the front subframe.
  11. Disconnect the oil pump connector from the oil pump.
  12. Release the front subframe harness clip from by the resolver housing.
  13. Release the front drive unit resolver connector, and then remove the front subframe harness from the front subframe.

Install

  1. Place the front subframe harness to the front subframe, and then secure the front subframe harness clips (x2) to the inverter housing.
  2. Fully seat the connector, and then secure the locking tab to connect the front drive unit inverter logic connector.
  3. Install the front subframe harness clips (x3) to the left side of the front subframe.
  4. Install the front subframe main harness connector clip to the front subframe.
  5. Engage the grey locking tab, and then press on the tab to connect the front subframe harness connector.
  6. Install the front subframe harness clips to the front and the right side of the front subframe.
  7. Connect the oil pump connector to the oil pump.
  8. Secure the front subframe harness clip to by the resolver housing.
  9. Secure the front drive unit resolver connector.
  10. Install the front aero shield panel. See Panel - Aero Shield - Front (Remove and Replace).
  11. Connect the LV battery power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
  12. Raise the LH front window and close the LH front door.
  13. Remove the lift arms from below the vehicle.
