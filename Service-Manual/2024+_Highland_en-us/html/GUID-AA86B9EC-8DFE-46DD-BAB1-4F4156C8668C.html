Brake Rotor - Front - LH (Measure Runout) (Test/Adjust)

Correction code 33010100 FRT 0.35 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Equipment:
  • 1090881-00-A Brake Pedal Depressor
  • 1044206-00-A Wheel Trim Removal Tool
  • 1064697-00-B Brake Rotor Run Out Kit
  • 1705887-00-A 1/2" Dr Electric Impact
  • 1082458-00-A Breaker Bar 1/2in Dr. x Long

Procedure

  1. Open the LH front door and lower the LH front window.
  2. Apply the brake pedal depressor.
    Note
    Verify the brake pedal is fully pressed to avoid wheels from spinning.
  3. Raise and support the vehicle. See Raise Vehicle - 2 Post Lift.
  4. Remove the LH front wheel. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
  5. Remove the bolt that attaches the LH front rotor to the knuckle.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  6. Install the brake rotor run out clamping plate onto the LH front rotor.
    100 Nm (73.7 lbs-ft)
    Note
    This simulates the wheel being installed, thus preventing inaccurate measurements.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Brake Rotor Run Out Kit - 1064697-00-B
    • 21 mm deep socket
    • Breaker bar
  7. Lower the vehicle to suitable height, and then remove the brake pedal depressor.
  8. Raise the vehicle partially and lower the lift onto locks.
    Note
    Make sure that there is an audible click of locks on both sides before lowering, otherwise the vehicle may tilt to one side.
  9. Install the brake rotor run out flex bar onto the LH front tie rod nut and setup for reading.
  10. Measure 10 mm inward from the LH front rotor end and align digital indicator, and then press the indicator rod and zero measurement.
    Note
    Make sure that the indicator rod is pressed to allow travel in both directions and does not bottom out.
  11. Mark the top center of the LH front rotor to identify the starting point.
  12. Slowly rotate the LH front rotor one full revolution.
    Note
    • Record the highest positive and lowest negative values as shown on the digital indicator as you complete the rotation.
    • The total runout is the difference between the highest positive and lowest negative values and must not exceed 0.050 mm (for base brake rotors) or 0.150 mm (for performance brake rotors). If the total runout exceeds 0.050 mm (for base brake rotors) or 0.150 mm (for performance brake rotors), the brake rotor must be replaced due to excessive runout.
  13. Measure 25 mm inward from the LH front rotor end and align digital indicator, and press the indicator rod and zero measurement.
    Note
    Make sure that the indicator rod is pressed to allow travel in both directions and does not bottom out.
  14. Slowly rotate the LH front rotor one full revolution.
    Note
    • Record the highest positive and lowest negative values as shown on the digital indicator as you complete the rotation.
    • The total runout is the difference between the highest positive and lowest negative values and must not exceed 0.050 mm (for base brake rotors) or 0.150 mm (for performance brake rotors). If the total runout exceeds 0.050 mm (for base brake rotors) or 0.150 mm (for performance brake rotors), the brake rotor must be replaced due to excessive runout.
  15. Measure 40 mm inward from the LH front rotor end and align digital indicator, and press the indicator rod and zero measurement.
    Note
    Make sure that the indicator rod is pressed to allow travel in both directions and does not bottom out.
  16. Slowly rotate the LH front rotor one full revolution.
    Note
    • Record the highest positive and lowest negative values as shown on the digital indicator as you complete the rotation.
    • The total runout is the difference between the highest positive and lowest negative values and must not exceed 0.050 mm (for base brake rotors) or 0.150 mm (for performance brake rotors). If the total runout exceeds 0.050 mm (for base brake rotors) or 0.150 mm (for performance brake rotors), the brake rotor must be replaced due to excessive runout.
  17. Remove the brake rotor run out flex bar onto the LH front tie rod nut.
  18. Apply the brake pedal depressor.
    Note
    Verify the brake pedal is fully pressed to avoid wheels from spinning.
  19. Raise the vehicle partially and lower the lift onto locks.
    Note
    Make sure that there is an audible click of locks on both sides before lowering, otherwise the vehicle may tilt to one side.
  20. Remove the brake rotor run out clamping plate from the LH front rotor.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Brake Rotor Run Out Kit - 1064697-00-B
    • 21 mm deep socket
    • Breaker bar
  21. Install the bolt that attaches the LH front rotor to the knuckle.
    5 Nm (3.7 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  22. Install the LH front wheel. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
  23. Remove the brake pedal depressor.
  24. Raise the LH front window and close the LH front door.
