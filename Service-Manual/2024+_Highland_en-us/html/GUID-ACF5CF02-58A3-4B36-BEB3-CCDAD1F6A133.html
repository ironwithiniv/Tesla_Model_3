Exterior Trim Fittings - Mud Flaps - Pair

Correction code 1220020072 0.06 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2024-02-04: Updated the torque value and the recommended tools for the bolts that attach the mud flaps and the rocker covers to the body.
Table 1. Torque Specifications: Exterior Trim Fittings - Mud Flaps - Pair
Description Torque Value Recommended Tools Reuse/Replace Notes
Bolt that attaches the LH mud flap and the rocker cover to the body
3 Nm (2.2 lbs-ft)
  • 10 mm deep socket
 Reuse
Parent topic: 1220 - Exterior Trim

Procedure

  1. Open the LH front door.
  2. Turn the steering wheel fully to the left to provide access to the working area.
  3. Remove the bolt that attaches the LH mud flap and rocker cover to the body.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm deep socket
  4. Remove the clips (x2) that attach the LH mud flap to front LH wheel arch liner and the rocker cover, and then remove the mud flap from the vehicle.
  5. Position the LH mud flap onto the wheel liner and the rocker cover, then secure the clips (x2).
  6. Install the bolt that attaches the LH mud flap and rocker cover to the body.
    3 Nm (2.2 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm deep socket
  7. Repeat step 2 to 6 for the RH side.
  8. Center the steering wheel.
  9. Close the LH front door.
