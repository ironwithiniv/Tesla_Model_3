Carpet - Side - Center Console - RH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1519010062 FRT 0.06 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 1519 - Center Console

Remove

  1. Open the RH front door and lower the RH front window.
  2. Move the front RH front seat fully rearward.
  3. Remove the front RH floor mat from the vehicle.
  4. Release the clips (x12), locator clips (x2) and tab that attach the center console side panel to the center console, and then remove the side panel from the vehicle.
    Note
    Use a plastic trim tool to release the rearmost clips first and work toward the front of the vehicle. If the clips are too difficult to release, use a clip prytool.
    Note
    Replace the clips if they are damaged.

Install

  1. Install the clips (x12), locator clips (x2) and tab that attach the center console side panel to the center console, starting from the front of the side panel and work rearward.
    Note
    Align the tab in the front of the RH side panel carpet before attaching the clips.
    Note
    Replace the clips if they are damaged.
  2. Install the front RH floor mat to the vehicle.
  3. Move the front RH front seat to its original position.
  4. Raise the RH front window and close the RH front door.
