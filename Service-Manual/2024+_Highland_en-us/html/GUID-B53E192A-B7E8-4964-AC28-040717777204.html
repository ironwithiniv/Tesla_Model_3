Glass - Door - Front - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1020010052 FRT 0.30 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2024-12-10: Added new caution about Pre-Installation Glass Inspection service requirements.
Warning
The side impact airbag deployment depends on the front door sensor's ability to detect a side impact from air pressure. Make sure that there are no uncovered holes in the front door shells. Additionally, always reinstall plugs or tape when servicing or inspecting the front doors and their components.
CAUTION
New glass components (windshield, roof, door, and fixed glass) must be inspected before installation to meet Tesla’s internal standards of quality. Perform the inspection described in Pre-Installation Glass Inspection before beginning this procedure.
Parent topic: 1020 - Windshield and Body Glass

Description Torque Value Recommended Tools Reuse/Replace Notes
Patched bolts that attach the door glass to the regulator isolator clamps
9.5 Nm (7.0 lbs-ft)

 Replace 1101805-00-B
Equipment:
  • 1454796-00-A TOOLKIT, WINDOW SWITCH

Remove

  1. Remove the LH front door reflector. See Reflector - Door - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the LH front door tweeter. See Tweeter - Front Door - LH (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the LH front door puddle light. See Puddle Lamp - Front Door (LH) (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the LH front door trim panel. See Panel - Door Trim - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the LH front door outer belt seal. See Seal - Outer Belt - Front Door - LH (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the plugs that cover the openings in door for access to the regulator isolator clamps.
  7. Connect the window switch tool to the LH front door electrical harness, and then position the door glass so that the regulator isolator clamp bolts are accessible from the holes.
  8. Remove and discard the bolts (x2) that attach the door glass to the regulator isolator clamps, and then remove the door glass from the door.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm socket
    Warning
    The video(s) included in this procedure are meant as an overview for supplemental purposes only. Follow all of the steps listed in the procedure to avoid damage to components and/or personal injury.
    Note
    Replace the regulator isolation clamps, if damaged.
  9. Remove the LH front door glass.

Install

  1. Install the LH front door glass.
  2. Inspect the regulator isolator clamps for glass splinters or other debris, and clean them if necessary.
    CAUTION
    Debris and splinters may damage or shatter the new door glass.
  3. Position the front LH door glass onto the front LH door, and then hand tighten the bolts (x2) that secure the glass to the regulator isolation clamps.
    Note
    Make sure the clamps are not bottomed out on the regulator.
  4. Align the center of the glass with the guide block opening.
  5. Install the LH front door outer belt seal. See Seal - Outer Belt - Front Door - LH (Remove and Replace).
  6. Lower the front LH window fully and close the LH front door.
  7. Raise the LH front window fully by accessing the window switch tool through the LH rear door.
  8. Use the gap and flush tool to inspect the front LH window for proper gap and flush.
    • Gap: 8 mm +/- 1.7mm
    • Flush: 0 mm +/- 1.7mm
  9. Open the LH front door.
  10. If the glass gap and flush is out of specifications: Loosen the bolts that attach the glass to the regulator isolator clamps approximately 1/2 turn to maintain position of the glass but still allow adjustment of the glass, and then adjust the glass to gap and flush specifications.
  11. Once the glass gap and flush is in specifications, hand tighten the patched bolts (x2) that attach the glass to the regulator isolator clamps.
  12. Raise the LH front window fully by accessing the window switch tool through the LH rear door.
  13. Close the LH front door.
  14. Check again the gap and flush of the front LH door glass and make adjustments, if necessary.
  15. Open the LH front door.
  16. Torque the patched bolts (x2) that secure the glass to the regulator isolator clamps
    9.5 Nm (7.0 lbs-ft)
    .
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm socket
  17. Lower the front LH window.
  18. Disconnect the window switch special tool.
  19. Install the plugs that cover the openings in the door regulator isolation clamps.
  20. Install the LH front door trim panel. See Panel - Door Trim - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  21. Install the LH front door puddle light. See Puddle Lamp - Front Door (LH) (Remove and Replace).
  22. Install the LH front door tweeter. See Tweeter - Front Door - LH (Remove and Replace).
  23. Install the LH front door reflector. See Reflector - Door - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  24. Unlock the vehicle gateway. See Gateway Unlock.
  25. On the touchscreen, touch Controls > Service Mode > Windows > Calibrate Windows, then select Start to calibrate the window.
  26. Disable Service Mode. See Service Mode.
