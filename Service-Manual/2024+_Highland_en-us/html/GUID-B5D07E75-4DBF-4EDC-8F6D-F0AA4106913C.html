Front Bin - Center Console (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1519010172 FRT 0.48 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 1519 - Center Console

Remove

Note
This procedure is performed with the center console installed in the vehicle. Some images may show the center console outside of the vehicle, but that is only to provide more clarity on the fasteners and components.
  1. Remove the center console sliding bin lid assembly. See Sliding Door Assembly - Center Console (Remove and Replace).
  2. Move the cup holder sliding door forward to the locking position, and then remove the cup holder sliding door limit blocks (x2).
    Warning
    The video(s) included in this procedure are meant as an overview for supplemental purposes only. Follow all of the steps listed in the procedure to avoid damage to components and/or personal injury.
  3. Move the sliding door rearward and lift it to remove the cup holder sliding door.
  4. Release the clips (x6) that attach the cup holder to the center console, and then remove the cup holder.
  5. Remove the cup holder sliding door springs (x2).
  6. Remove front media bin sliding door assembly.
    Note
    Pull the cup holer cover sliding device rearward to release the cup holder cover retractors and locking hook, and then remove sliding device from the front media bin assembly.

Install

  1. Install the cup holder cover sliding device to the front media bin sliding door assembly.
    Note
    Ensure the retractors and locking hook are installed in place.
  2. Install the cup holder into position, and then secure it with clips (x6).
  3. Install the cup holder sliding door.
  4. Install the cup holder sliding door limit blocks (x2).
  5. Install the center console sliding bin lid assembly. See Sliding Door Assembly - Center Console (Remove and Replace).
