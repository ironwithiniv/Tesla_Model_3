Sliding Lid - Center Console (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1519010182 FRT 0.48 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Note
The cup holder sliding door assembly includes 3 sub-parts, which are cup holder, sliding device and springs (x2).
Parent topic: 1519 - Center Console

Remove

  1. Remove the center console phone dock.
    • Phone Dock (Clip) - Center Console (Remove and Replace)
    • Phone Dock (Screw) - Center Console (Remove and Replace)
  2. Remove center console rear end panel. See Panel - Rear End - Center Console (Remove and Replace)
  3. Remove LH rear side panel from center console assembly. See Panel - Rear Side - Center Console - LH (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove LH front side panel from center console assembly. See Carpet - Side - Center Console - LH (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove LH center décor trim. See Hockey Stick Assembly -LH (with a Clip-secured Phone Dock) (Remove and Replace).
  6. Repeat step 3 through step 5 for the RH side of the vehicle.
  7. Remove the top face panel assembly from center console. See Top Face Panel - Center Console (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the center console sliding bin lid assembly. See Sliding Door Assembly - Center Console (Remove and Replace).
  9. Move the cup holder sliding door forward to the locking position, and then remove the cup holder sliding door limit blocks (x2).
  10. Move the sliding door rearward and lift it to remove the cup holder sliding door.
  11. Release the clips (x6) that attach the cup holder to the center console, and then remove the cup holder.
  12. Remove the cup holder sliding door springs (x2).
  13. Remove the cup holder sliding device from the sliding assembly, release the retractable springs (x2) that attach the cup holder sliding device, and then remove the slide from the vehicle.

Install

  1. Secure the retractable spring (x2) that attach the cup holder sliding device, and then install the device to the sliding assembly.
    Note
    Ensure the retractable springs and locking hook are installed in place.
  2. Install the cup holder sliding door springs (x2).
  3. Install the cup holder into position, and then secure the clips (x6) that attach the cup holder to the center console framework.
  4. Install the cup holder sliding door.
  5. Install the cup holder sliding door limit blocks (x2).
  6. Install the center console sliding bin lid assembly. See Sliding Door Assembly - Center Console (Remove and Replace).
  7. Install the top face panel assembly. See Top Face Panel - Center Console (Remove and Replace).
  8. Install LH center console décor trim. See Hockey Stick Assembly -LH (with a Clip-secured Phone Dock) (Remove and Replace).
  9. Install LH center console front side panel. See Carpet - Side - Center Console - LH (Remove and Replace).
  10. Install LH center console rear side panel. See Panel - Rear Side - Center Console - LH (Remove and Replace).
  11. Repeat step 8 through step 10 for the RH side of the vehicle.
  12. Install the center console rear end panel. See Panel - Rear End - Center Console (Remove and Replace).
  13. Install the center console phone dock.
    • Phone Dock (Clip) - Center Console (Remove and Replace)
    • Phone Dock (Screw) - Center Console (Remove and Replace)
