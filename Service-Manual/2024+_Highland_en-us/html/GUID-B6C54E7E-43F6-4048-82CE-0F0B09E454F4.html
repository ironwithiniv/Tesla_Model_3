Motor - Brake Caliper - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 33052302 0.30 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-11-03: Removed LH rear EPB harness bracket removal/installation steps.
  • 2026-10-08: Updated the bolt torque for fixing the motor from 10.8 Nm to 13 Nm.
Parent topic: 3305 - Parking Brake

Torque Specifications

Table 1. Torque Specifications: Motor - Brake Caliper - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace)
Description Torque Value Recommended Tools Reuse/Replace Notes
Bolts (x2) that secure the LH rear brake caliper motor
13 Nm (9.6 lbs-ft)
  • 5 mm socket
 Replace New bolts are provided in the service kit.

Remove

  1. Raise and support the vehicle. See Raise Vehicle - 2 Post Lift.
  2. Open the LH front door and lower the LH front window.
  3. Enable Service Mode through the vehicle touchscreen. See Service Mode.
  4. Unlock the vehicle gateway. See Gateway Unlock.
  5. Enter the EPB service mode through the vehicle touchscreen. See Parking Brake - Caliper - Rear - LH (Release).
  6. Remove the LH rear wheel. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
  7. Release the locking tab and disconnect the LH rear EPB caliper connector.
  8. Remove and discard the bolts (x2) that secure the LH rear brake caliper motor, and then remove the motor from the vehicle.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 5 mm socket
  9. Remove and discard the old O-ring from the LH rear brake caliper.

Install

  1. Install the new O-ring provided in the service kit onto the LH rear brake caliper.
  2. Install new bolts (x2) that secure the LH rear brake caliper motor.
    13 Nm (9.6 lbs-ft)
    Note
    The new bolts are provided in the service kit.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 5 mm socket
  3. Connect the LH rear EPB caliper connector and engage the locking tab.
  4. Install the LH rear wheel. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
  5. Exit the EPB service mode through the touchscreen.
    1. Touch Chassis > Brakes > EPB Service Mode.
    2. Under Parameters, choose BOTH from the EPB Selection drop-down menu and choose STOP from the Action Selection drop-down menu, then touch Run.
  6. Exit the Service Mode. See Service Mode.
  7. Raise the LH front window and close the LH front door.
  8. Remove lift arms from below the vehicle.
