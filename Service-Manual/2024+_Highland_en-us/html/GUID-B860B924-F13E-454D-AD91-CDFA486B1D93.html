Module - Homelink Transmitter (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1715010512 FRT 0.30 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2024-04-17: Changed the method of accessing the Homelink module.
Parent topic: 1715 - Electronic Control Modules

Remove

  1. Remove the underhood storage unit. See Underhood Storage Unit (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the bolts (x5) that attach the upper portion of the front fascia to the vehicle.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
  3. Remove the clips (x5) that attach the LH front wheel arch liner to the front fascia, and then move the liner aside for access.
  4. Remove the bolt that attaches the front fascia to the LH front fender.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
  5. Repeat step 3 through step 4 on the RH side of the vehicle.
  6. Release the clips (x16) that attach the front fascia assembly to the vehicle, and then partially release the front fascia assembly from the vehicle to gain access to the Homelink module.
    Note
    Put shop towels on the fascia to prevent damage.
  7. Disconnect the electrical connector from the HomeLink transmitter module.
  8. Release the tab, and then slide the HomeLink transmitter module to the right to remove it from the vehicle.

Install

  1. Install the HomeLink transmitter module on the bracket.
  2. Connect the electrical connector to the HomeLink transmitter module.
  3. Remove the towels, and fasten the clips (x16) that attach the front fascia assembly to the vehicle.
  4. Install the bolt that attaches the front fascia to the LH front fender.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
  5. Fasten the clips (x5) that attach the LH front wheel arch liner to the front fascia, and then move the liner aside for access.
  6. Repeat step 4 through step 5 on the RH side of the vehicle.
  7. Install the bolts (x5) that attach the upper portion of the front fascia to the vehicle.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
  8. Install the underhood storage unit. See Underhood Storage Unit (Remove and Replace).
  9. Use the touchscreen to enable Service Mode. See Service Mode.
  10. Unlock the vehicle gateway. See Gateway Unlock
  11. On the touchscreen, touch Controls > Low Voltage > HomeLink > Test HomeLink > Run and allow the routine to complete.
  12. Exit Service Mode. See Service Mode.
