Suspension - Front (Check Torque) (Test/Adjust)

Correction code 31019000 FRT 0.48 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2025-07-23: Updated the torque of the bolts that attach the front lower lateral links to the front subframe to 190 Nm.
Equipment:
  • 1459409-00-A ADAPTER, HUB JACK, MODEL 3
  • 1081765-00-A 3/4 TON UNDERHOIST STAND
  • 1137855-00-A TOOL, REAR RIDE HEIGHT TORQUE, MODEL 3
Parent topic: 3101 - Front Suspension (Including Hubs)

Procedure

  1. Raise and support the vehicle. See Raise Vehicle - 2 Post Lift.
  2. Remove the LH and RH front wheel. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
  3. Check the torque for the nut that attaches the front stabilizer end link to the LH front strut, and then mark the nut with a paint pen.
    98 Nm (72.3 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 18 mm combination wrench
    • Torx T40 socket
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  4. Check the torque for the nut that attaches the LH front tie rod end to the LH front suspension knuckle, and then mark the nut with a paint pen.
    180 Nm (132.7 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 22 mm socket
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  5. Check the torque for the nut that attaches the LH front upper control arm to the knuckle, and then mark the nut with a paint pen.
    56 Nm (41.3 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 15 mm combination wrench
    • Torx T50 socket
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
  6. Repeat step 4 and step 5 on the RH side of the front suspension.
  7. Remove the front aero shield panel. See Panel - Aero Shield - Front (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the front fascia valance. See Valance - Front Fascia (Remove and Replace).
    Note
    No need to remove the pedestrian warning speaker from the valance.
  9. Check the torque for the nut that attaches the LH front lower aft link to the front subframe, and then mark the nut with a paint pen.
    115 Nm (84.8 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 6 in extension
    • 21 mm socket
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  10. Check the torque for the nuts that attach the LH front lower lateral link to the front subframe, and then mark the nuts with a paint pen.
    190 Nm (140.1 lbs-ft)
    Note
    There are two types of bolts that are difficult to distinguish visually, each requiring different torque values.
    • 2123635-00-A (Currently only available in EMEA and APAC): 190 Nm
    • 1109912-00-B (NA, use only): 135 Nm

    Refer to article 27022 to confirm the residue torque and set the torque value in the digital torque wrench to 115Nm:

    1. If the bolt rotation is less than 180 degrees, retighten to 135 Nm;
    2. Otherwise replace the bolts and tighten them to the standard torque value listed in the Link - Lateral - Lower - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 6 in extension
    • 21 mm socket
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  11. Check the torque for the nut that attaches the LH front lower aft link to the knuckle, and then mark the nut with a paint pen.
    180 Nm (132.7 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 22 mm socket
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  12. Check the torque for the nut that attaches the LH front lower lateral link to the knuckle, and then mark the nut with a paint pen.
    180 Nm (132.7 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 22 mm socket
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  13. Check the torque for the nut to attach the stabilizer bar link to the stabilizer bar, and then mark the nut with a paint pen.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
  14. Install the hub jack adapter onto the LH front hub and hand-tighten the lug nuts (x5).
  15. Position and raise the underhoist stand to simulate the suspension at ride height.
  16. Measure the distance between the bottom of the fender and the center of the front axle to make sure that the front suspension is set to ride height. The distance should measure 423 mm.
  17. Check the torque for the nut that attaches the LH front strut to the LH front lower control arm, and then mark the nut with a paint pen.
    106 Nm (78.2 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 21 mm combination wrench
    • 21 mm socket
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
  18. Remove the underhoist stand from underneath the LH front suspension.
  19. Remove the lug nuts (x5) that attach the hub jack adapter, and then remove the hub jack adapter from the vehicle.
    Note
    Gently tap with a dead blow to aid
  20. Repeat step 9 through step 19 on the RH side of the front suspension.
  21. Check the torque for the nuts (x4) that attaches the LH and RH front stabilizer bar to the front subframe, and then mark the nuts with a paint pen.
    35 Nm (25.8 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 3 in extension
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
    • 15 mm deep socket
  22. Check the torque for the nuts (x2) that attach the front of the LH and RH front subframe to the body, and then mark the nuts with a paint pen.
    41 Nm (30.2 lbs-ft)
    CAUTION
    The minimum residue torque is 41 Nm. If the torque value is lower than 41 Nm, check the reasons for the torque reduction, and tighten or replace the bolts as necessary.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 2 in extension
    • Torque wrench with angle measurement
    • 18 mm socket
  23. Check the torque for the small nuts (x2) that attach the rear of the LH and RH front subframe to the body, and then mark the nuts with a paint pen.
    50 Nm (36.9 lbs-ft)
    CAUTION
    The minimum residue torque is 41 Nm. If the torque value is lower than 41 Nm, check the reasons for the torque reduction, and tighten or replace the bolts as necessary.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 3 in extension
    • Torque wrench with angle measurement
    • 15 mm socket
  24. Check the torque for the larger nuts (x2) that attach the rear of the LH and RH front subframe to the body, and then mark the nuts with a paint pen.
    125 Nm (92.2 lbs-ft)
    CAUTION
    The minimum residue torque is 41 Nm. If the torque value is lower than 41 Nm, check the reasons for the torque reduction, and tighten or replace the bolts as necessary.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 3 in extension
    • Torque wrench with angle measurement
    • 18 mm socket
  25. Install the front fascia valance. See Valance - Front Fascia (Remove and Replace).
  26. Install the front aero shield panel. See Panel - Aero Shield - Front (Remove and Replace).
  27. Install the LH and RH front wheel. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
  28. Remove the vehicle from the 2 post lift. See Raise Vehicle - 2 Post Lift.
