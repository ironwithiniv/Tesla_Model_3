Structure - Center Console (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1519010022 FRT 0.72 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Equipment:
  • 1060071-00-A Torque Wrench 1/4in Dr Hexagon
Parent topic: 1519 - Center Console

Remove

  1. Open all the doors and lower all the windows.
  2. Remove the phone dock assembly. See Phone Dock (Clip) - Center Console (Remove and Replace)
  3. Remove the center console rear end panel. See Panel - Rear End - Center Console (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the bolt that attaches the LH rear of the center console to the body. Repeat this step for the RH rear of the center console.
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  5. Remove the LH center console rear side panel. See Panel - Rear Side - Center Console - LH (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the LH center console front side panel. See Carpet - Side - Center Console - LH (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the bolts (x2) that attach the LH front of the center console to the body.
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  8. Remove LH center console decor trim. See Hockey Stick Assembly -LH (with a Clip-secured Phone Dock) (Remove and Replace).
  9. Remove the RH center console rear side panel. See Panel - Rear Side - Center Console - LH (Remove and Replace).
  10. Remove the RH center console front side panel. See Carpet - Side - Center Console - RH (Remove and Replace).
  11. Remove the bolts (x2) that attach the RH front of the center console to the body.
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  12. Remove the RH center console decor trim. See Hockey Stick Assembly -LH (with a Clip-secured Phone Dock) (Remove and Replace).
  13. Disconnect the LV power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
  14. Remove the center console armrest assembly. See Armrest - Center Console (Remove and Install).
  15. Remove the center console top face panel. See Top Face Panel - Center Console (Remove and Replace).
  16. Remove the center console sliding bin lid assembly. See Sliding Door Assembly - Center Console (Remove and Replace).
  17. Disconnect the electrical connector from the rear of the center console.
    Note
    After disconnecting the electrical harness, release the clip that attaches the electrical connector to the center console with a clip prytool.
  18. Release the clip that attaches the electrical harness, and then disconnect the electrical connector from the right front of the center console.
  19. With an assistant, remove the center console structure assembly from the vehicle as follows:
    1. Tilt the rear of the console structure upwards.
    2. Pull the console structure towards the rear of the vehicle.
    3. Tilt the front of the console structure upwards.
    4. Remove the console structure through the passenger front door.

Install

  1. With an assistant, install the center console structure assembly into the vehicle as follows:
    1. Tilt the front of the console structure downwards.
    2. Slide the console structure towards the front of the vehicle.
    3. Tilt the rear of the console structure downwards until it is in place.
  2. Connect the electrical connector to the right front of the center console, and then install the clip that attaches the electrical harness.
  3. Connect the electrical connector to the rear of the center console.
    Note
    Install the clip that attaches the electrical connector to the center console first and then connect the electrical harness.
    CAUTION
    Ensure the connector is properly installed. Perform a push-pull test before continuing this procedure.
  4. Install the center console sliding bin lid assembly. See Sliding Door Assembly - Center Console (Remove and Replace).
  5. Install the center console top face panel. See Top Face Panel - Center Console (Remove and Replace).
  6. Install the center console armrest. See Armrest - Center Console (Remove and Install) .
  7. Reconnect the LV power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
  8. Install the RH center console decor trim. See Hockey Stick Assembly -LH (with a Clip-secured Phone Dock) (Remove and Replace).
  9. Install the bolts (x2) that attach the RH front of the center console to the body.
    7 Nm (5.2 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  10. Install the RH center console front side panel. See Carpet - Side - Center Console - RH (Remove and Replace).
  11. Install the RH center console rear side panel. See Panel - Rear Side - Center Console - LH (Remove and Replace).
  12. Install the LH center console decor trim. See Hockey Stick Assembly -LH (with a Clip-secured Phone Dock) (Remove and Replace).
  13. Install the bolts (x2) that attach the LH front of the center console to the body.
    7 Nm (5.2 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  14. Install the LH center console front side panel. See Carpet - Side - Center Console - LH (Remove and Replace).
  15. Install the LH center console rear side panel. See Panel - Rear Side - Center Console - LH (Remove and Replace).
  16. Install the bolt that attaches the RH rear of the center console to the body. Repeat this step for the LH rear of the center console.
    7 Nm (5.2 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  17. Install the center console rear end panel. See Panel - Rear End - Center Console (Remove and Replace).
  18. Install the phone dock assembly. See Phone Dock (Clip) - Center Console (Remove and Replace)
  19. Raise all the windows and close all the doors.
