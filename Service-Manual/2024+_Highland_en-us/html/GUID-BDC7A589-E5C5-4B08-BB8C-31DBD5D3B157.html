Cover - Busbar Module 3 to Shunt - Ancillary Bay (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1630010162 FRT 2.04 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Warning

Only technicians who have completed all required certification courses are permitted to perform this procedure. Tesla recommends third party service provider technicians undergo equivalent training before performing this procedure. For more information on Tesla Technician requirements, or descriptions of the subject matter for third parties, see HV Certification Requirements. Proper personal protective equipment (PPE) and insulating HV gloves with a minimum rating of class 0 (1000V) must be worn at all times a high voltage cable, busbar, or fitting is handled. Refer to Tech Note TN-15-92-003, High Voltage Awareness Care Points for additional safety information.

Warning

Remove all jewelry (watches, bracelets, rings, necklaces, earrings, ID tags, piercings, etc.) from your person, and all objects (keys, coins, pens, pencils, tools, fasteners, etc.) from your pockets before performing any procedure that exposes you to high voltage.

Warning
If corrective eyewear is necessary to safely perform any procedure, make sure that the eyewear is securely restrained to the head and cannot fall off.
Parent topic: 1630 - HV Battery Electrical Components

Remove

  1. Move the vehicle to a 2 post lift. See Raise Vehicle - 2 Post Lift.
  2. Remove the rear underhood apron. See Underhood Apron - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  3. Disconnect the LV power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
  4. Remove the rear aero shield panel. See Panel - Aero Shield - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the rear HV battery skid plate. See Skid Plate - HV Battery - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  6. Drain the coolant from the power conversion system. See Ancillary Bay Coolant (Drain and Refill) (Test/Adjust).
  7. Disconnect the coolant drain hose adapters from the power conversion system inlet and outlet hoses and plug the power conversion inlet and outlet hoses.
  8. Remove the coolant collection tools, and then remove the coolant pressure tester from underneath the vehicle.
  9. Lower the vehicle fully.
  10. Remove the 2nd row lower seat cushion. See Seat Cushion - 2nd Row (Remove and Replace).
  11. Perform the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure. See Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure.
  12. Remove the ancillary bay cover. See Ancillary Bay Cover (Remove and Replace).
    Warning
    HV insulating gloves and leather glove protectors must be worn throughout the remainder of this procedure. Do not remove gloves or protectors until otherwise noted.
  13. Remove the pyrotechnic battery disconnect. See Pyro Disconnect - HV Battery (Remove and Replace).
  14. Remove the power conversion system. See Power Conversion System (Remove and Replace).
  15. Check whether all HV insulators are installed; if not, install insulators as needed.
  16. Remove the insulator cap from the HV battery shunt busbar nut.
  17. Release the clips (x3) that attach the HV battery shunt busbar cover to the HV battery shunt busbar, and then remove the cover from the busbar.

Install

  1. Fasten the clips (x3) that attach the HV battery shunt busbar cover to the HV battery shunt busbar.
  2. Install the insulator cap to the HV battery shunt busbar nut.
  3. Install the power conversion system. See Power Conversion System (Remove and Replace).
  4. Measure the voltage across the pyrotechnic battery disconnect mount points, and then install the pyrotechnic battery disconnect. See Pyro Disconnect - HV Battery (Remove and Replace).
  5. Install the ancillary bay cover. See Ancillary Bay Cover (Remove and Replace).
    Note
    Before installing the ancillary bay cover, inspect the ancillary bay cover seal and confirm there is no visual damage. If the seal is torn and/or delaminated, replace the ancillary bay cover.
  6. Install the 2nd row lower seat cushion. See Seat Cushion - 2nd Row (Remove and Replace).
  7. Raise the vehicle to a comfortable working height.
  8. Connect the coolant drain hose adapters to the power conversion system inlet and outlet hoses, and then put the coolant container underneath the vehicle.
  9. Perform ancillary bay air leak test. See Ancillary Bay Air Leak Test (Inspection).
  10. Install the rear HV battery skid plate. See Skid Plate - HV Battery - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  11. Install the rear aero shield panel. See Panel - Aero Shield - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  12. Refill the coolant. See Ancillary Bay Coolant (Drain and Refill) (Test/Adjust).
  13. Install the rear underhood apron. See Underhood Apron - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  14. Exit Service mode. See Service Mode.
  15. Remove the vehicle from the 2 post lift. See Raise Vehicle - 2 Post Lift.
