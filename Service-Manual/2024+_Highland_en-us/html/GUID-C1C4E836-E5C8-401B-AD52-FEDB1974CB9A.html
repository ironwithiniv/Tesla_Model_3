Glove Box (Remove and Install)

Correction code 1405020061 FRT 0.36 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 1405 - Instrument Panel

Torque Specifications

Table 1. Torque Specifications: Glove Box (Remove and Install)
Description Torque Value Recommended Tools Reuse/Replace Notes
Screws (x4) that attach the glove box to the instrument panel carrier
2.3 Nm (1.7 lbs-ft)
  • Torx T20 socket
  • 2 in extension
  • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
  • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
 Reuse

Remove

  1. Disconnect the LV battery power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
  2. Remove the passenger footwell cover. See Cover - Footwell - Front Passenger (Remove and Replace).
  3. For NA vehicles only: Remove the front passenger knee airbag. See Airbag - Knee - RH (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the RH instrument panel end cap. See End Cap - Instrument Panel - LH (Remove and Replace).
  5. Release the tree clips (x2) that attach the passenger footwell duct to the instrument panel.
  6. Disconnect the glove box USB connector from the car computer.
  7. Remove the screws (x4) that attach the glove box to the instrument panel carrier.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T20 socket
    • 2 in extension
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
  8. On the touchscreen, click Controls > Glovebox to open the glove box.
  9. Release the clips (x10), locator pins (x3) and datum that attach the glove box to the instrument panel carrier.
    1. Release the clips (x2) at the bottom of the glove box.
    2. Pull the glove box outward from the inner side to release it from its mounting points.
    3. After releasing the inner side, use a clip pry tool to carefully loosen the remaining clips securing the glove box.
    CAUTION
    Be careful not to damage the 2 electromagnetic switches on the both sides of the glove box.
    Note
    The clips are tightly secured. Take care not to damage the leather surface.
    Note
    Replace any broken clips.
  10. Disconnect the electrical connectors (x2) from the glove box, and then remove the glove box from the vehicle.

Install

  1. Connect the electrical connectors (x2) to the glove box.
  2. Secure the clips (x10), locator pins (x3) and datum that attach the glove box to the instrument panel carrier.
  3. Install the screws (x4) that attach the glove box to the instrument panel carrier.
    2.3 Nm (1.7 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T20 socket
    • 2 in extension
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
  4. Connect the glove box USB connector to the car computer.
  5. Fasten the tree clips (x2) that attach the passenger footwell duct to the instrument panel.
  6. For NA vehicles only: Install the front passenger knee airbag. See Airbag - Knee - RH (Remove and Replace).
  7. Install the RH instrument panel end cap. See End Cap - Instrument Panel - LH (Remove and Replace).
  8. Install the passenger footwell cover. See Cover - Footwell - Front Passenger (Remove and Replace).
  9. Connect the LV battery power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
