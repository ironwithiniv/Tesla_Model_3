Steering Wheel (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 3205010062 FRT 0.18 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2025-09-10: Clarified that the routine is for vehicles without a turn stalk; the routine setup should be skipped for a vehicle with a turn stalk.
  • 2025-02-04: Swapped steps to perform the steering wheel post replacement procedure and reinstall the firmware.
  • 2024-12-06: Rearranged install steps.
  • 2024-11-06: Added step to perform Steering Wheel Post Replacement Procedure.
CAUTION
Supplemental Restraint System (SRS) components can be hazardous if not serviced and handled correctly. See Airbag and Supplementary Restraint System (SRS) Precautions
CAUTION
In the event of an airbag or seatbelt pretensioner deployment, certain components must be replaced, and other components must be inspected for damage. Refer to the Collision Repair Manual for a specific list of items that must be inspected and replaced.

 Replace The bolt is patched

Remove

  1. Set the vehicle's front wheels straight and the steering wheel centered.
  2. Remove the rear underhood apron. See Underhood Apron - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  3. Disconnect the LV power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
  4. Remove the driver airbag. See Airbag - Driver (Remove and Replace).
  5. Disconnect the steering wheel electrical wiring harness connector.
  6. Remove and discard the bolt that attaches the steering wheel to the steering column.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 3 in extension
    • 10 mm hex socket
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench (for installation only)
    Warning
    The video(s) included in this procedure are meant as an overview for supplemental purposes only. Follow all of the steps listed in the procedure to avoid damage to components and/or personal injury.
  7. Remove the steering wheel from the steering column.

Install

  1. Position the steering wheel onto the steering column.
    Warning
    The video(s) included in this procedure are meant as an overview for supplemental purposes only. Follow all of the steps listed in the procedure to avoid damage to components and/or personal injury.
  2. Install the bolt that attaches the steering wheel to the steering column.
    80 Nm (59.0 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 3 in extension
    • 10 mm hex socket
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench (for installation only)
  3. Connect the steering wheel electrical wiring harness connector.
  4. Install the driver airbag. See Airbag - Driver (Remove and Replace).
  5. Connect LV power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
  6. Reinstall the firmware. See Software Reinstall - Touchscreen.
  7. For vehicles without a turn stalk: perform the Steering Wheel Post Replacement Procedure: PROC_VCLEFT_X_STEERING-WHEEL-POST-REPLACEMENT-PROCEDUREvia Service Mode:Chassis ➜ SCCM ➜ Steering Wheel Post Replacement Procedurevia Toolbox:(link)
    Note
    Skip this step for vehicles equipped with a turn stalk.
  8. Install the rear underhood apron. See Underhood Apron - Rear (Remove and Replace).
