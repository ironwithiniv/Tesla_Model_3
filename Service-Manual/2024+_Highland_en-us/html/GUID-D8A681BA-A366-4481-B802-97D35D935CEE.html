Hose - Radiator Inlet (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1830020072 FRT 0.66 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-05-22: Updated the radiator inlet hose disconnect/connect images for clarity purposes.
Parent topic: 1830 - Cooling System

Remove

  1. Raise and support the vehicle. See Raise Vehicle - 2 Post Lift.
  2. Remove the rear underhood apron. See Underhood Apron - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the cabin intake duct. See Assembly - Fresh Air Gutter - HVAC (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the underhood storage unit. See Underhood Storage Unit (Remove and Replace).
  5. Disconnect the LV power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
  6. Remove the front aero shield panel. See Panel - Aero Shield - Front (Remove and Replace).
  7. Lower the vehicle fully.
  8. Position a fluid catcher underneath the front of vehicle.
  9. Release the spring clip, disconnect the radiator inlet hose from the radiator, and then plug the hose and radiator.
  10. Release the spring clip, disconnect the radiator inlet hose from the supermanifold, and then plug the hose and supermanifold.
  11. Release the clips (x2) that attach the radiator inlet hose to the vehicle.
  12. Remove the radiator inlet hose.

Install

  1. Remove the plugs from the supermanifold and radiator inlet hose, immediately connect the radiator inlet hose to the supermanifold, and then fasten the clip.
    Note
    Perform a push-pull-push test on the fittings to make sure that they are secure.
  2. Remove the plugs from the radiator and radiator inlet hose, immediately connect the radiator inlet hose to the radiator, and then fasten the clip.
    Note
    Perform a push-pull-push test on the fittings to make sure that they are secure.
  3. Install the clips (x2) that attach the radiator inlet hose to the vehicle.
  4. Remove the fluid catcher underneath the front of the vehicle.
  5. Install the front aero shield panel. See Panel - Aero Shield - Front (Remove and Replace).
  6. Lower the vehicle fully.
  7. Remove the coolant bottle cap, and then fill the coolant to the proper level.
    Note
    Ensure that the coolant level is at the "Max" line.
  8. Connect the LV power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
  9. Connect the vehicle to a laptop with Toolbox.
  10. In Toolbox, click Actions/Autodiag tab, type "Purge" into the search field, click via Toolbox:(link) , click Run, and allow the routine to complete.
    Note
    Make sure vehicle is not in Drive, shifting the vehicle into Drive will stop this routine. The test will last approximately 10 mins. The test will vary speeds from 3500-6500 RPM (idle speed = ~1500 RPM) and actuate the valve between series and parallel. If speed hovers at 7000 RPM, it indicates the pump is air locked, perform the vacuum fill again. Continue to add coolant and purge until the coolant level reaches between the NOM and MAX Lines on the bottle.
  11. Inspect the coolant level and top off as necessary, and then install the coolant bottle cap.
    Note
    Ensure that the coolant level is at the "Max" line.
  12. Install the under hood storage unit. See Underhood Storage Unit (Remove and Replace).
  13. Install the cabin intake duct. See Assembly - Fresh Air Gutter - HVAC (Remove and Replace).
  14. Install the rear underhood apron. See Underhood Apron - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  15. Remove the vehicle from the 2 post lift. See Raise Vehicle - 2 Post Lift.
