BLE Assembly - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1745020012 FRT 0.24 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 1745 - Keyless Entry and Security

Remove

  1. Open the LH front door and lower the LH front window.
  2. Open the trunk.
  3. Remove the rear underhood apron. See Underhood Apron - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  4. Disconnect LV power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
  5. Remove the bolt and clips (x3) that attach the LH rear trough trim to the vehicle.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T20 bit
  6. Remove the bolt that attaches the rear fascia to the vehicle.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  7. Release the clip that attaches the aft lower section of the LH rear wheel arch liner to the vehicle.
  8. Remove the bolt and the U-nut that attach the rear fascia to the vehicle.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T25 socket
    Warning
    The video(s) included in this procedure are meant as an overview for supplemental purposes only. Follow all of the steps listed in the procedure to avoid damage to components and/or personal injury.
  9. Partially loosen the LH rear fascia away from the vehicle and insert a inflatable air bag, and then inflate the air bag to gain access to the LH rear BLE module.
    Warning
    Take care not to damage the rear fascia.
  10. Disconnect the electrical harness from the LH rear BLE module connector.
  11. Release the tabs (x2) that attach the LH rear BLE module to the rear fascia, and them remove it from the vehicle.

Install

  1. Fasten the tabs (x2) that attach the LH rear BLE module to the rear fascia.
  2. Connect the electrical harness to the LH rear BLE module connector.
  3. Deflate and remove the inflatable air bag between the LH front section of the rear fascia and the vehicle, and then install the rear fascia back to the vehicle.
  4. Install the bolt and the U-nut that attach the rear fascia to the vehicle.
    2 Nm (1.5 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T25 socket
  5. Fasten the clip that attaches the aft lower section of the LH rear wheel arch liner to the vehicle.
  6. Install the bolt that attaches the rear fascia to the vehicle.
    4 Nm (2.9 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  7. Install the bolt and clips (x3) that attach the LH rear trough trim to the vehicle.
    1.2 Nm (.9 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T20 bit
  8. Connect LV power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
  9. Install the rear underhood apron. See Underhood Apron - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  10. Close the trunk.
  11. Connect a laptop with Toolbox to the vehicle. See Toolbox (Connect and Disconnect).
  12. In Toolbox, click the Actions/Autodiag tab, type "Endpoint" in the search field.
  13. Click on TEST-SELF_VCSEC_X_ENDPOINT-COMMvia Toolbox:(link), and then click Run Networkand allow the routine to complete.
  14. Disconnect the laptop with Toolbox from the vehicle. See Toolbox (Connect and Disconnect).
  15. Reinstall the vehicle firmware. See Software Reinstall - Touchscreen.
  16. Raise the LH front window and close the LH front door.
