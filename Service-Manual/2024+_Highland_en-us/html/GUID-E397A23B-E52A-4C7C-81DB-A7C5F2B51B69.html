Gateway Unlock

Parent topic: 0005 - Service Modes

Unlocking

  1. If Service Mode or Service Mode Plus is not yet enabled, enable Service Mode through the touchscreen. See Service Mode.
    Note
    GTW LOCKED will be displayed on the instrument cluster and the MCU.
  2. Place the key card on the card reader located in the wireless phone charger on the center console.
  3. Press and hold the brake pedal, and then hold the right turn signal indicator for at least ten seconds.
    Note
    The instrument cluster and MCU briefly display GTW UNLOCKING. Once the gateway is unlocked, the instrument cluster and MCU will display GTW UNLOCKED and a countdown timer that indicates the remaining time in seconds for the gateway to be unlocked (max. 90 minutes).

Locking and re-unlocking

  1. The gateway will lock:
    1. If the countdown timer has reached the maximum time of 90 minutes.
    2. When leaving the vehicle and closing the doors.
  2. If the gateway needs to be unlocked for a longer period of time, re-unlock the gateway. See Unlocking.
