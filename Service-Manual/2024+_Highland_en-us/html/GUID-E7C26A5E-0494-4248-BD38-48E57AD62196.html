Beam - Cross Car (RHD) (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1405021012 FRT 2.40 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Parent topic: 1405 - Instrument Panel

Remove

  1. Open both front doors and lower both front windows.
  2. Move the LH and RH front seats backward.
  3. Disconnect the LV battery power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
  4. Remove the wiper module. See Wiper Module (RHD) (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the driver footwell cover. See Cover - Footwell - Driver (RHD) (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the LH and RH instrument panel end caps. See End Cap - Instrument Panel - LH (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the LH and RH upper A-pillar trims. See Trim - A-Pillar - Upper - LH (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the instrument panel lower assembly. See Assembly - Instrument Panel - Lower (Remove and Replace).
  9. Remove the passenger footwell cover. See Cover - Footwell - Front Passenger (RHD) (Remove and Replace).
  10. Remove the glove box. See Glove Box (RHD) (Remove and Replace).
  11. Remove the passenger footwell duct. See Duct - Footwell - LH (RHD) (Remove and Replace).
  12. Remove the LH instrument panel air duct. See Duct - Air Wave - LH (RHD) (Remove and Replace).
  13. Remove the center console assembly. See Center Console (Remove and Replace).
  14. Remove the driver airbag assembly. See Airbag - Driver (Remove and Replace).
  15. Remove the steering wheel. See Steering Wheel (Remove and Replace).
    Note
    Mark the steering wheel to steering column before removing the steering wheel.
  16. Remove the lower shroud from the steering column. See Shroud - Lower - Steering Column (Remove and Replace).
  17. Remove the steering column control module (SCCM). See Module - Steering Column Control (Remove and Replace).
  18. Remove the touchscreen. See Touchscreen - Front (Remove and Replace).
  19. Remove the instrument panel speaker grille. See Speaker Grille - Instrument Panel (Remove and Replace).
  20. Remove the LH and RH instrument panel speakers. See Speaker - Dash - LH (Remove and Replace).
  21. Remove the center instrument panel speaker. See Speaker - Dash - Center (Remove and Replace).
  22. Lift the white tab to disconnect the passenger air bag connector.
  23. Remove and discard the patch bolts (x2) that secure the instrument panel carrier to the cross car beam near the passenger airbag.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 6 in extension
    • ¼ in to 3/8 in adapter
    • External Torx E10
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  24. Disconnect the instrument panel harness connector from instrument panel LH side.
  25. Disconnect instrument panel harness connector from instrument panel RH side.
  26. Remove the screws (x9) and the datums (x2) that attach the instrument panel carrier to the cross car beam.
    Note
    There is 1 screw and 1 datum on the upper LH side, 1 screw on the LH side, 3 screws at the lower center, 3 screws on the RH side, and 1 screw and 1 datum on the upper RH side.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 2 in extension
    • Torx T20 socket
    • 4 in extension
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  27. Remove the LH and RH instrument panel speaker harnesses from the instrument panel.
    Note
    Gently pull out the instrument panel and lift it to facilitate removing the harnesses.
  28. With assistance, release guide tabs (x4) and tabs (x2) to remove the instrument panel carrier from the vehicle.
  29. Disconnect the thermistor connector from the RH instrument panel air wave duct.
  30. Remove the push clips (x2) that attach the RH air wave duct to the instrument panel, and then remove the duct.
  31. Remove the headliner harness clips (x2) on the LH lower A-pillar.
  32. Remove the instrument panel harness connector from the lower part of the cross car beam assembly.
  33. Remove the harness clips (x2) from the instrument panel near the LH controller.
  34. Remove the instrument panel harness clips (x8) from the top of cross car beam.
    Note
    Number of clips on newer vehicles may vary.
  35. Remove the blower motor harness clips (x2) from the cross car beam.
  36. Remove the UI harness fixing clip from the cross car beam.
  37. Disconnect the instrument panel harness connector from the electronic steering column.
  38. Remove the clip that attaches the instrument panel harness connector to the cross car beam assembly.
  39. Remove the RH wiring harness from the instrument panel mounting brackets (x2).
  40. Remove the wiring harness fixing clips (x4) from the lower part of the dashboard cross car beam.
    Note
    Number of clips on newer vehicles may vary.
  41. Remove the headliner harness clips (x2) on the RH lower A-pillar.
  42. Remove the steering column control module (SCCM) wiring harness clip from the cross car beam.
  43. Remove the nuts (x4) that attach the steering column to the cross car beam.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
    • 3 in extension
    • 13 mm socket
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
  44. Lower the steering column and move it aside.
  45. Remove the clips (x2) that attach the LH and RH front main carpets to vehicle.
    Note
    Number of clips on new vehicles may vary.
  46. Remove the screws (x4) and datums (x4) that secure the LH and RH center display brackets.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 4 in extension
    • Torx T20 socket
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  47. Remove the bolts (x2) that attach the LH and RH cross car beam brackets to the cross car beam, and the nuts (x2) that attach the LH and RH cross car beam brackets to the body.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 13 mm socket
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
  48. Remove the LH and RH cross car beam brackets from the vehicle.
  49. Remove the bolts (x4) that attach the cross car beam to the body.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
    • 15 mm socket
    • 3 in extension
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
  50. Remove the bolts (x5) that attach the cross car beam to the HVAC assembly.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
    • 10 mm socket
    • 3 in extension
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
  51. Loosen the bolt that attaches the cross car beam to the upper cowl.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13mm 12pt combination wrench
  52. Remove the bolts (x2) that attach the steering column to body.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
    • 3 in extension
    • 13 mm socket
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
  53. With assistance, remove the cross car beam from the vehicle.

Install

  1. With assistance, position the cross car beam to vehicle for installation.
    Note
    Use caution not to damage the interior.
  2. Install the bolts (x4) that attach the cross car beam to the body.
    29 Nm (21.4 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
    • 15 mm socket
    • 3 in extension
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
  3. Install the bolts (x2) that attach the steering column to body.
    25 Nm (18.4 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
    • 3 in extension
    • 13 mm socket
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
  4. Torque the bolt that attaches the cross car beam to the upper cowl.
    25 Nm (18.4 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Ratchet 3/8in Dr Stubby
    • 3/8in Dr. Open Crowfoot 13 mm Socket
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
  5. Install the bolts (x5) that attach the cross car beam to the HVAC assembly.
    5.5 Nm (4.1 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
    • 10 mm socket
    • 3 in extension
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
  6. Install the bolts (x2) that attach the LH and RH cross car beam brackets to the cross car beam, and the nuts (x2) that attach the LH and RH cross car beam brackets to the body.
    25 Nm (18.4 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 13 mm socket
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
  7. Install the screws (x4) and datums (x4) that secure the LH and RH center display brackets.
    2.3 Nm (1.7 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 4 in extension
    • Torx T20 socket
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  8. Install the clips (x2) that attach the LH and RH front main carpets to vehicle.
    Note
    Number of clips on new vehicles may vary.
  9. Position the steering column for installation.
  10. Install the nuts (x4) that attach the steering column to the cross car beam.
    15 Nm (11.1 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
    • 3 in extension
    • 13 mm socket
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
  11. Install the steering column control module (SCCM) wiring harness clip to the cross car beam.
  12. Install the headliner harness clips (x2) on the RH lower A-pillar.
  13. Install the wiring harness fixing clips (x4) from the lower part of the dashboard cross car beam.
    Note
    Number of clips on newer vehicles may vary.
  14. Install the RH wiring harness to the instrument panel mounting brackets (x2).
  15. Install the clip that attaches the instrument panel harness connector to the cross car beam assembly.
  16. Connect the instrument panel harness connector to the electronic steering column.
  17. Install the UI harness fixing clip onto the cross car beam.
  18. Install the blower motor harness clips (x2) to the cross car beam.
  19. Install the instrument panel harness clips (x8) to the top of cross car beam.
    Note
    Number of clips on newer vehicles may vary.
  20. Install the harness clips (x2) to the instrument panel near the LH controller.
  21. Install the instrument panel harness connector to the lower part of the cross car beam assembly.
  22. Install the headliner harness clips (x2) on the LH lower A-pillar.
  23. Place the RH air wave duct to the proper position, and then install the push clips (x2) that attach the RH air wave duct to the instrument panel.
  24. Connect the thermistor connector to the RH instrument panel air wave duct.
  25. With assistance, secure the guide tabs (x4) and tabs (x2) to install the instrument panel carrier to the vehicle.
    Note
    Be careful not to press the speaker connector and the ambient light connector underneath the instrument panel.
  26. Install the LH and RH instrument panel speaker harnesses to the instrument panel.
    Note
    Gently pull out the instrument panel and lift it to facilitate installing the harnesses.
  27. Install the screws (x9) and the datums (x2) that attach the instrument panel carrier to the cross car beam.
    2.3 Nm (1.7 lbs-ft)
    Note
    There is 1 screw and 1 datum on the upper LH side, 1 screw on the LH side, 3 screws at the lower center, 3 screws on the RH side, and 1 screw and 1 datum on the upper RH side.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 2 in extension
    • Torx T20 socket
    • 4 in extension
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  28. Connect the instrument panel harness connectors (x2) to the instrument panel LH and RH side.
  29. Connect the passenger airbag connector.
    Note
    When the connector is fully seated, you will hear the click sound of the secondary locking pin returning.
  30. Install new patch bolts (x2) (1112138-00-C) that secure the instrument panel carrier to the cross car beam near the passenger airbag.
    8 Nm (5.9 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 6 in extension
    • ¼ in to 3/8 in adapter
    • External Torx E10
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  31. Install the center instrument panel speaker. See Speaker - Dash - Center (Remove and Replace).
  32. Install the LH and RH instrument panel speakers. See Speaker - Dash - LH (Remove and Replace).
  33. Install the instrument panel speaker grille. See Speaker Grille - Instrument Panel (Remove and Replace).
  34. Install the touchscreen. See Touchscreen - Front (Remove and Replace).
  35. Install the steering column control module (SCCM). See Module - Steering Column Control (Remove and Replace).
  36. Install the lower shroud from the steering column. See Shroud - Lower - Steering Column (Remove and Replace).
  37. Install the steering wheel. See Steering Wheel (Remove and Replace).
    Note
    Mark the steering wheel to steering column before removing the steering wheel.
  38. Install the driver airbag assembly. See Airbag - Driver (Remove and Replace).
  39. Install the center console assembly. See Center Console (Remove and Replace).
  40. Install the LH instrument panel air duct. See Duct - Air Wave - LH (RHD) (Remove and Replace).
  41. Install the passenger footwell duct. See Duct - Footwell - LH (RHD) (Remove and Replace).
  42. Install the glove box. See Glove Box (RHD) (Remove and Replace).
  43. Install the LH and RH upper A-pillar trims. See Trim - A-Pillar - Upper - LH (Remove and Replace).
  44. Install the passenger footwell cover. See Cover - Footwell - Front Passenger (RHD) (Remove and Replace).
  45. Install the instrument panel lower assembly. See Assembly - Instrument Panel - Lower (Remove and Replace).
  46. Install the LH and RH instrument panel end caps. See End Cap - Instrument Panel - LH (Remove and Replace).
  47. Install the driver footwell cover. See Cover - Footwell - Driver (RHD) (Remove and Replace).
  48. Install the wiper module. See Wiper Module (RHD) (Remove and Replace).
  49. Connect the LV battery power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
  50. Install the rear underhood apron. See Underhood Apron - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  51. Move the LH and RH front seats to original position.
  52. Raise both front windows and close both front doors.
