License Plate Bracket - Front (Europe and Asia Pacific excl. China) (Model 3 Standard) (Installation)

Correction code 12070101426 FRT 0.06 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Note
This procedure applies to the countries/regions below:
  • EMEA
  • Hong Kong
  • Macau
  • South Korea
  • Malaysia
  • United Arab Emirates
  • Israel
  • Qatar
  • Singapore
Parent topic: 1207 - License Plate Mountings

Procedure

  1. Clean the front center surface of the front fascia with isopropyl alcohol (IPA) wipe, and then allow surface to dry before continuing.
  2. Position the license plate bracket onto the front fascia assembly, and align the center hole of the bracket with the front fascia camera and the center vertical line of the grille.
    Note
    Make sure that the license plate bracket is properly installed and no gap exists between the bracket and the front fascia assembly.
  3. Install the self tapping screws (x2) that attach the front license plate bracket to the front fascia.
    0.6 Nm (5 lbs-in)
    CAUTION
    Do not over torque the screws to prevent damage to the front fascia.
