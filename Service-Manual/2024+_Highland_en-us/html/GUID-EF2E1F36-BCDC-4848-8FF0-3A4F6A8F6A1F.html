Chiller (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1840010072 FRT 1.20 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Note
This procedure requires the A/C Refrigerant (Recovery and Recharge) procedure to be performed. If not already automatically added to the Service Visit, add correction code 18200102 as a separate correction within the same activity to the Service Visit.
  • 2026-11-12: Added steps to completely remove the A/C lines to avoid excessive bending.

Remove

  1. Raise and support the vehicle. See Raise Vehicle - 2 Post Lift.
  2. Remove the underhood storage unit. See Underhood Storage Unit (Remove and Replace).
  3. Enable Service Mode via vehicle touchscreen. See Service Mode.
  4. Unlock the vehicle gateway. See Gateway Unlock.
  5. On the touchscreen, touch Service Mode > Thermal > Refrigerant System > Start Thermal Fill Drain and select Run to start the "Start Thermal Fill Drain" routine.
  6. Recover the A/C refrigerant. See A/C Refrigerant (Recovery and Recharge) (Restore).
  7. On the vehicle touchscreen, tap Thermal > Refrigerant System and make sure that the refrigerant system pressure is at atmospheric level (approx. 1 bar).
  8. Disconnect the LV battery power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
  9. Remove A/C machine hoses from the vehicle.
  10. Carefully depress the Schrader valves (x2) to level out any pressure difference between the A/C system pressure and the actual ambient atmospheric pressure.
    CAUTION
    Make sure to depress both Schrader valves. Failure to do so may result in injury.
  11. Remove the front aero shield panel. See Panel - Aero Shield - Front (Remove and Replace).
  12. Position a fluid catcher underneath the front of the vehicle.
  13. Remove the coolant bottle cap. See Cap - Coolant Reservoir (Remove and Replace).
  14. Disconnect the powertrain bypass hose from the Supermanifold.
    Note
    Plug the hose and supermanifold as needed.
  15. Disconnect the radiator outlet hose from the Supermanifold.
    Note
    Plug the hose and supermanifold as needed.
  16. Disconnect the radiator inlet hose from the Supermanifold
    Note
    Plug the hose and supermanifold as needed.
  17. Remove the nut that attaches the low pressure fitting to the compressor, then move the line aside for clearance.
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm deep socket
  18. Remove the nut that attaches the high pressure fitting to the compressor, then move the line aside for clearance.
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm deep socket
  19. Remove the bolt that attaches the supermanifold-to-compressor A/C line to the supermanifold.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm socket
  20. Remove the supermanifold-to-compressor A/C line from the supermanifold, and then set it aside.
    Note
    Carefully position the line so that oil does not drip out.
  21. Release the locking tab, and then disconnect the electrical connector from the chiller EXV (expansion valve).
    CAUTION
    DO NOT push down on the red locking tab. Pull the tab away from the connector until the connector is unlocked, and then continue pulling the main body of the connector to fully disconnect it.
  22. Remove the chiller EXV coil.
    Note
    Release the locking tab at bottom of the coil and then remove it towards front of the vehicle.
  23. Remove the bolts (x4) that attach the chiller to Supermanifold.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 8 mm socket
    Warning
    The video(s) included in this procedure are meant as an overview for supplemental purposes only. Follow all of the steps listed in the procedure to avoid damage to components and/or personal injury.
  24. Slightly release the RH side of chiller from Supermanifold and allow excess coolant to drain.
    Note
    Leave the AC ports intact to Supermanifold while coolant drains to avoid cross contamination.
  25. Remove chiller from Supermanifold.
  26. Remove and discard the chiller gasket from Supermanifold.

Install

  1. Install new chiller gasket into Supermanifold.
    Warning
    The video(s) included in this procedure are meant as an overview for supplemental purposes only. Follow all of the steps listed in the procedure to avoid damage to components and/or personal injury.
  2. Install the chiller to Supermanifold.
    Note
    Lubricate the O-rings on the chiller with the appropriate A/C oil. See A/C Refrigerant (Recovery and Recharge) (Restore) for A/C oil specifications. Then hand tighten the bolts that attach the chiller to Supermanifold.
  3. Torque the bolts (x4) that attach the chiller to Supermanifold in a cross-pattern.
    8 Nm (5.9 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 8 mm socket
  4. Install the chiller EXV coil.
    Note
    Align the coil locking tab to Supermanifold when installing.
  5. Connect the electrical connector on the chiller EXV.
    CAUTION
    Push the red locking tab towards the connector to engage the locking mechanism. DO NOT push down or pull up on the red locking tab.
  6. Install new seal washers on the supermanifold side of the supermanifold-to-compressor A/C line, and then lubricate the washers with a small amount of the appropriate A/C oil. See A/C Refrigerant (Recovery and Recharge) (Restore) for A/C oil specifications.
  7. Replace the seal washer on the high side pipe, lubricating the new seal washer with ND-11 oil prior to installation.
  8. Replace the seal washer on the low side pipe, lubricating the new seal washer with ND-11 oil prior to installation.
  9. Install the nut that attaches the high pressure fitting to the compressor.
    22 Nm (16.2 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm socket
  10. Install the nut that attaches the low pressure fitting to the compressor.
    22 Nm (16.2 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm socket
  11. Position the supermanifold-to-compressor A/C line on the supermanifold, and then hand tighten the bolt that attaches the A/C line to the supermanifold.
  12. Tighten the bolt that attaches the A/C line to the supermanifold.
    22 Nm (16.2 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 8 mm socket
  13. Install the radiator inlet hose to Supermanifold.
    Note
    Remove plugs from both ends of coolant connections before installation. Perform a push-pull-push test to make sure the hose is fully seated.
  14. Install the radiator outlet hose to Supermanifold.
    Note
    Remove plugs from both ends of coolant connections before installation. Perform a push-pull-push test to make sure the hose is fully seated.
  15. Install the powertrain bypass hose to Supermanifold.
    Note
    Remove plugs from both ends of coolant connections before installation. Perform a push-pull-push test to make sure the hose is fully seated.
  16. Remove the fluid catcher from underneath the vehicle.
  17. Install the front aero shield panel. See Panel - Aero Shield - Front (Remove and Replace).
  18. Connect the LV battery power. See LV Power (Disconnect and Connect) (Modify).
  19. Perform the vacuum leak test and oil injection. See Vacuum Leak Test and Oil Injection.
  20. Recharge the A/C refrigerant. See A/C Refrigerant (Recovery and Recharge) (Restore).
    CAUTION
    Recharge the refrigerant exactly as instructed. Failure to adhere to the instructions can cause catastrophic damage to the cooling system.
  21. Perform a cooling system vacuum refill. See Cooling System - Vacuum Refill (Test/Adjust).
  22. Unlock the vehicle gateway. See Gateway Unlock.
  23. Access the thermal system routines on the touchscreen: Touch Quick Controls > Service Mode > Diagnostics > Thermal.
  24. Touch Thermal > Refrigerant System > Stop Thermal Fill Drain and then allow the routine to complete.
  25. Touch Thermal > Coolant System > Coolant Purge Start > Run and then allow the routine to complete.
    Note
    Make sure the vehicle is not in Drive.
    Note
    The test will last for approximately 10 minutes. Monitor and keep the superbottle topped off while the test is running.
  26. Inspect the coolant level and top off as necessary.
    Note
    Ensure that the coolant is filled to the "Max" line.
  27. Install the coolant bottle cap.
  28. Perform the heat pump commissioning through UI: Touch Thermal > Sensors and Valves > Run Heat Pump Commissioning and then touch Run.
    Note
    Touch Close once the routine is passed.
  29. Perform the thermal performance test through UI: Touch Thermal > Actions > Test Thermal Performance and then touch Run.
    Note
    Touch Close once the routine is passed.
  30. Perform the HVAC performance test through UI: Touch Thermal > Actions > Test HVAC Performance and then touch Run.
    Note
    Touch Close once the routine is passed.
  31. Exit Service Mode through UI. See Service Mode.
  32. Install the underhood storage unit. See Underhood Storage Unit (Remove and Replace).
  33. Lower the rack arms fully and remove them from under the vehicle.
