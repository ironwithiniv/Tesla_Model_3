Tesla Badge - Rear (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1205010012 FRT 0.24 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2023-12-07: Updated the procedure according to the latest validation.
  • 2024-08-29: Added a note to remind that the rear badge is not reusable due to the positioning problem.
Equipment:
  • 1453028-00-A Badge Removal Kit
  • 1465974-00-A Pressure Roller
  • 1944666-00-A REAR TESLA LETTER BADGE JIG, HIGHLAND
Note
The badge is not reusable due to the required positioning accuracy. If the badge is removed for any reason, it must be replaced with a new one.
Parent topic: 1205 - Badges and Films

Remove

  1. Prepare the badge removal tool (1453028-00-A) with approximately 24 inches/60 centimeters of monofilament line.
  2. Tie slip knots at both ends of the fishing line and attach them each to the center groove of a handle.
  3. Move the badge removal tool in a sawing motion to separate the rear Tesla badge from the trunk lid.
  4. Use IPA wipes to clean all adhesive residue and debris from the trunk lid surface.

Install

  1. Install the rear Tesla badge alignment tool (1944666-00-A) into position on the trunk lid and tape the tool in place.
    Note
    Ensure that the tool is aligned with the left edge of the rear camera.
  2. Peel the adhesive backing from the trunk badge, align the holes on the badge protective film to the locating pins of the alignment tool, and then press the badge to the trunk.
  3. Use a pressure roller (1465974-00-A) to fully adhere the badge.
    CAUTION
    Be careful not to damage the badge during the rolling process.
  4. Remove the protective film from the badge, and then remove the alignment tool from the trunk lid.
  5. Use an IPA wipe to clean the badge and the area around the badge.
