Steering Rack (Dual Motor) (RHD) (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 3201011012 FRT 1.68 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See Ergonomic Precautions for safe and healthy working practices.

Equipment:
  • 1090880-00-A Steering Wheel Holder **Std. w/ Hunter Alignment Equip.**
  • 1065131-00-A Kit, Battery Coolant Drain
  • 1044206-00-A Wheel Trim Removal Tool
  • 1049463-00-A Steering Wheel Level

Remove

  1. Inspect the tires for abnormal wear.
    Note
    Note any abnormal tire wear that could indicate the need for an alignment.
  2. Raise and support the vehicle. See Raise Vehicle - 2 Post Lift.
  3. Remove the wheel center caps (x2) of both front wheels.
  4. Loosen the LH and RH front wheel lug nuts.
    CAUTION
    Use only hand tools to break the lug nuts loose. Power tools should only be used to remove the lug nuts after they have been loosened.
    CAUTION
    Use a 6 point socket. Do not use a 12 point socket or a specialty socket.
  5. Unlock the vehicle gateway. See Gateway Unlock.
  6. On the touchscreen, touch Thermal > Coolant System > Coolant Fill/Drain and select Run to start the "Start Thermal Fluid Fill/Drain (Coolant Only)" routine.
    Note
    If the routine takes more than 5 hours, perform the routine again.
  7. Set the steering wheel straight ahead.
  8. Using the steering wheel holder, lock the steering wheel into position.
  9. Remove the underhood storage unit. See Underhood Storage Unit (Remove and Replace).
  10. Remove the front wheels. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
  11. Remove the front fascia. See Fascia - Front (Remove and Replace).
  12. Remove the cooling fan module. See Cooling Fan Module (Remove and Replace).
  13. Lower the vehicle to a comfortable working height and set the lift onto locks.
  14. Disconnect the connector and the clip that attach the front drive unit front subframe harness to the vehicle.
  15. Release the clip that secures the radiator outlet hose to the vehicle.
  16. Disconnect the steering gear assembly connectors (x4).
  17. Remove the bolt that attaches the steering gear assembly to the electric steering column.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm socket
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  18. Slide the electric steering column upward to remove it from the steering gear assembly.
  19. Loosen the jamb nut to the RH outer tie rod end.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Combination wrench 12pt 21mm
  20. Remove and discard the nut that attaches the RH front tie rod end to the RH front suspension knuckle, and then remove the tie rod end from the knuckle.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless 1/2in Dr Impact
    • 22 mm deep socket
    • 1/2 in to 3/4 in adapter - 1717365-00-A
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  21. Rotate the RH tie rod end counter-clockwise to remove it from the steering gear assembly.
    Note
    Record the number of turns during removal.
  22. Repeat step 19 and step 20 on the RH side of the vehicle.
  23. Hold the LH tie rod end and pull it outward fully.
  24. Rotate the LH tie rod end counter-clockwise to remove it from the steering gear assembly.
    Note
    Record the number of turns during removal.
  25. Remove the outer bolts (x2) that attach the steering gear assembly to the front subframe.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
    • 11 in extension
    • 15 mm socket
  26. Remove the inner bolts (x2) that attach the steering gear assembly to the front subframe.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
    • 6 in extension
    • 18 mm socket
    • 3 in extension
  27. With assistance, slide the steering rack assembly fully towards the LH side of the vehicle, and then remove the rack through the underhood storage unit opening.
    CAUTION
    Be careful around the AC lines and the superbottle.
    Note
    Lift the RH inner tie rod up first, and then maneuver the rest of the rack outward, avoiding the AC lines and surrounding components.

Install

  1. Loosen the jamb nuts to the RH and the LH tie rod ends on the new steering gear assembly.
    80 Nm (59.0 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Combination wrench 12pt 21mm
  2. Rotate the tie rod counter clockwise to remove the LH and the RH outer tie rod ends from the new steering gear assembly.
  3. With assistance, fully disperse the pressure of the steering rack to the LH side.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Combination 12pt 15mm wrench
  4. Position the LH side of the steering gear assembly into the underhood storage area, and then slide the RH side into the vehicle. Position the steering rack so that the bolt holes are aligned.
  5. Loosely install the inner bolts (x2) that attach the steering gear assembly to the front subframe.
  6. Install the outer bolts (x2) that attach the steering gear assembly to the front subframe.
    47 Nm (34.7 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
    • 11 in extension
    • 15 mm socket
  7. Tighten the inner bolts (x2) that attach the steering gear assembly to the front subframe.
    75 Nm (55.3 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
    • 6 in extension
    • 18 mm socket
    • 3 in extension
  8. Decompress the LH tie rod end from the steering gear, and then verify both LH and RH tie rod ends are as evenly distributed as possible.
    Note
    Turn the shaft counterclockwise using open end wrench.
  9. Rotate the RH tie rod end clockwise with the exact number of turns noted during removal to install it to the steering gear assembly.
  10. Rotate the LH tie rod end clockwise with the exact number of turns noted during removal to install it to the steering gear assembly.
  11. Install the new nuts (x2) that attach the LH and the RH front tie rod ends to the LH and the RH front suspension knuckles to install the both tie rod ends to the knuckles.
    180 Nm (132.7 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless 1/2in Dr Impact
    • 22 mm deep socket
    • 1/2 in to 3/4 in adapter - 1717365-00-A
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  12. Tighten the jamb nuts (x2) to the LH and the RH outer tie rod ends.
    80 Nm (59.0 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Combination wrench 12pt 21mm
  13. Slide the electric steering column downward to install it to the steering gear assembly.
  14. Install the bolt that attaches the steering gear assembly to the electric steering column.
    18 Nm (13.3 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm socket
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  15. Connect the steering gear assembly connectors (x4).
  16. Install the clip that secures the radiator outlet hose to the vehicle.
  17. Install the clip and the connector that attach the front drive unit front subframe harness to the vehicle.
  18. Raise the vehicle fully and lower the lift onto locks.
    CAUTION

    Make sure there is an audible click of the locks on both sides before lowering, otherwise the vehicle may tilt to one side.

    Make sure that the doors are clear of surrounding objects.

  19. Install the cooling fan module. See Cooling Fan Module (Remove and Replace).
  20. Install the front fascia. See Fascia - Front (Remove and Replace).
  21. Install the front wheels. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
  22. Reinstall the vehicle firmware. See Software Reinstall - Touchscreen.
  23. Remove the steering wheel holder.
  24. Raise the LH front window and close the LH front door.
  25. Remove the lift arms from below the vehicle.
  26. Perform the four-wheel alignment check. See Four Wheel Alignment Check (No Adjustment Needed) (Test/Adjust).
