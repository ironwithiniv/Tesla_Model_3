Approved Fasteners and Fastener Tools for Structural Repairs

Describes the fasteners and fastener tools approved for use in structural repairs on Tesla vehicles.

The following fasteners are approved for structural repairs on Tesla vehicles:
  • Standard Rivets
  • Countersunk Rivets
  • Structural Bulb Rivet
  • Structural Flange Rivet
  • Self-Piercing Rivets
  • Flow Form Rivets
  • BOMs and 8 mm Structural Rivets
  • NeoBolts, AVBolts, and Huck Bobtail Fasteners
  • Pin and Collars
  • Bolts and Nuts
  • Rivet Nuts and Rivet Studs
Warning
Use only approved fasteners when performing structural repairs on Tesla vehicles. Using non-approved fasteners might compromise the integrity of the repair and vehicle safety. Using non-approved fasteners might also affect whether Tesla supports the vehicle.

Refer to Approved Fastener Installation Tools for information on the tools used to install fasteners.

Structural Bulb Rivet

Structural bulb rivets are used where clearance with trim is an issue or when replacing a short rivet, as the back portion of the bulb rivet is smaller than a regular structural rivet. Structural bulb rivets can typically be used in thin sheet metal applications where the total thickness of the panels is 4.5 mm or less.

NeoBolts, AVBolts, and Huck Bobtail Fasteners

NeoBolts, Huck Bobtail fasteners, and AV Bolt Rivets are available in 3/4 and 5/8 in diameter.
Note
Instead of NeoBolts, a Huck Bobtail fastener may be used. In any repair procedure, do not mix NeoBolts and Huck Bobtail fasteners.
Warning
Do not use NeoBolt collars with Huck Bobtail fasteners and do not use Huck Bobtail collars with NeoBolt fasteners.
