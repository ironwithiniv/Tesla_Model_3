Resistance Meter (Zero Adjust)

Correction code 16300100 0.05 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See for safe and healthy working practices.

Equipment:
  • 1076927-00-A Hioki RM 3548
  • 1076928-00-A Test Probes - Hioki 9461
  • 2172013-00-A Zero Adjustment Board
CAUTION
Never use the resistance meter to measure voltage.

Zero Adjust With Zero Adjustment Board

  1. Power on the Hioki resistance meter, and allow a few minutes for it to thermally stabilize.
  2. Plug the Hioki 9461 test probe leads into the resistance meter.
    Note
    Both the positive (red) and negative (black) lead connectors have arrows or marks that align to arrows on the resistance meter.
  3. Connect the temperature sensor to the back of the resistance meter.
  4. Power on the Hioki resistance meter, and allow a few minutes for it to thermally stabilize.
  5. Use the up arrow and down arrow buttons to set the resistance meter to the 3mΩ range.
  6. Press and hold the 0ADJ button to start the zero adjust operation.
    Note
    The text "0 ADJ" blinks in the upper right corner of the resistance meter's display.
  7. Touch the probes to the indentations next to the center fastener on the zero adjustment board.
    Tip
    Hold the probes vertically for the most accurate reading.
  8. Verify that a resistance is displayed.
    Note
    If " -.- - - - " (no value) is displayed, check the lead connections to the resistance meter and the how the probe tips contact the zero adjustment board.
  9. Approximately 2 seconds later, the resistance meter beeps and "0 ADJ" is now constant.
    Note
    If "FAiL" is displayed, check the probe tips, the leads, and the connection to the resistance meter, and repeat from step 6.
  10. Remove the probes from the zero adjustment board.
    CAUTION
    Do not change the range. Otherwise, the resistance meter must be adjusted again.
  11. Disconnect the temperature sensor from the resistance meter.
  12. The resistance meter is now ready to measure resistance. Return to the procedure that required the zero adjust to be performed.
