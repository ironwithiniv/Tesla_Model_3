Hinge - Door - Front - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 11336602 0.24 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See for safe and healthy working practices.

Equipment:
  • 1588903-00-A Body Gap and Flush Tool
  • 2026-09-16: Updated the torque value of the bolts attaching the lower front door hinge to the body to 23 Nm.

Remove

  1. Open the LH front door.
  2. Lower the LH front window.
  3. Use a jack to support the LH front door from the bottom.
    Note
    Make sure to use soft material to protect the door paint.
  4. Apply masking tape to the area surrounding the LH front door lower hinge on the body and door to aid in alignment during installation.
  5. Remove the bolts (x2) that attach the LH front lower door hinge to the door.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm combination wrench
  6. Remove the bolts (x2) that attach the LH front lower door hinge to the body.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm combination wrench
  7. Rotate the LH front lower hinge and pull it out to remove it.

Install

  1. Install the new LH front lower hinge.
  2. Hand-tighten the bolts (x2) that attach the LH front lower door hinge to the body.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm combination wrench
  3. Hand-tighten the bolts (x2) that attach the LH front lower door hinge to the door.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm combination wrench
  4. Close the LH front door and check for gap and flushness. Adjust as necessary.
  5. Tighten the bolts (x2) that attach the LH front lower door hinge to the body.
    23 Nm (17.0 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
    • 13 mm socket
  6. Tighten the bolts (x2) that attach the LH front lower door hinge to the door.
    27 Nm (19.9 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
    • 10 mm socket
  7. Remove the masking tape that surrounds the LH front door lower hinge on the body and door.
  8. Remove the support from the LH front door.
  9. Use IPA wipes to clean the lower hinge bolts and wait at least 1 minute to allow it to completely dry.
  10. Apply touch up paint to the LH front lower door hinge bolts as needed.
    Note
    Make sure to shake touch up paint before applying, refer to the label for dry time instructions.
  11. Raise the LH front window.
  12. Close the LH front door.
