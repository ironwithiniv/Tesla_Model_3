Panel - Aero Shield - Front (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 12030102 0.18 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-05-27: Updated the torque values of the nuts (x2) securing the front aero shield from 5.5 Nm to 8 Nm.

Torque Specifications

Table 1. Torque Specifications: Panel - Aero Shield - Front (Remove and Replace)
Description Torque Value Recommended Tools Reuse/Replace Notes
Aero shield panel to body nuts
8 Nm (5.9 lbs-ft)
  • 15 mm socket
 Reuse
Aero shield panel to body bolts
5 Nm (3.7 lbs-ft)
  • 10 mm socket
 Reuse

Remove

  1. Raise and support the vehicle. See Raise Vehicle - 2 Post Lift.
  2. Remove the bolts (x9) that attach the front aero shield panel to the vehicle.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  3. Remove the nuts (x2) that attach the front aero shield panel to the vehicle, and then remove the panel from the vehicle
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 15 mm socket

Replace

  1. Apply a thin bead of Loctite 222 to the threads of the bolts (x9) that attach the front aero shield panel to the vehicle.
  2. Install the front aero shield panel to the underside of the vehicle, and then hand-tighten the nuts (x2) to temporarily hold the panel in place.
  3. Hand-tighten the bolts (x9) that attach the front aero shield panel to the vehicle.
  4. Torque the nuts (x2) and bolts (x9) to the proper specification.
    8 Nm (5.9 lbs-ft)
    5 Nm (3.7 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 15 mm socket and 10 mm socket
    • ¼ in std ratchet
    • ¼ in torque wrench (installation only)
    • 4 in extension
  5. Lower the vehicle. See Raise Vehicle - 2 Post Lift.
