Rear Spring and Subframe Mount

Correction code: 10101814902 10101814802 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Rear End Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • Properly mount the vehicle on a frame bench when performing this procedure.

Parts List

Quantity Description Image / Notes
1 M3 Asy-Rr Spring & SubFr Mt (Rear Spring and Subframe Mount)
51 High Strength Structural Rivet, 6.5 mm

When ordering parts, refer to the Parts Catalog and enter the VIN of the vehicle being repaired to find the correct parts (and the part numbers) for the vehicle. Alternatively, use the search function in the Parts Catalog to find a specific part for the vehicle.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Trim - Floor - Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the Trim - Sill - Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Seat Back - 2nd Row - LH (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Seat Back - 2nd Row - RH (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the Carpet - Rear Trunk (1-Piece) (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the Garnish - Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the Taillight - LH (Remove and Install).
  8. Remove the Parking Brake - Caliper - Rear - LH (Release).
  9. Remove the Diffuser - Rear Fascia (Remove and Replace).
  10. Remove the Stabilizer Bar - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  11. Remove the Skid Plate - HV Battery - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  12. Remove the Subframe Assembly - Rear (Remove and Install).
  13. Remove the Cooling System (Check and Fill).
  14. Remove the Trunk Floor Assembly (Complete).
  15. Remove the Rear Wheel House Rear Stiffener.
  16. Cut the Wheelhouse Inner as shown and bend back the cut section to expose the spot welds underneath.
    Cut Line
    • A = 95 mm
  17. Remove the original component.

    Bolt

    Factory SPR

    Factory Spot Weld

    Factory Spot Weld Area

  18. Prepare for installation.

    Bolt

    High Strength Structural Rivet, 6.5 mm

  19. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
    Note
    Apply adhesive to the mating surface of the cut section of the Wheelhouse Inner.
  20. Install the new component or components.
    Note
    Bend the cut section of the Wheelhouse Inner back into position before installing the bolts in that area.
    Note
    Torque the bolts to 24 Nm.
  21. Install the Rear Wheel House Rear Stiffener.
  22. Install the Trunk Floor Assembly (Complete).
  23. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  24. Install the Cooling System (Check and Fill).
  25. Install the Subframe Assembly - Rear (Remove and Install).
  26. Install the Skid Plate - HV Battery - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  27. Install the Stabilizer Bar - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  28. Install the Diffuser - Rear Fascia (Remove and Replace).
  29. Install the Parking Brake - Caliper - Rear - LH (Release).
  30. Install the Taillight - LH (Remove and Install).
  31. Install the Garnish - Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  32. Install the Carpet - Rear Trunk (1-Piece) (Remove and Replace).
  33. Install the Seat Back - 2nd Row - RH (Remove and Replace).
  34. Install the Seat Back - 2nd Row - LH (Remove and Replace).
  35. Install the Trim - Sill - Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  36. Install the Trim - Floor - Trunk (Remove and Replace).
