Parcel Shelf Extension (Front)

Correction code: 10101900702 10100103502 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Rear Quarter Inner Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • This procedure can be completed without using a frame bench.

Parts List

When ordering parts, refer to the Parts Catalog and enter the VIN of the vehicle being repaired to find the correct parts (and the part numbers) for the vehicle. Alternatively, use the search function in the Parts Catalog to find a specific part for the vehicle.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Door Assembly - Charge Port (EMEA) (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the Busbars - Charge Port to HV Battery (CCS) (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Airbag - Curtain - LH (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Seatbelt - 2nd Row - LH (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the Carpet - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the Cover - Rocker Panel - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the Wheel Arch Liner - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the Trim - Sill Panel - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
  9. Remove the Carpet - Rear Trunk (1-Piece) (Remove and Replace).
  10. Remove the Trim - Side - Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace).
  11. Remove the Glass - Body - Rear Quarter - LH (Remove and Install).
  12. Remove the Glass - Backlight (Heat Pump) (Remove and Install).
  13. Remove the Striker - Door - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
  14. Remove the Door - Rear - LH (Remove and Install).
  15. Remove the Trunk (Remove and Install).
  16. Remove the Bracket - Rear - Center (Remove and Replace).
  17. Remove the Rear Quarter Inner (Panel).
  18. Remove the Parcel Shelf Rear Extension.
  19. Remove the original component.
    Note
    In the following images, the Cantrail Assembly has been removed to enhance clarity. There is no need to remove the Cantrail Assembly when performing this procedure.

    Factory Spot Weld

    or Drill through factory spot welds
    Note
    When drilling out spot welds, use a drill bit that creates a hole correctly sized for the fastener that will replace the spot weld.
  20. Prepare for installation.

    Structural Bulb Rivet, 6.5 mm

    Countersunk Rivet, 4.8 mm Short

    Bolt, Torx-head and Nut

    Flow Form Rivet S18

  21. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  22. Install the new component or components.

    Bolt, Torx-head torque to 16 Nm.

  23. Install the Rear Quarter Inner (Panel).
  24. Install the Parcel Shelf Rear Extension.
  25. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  26. Install the Bracket - Rear - Center (Remove and Replace).
  27. Install the Trunk (Remove and Install).
  28. Install the Door - Rear - LH (Remove and Install).
  29. Install the Striker - Door - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
  30. Install the Glass - Backlight (Heat Pump) (Remove and Install).
  31. Install the Glass - Body - Rear Quarter - LH (Remove and Install).
  32. Install the Trim - Side - Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace).
  33. Install the Carpet - Rear Trunk (1-Piece) (Remove and Replace).
  34. Install the Trim - Sill Panel - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
  35. Install the Wheel Arch Liner - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
  36. Install the Cover - Rocker Panel - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  37. Install the Carpet - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  38. Install the Seatbelt - 2nd Row - LH (Remove and Replace).
  39. Install the Airbag - Curtain - LH (Remove and Replace).
  40. Install the Busbars - Charge Port to HV Battery (CCS) (Remove and Replace).
  41. Install the Door Assembly - Charge Port (EMEA) (Remove and Replace).
