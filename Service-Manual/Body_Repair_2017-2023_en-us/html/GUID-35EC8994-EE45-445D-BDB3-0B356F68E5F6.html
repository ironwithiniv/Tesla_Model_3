Hinge Pillar (Complete)

Correction code: RH: 10100102602 LH: 10100103002 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Body Side Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • Properly mount the vehicle on a frame bench when performing this procedure.

Parts List

Quantity Description Image / Notes
1 M3 ASY, HINGE PILLAR (Hinge Pillar Assembly)

When ordering parts, refer to the Parts Catalog and enter the VIN of the vehicle being repaired to find the correct parts (and the part numbers) for the vehicle. Alternatively, use the search function in the Parts Catalog to find a specific part for the vehicle.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Seal - Body - Side - Front - Primary - LH (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the Cover - Rocker Panel - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Hood (Remove and Install).
  4. Remove the Fender Assembly - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
  5. Remove the Windshield (Vehicles with 2nd Generation or Later Autopilot) (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the Wheel Arch Liner - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the Airbag - Curtain - LH (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the IP Carrier (Remove and Install).
  9. Remove the Door - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
  10. Remove the Module - Body Controller - LH (LHD) (Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace).
  11. Remove the A-Pillar Outer Reinforcement (Section).
  12. Remove the original component.

    or Factory Spot Welds

  13. Prepare for installation.
    Note
    For each rivet being installed through the Sill Outer, drill a 6.7 mm hole through both the Sill Outer and the top part of the Sill Insert. These holes provide clearance for the rivets used in the Sill Outer section.
    or High Strength Structural Rivets, 6.5 mm
    Note
    The center of all rivets in the bottom row of structural rivets must be at the indicated height from the lower edge of the new component, as shown below.
    • A = 15 mm

    or Structural Bulb Rivets, 6.5 mm

    or Structural Countersunk Rivets, 6.5 mm

    or Installation Spot Welds

  14. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  15. Install the new component or components.
  16. Perform resistance spot welding.

    or Installation Spot Welds

    Warning
    Failure to follow all welding safety precautions, including the use of personal protective equipment, could result in serious injury or property damage. Only technicians who have completed Tesla’s approved welding training are authorized to weld structural components on Tesla vehicles.
    CAUTION
    Do not weld on a Tesla vehicle before performing the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure (refer to the vehicle-specific Service Manual for more information on the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure). Welding on a Tesla vehicle with an energized high or low voltage system might damage vehicle components.
  17. Install the new A-Pillar Outer Reinforcement (Section).
  18. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  19. Install the Module - Body Controller - LH (LHD) (Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace).
  20. Install the Door - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
  21. Install the IP Carrier (Remove and Install).
  22. Install the Airbag - Curtain - LH (Remove and Replace).
  23. Install the Wheel Arch Liner - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  24. Install the Windshield (Vehicles with 2nd Generation or Later Autopilot) (Remove and Replace).
  25. Install the Fender Assembly - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
  26. Install the Hood (Remove and Install).
  27. Install the Cover - Rocker Panel - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  28. Install the Seal - Body - Side - Front - Primary - LH (Remove and Replace).
