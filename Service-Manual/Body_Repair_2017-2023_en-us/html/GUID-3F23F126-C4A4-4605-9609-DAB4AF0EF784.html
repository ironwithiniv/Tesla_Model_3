Phone Tray - Center Console (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 15196402 0.12 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See for safe and healthy working practices.

Torque Specifications

Table 1. Torque Specifications: Phone Tray - Center Console (Remove and Replace)
Description Torque Value Recommended Tools Reuse/Replace Notes
Screws that attach the center console phone tray to the center console
1 Nm (.7 lbs-ft)
  • Torx T20 socket
 Reuse

Remove

  1. Open both the forward and rearward center console rotating lids.
  2. Remove and secure the customer's belongings from the phone tray, if needed.
  3. Remove the phone dock mat from the center console.
  4. If any cables are installed into the center console phone tray routing, raise the phone tray, slide the phone tray bar to the left, and then remove the bar from the phone tray.
  5. Remove any cables from the center console phone tray routing, if installed, and then move the cables through the opening in the center console phone tray.
  6. Raise the center console phone tray to access the screws.
  7. Remove the screws that attach the center console phone tray to the center console.
    1 Nm (.7 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T20 socket
  8. Release the clips (x5) that attach the center console phone tray to the center console, and remove the tray from the vehicle.
