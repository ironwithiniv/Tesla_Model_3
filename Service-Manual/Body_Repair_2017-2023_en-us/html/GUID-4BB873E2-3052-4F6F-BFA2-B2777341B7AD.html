A-Pillar Outer Reinforcement (Complete)

Correction code: RH: 10100102202 LH: 10100102802 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Body Side Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • This procedure can be completed without using a frame bench.

Parts List

Quantity Description Image / Notes
1 M3 ASY, A PILLAR OUTER (A-Pillar Outer Reinforcement)
5 Structural Countersunk Rivet, 6.5 mm
6 High Strength Structural Rivet, 6.5 mm

When ordering parts, refer to the Parts Catalog and enter the VIN of the vehicle being repaired to find the correct parts (and the part numbers) for the vehicle. Alternatively, use the search function in the Parts Catalog to find a specific part for the vehicle.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Reservoir - Windshield Washer (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the Airbag - Curtain - LH (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Seat Assembly - 1st Row - LH (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Beam - Cross Car (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the Seatbelt - 2nd Row - LH (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the Retractor - Seatbelt - 1st Row - LH (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the necessary portion of the Body Side Outer Assembly to expose the underlying component being replaced.
  8. Remove the Shotgun Outer.
  9. Remove the Front Header Upper.
  10. Remove the Rear Header Upper.
  11. Remove the B-Pillar Outer.
  12. Remove the Roof Rail Extension.
  13. Remove the original component.
    Factory Spot Weld
    Factory Spot Weld Area
  14. Prepare for installation.
    Bolt

    Installation Spot Weld Area

    High Strength Structural Rivet, 6.5 mm

    Structural Countersunk Rivet, 6.5 mm

  15. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  16. Install the new component or components.
  17. Perform resistance spot welding.
    Warning
    Failure to follow all welding safety precautions, including the use of personal protective equipment, could result in serious injury or property damage. Only technicians who have completed Tesla’s approved welding training are authorized to weld structural components on Tesla vehicles.
    CAUTION
    Do not weld on a Tesla vehicle before performing the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure (refer to the vehicle-specific Service Manual for more information on the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure). Welding on a Tesla vehicle with an energized high or low voltage system might damage vehicle components.
  18. Replace the Roof Rail Extension.
  19. Replace the B-Pillar Outer.
  20. Replace the Front Header Upper.
  21. Replace the Rear Header Upper.
  22. Replace the Shotgun Outer.
  23. Install the portion of the Body Side Outer Assembly that was removed to expose the component being replaced.
  24. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  25. Install the Retractor - Seatbelt - 1st Row - LH (Remove and Replace).
  26. Install the Seatbelt - 2nd Row - LH (Remove and Replace).
  27. Install the Beam - Cross Car (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  28. Install the Seat Assembly - 1st Row - LH (Remove and Replace).
  29. Install the Airbag - Curtain - LH (Remove and Replace).
  30. Install the Reservoir - Windshield Washer (Remove and Replace).
