Correction code

Remove

  1. Open the liftgate.
  2. Release the LH rear passenger seat back, and then fold the seat back down.
  3. Remove the rear trunk load floor. See GUID-D937E149-8C0B-486F-A555-376066BE8144.html.
  4. Remove the front trunk load floor. See GUID-EFA5DE2D-2C91-48BC-94B0-8B596AB74B95.html.
  5. Remove the trunk sill trim. See Trim - Sill - Trunk (Remove and Replace)
  6. Pull upward on the LH load floor bracket to release the tabs (x3) and clips (x2) that attach the bracket to the vehicle, and then remove the bracket from the vehicle.
    Note
    The 7-seater bracket is attached to the vehicle with 2 clips.
    Note
    Confirm each installation with a push-pull test. The clip position should not be adjusted after the bracket is removed or it will result in the bracket not seating properly. If the clip position is changed or the push-pull action shows the bracket is not secure, remove the clip from the tab, and then seat the clip into the doghouse on the bracket and reinstall.

Install

Installation procedure is the reverse of removal.
