Module - Body Controller - LH (LHD) (Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 17152112 0.54 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2023-11-13: Fixed error in routine.

Remove

  1. Open all doors and lower all windows.
  2. Remove the rear underhood apron. See Underhood Apron - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  3. Move the driver seat fully rearward.
  4. Disconnect 12V power. See 12V/LV Power (Disconnect and Connect).
  5. Remove the LH instrument panel end cap. See End Cap - Instrument Panel - LH (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the LH middle A-pillar trim. See Trim - A-Pillar - Middle - LH (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the LH lower A-pillar trim. See Trim - A-Pillar - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the driver footwell cover. See Cover - Footwell - Driver (Remove and Replace).
  9. Remove the driver knee airbag. See Airbag - Knee - Driver (Remove and Replace).
  10. Remove the LH footwell duct. See Duct - Footwell - LH (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  11. Release the connector lock, and then disconnect the J7 LH front door electrical connector from the LH body controller module.
  12. Release the connector lock, and then disconnect the J9 LH front seat electrical connector from the LH body controller module.
  13. Release the connector lock, and then disconnect the J10 instrument panel electrical connector from the LH body controller module.
  14. Disconnect the J11 HVAC electrical connector from the LH body controller module.
  15. Disconnect the inline electrical harness connector X909 from the LH body harness, and then separate it from the LH body controller module.
  16. Release the connector lock, and then disconnect the J1 power connector from the LH body controller module.
  17. Disconnect the J6 front underhood storage connector from the LH body controller module.
  18. Disconnect the J5 body 3 electrical connector from the LH body controller module.
  19. Release the connector lock, and then disconnect the J4 body 2 connector from the LH body controller module.
  20. Disconnect the J2 body 1 electrical connector from the LH body controller module.
  21. Release the electrical harness clips that attach the LH body controller module to the vehicle.
  22. Move the LH body harness away from the LH body controller module.
  23. Remove and discard the nut that attaches the LH body controller module to the vehicle.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm 12-point deep socket
  24. Slide the LH body controller upwards to release the W-clip, and then move the LH body controller module out from underneath the instrument panel.
  25. Remove the LH body controller module from the vehicle.

Install

  1. Maneuver the LH body controller module into position under the IP carrier. Align the W-clip with the body cutout, and then slide down to attach the LH body controller module to the body.
  2. Install a new nut that attaches the LH body controller module onto the body.
    6 Nm (4.4 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm 12-point deep socket
  3. Move the LH body harness towards the left side of the LH body controller module, and then attach the harness clips to the LH body controller module.
  4. Connect the J2 body 1 electrical connector to the LH body controller module , and then engage the connector lock.
    Note
    Ensure the electrical connector is fully seated.
  5. Connect the J4 body 2 electrical connector to the LH body controller module, and then engage the connector lock.
    Note
    Ensure the electrical connector is fully seated.
  6. Connect the J5 body 3 electrical connector to the LH body controller module, and then engage the connector lock.
    Note
    Ensure the electrical connector is fully seated.
  7. Connect the J6 front underhood storage unit connector to the LH body controller module.
  8. Connect the J1 power connector to the LH body controller module, and then engage the connector lock.
    Note
    Ensure the electrical connector is fully seated.
  9. Connect the inline electrical connector X909 to the LH body harness, and then clip it to the LH body controller module.
  10. Connect the J11 HVAC electrical connector to the LH body controller module.
  11. Install the J10 instrument panel electrical connector to the LH body controller module, and then engage the connector lock.
    Note
    Ensure the electrical connector is fully seated.
  12. Connect the J9 LH front seat electrical connector to the LH body controller module, and then engage the connector lock.
    Note
    Ensure the electrical connector is fully seated.
  13. Connect the J7 LH front door electrical connector to the LH body controller module, and then engage the connector lock.
    Note
    Ensure the electrical connector is fully seated.
  14. Install the LH footwell duct. See Duct - Footwell - LH (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  15. Install the driver knee airbag. See Airbag - Knee - Driver (Remove and Replace).
  16. Install the driver footwell cover. See Cover - Footwell - Driver (Remove and Replace).
  17. Install the LH lower A-pillar trim. See Trim - A-Pillar - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  18. Install the LH middle A-pillar trim. See Trim - A-Pillar - Middle - LH (Remove and Replace).
  19. Install the LH instrument panel end cap. See End Cap - Instrument Panel - LH (Remove and Replace).
  20. Connect 12V power. See 12V/LV Power (Disconnect and Connect).
    CAUTION
    Do not power on the climate control system until this procedure has been fully completed.
  21. Install the rear underhood apron. See Underhood Apron - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  22. Perform a service CAN redeploy. See Software Reinstall - Toolbox.
  23. Sit in the front passenger seat and close the RH front door.
  24. Perform the following routine using Service Mode or Toolbox (see 0005 - Service Modes): PROC_CONTROLLER_LEFT_POST-REPLACEMENT-PROCEDUREvia Service Mode Plus:Left Controller ➜ Left Controller Post Replacement ➜ Left Controller Post Replacement Procedurevia Toolbox:(link)
  25. Move the front passenger seat to the original position.
  26. Raise the RH front window and close the RH front door.
  27. Move the driver seat to the original position.
  28. Close the LH front door.
  29. Inform the customer that their liftgate opening height settings have been reset and to refer to the owner's manual for instructions on setting the liftgate opening height.
