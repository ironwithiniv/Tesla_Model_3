Rear Subframe Mount

Correction code: 10101814202 10101814302 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Rear End Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • Properly mount the vehicle on a frame bench when performing this procedure.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the HV Battery (AWD) (Remove and Install).
  2. Remove the Subframe Assembly - Rear (Remove and Install).
  3. Remove the Panel - Aero Shield - Front (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Brake Lines - LH Sill (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the Brake Lines - Rear - RH to Sill (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the Carpet - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the Busbars - Charge Port to HV Battery (NACS) (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the Carpet - Rear Trunk (1-Piece) (Remove and Replace).
  9. Cut the floor panel at the indicated locations, and bend it back to create a flap (shown in red) large enough to allow removal of the spot weld (under the flap).

    Cut Line

    Reference Line/Point

    • A = 20 mm
  10. Remove the outer portion of the original component.
    or Factory Spot Welds
    or Drill through factory spot welds
    Note
    When drilling out spot welds, use a drill bit that creates a hole correctly sized for the fastener that will replace the spot weld.
  11. Remove the inner portion of the original component.
    or Factory Spot Welds
    or Drill through factory spot welds
    Note
    When drilling out spot welds, use a drill bit that creates a hole correctly sized for the fastener that will replace the spot weld.
  12. Prepare for installation.
    Note
    A red X indicates a location where a factory-installed fastener is not being replaced. Secure this location using structural adhesive only.

    or Installation Spot Welds

    or Structural Bulb Rivets, 6.5 mm
    • B = 6 mm
    Note
    Return the floor panel flap (shown in red) to its original location before installing the Structural Bulb rivet circled in red.
    or High Strength Structural Rivets, 6.5 mm
    • C = 7 mm
  13. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
    Note
    Apply a small amount of structural adhesive to the upper side of the floor panel flap created earlier.
  14. Bend the floor panel flap (shown in red) to its original location.
  15. Install the new component or components.
    Note
    Return the floor panel flap (shown in red) to its original location before installing the Structural Bulb rivet circled in red.
  16. Perform resistance spot welding.
    or Installation Spot Welds
    Warning
    Failure to follow all welding safety precautions, including the use of personal protective equipment, could result in serious injury or property damage. Only technicians who have completed Tesla’s approved welding training are authorized to weld structural components on Tesla vehicles.
    CAUTION
    Do not weld on a Tesla vehicle before performing the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure (refer to the vehicle-specific Service Manual for more information on the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure). Welding on a Tesla vehicle with an energized high or low voltage system might damage vehicle components.
  17. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  18. Install the Carpet - Rear Trunk (1-Piece) (Remove and Replace).
  19. Install the Busbars - Charge Port to HV Battery (NACS) (Remove and Replace).
  20. Install the Carpet - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  21. Install the Brake Lines - Rear - RH to Sill (Remove and Replace).
  22. Install the Brake Lines - LH Sill (Remove and Replace).
  23. Install the Panel - Aero Shield - Front (Remove and Replace).
  24. Install the Subframe Assembly - Rear (Remove and Install).
  25. Install the HV Battery (AWD) (Remove and Install).
