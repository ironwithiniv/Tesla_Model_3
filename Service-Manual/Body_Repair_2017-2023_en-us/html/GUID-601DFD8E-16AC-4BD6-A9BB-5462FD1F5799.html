Rear Frame Rail Glove (Lower)

Correction code: 10101813602 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Rear End Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • This procedure can be completed without using a frame bench.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Seal - Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the Air Extractor - LH (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Bracket - Taillight - LH (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Bumper Beam - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the Bracket - Rear - Center (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the Rear Body Panel.
  7. Remove the Rear Frame Rail to Lower Quarter Extension.
  8. Remove the Trunk Floor Assembly (Side Panel).
  9. Remove the original component.
    or Factory Spot Welds

    or Factory SPRs

  10. Prepare for installation.
    or High Strength Structural Rivets, 6.5 mm

    or Structural Countersunk Rivets, 6.5 mm

  11. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  12. Install the new component or components.
  13. Install the Trunk Floor Assembly (Side Panel).
  14. Install the Rear Frame Rail to Lower Quarter Extension.
  15. Install the Rear Body Panel.
  16. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  17. Install the Bracket - Rear - Center (Remove and Replace).
  18. Install the Bumper Beam - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  19. Install the Bracket - Taillight - LH (Remove and Replace).
  20. Install the Air Extractor - LH (Remove and Replace).
  21. Install the Seal - Trunk (Remove and Replace).
