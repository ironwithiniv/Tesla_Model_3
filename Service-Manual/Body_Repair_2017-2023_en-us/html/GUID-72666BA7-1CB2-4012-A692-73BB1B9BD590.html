Puddle Light - Front Door - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 17351202 0.05 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See for safe and healthy working practices.

  1. 2025-05-28: Added new caution to avoid damage.
CAUTION
Do not close the door during installation. Closing the door may damage the window.

Remove

  1. Open the LH front door and fully lower the LH front window.
  2. Use a small screwdriver or pry tool in the slot at the front of the puddle light to pry the light out of the bottom of the front LH door trim panel.
  3. Release the clips that attach the front of the puddle light to the bottom of the door trim panel, and then separate the light from the panel.
    CAUTION
    Do not pull the puddle light too far from the door panel, as the puddle light connector is still attached.
  4. Disconnect the electrical wiring harness from the puddle light connector, and then remove the light from the vehicle.

Install

Installation procedure is the reverse of removal, except for the following:

  • Align the slot of the light toward the front of the vehicle.
