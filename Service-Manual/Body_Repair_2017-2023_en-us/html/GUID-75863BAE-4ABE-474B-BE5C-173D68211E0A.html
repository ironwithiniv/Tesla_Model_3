Trunk Floor Assembly (Side Panel)

Correction code: 10101802702 10101816002 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Rear End Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • This procedure can be completed without using a frame bench.

Parts List

Quantity Description Image / Notes
1 Extn-Trunk Floor (Rear Trunk Assembly Side Panel)
12 Rivet, 4.8 mm
4 Structural Bulb Rivet, 6.5 mm

When ordering parts, refer to the Parts Catalog and enter the VIN of the vehicle being repaired to find the correct parts (and the part numbers) for the vehicle. Alternatively, use the search function in the Parts Catalog to find a specific part for the vehicle.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Raise Vehicle - 2 Post Lift.
  2. Remove the Fascia - Rear (Remove and Install).
  3. Remove the Bumper Beam - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Panel - Aero Shield - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the Bracket - Rear - Center (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the Antenna - Bluetooth Passive Entry - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the Carpet - Rear Trunk (1-Piece) (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the Front Carpet - Trunk (2-Piece) (Remove and Replace).
  9. Remove the Trim - Side - Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace).
  10. Remove the Trim - Floor - Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  11. Remove the original component.

    Factory Spot Weld Area

  12. Prepare for installation.

    Rivet, 4.8 mm

    Structural Bulb Rivet, 6.5 mm

  13. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  14. Install the new component or components.
  15. Install the Rear Aero Shield Harness Attachment Bracket.
  16. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  17. Install the Trim - Floor - Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  18. Install the Trim - Side - Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace).
  19. Install the Front Carpet - Trunk (2-Piece) (Remove and Replace).
  20. Install the Carpet - Rear Trunk (1-Piece) (Remove and Replace).
  21. Install the Antenna - Bluetooth Passive Entry - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  22. Install the Bracket - Rear - Center (Remove and Replace).
  23. Install the Panel - Aero Shield - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  24. Install the Bumper Beam - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  25. Install the Fascia - Rear (Remove and Install).
  26. Install the Raise Vehicle - 2 Post Lift.
