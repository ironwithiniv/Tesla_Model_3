Vehicle and Wheel Paint Codes

Provides paint color and code information for Tesla vehicles and wheels.

Vehicle Paint Codes

The vehicle paint code can be found in the vehicle's Car Details (refer to the Owner's Manual for information on how to display the Car Details). Additionally, the vehicle paint code may be printed on one of the labels affixed to the vehicle.
