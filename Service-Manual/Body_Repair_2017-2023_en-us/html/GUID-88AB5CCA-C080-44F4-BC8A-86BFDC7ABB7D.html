Center Underbody

Correction code: 10100400502 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Center Under Body Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • This procedure can be completed without using a frame bench.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Carpet - Interior Complete (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the HVAC Assembly (Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Trim - Sill Panel - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Seat Cushion - Lower - 2nd Row (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the HV Battery (AWD) (Remove and Install).
  6. Remove the original component.
    or Factory Spot Welds
  7. Prepare for installation.
    Note
    A red X indicates a location where a factory-installed fastener is not being replaced. Secure this location using structural adhesive only.
    or Countersunk Rivets, 4.8 mm Long
    • A = 263 mm.
    • B = 210 mm.
    • C = 158 mm.
    • D = 105 mm.
    • E = 11 mm.

    or Countersunk Rivets, 4.8 mm Short

    or High Strength Structural Rivets, 6.5 mm

    or Rivets, 4.8 mm

  8. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  9. Install the new component or components.
  10. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  11. Install the HV Battery (AWD) (Remove and Install).
  12. Install the Seat Cushion - Lower - 2nd Row (Remove and Replace).
  13. Install the Trim - Sill Panel - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
  14. Install the HVAC Assembly (Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace).
  15. Install the Carpet - Interior Complete (Remove and Replace).
