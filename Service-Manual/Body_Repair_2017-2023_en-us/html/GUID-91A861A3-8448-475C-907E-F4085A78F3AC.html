Ancillary Bay Air Leak Test (Inspection)

Correction code 16100400 0.54 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-11-17: Included subtile wording changes.
  • 2026-03-12: Added 1586825-00-A DCFC Leak Plug for models with DCFC bus bars.
Equipment:
  • 1026636-00-A Pack Enclosure Leak Tester, HV Battery
  • 1140501-00-A Pack Kit, Enclosure, Leak Test, HV Battery, Complete
  • 1144879-00-A Kit, Encl Leak Test Adapters, HV Battery
  • 1813553-00-A MYSP Platter, Enclosure Leak Test, Air Splitter

Procedure

  1. Perform the vehicle HV disablement procedure. See Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure.
  2. Where applicable, make sure that the logic connector cap is snug on the high voltage controller connector to prevent leaks.
  3. Remove and discard the breather plug from the ancillary bay probe lid cover.
  4. Install the leak test adapter into the ancillary bay probe lid cover.
  5. Connect the pack enclosure leak tester to the leak test adapter.
  6. Close both valves on the enclosure leak tester by turning the valve knobs to the vertical position.
  7. Connect the enclosure leak tester to air supply.
    Note
    Make sure the pack enclosure leak tester valves are both in the closed position.
  8. Turn the inlet valve to the open position.
  9. Set the pack enclosure leak tester to 0.150 psi.
  10. Open the outlet valve to inject air into the ancillary bay, and allow 60 seconds for the pressure to settle to 0.150 psi.
  11. Close the inlet valve and wait 45 seconds for pressure to stabilize.
  12. Check the air pressure.
    • If pressure is below 0.142 psi, check the hose, adapter, plug fittings, and check the pressure regulator, then retest. If pressure is still below 0.142 psi, diagnose enclosure for leaks.
    • If pressure is at 0.142 psi or above, continue to the next step.
  13. Record the value of the starting pressure.
  14. Wait 60 seconds to test pressure, and then record the value of the ending pressure.
  15. Subtract the ending pressure from the starting pressure.
    • If the difference is greater than 0.024 psi, check the hose, adapter, and plug fittings, close the outlet valve and open the inlet valve, check the pack enclosure leak tester pressure, and then retest from step 10. If the difference is still greater than 0.024 psi, further investigate possible causes for the pressure leak, starting with Toolbox article 41588.
    • If the difference is 0.024 psi or less, continue the procedure.
  16. Disconnect the air supply from the enclosure leak tester.
  17. Disconnect the enclosure leak tester from the leak test adapter.
  18. Remove the leak test adapter from the ancillary bay probe lid cover.
  19. Install a new breather plug (1108907-00-B) onto the ancillary bay probe lid cover.
  20. Install the rear drive unit HV cable connector to the HV battery and install other components that were removed to perform the vehicle HV disablement procedure. See Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure.
  21. Connect 12V/LV power. See 12V/LV Power (Disconnect and Connect).
