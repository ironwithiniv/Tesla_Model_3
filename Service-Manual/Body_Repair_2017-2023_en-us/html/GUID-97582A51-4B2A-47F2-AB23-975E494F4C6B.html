Torque Box Cover Assembly

Correction code: LH: 10101016102 RH: 10101016202 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Front Inner Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • This procedure can be completed without using a frame bench.

Parts List

When ordering parts, refer to the Parts Catalog and enter the VIN of the vehicle being repaired to find the correct parts (and the part numbers) for the vehicle. Alternatively, use the search function in the Parts Catalog to find a specific part for the vehicle.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Noise Absorber - Motor Bay - Front (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the Beam - Cross Car (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Carpet - Interior Complete (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Brake Lines - 4 Tube Bundle - ABS to Wheels (RWD) (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the Brake Lines - LH Sill (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the Fender Assembly - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
  7. Remove the Brake Pedal (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the Knuckle - Suspension - Front - LH (RWD) (Remove and Replace).
  9. Remove the Damper Assembly - Front - LH (RWD) (Remove and Replace).
  10. Remove the Link - Lateral - Lower - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  11. Remove the Link - Compliance - Lower - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  12. Remove the Module - Body Controller - RH (LHD) (Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace).
  13. Remove the Module - Body Controller - LH (LHD) (Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace).
  14. Remove the Hose - Return - HV Battery - Front (Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace).
  15. Remove the Hose - Powertrain Supply - Supermanifold (Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace).
  16. Remove the HV Battery (AWD) (Remove and Install).
  17. Remove the original component.

    or Factory Spot Welds

  18. Prepare for installation.

    or High Strength Structural Rivets, 6.5 mm

    or Bolts
    • A = 34 mm
    • B = 8 mm

    or Installation Spot Welds

  19. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  20. Install the new component or components.
    Note
    Torque the bolt to 22 Nm.
    Note
    For all rivets installed through the Torque Box Cover Assembly and into the cabin of the vehicle, make sure the rivets do not rub against the under-carpet insulation pad. If necessary, trim the insulation pad slightly to provide clearance.
  21. Perform resistance spot welding.
    Warning
    Failure to follow all welding safety precautions, including the use of personal protective equipment, could result in serious injury or property damage. Only technicians who have completed Tesla’s approved welding training are authorized to weld structural components on Tesla vehicles.
    CAUTION
    Do not weld on a Tesla vehicle before performing the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure (refer to the vehicle-specific Service Manual for more information on the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure). Welding on a Tesla vehicle with an energized high or low voltage system might damage vehicle components.
  22. After painting is complete, use a butyl patch to cover the rivets shown. Use several small butyl patches if necessary.
  23. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  24. Install the HV Battery (AWD) (Remove and Install).
  25. Install the Hose - Powertrain Supply - Supermanifold (Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace).
  26. Install the Hose - Return - HV Battery - Front (Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace).
  27. Install the Module - Body Controller - LH (LHD) (Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace).
  28. Install the Module - Body Controller - RH (LHD) (Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace).
  29. Install the Link - Compliance - Lower - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  30. Install the Link - Lateral - Lower - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  31. Install the Damper Assembly - Front - LH (RWD) (Remove and Replace).
  32. Install the Knuckle - Suspension - Front - LH (RWD) (Remove and Replace).
  33. Install the Brake Pedal (Remove and Replace).
  34. Install the Fender Assembly - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
  35. Install the Brake Lines - LH Sill (Remove and Replace).
  36. Install the Brake Lines - 4 Tube Bundle - ABS to Wheels (RWD) (Remove and Replace).
  37. Install the Carpet - Interior Complete (Remove and Replace).
  38. Install the Beam - Cross Car (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  39. Install the Noise Absorber - Motor Bay - Front (Remove and Replace).
