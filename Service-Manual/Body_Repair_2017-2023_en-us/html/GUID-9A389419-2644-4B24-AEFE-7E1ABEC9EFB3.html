Cap - Wheel (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 18 inch Pinwheel: 34015021 0.05 Correction code 19 inch Stiletto or 20" Sport: 34015011 0.06 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See for safe and healthy working practices.

Equipment:
  • 1044206-00-A Wheel Trim Removal Tool

Remove

  1. Remove the wheel cap according to the type of wheel.
    • 18 inch pinwheel wheel: See step 2
    • 19 inch stiletto or 20 inch sport wheel: See step 3
  2. Gently pull the wheel cap with both hands to release the clips that attach the wheel cap to the wheel.
  3. Insert the wheel trim removal tool in the hole on the center cap, and then pull to remove the center cap.

Install

  1. Position the wheel cap over the wheel with the bottom of the Tesla "T" pointing to the valve stem.
  2. Press the center of the cap firmly onto the wheel until it snaps into place.
