Panel - Door Trim - Front - LH (Remove and Install)

Correction code 15130201 0.12 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See for safe and healthy working practices.

Torque Specifications

Table 1. Torque Specifications: Panel - Door Trim - Front - LH (Remove and Install)
Description Torque Value Recommended Tools Reuse/Replace Notes
Front door trim panel to front door screws
6 Nm (4.4 lbs-ft)
  • Torx T30 socket
 Reuse
CAUTION
Use ONLY the following fastener PN(s):
  • 1488028-00-A or later
  • 1526998-99-A or later

Remove

CAUTION
Use only the trim panel screw PN(s) listed in this procedure. See step 15 for more information.
  1. Open the LH front door, and fully lower the window.
  2. Remove the LH front door tweeter. See Tweeter - Front Door - LH (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the LH front door puddle light. See Puddle Light - Front Door - LH (Remove and Replace).
  4. Release the clips that attach the reflector to the rear edge of the LH front door trim panel and the screw cover to the front of the armrest, and then remove the reflector and cover from the trim panel.
  5. Remove the screws that attach the trim panel to the door.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T30 socket
  6. Pull out on the trim panel from the puddle light opening to release the clips that attach the trim panel to the door.
    CAUTION
    Do not remove the trim panel from the door at this time.
  7. Continue to pull and release all of the clips around the edge of the trim panel, working from the bottom up.
  8. Raise the trim panel up to unhook it from the door.
    CAUTION
    Do not pull the trim panel far away from the door as the panel is still attached by connectors and cables.
  9. Lift the NVH pad to access the working area.
  10. Remove the manual release cable collar from the trim panel, and pivot the collar down to a vertical orientation.
  11. Slide the cable barrel end out of the release lever, and remove the cable from the trim panel.
  12. Disconnect the electrical harness from the connectors, and then release the clips that attach the electrical harness to the trim panel.
  13. If present, remove and discard the cable ties that attach the electrical harness to the door trim panel.
  14. If present, release the electrical harness from the door trim harness hooks.
  15. Remove the panel from the vehicle, and then place the panel on a clean surface.
  16. Check the LH front door for any remaining push clip retainers, and if present, remove them from the door.

Install

  1. Inspect the condition of the LH front door and the LH front door trim panel. Replace any broken clips and repair any damage.
  2. Bring the trim panel to the door, and rest it on the door edge.
  3. If present, remove and discard the highlighted clip.
  4. Route the harness as illustrated.
    • If harness hooks are present, attach the harness to the hooks.
    • If harness hooks are not present, attach the harness to the door trim panel with cable ties.
  5. Fasten the clips that attach the electrical harnesses to the trim panel.
  6. Connect the electrical harness to the LH front door trim panel connectors.
  7. Position the manual release cable in a vertical orientation, and slide the cable barrel end into the release lever.
  8. Pivot the manual release cable collar to a horizontal orientation, and press the collar into the trim panel.
    Note
    Make sure the manual release cable is routed underneath the door harness.
  9. Lift the trim panel, compress the LH front door inner belt seal slightly, and then hook the three trim panel tabs into the top edge of the door.
  10. Move the electrical harness connector for the puddle lamp out the hole in the bottom of the trim panel.
  11. Install the LH front door puddle lamp. See Puddle Light - Front Door - LH (Remove and Replace).
  12. Make sure that the puddle lamp and map pocket ambient light illuminate when connected.
  13. Fasten the clips that attach the trim panel to the door.
  14. Make sure that the trim panel fits tightly, and that there are no gaps from misaligned or bent clips.
  15. Install the screws that attach the trim panel to the door.
    6 Nm (4.4 lbs-ft)
    CAUTION
    Use ONLY the following fastener PN(s):
    • 1488028-00-A or later
    • 1526998-99-A or later
    CAUTION
    Make sure that all fasteners are properly torqued and not cross-threaded.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T30 socket
  16. Install the reflector to the rear edge of the trim panel and the screw cover to the front of the armrest.
  17. Install the LH front door tweeter. See Tweeter - Front Door - LH (Remove and Replace).
  18. Close the door and verify the operation of the manual door release lever, that the door latch releases smoothly and easily.
  19. Raise the window and close the LH front door.
