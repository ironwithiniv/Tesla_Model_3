Sill Outer (Front Section)

Correction code: RH: 10102900602 LH: 10102900502 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Center Under Body Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • This procedure can be completed without using a frame bench.

Parts List

2 M3 ASY - SIDE SILL OUTER (Sill Outer Assembly)

When ordering parts, refer to the Parts Catalog and enter the VIN of the vehicle being repaired to find the correct parts (and the part numbers) for the vehicle. Alternatively, use the search function in the Parts Catalog to find a specific part for the vehicle.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the HV Battery (AWD) (Remove and Install).
  2. Remove the Brake Lines - LH Sill (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Beam - Cross Car (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Steering Column (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the Module - Body Controller - LH (LHD) (Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the Module - Body Controller - RH (LHD) (Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the Door - Rear - LH (Remove and Install).
  8. Remove the Door - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
  9. Remove the Fender Assembly - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
  10. Remove the necessary portion of the Body Side Outer Assembly to expose the underlying component being replaced.
  11. Remove the Hinge Pillar (Section).
  12. Cut the original component at the indicated locations.

    Cut Line

    Reference Line/Point

    • A = 41 mm
  13. Remove the original component.

    or Factory Spot Welds

  14. Cut the new component in the indicated location.

    Cut Line

    Reference Line/Point

    • B = 942 mm
  15. Create an 80 mm wide reinforcement plate from the second new Sill Outer.
  16. Prepare for installation.
    Note
    A red X indicates a location where a factory-installed fastener is not being replaced. Secure this location using structural adhesive only.
    Note
    For each rivet being installed, drill a 6.7 mm hole through both the Sill Outer and the top part of the Sill Insert. These holes provide clearance for the rivets used in the Sill Outer section.

    or Structural Bulb Rivets, 6.5 mm

    or Installation Spot Welds

  17. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  18. Install the new component or components.
  19. Perform resistance spot welding.
    Warning
    Failure to follow all welding safety precautions, including the use of personal protective equipment, could result in serious injury or property damage. Only technicians who have completed Tesla’s approved welding training are authorized to weld structural components on Tesla vehicles.
    CAUTION
    Do not weld on a Tesla vehicle before performing the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure (refer to the vehicle-specific Service Manual for more information on the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure). Welding on a Tesla vehicle with an energized high or low voltage system might damage vehicle components.
  20. Install the Hinge Pillar (Section).
  21. Install the portion of the Body Side Outer Assembly that was removed to expose the component being replaced.
    Note
    The Body Side Outer lower flange is secured to the Sill Outer using structural adhesive only.
  22. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  23. Install the Fender Assembly - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
  24. Install the Door - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
  25. Install the Door - Rear - LH (Remove and Install).
  26. Install the Module - Body Controller - RH (LHD) (Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace).
  27. Install the Module - Body Controller - LH (LHD) (Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace).
  28. Install the Steering Column (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  29. Install the Beam - Cross Car (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  30. Install the Brake Lines - LH Sill (Remove and Replace).
  31. Install the HV Battery (AWD) (Remove and Install).
