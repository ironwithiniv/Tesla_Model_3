Front Shock Tower Support (Rear)

Correction code: RH: 10101003602 LH: 10101003502 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Front Inner Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • Properly mount the vehicle on a frame bench when performing this procedure.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Reservoir - Windshield Washer (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the Subframe Assembly - Front (Dual Motor) (Remove and Install).
  3. Remove the A/C Compressor and Supermanifold (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Wheel Arch Liner - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the Cover - Shock Tower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the Front Upper Control Arm (FUCA) Mount - LH (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the Control Arm - Upper - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the ABS Modulator (Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace).
  9. Remove the Booster - Brake - Electromechanical (Heat Pump) (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  10. Remove the original component.
    or Factory Spot Welds
    or Drill through factory spot welds
    Note
    When drilling out spot welds, use a drill bit that creates a hole correctly sized for the fastener that will replace the spot weld.
  11. Prepare for installation.
    Note
    A red X indicates a location where a factory-installed fastener is not being replaced. Secure this location using structural adhesive only.
    Note
    Install a rivet in the location circled in red only if a weld was removed from that location when the original component was removed.
    or High Strength Structural Rivets, 6.5 mm
    • A = 15 mm.
    • B = 12 mm.

    or Bolts

    Nut

    GMA Weld

  12. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  13. Install the new component or components.
    Torque the bolts to 22 Nm.
  14. Perform GMA welding.
    GMA Weld
    Warning
    Failure to follow all welding safety precautions, including the use of personal protective equipment, could result in serious injury or property damage. Only technicians who have completed Tesla’s approved welding training are authorized to weld structural components on Tesla vehicles.
    Warning
    To maintain vehicle crash integrity, use only approved welding wire and an approved GMA welder to perform GMA welding on Tesla vehicles. Refer to Approved Gas Metal Arc (GMA) Welders and Welding Wire for information on approved GMA welders and welding wire.
    CAUTION
    Do not weld on a Tesla vehicle before performing the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure (refer to the vehicle-specific Service Manual for more information on the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure). Welding on a Tesla vehicle with an energized high or low voltage system might damage vehicle components.
    Note
    Before GMA welding, a test weld using material of the same gauge and type should be performed to make sure that the welding equipment settings produce a satisfactory joint.
  15. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  16. Install the Booster - Brake - Electromechanical (Heat Pump) (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  17. Install the ABS Modulator (Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace).
  18. Install the Control Arm - Upper - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  19. Install the Front Upper Control Arm (FUCA) Mount - LH (Remove and Replace).
  20. Install the Cover - Shock Tower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  21. Install the Wheel Arch Liner - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  22. Install the A/C Compressor and Supermanifold (Remove and Replace).
  23. Install the Subframe Assembly - Front (Dual Motor) (Remove and Install).
  24. Install the Reservoir - Windshield Washer (Remove and Replace).
