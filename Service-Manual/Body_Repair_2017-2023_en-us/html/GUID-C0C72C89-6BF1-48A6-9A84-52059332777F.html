Front Shock Tower

Correction code: LH: 10101015602 RH: 10101015702 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Front Inner Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • Properly mount the vehicle on a frame bench when performing this procedure.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Panel - Aero Shield - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the Hinge - Hood - LH (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Fender Assembly - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
  4. Remove the Reservoir - Windshield Washer (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the Booster - Brake - Electromechanical (Heat Pump) (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the Carrier - Front End (Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the Radiator (Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the Condenser (Remove and Replace).
  9. Remove the A/C Compressor and Supermanifold (Remove and Replace).
  10. Remove the Wheel Arch Liner - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  11. Remove the Bracket - 12V Battery (Dual Motor) (Remove and Replace).
  12. Remove the Subframe Assembly - Front (Dual Motor) (Remove and Install).
  13. Remove the Front Upper Control Arm (FUCA) Mount - LH (Remove and Replace).
  14. Remove the Control Arm - Upper - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  15. Remove the ABS Modulator (Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace).
  16. Remove the Shotgun Outer.
  17. Remove the original component.

    Factory Spot Weld Area

  18. Prepare to assemble the Shock Tower components.

    Structural Bulb Rivet, 6.5 mm

    High Strength Structural Rivet, 6.5 mm

    Note
    Rivets circled in red are installed from the back side of the panel.
    Bolt
    Note
    Bolts circled in red are installed from the back side of the panel.

    Installation Spot Weld

  19. Mount to the frame bench and apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the components that make up the Shock Tower.
  20. Assemble the components to create the new Shock Tower.
    Note
    Torque the bolts to 22 Nm.
  21. Remove the new Shock Tower Assembly from the vehicle.
  22. Perform resistance spot welding.
    Warning
    Failure to follow all welding safety precautions, including the use of personal protective equipment, could result in serious injury or property damage. Only technicians who have completed Tesla’s approved welding training are authorized to weld structural components on Tesla vehicles.
    CAUTION
    Do not weld on a Tesla vehicle before performing the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure (refer to the vehicle-specific Service Manual for more information on the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure). Welding on a Tesla vehicle with an energized high or low voltage system might damage vehicle components.
  23. Prepare for installation.

    Structural Bulb Rivet, 6.5 mm

    High Strength Structural Rivet, 6.5 mm
    • A = 15 mm
    • B = 12 mm
    • C = 10 mm
    • D = 10 mm
    Note
    Rivets circled in red are installed from the back side of the panel.

    Installation Spot Weld Area

    GMA Weld

  24. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  25. Install the new component or components.
  26. Perform resistance spot welding.
    Warning
    Failure to follow all welding safety precautions, including the use of personal protective equipment, could result in serious injury or property damage. Only technicians who have completed Tesla’s approved welding training are authorized to weld structural components on Tesla vehicles.
    CAUTION
    Do not weld on a Tesla vehicle before performing the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure (refer to the vehicle-specific Service Manual for more information on the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure). Welding on a Tesla vehicle with an energized high or low voltage system might damage vehicle components.
  27. Perform GMA welding.
    Warning
    Failure to follow all welding safety precautions, including the use of personal protective equipment, could result in serious injury or property damage. Only technicians who have completed Tesla’s approved welding training are authorized to weld structural components on Tesla vehicles.
    Warning
    To maintain vehicle crash integrity, use only approved welding wire and an approved GMA welder to perform GMA welding on Tesla vehicles. Refer to Approved Gas Metal Arc (GMA) Welders and Welding Wire for information on approved GMA welders and welding wire.
    CAUTION
    Do not weld on a Tesla vehicle before performing the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure (refer to the vehicle-specific Service Manual for more information on the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure). Welding on a Tesla vehicle with an energized high or low voltage system might damage vehicle components.
    Note
    Before GMA welding, a test weld using material of the same gauge and type should be performed to make sure that the welding equipment settings produce a satisfactory joint.
  28. Install the Shotgun Outer.
  29. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  30. Install the ABS Modulator (Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace).
  31. Install the Control Arm - Upper - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  32. Install the Front Upper Control Arm (FUCA) Mount - LH (Remove and Replace).
  33. Install the Subframe Assembly - Front (Dual Motor) (Remove and Install).
  34. Install the Bracket - 12V Battery (Dual Motor) (Remove and Replace).
  35. Install the Wheel Arch Liner - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  36. Install the A/C Compressor and Supermanifold (Remove and Replace).
  37. Install the Condenser (Remove and Replace).
  38. Install the Radiator (Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace).
  39. Install the Carrier - Front End (Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace).
  40. Install the Booster - Brake - Electromechanical (Heat Pump) (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  41. Install the Reservoir - Windshield Washer (Remove and Replace).
  42. Install the Fender Assembly - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
  43. Install the Hinge - Hood - LH (Remove and Replace).
  44. Install the Panel - Aero Shield - Rear (Remove and Replace).
