Hinge - Door - Rear - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 11337002 0.30 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2026-09-16: Updated torque value of the nuts attaching the LH rear door lower hinge to the body to 23 Nm.

Remove

  1. Open the LH front and door and lower the LH front and rear windows.
  2. Apply tape to the rear door to mark the lower hinge location.
  3. Apply tape to the body to mark the rear lower hinge location.
  4. Use a jack to support the LH rear door from the bottom.
    Note
    Make sure to use soft material to protect the painted surface.
  5. Remove the bolts (x2) that attach the lower door hinge to the LH rear door.
  6. Remove the nuts (x2) that attach the rear lower door hinge to the body, and then remove the LH rear lower door hinge from the vehicle.
    Note
    Support the door hinge to make sure it does not fall in the door jam.

Install

  1. Align the new lower hinge with the applied tape on the body, and then install the nuts that attach the lower door hinge to body hand tight.
  2. Align the new lower hinge with the applied tape on the LH rear door, and then install the bolts that attach the lower hinge to the LH rear door hand tight.
  3. Close the LH front and rear doors, and then check for gap and flushness. Adjust if necessary.
  4. Hand-tighten the bolts (x2) and nuts (x2) that attach the LH rear door lower hinge to the door and the body.
  5. Remove the support from the LH rear door.
    Note
    Rest door at first detent. Use soft material to protect the painted surface.
  6. Close the front door.
  7. Check the gap and flushness of the LH rear door.
    Note
    Adjust the door as needed. Refer to CVIS for current specifications.
  8. Open the LH front and rear doors.
  9. Torque the nuts (x2) that attach the LH rear door lower hinge to the body.
    23 Nm (17.0 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm socket
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
  10. Torque the bolts that attach the LH rear lower door hinge to the door.
    27 Nm (19.9 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
    • Flex head ratchet/flex head torque wrench
  11. Remove the tape from the LH body side and the LH rear door.
  12. Clean the hinge bolts with IPA.
    Note
    Allow 1 minute dry time
  13. Apply touch up paint to bolt heads if necessary, and then allow paint to dry.
  14. Raise both LH windows and close both LH doors.
