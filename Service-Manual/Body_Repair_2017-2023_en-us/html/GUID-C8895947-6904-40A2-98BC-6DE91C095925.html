Suspension - Rear (Check Torque)

Correction code 31039000 0.36 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See for safe and healthy working practices.

Equipment:
  • 1135103-00-A Tool, Spring Compressor, Hook, Model 3
  • 1081765-00-A 3/4 Ton Underhoist Stand
  • 1063141-00-A 1/2 Ton Economy Transmission Jack
  • 1137855-00-A Tool, Rear Ride Height Torque, Model 3
  • 1459409-00-A ADAPTER, HUB JACK, MODEL 3

Procedure

Note
This procedure must be performed on both sides of the vehicle. After the torque is checked on the LH side of the rear suspension, repeat these steps on the RH side of the rear suspension.
  1. Remove both rear wheels. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
  2. Remove the mid aero shield panel. See Panel - Aero Shield - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the bolts that attach the LH and RH rear suspension covers, and then remove the covers from the vehicle.
    6 Nm (4.4 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  4. Remove the bolts (x4) that attach the front of the rear diffuser to the body.
  5. Position the transmission jack to support the front beam of the rear subframe.
  6. Release the fir tree clips (x2) that attach the coolant hoses to the LH and RH shear plates.
  7. Remove the bolts (x3) that attach the LH and RH shear plates to the HV battery, remove the shear plates, and then reinstall the long bolts directly into the rear subframe.
    35 Nm (25.8 lbs-ft)
    130 Nm (95.9 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm socket
    • 21 mm socket
  8. Remove the transmission jack out from under the front beam of the rear subframe.
  9. Remove the center clip that attaches the LH and RH wheel liners to the body.
  10. Install the spring compressor to the rear LH spring and compress the spring so that is it loose in the suspension.
    Note
    The lowest hook of the spring compressor goes under the coil at the strut. The highest hook of the spring compressor goes over the coil at the HV battery. The spring compressor lead screw should center to, and pass through, the opening in the body.
  11. Remove the bolt that attaches the brake rotor to the hub.
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
    • 2 in extension
  12. Install the hub jack adapter onto the LH rear hub and hand-tighten the lug nuts.
  13. Use an underhoist stand to support the hub jack adapter.
    Note
    Use the rear ride height torque gauge to verify that the rear suspension is set to ride height specifications and adjust the support stand or spring compressor tool , if necessary.
  14. Measure the distance between the bottom of the quarter panel to the center of the rear axle to make sure that the rear suspension is set to ride height: The distance should measure 378 mm.
  15. Check the torque for the nut that attaches the LH rear lower fore link to the rear subframe, and then mark the nut with a paint pen.
    76 Nm (56.0 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 18 mm socket
  16. Check the torque for the nut that attaches the LH rear lower fore link to the knuckle, and then mark the nut with a paint pen.
    76 Nm (56.0 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 18 mm socket
  17. Check the torque for the nut that attaches the rear stabilizer bar to the LH stabilizer bar link, and then mark the nut with a paint pen.
    55 Nm (40.6 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 15 mm socket
    • 5 mm hex socket
    • 15 mm flex head combination ratcheting wrench
  18. Check the torque for the nut that attaches the LH stabilizer bar link to the knuckle, and then mark the nut with a paint pen.
    55 Nm (40.6 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 15 mm socket
    • 5 mm hex socket
    • 15 mm flex head combination ratcheting wrench
  19. Check the torque for the nut that attaches the LH toe link to the rear subframe, and then mark the nut with a paint pen.
    85 Nm (62.7 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 18 mm deep socket
  20. Check the torque for the nut that attaches the LH toe link to the knuckle, and then mark the nut with a paint pen.
    76 Nm (56.0 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
  21. Check the torque for the nut that attaches the LH rear lower aft link to the rear subframe, and then mark the nut with a paint pen.
    115 Nm (84.8 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 21 mm socket
  22. Check the torque for the nut that attaches the LH rear lower aft link to the knuckle, and then mark the nut with a paint pen.
    115 Nm (84.8 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 21 mm socket
  23. Check the torque for the nut that attaches the LH rear damper assembly to the LH lower aft link, and then mark the nut with a paint pen.
    115 Nm (84.8 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
  24. Check the torque for the nut that attaches the LH rear upper fore link to the rear subframe, and then mark the nut with a paint pen.
    76 Nm (56.0 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 18 mm socket
  25. Check the torque for the nut that attaches the LH rear upper fore link to the knuckle, and then mark the nut with a paint pen.
    76 Nm (56.0 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
  26. Check the torque for the nut that attaches the LH rear upper aft link to the rear subframe, and then mark the nut with a paint pen.
    134 Nm (98.8 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 21 mm socket
  27. Check the torque for the nut that attaches the LH rear upper aft link to the knuckle, and then mark the nut with a paint pen.
    134 Nm (98.8 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
  28. Remove the underhoist stand from underneath the LH rear suspension.
  29. Remove the lug nuts that attach the hub jack adapter, and then remove the hub jack adapter from the vehicle.
  30. Install the bolt that attaches the LH rear brake rotor to the hub.
    5 Nm (3.7 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
    • 2 in extension
  31. Remove the spring compressor from the rear LH spring.
  32. Repeat step 3 through step 29 on the RH side of the rear suspension.
  33. Position the transmission jack to support the front beam of the rear subframe.
  34. Remove the long bolts (x2) from the front of the rear subframe, install the LH and RH shear plates to the subframe and HV battery, and then install the bolts (x3) that attach the shear plates to the subframe and HV battery.
    35 Nm (25.8 lbs-ft)
    130 Nm (95.9 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm socket
    • 21 mm socket
  35. Fasten the fir tree clips (x2) that attach the coolant hoses to the LH and RH shear plates.
  36. Remove the transmission jack out from under the front beam of the rear subframe.
  37. Install the center clip that attaches the LH and RH wheel liners to the body.
  38. Install the bolts (x4) that attach the front of the rear diffuser to the body.
    4 Nm (2.9 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  39. Install the LH and RH rear suspension covers.
    6 Nm (4.4 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  40. Install the mid aero shield panel. See Panel - Aero Shield - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  41. Install both rear wheels. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
