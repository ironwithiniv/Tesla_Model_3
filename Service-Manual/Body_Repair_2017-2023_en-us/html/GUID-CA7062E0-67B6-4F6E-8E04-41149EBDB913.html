Trim - C-Pillar - Upper - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 15186002 0.18 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See for safe and healthy working practices.

Remove

  1. Remove the LH 2nd row seat side bolster. See Bolster - Side - Seat - 2nd Row - LH (Remove and Replace).
  2. Lower the LH side rail trim. See Trim - Side Rail - LH (Remove and Install).
  3. Release the clips that attach the upper C-pillar trim, and then remove the trim from the vehicle.
    Tip
    Pull outward at the lower section of the upper C-pillar trim until both clips release, and then move the trim slightly downwards to remove the trim from the vehicle.

Install

  1. Install the LH upper C-pillar trim by sliding it under the side curtain airbag, and then align the upper clip. Secure the trim with clips (x2).
  2. Install the LH side rail trim. See Trim - Side Rail - LH (Remove and Install).
  3. Install the LH 2nd row seat side bolster. See Bolster - Side - Seat - 2nd Row - LH (Remove and Replace).
