Hinge Pillar (Section)

Correction code: LH: 10100103202 RH: 10100102702 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Body Side Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • Properly mount the vehicle on a frame bench when performing this procedure.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Hood (Remove and Install).
  2. Remove the Wheel Arch Liner - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Door - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
  4. Remove the A-Pillar to Shotgun Reinforcement.
  5. Remove the necessary portion of the Body Side Outer Assembly to expose the underlying component being replaced.
  6. Remove the A-Pillar to Shotgun Reinforcement from the new Hinge Pillar Assembly.

    or Factory Spot Welds

  7. Cut the original component at the indicated locations.
    Cut Line
    • A = 80 mm
  8. Remove the original component.

    or Factory Spot Welds

  9. Remove any remaining adhesive from the mating surfaces of the vehicle.
  10. Cut the first new Hinge Pillar Panel (M3 ASY PNL HINGE PILLAR) as shown to create a backing plate.
    Cut Line
    • B = 130 mm
    • C = 310 mm
  11. Cut the backing plate down the middle to create 2 sections, then fit both sections of the backing plate in place and tack weld the backing plate sections together.
    Note
    Do not fully weld the backing plate sections together while the sections are on the vehicle.
    Warning
    Failure to follow all welding safety precautions, including the use of personal protective equipment, could result in serious injury or property damage. Only technicians who have completed Tesla’s approved welding training are authorized to weld structural components on Tesla vehicles.
    CAUTION
    Do not weld on a Tesla vehicle before performing the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure (refer to the vehicle-specific Service Manual for more information on the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure). Welding on a Tesla vehicle with an energized high or low voltage system might damage vehicle components.
  12. GMA weld the backing plate sections together to create the backing plate.

    GMA Weld

    Warning
    Failure to follow all welding safety precautions, including the use of personal protective equipment, could result in serious injury or property damage. Only technicians who have completed Tesla’s approved welding training are authorized to weld structural components on Tesla vehicles.
  13. Put the backing plate into position and clamp it into place.
  14. Cut the second new Hinge Pillar Assembly on the cut line shown.
    Cut Line
    • D = 157 mm
  15. Prepare for installation.
    or Structural Countersunk Rivets, 6.5 mm
    CAUTION
    Make sure to locate the forward-most structural countersunk rivet inboard enough so that it does not conflict with the fasteners used to install the A-Pillar to Shotgun Reinforcement in a later step.
    or High Strength Structural Rivets, 6.5 mm
    Note
    The center of all rivets in the bottom row of structural rivets must be at the indicated height from the lower edge of the new component, as shown below.
    • E = 15 mm

    or Structural Bulb Rivets, 6.5 mm

    or Installation Spot Welds

  16. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  17. Install the new component or components.
  18. Perform resistance spot welding.

    or Installation Spot Welds

    Warning
    Failure to follow all welding safety precautions, including the use of personal protective equipment, could result in serious injury or property damage. Only technicians who have completed Tesla’s approved welding training are authorized to weld structural components on Tesla vehicles.
    CAUTION
    Do not weld on a Tesla vehicle before performing the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure (refer to the vehicle-specific Service Manual for more information on the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure). Welding on a Tesla vehicle with an energized high or low voltage system might damage vehicle components.
  19. Install the portion of the Body Side Outer Assembly that was removed to expose the component being replaced.
  20. Install the A-Pillar to Shotgun Reinforcement.
  21. Install the fasteners as indicated.

    Structural Countersunk Rivet, 6.5 mm

  22. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  23. Install the Door - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
  24. Install the Wheel Arch Liner - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  25. Install the Hood (Remove and Install).
