Third Row Seat Crossmember (Upper)

Correction code: 10101814002 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Rear End Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • This procedure can be completed without using a frame bench.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the HV Battery (AWD) (Remove and Install).
  2. Remove the Carpet - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Trim - Side - Trunk - RH (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Trim - Side - Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the Carpet - Rear Trunk (1-Piece) (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the Wheel Arch Liner - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the Subframe Assembly - Rear (Remove and Install).
  8. Remove the Cooling System (Check and Fill).
  9. Remove the Rear Motor Cover Floor.
  10. Remove the original component.

    or Factory Spot Welds

  11. Prepare for installation.

    or Structural Bulb Rivets, 6.5 mm

    or Installation Spot Welds

  12. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  13. Install the new component or components.
  14. Perform resistance spot welding.
    Warning
    Failure to follow all welding safety precautions, including the use of personal protective equipment, could result in serious injury or property damage. Only technicians who have completed Tesla’s approved welding training are authorized to weld structural components on Tesla vehicles.
    CAUTION
    Do not weld on a Tesla vehicle before performing the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure (refer to the vehicle-specific Service Manual for more information on the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure). Welding on a Tesla vehicle with an energized high or low voltage system might damage vehicle components.
  15. Install the Rear Motor Cover Floor.
  16. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  17. Install the Cooling System (Check and Fill).
  18. Install the Subframe Assembly - Rear (Remove and Install).
  19. Install the Wheel Arch Liner - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
  20. Install the Carpet - Rear Trunk (1-Piece) (Remove and Replace).
  21. Install the Trim - Side - Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace).
  22. Install the Trim - Side - Trunk - RH (Remove and Replace).
  23. Install the Carpet - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  24. Install the HV Battery (AWD) (Remove and Install).
