Performing a Structural Repair on a Tesla Vehicle

Before starting to perform a structural repair on a Tesla vehicle, read the specific Tesla Collision Repair procedure document for the repair. Use the specific repair procedure and the information in the following sections to complete the repair.

Note
When performing any Collision Repair procedure, make sure to wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment).
In general, there are 4 steps to repairing a Tesla vehicle, see the sections below.
  1. Removing the old component.
  2. Preparing to install the new component.
  3. Securing the replacement component.
  4. Applying corrosion protection and seam sealer.
Note
To ensure the safety of the repair technician, and to ensure a successful and permanent repair, follow all Cautions, Warnings, and Notes.
