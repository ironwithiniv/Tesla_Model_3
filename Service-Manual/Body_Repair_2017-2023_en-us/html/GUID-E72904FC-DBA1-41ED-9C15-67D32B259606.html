Hinge - Power Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 11332602 0.30 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See for safe and healthy working practices.

Torque Specifications

Table 1. Torque Specifications: Hinge - Power Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace)
Description Torque Value Recommended Tools Reuse/Replace Notes
Bolt that attaches the power trunk hinge to the trunk lid
24 Nm (17.7 lbs-ft)
  • 13 mm socket
 Reuse
Nut that attaches the power trunk hinge to the body
24 Nm (17.7 lbs-ft)
  • 13 mm socket
 Reuse

Remove

  1. Remove the trunk power strut. See Power Strut - Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  2. Apply masking tape on the trunk lid and body of the vehicle to mark the position of the currently installed power trunk hinge.
  3. With assistance, remove the bolts (x2) that attach the trunk power hinge to the trunk lid.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm socket
  4. Remove the nuts (x2) that attach the power trunk hinge to the body, and then remove the hinge from the vehicle.
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm socket

Install

  1. Position the new power trunk hinge on the vehicle, and then install the nuts (x2) that attach the power trunk hinge to the vehicle.
    24 Nm (17.7 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Align the hinge with the masking tape on the body.
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm socket
  2. With assistance, hand-tighten the bolts (x2) that attach the trunk power hinge to the trunk lid.
    Note
    Align the hinge with the masking tape on the trunk lid.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm socket
  3. Remove the masking tape from the vehicle.
  4. Install the trunk power strut and trunk garnish. See Power Strut - Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  5. Close the trunk, and then check trunk gap and flush.
    Note
    If necessary, adjust the hinge bolts and trunk latch to modify the trunk lid gap/flush. Assistance is recommended.
  6. When trunk lid gap/flush is finalized, open the trunk and torque the bolts that attach the power trunk hinge to the trunk lid.
    24 Nm (17.7 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm socket
  7. If necessary, apply touch-up paint to the trunk hinge bolt heads.
    Note
    Shake the paint container before applying paint.
