Dash Reinforcement Assembly (With Cowl)

Correction code: 10101014902 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Front Inner Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • This procedure can be completed without using a frame bench.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Noise Absorber - Motor Bay - Front (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the Hinge - Hood - LH (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Steering Column (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Hinge - Hood - LH (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the Accelerator (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the Carpet - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the Carpet - Front - RH (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the Subframe Assembly - Front (Dual Motor) (Remove and Install).
  9. Remove the Fender Assembly - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
  10. Remove the Hood (Remove and Install).
  11. Remove the Windshield (Reuse Humidity and Temperature Sensor) (Remove and Replace).
  12. Remove the A/C Compressor and Supermanifold (Remove and Replace).
  13. Remove the HVAC Assembly (Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace).
  14. Remove the Booster - Brake - Electromechanical (Heat Pump) (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  15. Remove the Brake Pedal (Remove and Replace).
  16. Remove the Module - Body Controller - Front (Dual Motor) (Non-Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace).
  17. Remove the Module - Body Controller - LH (LHD) (Heat Pump) (Remove and Replace).
  18. Remove the Panel - Lower Rear - Center Console (Remove and Replace).
  19. Remove the HV Battery (AWD) (Remove and Install).
  20. Remove the original component.
