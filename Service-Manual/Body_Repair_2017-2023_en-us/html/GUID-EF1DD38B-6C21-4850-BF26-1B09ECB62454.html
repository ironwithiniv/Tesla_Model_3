Approved Chemicals

Lists approved chemical products (adhesives, sealants, foam products, etc.) for use when performing collision repairs on Tesla vehicles.

Before applying any chemical product, review the manufacturer's Safety Data Sheet (SDS) for that product. Before beginning the repair procedure, review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment).
Note
Refer to the model-specific Service Manual for chemicals and fluids used in Service Manual Procedures.
Parent topic: Approved Parts, Tools, and Supplies

Structural Adhesives

Warning
Do not use non-approved structural adhesives for a structural repair on a Tesla vehicle.
Warning
Do not use structural adhesive as a substitute for welds, rivets, or any other fastening method listed in a Tesla Body Repair procedure. Apply structural adhesive only to the areas specified in the relevant Body Repair procedure.
Warning
Never use expired structural adhesive. Always check the expiration date on the structural adhesive before using.
The structural adhesives listed below are approved for structural repairs on Tesla vehicles:
