Brake Caliper - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 33010502 0.60 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See for safe and healthy working practices.

Equipment:
  • 1134520-00-A Kit, EPB Release, Handheld
  • 1129348-00-A XP-10 Power Supply, XP-10
Warning
Upon installation, make sure that the flexible brake hose is not twisted and can bend forward. The flexible brake hose must not make contact with the wheel arch liner when the vehicle is lowered.
Note
This procedure is applicable to both Performance and non-Performance rear brakes.
CAUTION
Brake fluid dissolves paint. Have clean towels and plenty of water standing by to wash spilled brake fluid off painted surfaces.
Note
Dispose of used brake fluid in a manner consistent with local environmental codes.
Note
This procedure is applicable to both Model 3 base and sport brake calipers.

Reuse

Remove

  1. Raise and support the vehicle. See Raise Vehicle - 2 Post Lift.
  2. Place the vehicle into EPB Service Mode. See Parking Brake - Caliper - Rear - LH (Release).
  3. Remove the LH rear wheel. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
  4. Disconnect the electrical connector from the LH rear brake caliper.
  5. Remove the bolt that attaches the electrical harness bracket to the LH rear brake caliper.
  6. Position the oil drain under the LH rear brake caliper area.
  7. Remove the LH rear jounce hose. See Brake Hose - Flexible - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove and discard the bolts that attach the LH rear caliper bracket to the knuckle, and then remove the caliper from the vehicle.

Install

  1. If installed, remove the brake pads from the rear brake caliper.
  2. Use needle-nose pliers to ensure that the slots in the rear brake caliper piston face are at the 12 o'clock and 6 o'clock positions.
  3. Install the brake pads into the rear brake caliper.
  4. Install the LH rear brake caliper onto the LH rear knuckle, and then install new bolts to attach the caliper to the knuckle.
    83 Nm (61.2 lbs-ft)
  5. Install a new LH rear jounce hose. See Brake Hose - Flexible - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the oil drain from underneath the vehicle.
  7. Connect the electrical connector to the LH rear brake caliper.
  8. Install the LH rear brake caliper electrical harness clips.
    5 Nm (3.7 lbs-ft)
  9. Lower the vehicle.
  10. Remove any wheel chocks, and then shift the vehicle into Park.
  11. Reinstall the vehicle firmware. See Software Reinstall - Touchscreen.
  12. Perform the bleed procedure for the LH rear brake caliper. See Brake Fluid Bleed - One Caliper.
  13. Install the LH rear wheel. See Wheel Assembly (Remove and Install).
  14. Perform a vehicle road test using a series of ABS-active stops and make sure proper brake pedal feel and performance is achieved.
