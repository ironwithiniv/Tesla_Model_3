Rear Torque Box Inner

Correction code: 10101802102 10101802302 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Rear Quarter Inner Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • This procedure can be completed without using a frame bench.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Carpet - Interior Complete (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the Door - Rear - LH (Remove and Install).
  3. Remove the Carpet - Rear Trunk (1-Piece) (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Trim - Side - Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the Trim - Side - Trunk - RH (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the Trim - Package Tray (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the Seatbelt - 2nd Row - LH (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the Wheel Arch Liner - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
  9. Remove the HV Battery (AWD) (Remove and Install).
  10. Remove the Subframe Assembly - Rear (Remove and Install).
  11. Remove the Rear Subframe Upper Crossmember.
  12. Remove the Third Row Seat Crossmember (Upper).
  13. Remove the Heelboard Crossmember.
  14. Remove the original component.

    or Factory Spot Welds

    or Drill through factory spot welds
    Note
    When drilling out spot welds, use a drill bit that creates a hole correctly sized for the fastener that will replace the spot weld.

    or Factory SPRs

    or Bolts

  15. Prepare for installation.
    or High Strength Structural Rivets, 6.5 mm
    • A = 33 mm.
    • B = 9 mm.
    • C = 15 mm.
    • D = 6 mm.
    • E = 33 mm.

    or Structural Bulb Rivets, 6.5 mm

    or Structural Countersunk Rivets, 6.5 mm

    or Countersunk Rivets, 4.8 mm Short

    or Bolts

    or Installation Spot Welds

  16. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  17. Install the new component or components.
    Torque the bolts to 24 Nm.
  18. Perform resistance spot welding.

    or Installation Spot Welds

    Warning
    Failure to follow all welding safety precautions, including the use of personal protective equipment, could result in serious injury or property damage. Only technicians who have completed Tesla’s approved welding training are authorized to weld structural components on Tesla vehicles.
    CAUTION
    Do not weld on a Tesla vehicle before performing the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure (refer to the vehicle-specific Service Manual for more information on the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure). Welding on a Tesla vehicle with an energized high or low voltage system might damage vehicle components.
  19. Install the Heelboard Crossmember.
  20. Install the Third Row Seat Crossmember (Upper).
  21. Install the Rear Subframe Upper Crossmember.
  22. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  23. Install the Subframe Assembly - Rear (Remove and Install).
  24. Install the HV Battery (AWD) (Remove and Install).
  25. Install the Wheel Arch Liner - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
  26. Install the Seatbelt - 2nd Row - LH (Remove and Replace).
  27. Install the Trim - Package Tray (Remove and Replace).
  28. Install the Trim - Side - Trunk - RH (Remove and Replace).
  29. Install the Trim - Side - Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace).
  30. Install the Carpet - Rear Trunk (1-Piece) (Remove and Replace).
  31. Install the Door - Rear - LH (Remove and Install).
  32. Install the Carpet - Interior Complete (Remove and Replace).
