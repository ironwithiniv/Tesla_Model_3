A-Pillar Outer Reinforcement (Section)

Correction code: LH: 10100102902 RH: 10100102302 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Body Side Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • This procedure can be completed without using a frame bench.

Parts List

When ordering parts, refer to the Parts Catalog and enter the VIN of the vehicle being repaired to find the correct parts (and the part numbers) for the vehicle. Alternatively, use the search function in the Parts Catalog to find a specific part for the vehicle.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Reservoir - Windshield Washer (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the Airbag - Curtain - LH (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Brightwork - Upper - LH (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Seal - Body - Side - Front - Primary - LH (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the IP Carrier (Remove and Install).
  6. Remove the Carpet - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the Hinge - Hood - LH (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the necessary portion of the Body Side Outer Assembly to expose the underlying component being replaced.
  9. Remove the Shotgun Outer.
  10. Cut the original component at the indicated locations.

    Cut Line

    Reference Line/Point

    • A = 75 mm
  11. Remove the original component.

    Factory Spot Weld

    Factory Spot Weld Area

  12. Cut the new component as shown to create a backing plate.
    Cut Line
    • A = 120 mm
  13. Prepare to install the backing plate.

    High Strength Structural Rivet, 6.5 mm

    Note
    Two rivets are installed on the top face of the panel and then two on each angled side. Offset the top rivet locations from the side rivets to prevent interference during installation.
  14. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the backing plate.
  15. Install the backing plate.
  16. Prepare for installation.

    Installation Spot Weld Area

    High Strength Structural Rivet, 6.5 mm

    Structural Countersunk Rivet, 6.5 mm

    Bolt
    Note
    Re-use the bolts that were removed from the Hinge Pillar and Hood Hinge areas to secure the new component until the structural adhesive has completely cured.
  17. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  18. Apply a bead of urethane sealant to the foam dam edges.
  19. Install the new component or components.
  20. Perform resistance spot welding.
    Warning
    Failure to follow all welding safety precautions, including the use of personal protective equipment, could result in serious injury or property damage. Only technicians who have completed Tesla’s approved welding training are authorized to weld structural components on Tesla vehicles.
    CAUTION
    Do not weld on a Tesla vehicle before performing the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure (refer to the vehicle-specific Service Manual for more information on the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure). Welding on a Tesla vehicle with an energized high or low voltage system might damage vehicle components.
  21. Install the new Shotgun Outer.
  22. Install the portion of the Body Side Outer Assembly that was removed to expose the component being replaced.
  23. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  24. Install the Hinge - Hood - LH (Remove and Replace).
  25. Install the Carpet - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  26. Install the IP Carrier (Remove and Install).
  27. Install the Seal - Body - Side - Front - Primary - LH (Remove and Replace).
  28. Install the Brightwork - Upper - LH (Remove and Replace).
  29. Install the Airbag - Curtain - LH (Remove and Replace).
  30. Install the Reservoir - Windshield Washer (Remove and Replace).
