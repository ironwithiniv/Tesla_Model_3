Mirror - Rear View (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 15050202 0.06 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See Flat Rate Times to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure wearing proper PPE when performing the below procedure. NOTE: See for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2023-07-20: Added a video in step 2 of Remove part.

Remove

  1. Remove the lower quad camera cover. See Quad Camera Cover - Lower (Remove and Replace).
  2. Grip the rear view mirror stalk, and then turn counterclockwise to release the rear view mirror.
    CAUTION
    Do not turn the rear view mirror itself.
    CAUTION
    Do not yet completely remove the mirror, as an electrical harness is still attached.
    Warning
    The video(s) included in this procedure are meant as an overview for supplemental purposes only. Follow all of the steps listed in the procedure to avoid damage to components and/or personal injury.
  3. Disconnect the electrical connector from the rear view mirror, and then remove the mirror from the vehicle.

Install

  1. Connect the electrical connector to the rear view mirror.
  2. Grip the rear view mirror stalk, and then turn clockwise to install the rear view mirror.
    CAUTION
    Do not turn the rear view mirror itself.
  3. Install the lower quad camera cover. See Quad Camera Cover - Lower (Remove and Replace).
