Parcel Shelf Crossmember Assembly (Without Center Trough)

Correction code: 10101917902 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Rear Quarter Inner Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • This procedure can be completed without using a frame bench.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Trunk (Remove and Install).
  2. Remove the Garnish - Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Front Carpet - Rear Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Trim - Side - Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the Glass - Backlight (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the Amplifier - Audio - External (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the Woofer - Parcel Shelf (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the Retractor - Seatbelt - 2nd Row - Outboard (Single Side) (Remove and Replace).
  9. Remove the Rear Center Trough.
  10. Remove the original component.
    or Factory Spot Welds

    or Factory SPRs

    or Drill through factory spot welds
    Note
    When drilling out spot welds, use a drill bit that creates a hole correctly sized for the fastener that will replace the spot weld.
  11. Prepare for installation.
    or High Strength Structural Rivets, 6.5 mm

    or Structural Bulb Rivets, 6.5 mm

    or Rivets, 4.8 mm

    or Flow Form Rivets, S18

  12. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  13. Install the new component or components.
  14. Install the Rear Center Trough.
  15. Install the Retractor - Seatbelt - 2nd Row - Outboard (Single Side) (Remove and Replace).
  16. Install the Woofer - Parcel Shelf (Remove and Replace).
  17. Install the Amplifier - Audio - External (Remove and Replace).
  18. Install the Glass - Backlight (Remove and Replace).
  19. Install the Trim - Side - Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace).
  20. Install the Front Carpet - Rear Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  21. Install the Garnish - Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  22. Install the Trunk (Remove and Install).
