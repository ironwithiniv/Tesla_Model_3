Amplifier - Audio - External (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 2121010202 FRT 0.24 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2024-03-19: Updated the audio amplifier bolt torque from 10 Nm to 6 Nm.

Torque Specifications

Table 1. Torque Specifications: Amplifier - Audio - External (Remove and Replace)
Description Torque Value Recommended Tools Reuse/Replace Notes
Bolt that attaches the audio amplifier to the body
6 Nm (4.4 lbs-ft)
  • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
  • 10 mm socket
  • 4 in extension
  • Ratchet/torque wrench
 Reuse

Remove

  1. Remove the rear underhood apron. See Underhood Apron - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  2. Disconnect the LV battery power. See .
  3. Remove the trunk load floor. See .
  4. Remove the trunk sill trim. See Trim - Trunk Sill (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the RH load floor bracket. See Bracket - Trunk Load Floor - LH (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the trunk garnish. See Garnish - Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  7. Release the clip that attaches the RH trunk side trim to the body.
  8. Pull the RH trunk side trim carpet away from the body to gain access to the working area.
  9. Disconnect the connectors (x3) from the RH rear audio amplifier.
  10. Remove the bolt that attaches the audio amplifier to the body, and then remove the audio amplifier from the vehicle.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 10 mm socket
    • 4 in extension
    • Ratchet/torque wrench

Install

  1. Insert the tabs (x2) of the amplifier into the slots and then install the bolt that attaches the audio amplifier to the body.
    6 Nm (4.4 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 10 mm socket
    • 4 in extension
    • Ratchet/torque wrench
  2. Connect the connectors (x3) to the RH rear audio amplifier.
  3. Return the RH trunk side trim carpet back to its original position.
  4. Install the clip that attaches the RH trunk side trim to the body.
  5. Install the trunk garnish. See Garnish - Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  6. Install the RH load floor bracket. See Bracket - Trunk Load Floor - LH (Remove and Replace).
  7. Install the trunk sill trim. See Trim - Trunk Sill (Remove and Replace).
  8. Connect the LV battery power. See .
  9. Install the rear underhood apron. See Underhood Apron - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  10. Install the trunk load floor. See .
