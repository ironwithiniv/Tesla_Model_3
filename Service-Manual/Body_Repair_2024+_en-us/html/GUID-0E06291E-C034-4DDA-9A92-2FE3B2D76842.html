Front Header Reinforcement Connector

Correction code: 10102702802 10102702902 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Roof Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • This procedure can be completed without using a frame bench.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Windshield (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the Glass - Roof - Fixed (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Airbag - Curtain - RH (RHD) (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Airbag - Curtain - RH (RHD) (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the Shroud - Upper - Steering Column (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the Center Console (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the Carpet - Front - RH (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the Carpet - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  9. Remove the Carpet - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  10. Remove the original component.
    or Factory Spot Welds
  11. Put the new component into position and clamp it into place.
    Note
    Position the new component as shown, using the trim holes on the underside of each Header Reinforcement and clamp in place.
    • A = 1264 mm
  12. Prepare for installation.
    or High Strength Structural Rivets, 6.5 mm

    or Structural Bulb Rivets, 6.5 mm

    or Installation Spot Welds

  13. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  14. Install the new component or components.
  15. Perform resistance spot welding.
    or Installation Spot Welds
    Warning
    Failure to follow all welding safety precautions, including the use of personal protective equipment, could result in serious injury or property damage. Only technicians who have completed Tesla’s approved welding training are authorized to weld structural components on Tesla vehicles.
    CAUTION
    Do not weld on a Tesla vehicle before performing the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure (refer to the vehicle-specific Service Manual for more information on the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure). Welding on a Tesla vehicle with an energized high or low voltage system might damage vehicle components.
  16. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  17. Install the Carpet - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  18. Install the Carpet - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  19. Install the Carpet - Front - RH (Remove and Replace).
  20. Install the Center Console (Remove and Replace).
  21. Install the Shroud - Upper - Steering Column (Remove and Replace).
  22. Install the Airbag - Curtain - RH (RHD) (Remove and Replace).
  23. Install the Airbag - Curtain - RH (RHD) (Remove and Replace).
  24. Install the Glass - Roof - Fixed (Remove and Replace).
  25. Install the Windshield (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
