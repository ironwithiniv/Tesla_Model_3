Hinge - Door - Rear - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1133050072 FRT 0.36 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Remove

  1. Open all four doors and lower all four windows.
  2. Apply tape to the rear door to mark the lower hinge location.
  3. Apply tape to the body to mark the rear lower hinge location.
  4. Use a jack to support the LH rear door from the bottom.
    Note
    Make sure to use soft material to protect the painted surface.
  5. Remove the bolts (x2) that attach the lower door hinge to the LH rear door.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  6. Remove the bolts (x2) that attach the lower door hinge to the body.
    Note
    Support the door hinge to make sure it does not fall in the door jam.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm socket
  7. Carefully remove the LH rear door lower hinge from the vehicle.

Install

  1. Align the new lower hinge with the applied tape on the body, and then install the bolts (x2) that attach the lower door hinge to body hand tight.
  2. Align the new lower hinge with the applied tape on the LH rear door, and then install the bolts (x2) that attach the lower hinge to the LH rear door hand tight.
  3. Tighten the bolts (x2) that attach the LH rear door lower hinge to the body.
    27 Nm (19.9 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 13 mm socket
  4. Tighten the bolts (x2) that attach the LH rear door lower hinge to the door.
    27 Nm (19.9 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  5. Remove the jack that supports the LH rear door.
  6. Close the LH rear door and check for gap and flushness. Adjust as necessary.
    Note
    See
  7. Remove the tape from the LH body side and the LH rear door.
  8. Clean the LH rear door side upper hinge bolts with IPA wipes.
    Note
    Allow 1 minute for the IPA to dry.
  9. Apply touch up paint on the LH rear door hinge bolts as needed.
    Note
    Shake the touch up paint before applying. Allow the first coat to dry for 20 minutes before applying and second coat.
  10. Raise all four windows and close all four doors.
