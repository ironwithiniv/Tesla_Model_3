Sill Plate - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1205010062 FRT 0.12 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See for safe and healthy working practices.

Equipment:
  • 1049517-00-A Seam Roller, Silicone, 5"

Remove

  1. Open the LH rear door and lower the LH rear window.
  2. Use IPA wipes to clean around the sill plate.
  3. Apply masking tape around the perimeter of the sill plate to mark the installation positioning.
    Note
    Place the tape as closely around the sill plate as possible.
    Warning
    The video(s) included in this procedure are meant as an overview for supplemental purposes only. Follow all of the steps listed in the procedure to avoid damage to components and/or personal injury.
  4. Use a plastic trim tool to carefully pry up and bend the sill plate. Pull out the door sill plate when all adhesive is separated.
    CAUTION
    Be careful not to damage the vehicle trim or body.
  5. Use IPA wipes to clean off any remaining adhesive.

Install

  1. Remove the adhesive backing from the new sill plate.
  2. Using the masking tape as a guide, carefully place the new sill plate into place and press down on it to adhere it to the vehicle.
  3. Use a seam roller to fully wet out the adhesive.
  4. Remove the protective film from the sill plate.
  5. Remove the masking tape from the door sill.
  6. Raise the LH rear window and close the LH rear door.
