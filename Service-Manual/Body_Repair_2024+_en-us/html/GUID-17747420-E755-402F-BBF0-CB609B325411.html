B-Pillar Outer (Complete)

Correction code: Pending Pending NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Body Side Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • Properly mount the vehicle on a frame bench when performing this procedure.

Parts List

Quantity Description Image / Notes

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Glass - Backlight (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the Glass - Roof - Fixed (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Door - Rear - LH (Remove and Install).
  4. Remove the Airbag - Curtain - LH (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the Cover - Rocker Panel - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the Retractor - Seatbelt - Driver (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the Height Adjuster - Seatbelt - LH (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the Sensor - Impact Accelerometer - B-Pillar - LH (Remove and Replace).
  9. Remove the Seal - Body - Side - Front - Primary - LH (Remove and Replace).
  10. Remove the Seal - Body - Side - Rear - Primary - LH (Remove and Replace).
  11. Detach the harness from the B-Pillar.
  12. Remove the necessary portion of the Body Side Outer to expose the underlying component being replaced.
  13. Remove the B-Pillar Outer (Complete) from the new Door Ring Outer service as indicated below to make the new component.
  14. Remove the original component.

    or Factory Spot Welds

  15. Prepare for installation.
    Note
    A red X indicates a location where a factory-installed fastener is not being replaced. Secure this location using structural adhesive only.
    or Countersunk Rivets, 4.8 mm Short
    • A = 30 mm
    • B = 19 mm
    • C = 46 mm
    • D = 33 mm
    • E = 51 mm
    • F = 36 mm
    or Structural Bulb Rivets, 6.5 mm
    • G = 10 mm

    or Installation Spot Welds

  16. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  17. Install the new component or components.
  18. Perform resistance spot welding.
    or Installation Spot Welds
    Warning
    Failure to follow all welding safety precautions, including the use of personal protective equipment, could result in serious injury or property damage. Only technicians who have completed Tesla’s approved welding training are authorized to weld structural components on Tesla vehicles.
    CAUTION
    Do not weld on a Tesla vehicle before performing the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure (refer to the vehicle-specific Service Manual for more information on the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure). Welding on a Tesla vehicle with an energized high or low voltage system might damage vehicle components.
  19. Install the portion of the Body Side Outer that was removed to expose the component being replaced.
  20. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  21. Attach the harness to the B-Pillar.
  22. Install the Seal - Body - Side - Rear - Primary - LH (Remove and Replace).
  23. Install the Seal - Body - Side - Front - Primary - LH (Remove and Replace).
  24. Install the Sensor - Impact Accelerometer - B-Pillar - LH (Remove and Replace).
  25. Install the Height Adjuster - Seatbelt - LH (Remove and Replace).
  26. Install the Retractor - Seatbelt - Driver (Remove and Replace).
  27. Install the Cover - Rocker Panel - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  28. Install the Airbag - Curtain - LH (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  29. Install the Door - Rear - LH (Remove and Install).
  30. Install the Glass - Roof - Fixed (Remove and Replace).
  31. Install the Glass - Backlight (Remove and Replace).
