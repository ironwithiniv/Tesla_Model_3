Front Shock Tower Support (Front)

Correction code: 10101015402 10101002902 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Front Inner Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • Properly mount the vehicle on a frame bench when performing this procedure.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Fender Assembly - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
  2. Remove the Carrier - Bumper - Front (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Cooling Fan Module (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Radiator (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the Panel - Aero Shield - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the Knuckle - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the Damper - Front - LH (Dual Motor) (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the Mount - Front Upper Control Arm - LH (Remove and Replace).
  9. Remove the Brake Hydraulic Control Unit (Remove and Replace).
  10. Remove the Assembly - Brake Booster and Master Cylinder (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  11. Remove the Assembly - Brake Lines - Front (Remove and Install).
  12. Remove the original component.
    or Factory Spot Welds
  13. Prepare for installation.
    Note
    A red X indicates a location where a factory-installed fastener is not being replaced. Secure this location using structural adhesive only.
    or High Strength Structural Rivets, 6.5 mm
    • A = 16 mm.
    • B = 9 mm.

    or Structural Bulb Rivets, 6.5 mm

    or Bolts

    Nut

  14. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  15. Install the new component or components.
    Torque the bolts to 22 Nm.
  16. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  17. Install the Assembly - Brake Lines - Front (Remove and Install).
  18. Install the Assembly - Brake Booster and Master Cylinder (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  19. Install the Brake Hydraulic Control Unit (Remove and Replace).
  20. Install the Mount - Front Upper Control Arm - LH (Remove and Replace).
  21. Install the Damper - Front - LH (Dual Motor) (Remove and Replace).
  22. Install the Knuckle - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  23. Install the Panel - Aero Shield - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  24. Install the Radiator (Remove and Replace).
  25. Install the Cooling Fan Module (Remove and Replace).
  26. Install the Carrier - Bumper - Front (Remove and Replace).
  27. Install the Fender Assembly - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
