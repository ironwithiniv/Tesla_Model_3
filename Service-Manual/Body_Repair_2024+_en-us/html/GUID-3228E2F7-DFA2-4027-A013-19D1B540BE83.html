Roof Rail Extension

Correction code: 10101911202 10101911302 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Rear Quarter Inner Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • This procedure can be completed without using a frame bench.

Parts List

Quantity Description Image / Notes
1 EXTN, ROOF RAIL (Roof Rail Extension)
1 Structural Flange Rivet
1 Countersunk Rivet, 4.8 mm Short

When ordering parts, refer to the Parts Catalog and enter the VIN of the vehicle being repaired to find the correct parts (and the part numbers) for the vehicle. Alternatively, use the search function in the Parts Catalog to find a specific part for the vehicle.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Glass - Roof - Fixed (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the Glass - Backlight (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Glass - Body - Rear Quarter - LH (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Bracket - Rear Wing - LH (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the Door - Rear - LH (Remove and Install).
  6. Remove the Hinge - Door - Rear - Upper - LH (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the Hinge - Door - Rear - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the Wheel Arch Liner - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
  9. Remove the Cover - Rocker Panel - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  10. Remove the Trunk (Remove and Install).
  11. Remove the Hinge - Power Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace).
  12. Remove the Door Assembly - Charge Port (NA) (Remove and Replace).
  13. Remove the Air Extractor (Remove and Replace).
  14. Remove the Seal - Body - Side - Front - Primary - LH (Remove and Replace).
  15. Remove the Seal - Body - Side - Rear - Primary - LH (Remove and Replace).
  16. Remove the Seal - Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  17. Remove the Airbag - Curtain - RH (RHD) (Remove and Replace).
  18. Remove the necessary portion of the Body Side Outer to expose the underlying component being replaced.
  19. Remove the original component.
    or Factory Spot Welds
  20. Prepare for installation.

    or Countersunk Rivets, 4.8 mm Short

    or Structural Flange Rivets, 6.5 mm

    or Installation Spot Welds

  21. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  22. Install the new component or components.
  23. Perform resistance spot welding.
    Warning
    Failure to follow all welding safety precautions, including the use of personal protective equipment, could result in serious injury or property damage. Only technicians who have completed Tesla’s approved welding training are authorized to weld structural components on Tesla vehicles.
    CAUTION
    Do not weld on a Tesla vehicle before performing the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure (refer to the vehicle-specific Service Manual for more information on the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure). Welding on a Tesla vehicle with an energized high or low voltage system might damage vehicle components.
  24. Install the necessary portion of the Body Side Outer that was removed to expose the component being replaced.
  25. Install the Airbag - Curtain - RH (RHD) (Remove and Replace).
  26. Install the Seal - Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  27. Install the Seal - Body - Side - Rear - Primary - LH (Remove and Replace).
  28. Install the Seal - Body - Side - Front - Primary - LH (Remove and Replace).
  29. Install the Air Extractor (Remove and Replace).
  30. Install the Door Assembly - Charge Port (NA) (Remove and Replace).
  31. Install the Hinge - Power Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace).
  32. Install the Trunk (Remove and Install).
  33. Install the Cover - Rocker Panel - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  34. Install the Wheel Arch Liner - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
  35. Install the Hinge - Door - Rear - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  36. Install the Hinge - Door - Rear - Upper - LH (Remove and Replace).
  37. Install the Door - Rear - LH (Remove and Install).
  38. Install the Bracket - Rear Wing - LH (Remove and Replace).
  39. Install the Glass - Body - Rear Quarter - LH (Remove and Replace).
  40. Install the Glass - Backlight (Remove and Replace).
  41. Install the Glass - Roof - Fixed (Remove and Replace).
