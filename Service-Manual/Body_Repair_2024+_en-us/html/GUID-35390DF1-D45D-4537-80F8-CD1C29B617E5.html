Hinge Pillar

Correction code: 10100100602 10100101102 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Body Side Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • Properly mount the vehicle on a frame bench when performing this procedure.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Cover - Rocker Panel - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the Brake Line Assembly - Side Sill (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Module - Body Controller - RH (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Module - Body Controller - LH (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the Carpet - Front - RH (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the Carpet - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the Beam - Cross Car (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the Sill Plate - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  9. Remove the Windshield (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  10. Remove the Hood Assembly (Remove and Replace).
  11. Remove the Fender Assembly - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
  12. Remove the Door - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
  13. Remove the necessary portion of the Body Side Outer.
  14. Remove the Shotgun Outer.
  15. Cut the new Door Ring Outer as indicated below to make the new component.
  16. Remove the original component.

    or Factory Spot Welds

    or Drill through factory spot welds
    Note
    When drilling out spot welds, use a drill bit that creates a hole correctly sized for the fastener that will replace the spot weld.
  17. Remove the drill through factory spot welds shown, and separate the bottom portion of the A-Pillar Outer from the underlying panel.
    or Drill through factory spot welds
    Note
    When drilling out spot welds, use a drill bit that creates a hole correctly sized for the fastener that will replace the spot weld.
    Note
    The new Hinge Pillar service part is installed under the bottom portion of the A-Pillar Outer.
  18. Prepare for installation.
    Note
    A red X indicates a location where a factory-installed fastener is not being replaced. Secure this location using structural adhesive only.

    or Installation Spot Welds

    or Rivets, 4.8 mm

    or Structural Countersunk Rivets, 6.5 mm

    or High Strength Structural Rivets, 6.5 mm
    Note
    The new Hinge Pillar service part is installed under the bottom portion of the A-Pillar Outer.
    Note
    Temporarily install the door hinge bolts before installing the rivets. Torque the bolts to 27 Nm.
  19. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  20. Install the new component or components.
  21. Perform resistance spot welding.
    or Installation Spot Welds
    Warning
    Failure to follow all welding safety precautions, including the use of personal protective equipment, could result in serious injury or property damage. Only technicians who have completed Tesla’s approved welding training are authorized to weld structural components on Tesla vehicles.
    CAUTION
    Do not weld on a Tesla vehicle before performing the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure (refer to the vehicle-specific Service Manual for more information on the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure). Welding on a Tesla vehicle with an energized high or low voltage system might damage vehicle components.
  22. Install the necessary portion of the Body Side Outer.
  23. Install the Shotgun Outer.
  24. Install the fasteners as indicated.

    or Flow Form Rivets, S18

    or Flow Form Rivets, S38

  25. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  26. Install the Door - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
  27. Install the Fender Assembly - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
  28. Install the Hood Assembly (Remove and Replace).
  29. Install the Windshield (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  30. Install the Sill Plate - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  31. Install the Beam - Cross Car (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  32. Install the Carpet - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  33. Install the Carpet - Front - RH (Remove and Replace).
  34. Install the Module - Body Controller - LH (Remove and Replace).
  35. Install the Module - Body Controller - RH (Remove and Replace).
  36. Install the Brake Line Assembly - Side Sill (Remove and Replace).
  37. Install the Cover - Rocker Panel - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace).
