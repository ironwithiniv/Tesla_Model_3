Rear Subframe Crossmember (Upper)

Correction code: 10101815902 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Rear End Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • This procedure can be completed without using a frame bench.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Bumper Beam - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the Carpet - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Front Carpet - Rear Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Trim - Side - Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the Subframe Assembly - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the Trunk Floor Assembly (Complete).
  7. Remove the original component.
    Factory Spot Weld
    Factory SPR
    or Drill through factory spot welds
    Note
    When drilling out spot welds, use a drill bit that creates a hole correctly sized for the fastener that will replace the spot weld.
    Bolt
  8. Prepare for installation.

    High Strength Structural Rivet, 6.5 mm

    Bolt
  9. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  10. Install the new component or components.
    Torque the bolts to 24 Nm.
  11. Install the Trunk Floor Assembly (Complete).
  12. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  13. Install the Subframe Assembly - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  14. Install the Trim - Side - Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace).
  15. Install the Front Carpet - Rear Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  16. Install the Carpet - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  17. Install the Bumper Beam - Rear (Remove and Replace).
