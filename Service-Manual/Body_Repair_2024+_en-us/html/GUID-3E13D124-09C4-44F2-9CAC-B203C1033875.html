Harness - Body - LH (NA/EU) (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1710040052 FRT 3.54 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See for safe and healthy working practices.

Remove

  1. Raise and support vehicle.
  2. Open all four doors.
  3. Open trunk from exterior switch.
  4. Lower all four windows.
  5. Insert special removal tool into the bottom of the retractor, and then release it from LH front seat.
  6. Move LH and RH front seat forward and remove the front seat rear bolts.
  7. Move LH and RH front seat backward and remove the rear seat front bolts.
  8. Place vehicle into tow mode.
  9. Power off the vehicle from the center display.
  10. Remove the rear underhood apron. See Underhood Apron - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  11. Disconnect the LV battery. See .
  12. Remove the rear fascia . See Fascia Assembly - Rear (Remove and Install).
  13. Disconnect the electrical harness from the rear Bluetooth passive entry antenna connector.
  14. Remove the clips that attach the LH body harness to the body in the rear fascia and rear subframe areas.
  15. Disconnect the electrical harness from the rear subframe electrical harness connector and the LH electric parking brake connector.
  16. Release the clips that attach the electrical harness to the body, and then push the rear subframe portion of the electrical harness through the LH side trunk bulkhead.
    Note
    The LH body harness passes through the trunk near the rear section of the rear LH wheel liner area.
  17. Remove lift arms from below vehicle.
  18. Push vehicle away from lift.
  19. Place vehicle to wheel chocks.
  20. Remove the RH footwell cover. See .
  21. Remove the RH instrument panel end cap. See
  22. Remove the RH upper A-pillar trim. See
  23. Remove the RH lower A-pillar trim. See
  24. Remove the driver seats. See Seat Assembly - 1st Row - No Lumbar - RH (Remove and Replace)
  25. Repeat step 20through step 24 for the RH side of the vehicle.
  26. Remove the 2nd row LH seat back assembly. See Seatback - 2nd Row - LH (Remove and Replace)
  27. Remove the 2nd row RH seat back assembly. See
  28. Remove the LH side rear trunk trim. See Trim - Side - Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace)
  29. Remove the LH lower B-pillar trim. See Trim - B-Pillar - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace)
  30. Remove the LH middle B-pillar trim. See Trim - B-Pillar - Middle - LH (Remove and Replace)
  31. Remove the LH B-pillar applique. See Applique - B-Pillar - LH (Remove and Replace)
  32. Remove the sill rear trunk trim. See Trim - Trunk Sill (Remove and Replace)
  33. Release the trunk carpet clips from the body studs under the 2nd row seat cushion.
  34. Release the clips that attach the trunk carpet to the body, and then remove the trunk carpet from the vehicle.
  35. Remove the LH side rear trunk trim. See Trim - Side - Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace).
  36. Remove the center console assembly. See Center Console (Remove and Replace).
  37. Remove LH front main cabin carpet from vehicle. See Carpet - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  38. Remove RH front main cabin carpet from vehicle. See Carpet - Front - RH (Remove and Replace).
  39. Remove RH 2nd row floor duct. See
  40. Remove LH 2nd row floor duct. See
  41. Disconnect the LH headlight connector.
  42. Partially release front roof headliner from body.
  43. Remove connector from security controller and disconnect clips (X9) that secures LH body wiring harness to LH A-pillar to body.
  44. Disconnect front harness white connector (X909) from the LH body harness and separate it from the front left body controller.
  45. Disconnect Body 1 (J2), Body 2 (J4) and Body 3 (J5), connectors from the LH front body controller.
  46. Release LH body harness from LH body controller and A-pillar and move aside.
  47. Disconnect the electrical harness from the rear subframe electrical harness connector and the LH electric parking brake connector.
  48. Release the clips that attach the electrical harness to the body under the front LH seat and RH lower B-pillar areas.
  49. Disconnect LH body harness connector from RCM.
  50. Release the clips that attach the electrical harness to the body in the LH lower B-pillar areas.
  51. Disconnect the electrical harness from the connectors in the RH lower B-pillar area.
  52. Remove LH harness clips under front seats and center console, and disconnect LH body harness connector from LH rear door harness connector at outer B-pillar area.
  53. Disconnect harness connectors from LH retractor.
  54. Disconnect B-pillar impact accelerometer sensor connector.
  55. Release the clips that attach the harness to the body in the B-pillar area.
  56. Remove and discard the bolt that attaches the electrical harness ground strap to the body in the LH lower B-pillar area.
  57. Remove LH camera wire harness from B-pillar area and secure it to body clip.
  58. Disconnect 2nd row 60 seatback heater harness.
  59. Disconnect LH rear seat belt buckle connector.
  60. Disconnect clip securing LH rear seatbelt buckle harness to vehicle body.
  61. Disconnect harness connector for LH side curtain airbag.
  62. Disconnect LH 2nd row seat belt retractor connector.
  63. Remove LH body harness clips around LH side curtain airbag and retractor area.
  64. Remove LH body harness clips around lower C-pillar area.
  65. Disconnect LH power strut connector.
  66. Disconnect charge port ECU connector from LH body harness.
  67. Disconnect DC logic connector from charge port assembly.
  68. Release charge port actuator logic connector from charge port assembly.
  69. Release AC logic connector from charge port assembly.
  70. Remove the LH body harness ground strap below LH tail lamp.
  71. Remove LH body harness clips around LH side trunk area.
  72. Remove LH body harness from vehicle.

Install

  1. Install LH body harness clips around LH side trunk area.
  2. Install the LH body harness ground strap below LH tail lamp.
  3. Connect AC logic connector to charge port assembly.
  4. Connect charge port actuator logic connector to charge port assembly.
  5. Connect DC logic connector to charge port assembly.
  6. Connect charge port ECU connector to LH body harness.
  7. Connect LH power strut connector.
  8. Install LH body harness clips to lower C-pillar area.
  9. Install LH body harness clips to LH side curtain airbag and retractor area.
  10. Connect LH 2nd row seat belt retractor connector.
  11. Connect harness connector for LH side curtain airbag.
  12. Connect clip securing LH rear seatbelt buckle harness to vehicle body.
  13. Install LH camera wire harness to B-pillar area and secure it to body clip.
  14. Install the new bolt that attaches the electrical harness ground strap to the body in the LH lower B-pillar area.
  15. Connect the clips that attach the harness to the body in the B-pillar area.
  16. Connect B-pillar impact accelerometer sensor connector.
  17. Connect harness connectors from LH retractor.
  18. Install LH harness clips under front seats and center console, and connect LH body harness connector from LH rear door harness connector at outer B-pillar area.
  19. Connect the electrical harness to the connectors in the RH lower B-pillar area.
  20. Connect the clips that attach the electrical harness to the body in the LH lower B-pillar areas.
  21. Connect LH body harness connector to RCM.
  22. Connect the clips that attach the electrical harness to the body under the front LH seat and RH lower B-pillar areas.
  23. Connect the electrical harness to the rear subframe electrical harness connector and the LH electric parking brake connector.
  24. Connect LH body harness to LH body controller and A-pillar.
  25. Connect Body 1 (J2), Body 2 (J4) and Body 3 (J5), connectors to the LH front body controller.
  26. Connect front harness white connector (X909) to the LH body harness.
  27. Install connector to security controller and connect clips (X9) that secures LH body wiring harness to LH A-pillar to body.
  28. Connect the LH headlight connector.
  29. Install LH 2nd row floor duct. See
  30. Install RH 2nd row floor duct. See
  31. Install RH front main cabin carpet from vehicle. See Carpet - Front - RH (Remove and Replace).
  32. Install LH front main cabin carpet from vehicle. See Carpet - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  33. Install the center console assembly. See Center Console (Remove and Replace).
  34. Install the LH side rear trunk trim. See Trim - Side - Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace).
  35. Connect the clips that attach the trunk carpet to the body, and then install the trunk carpet to the vehicle.
  36. Connect the trunk carpet clips to the body studs under the 2nd row seat cushion.
  37. Install the sill rear trunk trim. See Trim - Trunk Sill (Remove and Replace)
  38. Install the LH B-pillar applique. See Applique - B-Pillar - LH (Remove and Replace)
  39. Install the LH middle B-pillar trim. See Trim - B-Pillar - Middle - LH (Remove and Replace)
  40. Install the LH lower B-pillar trim. See Trim - B-Pillar - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace)
  41. Install the LH side rear trunk trim. See Trim - Side - Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace)
  42. Install the 2nd row RH seat back assembly. See
  43. Install the 2nd row LH seat back assembly. See Seatback - 2nd Row - LH (Remove and Replace)
  44. Repeat step 38 through step 41 for the RH side of the vehicle.
  45. Install the driver seats. See Seat Assembly - 1st Row - No Lumbar - RH (Remove and Replace)
  46. Install the RH lower A-pillar trim. See
  47. Install the RH upper A-pillar trim. See
  48. Install the RH instrument panel end cap. See
  49. Install the RH footwell cover. See .
  50. Place vehicle to wheel chocks.
  51. Install lift arms from below vehicle.
  52. Connect the clips that attach the electrical harness to the body, and then pull the rear subframe portion of the electrical harness through the LH side trunk bulkhead.
    Note
    The LH body harness passes through the trunk near the rear section of the rear LH wheel liner area.
  53. Connect the electrical harness to the rear subframe electrical harness connector and the LH electric parking brake connector.
  54. Install the clips that attach the LH body harness to the body in the rear fascia and rear subframe areas.
  55. Connect the electrical harness to the rear Bluetooth passive entry antenna connector.
  56. Install the rear fascia . See Fascia Assembly - Rear (Remove and Install).
  57. Connect the LV battery. See .
  58. Install the rear underhood apron. See Underhood Apron - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  59. Power on the vehicle from the center display.
  60. Exit vehicle of tow mode.
  61. Move LH and RH front seat forward and install the rear seat front bolts.
  62. Move LH and RH front seat backward and install the front seat rear bolts.
  63. Remove special removal tool from the bottom of the retractor.
  64. Raise all four windows.
  65. Close trunk from exterior switch.
  66. Close all four doors.
