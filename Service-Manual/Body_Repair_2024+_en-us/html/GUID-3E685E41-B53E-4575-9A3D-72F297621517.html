Heelboard Crossmember (Front)

Correction code: 10101800402 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Center Under Body Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • This procedure can be completed without using a frame bench.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the HV Battery (RWD) (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the Carpet - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Foam - Seat Back - 2nd Row - RH (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Busbar - Charge Port to HV Battery (GB) (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the original component.
    or Factory Spot Welds

    or Bolts

  6. Prepare for installation.

    or High Strength Structural Rivets, 6.5 mm

    or Countersunk Rivets, 4.8 mm Short

    or Bolts

    or Installation Spot Welds

  7. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  8. Install the new component or components.
    Torque the bolts to 19 Nm.
  9. Perform resistance spot welding.
    or Installation Spot Welds
    Warning
    Failure to follow all welding safety precautions, including the use of personal protective equipment, could result in serious injury or property damage. Only technicians who have completed Tesla’s approved welding training are authorized to weld structural components on Tesla vehicles.
    CAUTION
    Do not weld on a Tesla vehicle before performing the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure (refer to the vehicle-specific Service Manual for more information on the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure). Welding on a Tesla vehicle with an energized high or low voltage system might damage vehicle components.
  10. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  11. Install the Busbar - Charge Port to HV Battery (GB) (Remove and Replace).
  12. Install the Foam - Seat Back - 2nd Row - RH (Remove and Replace).
  13. Install the Carpet - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  14. Install the HV Battery (RWD) (Remove and Replace).
