Rear Spring and Subframe Mount

Correction code: 10101811502 10101811602 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Rear End Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • Properly mount the vehicle on a frame bench when performing this procedure.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Bumper Beam - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the Front Carpet - Rear Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Tub Carpet - Rear Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Trim - Side - Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the Busbar - Charge Port to HV Battery (GB) (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the Wheel Arch Liner - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the Subframe Assembly - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the Noise Absorber - Motor Bay - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  9. Remove the Trunk Floor Assembly (Complete).
  10. Remove the Rear Damper Mount Upper Assembly with Stiffeners.
  11. Cut the Wheelhouse Inner as shown and bend back the cut section to expose the spot welds underneath.
    Cut Line
    • A = 92 mm
  12. Remove the original component.

    or Factory SPRs

    or Factory Spot Welds

    or Bolts

  13. Cut the original component as shown, then remove the section shown in red to expose the fasteners underneath.
    Cut Line
  14. Remove the original component.

    or Factory SPRs

    or Factory Spot Welds

  15. Secure the flange of the service part using Installation Spot Welds.
    or Installation Spot Welds
  16. Perform resistance spot welding.
    or Installation Spot Welds
    Warning
    Failure to follow all welding safety precautions, including the use of personal protective equipment, could result in serious injury or property damage. Only technicians who have completed Tesla’s approved welding training are authorized to weld structural components on Tesla vehicles.
    CAUTION
    Do not weld on a Tesla vehicle before performing the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure (refer to the vehicle-specific Service Manual for more information on the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure). Welding on a Tesla vehicle with an energized high or low voltage system might damage vehicle components.
  17. Prepare for installation.
    Note
    A red X indicates a location where a factory-installed fastener is not being replaced. Secure this location using structural adhesive only.

  18. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  19. Install the new component or components.
    Torque the bolts to 24 Nm.
  20. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the the cut section of the Wheelhouse Inner.
  21. Bend the cut section of the Wheelhouse Inner as shown and to cover the fasteners underneath.
  22. Install the Rear Damper Mount Upper Assembly with Stiffeners.
  23. Install the Trunk Floor Assembly (Complete).
  24. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  25. Install the Noise Absorber - Motor Bay - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  26. Install the Subframe Assembly - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  27. Install the Wheel Arch Liner - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
  28. Install the Busbar - Charge Port to HV Battery (GB) (Remove and Replace).
  29. Install the Trim - Side - Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace).
  30. Install the Tub Carpet - Rear Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  31. Install the Front Carpet - Rear Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  32. Install the Bumper Beam - Rear (Remove and Replace).
