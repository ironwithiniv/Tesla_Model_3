Link - Fore - Upper - Rear - RH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 3103030312 FRT 2.52 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See for safe and healthy working practices.

Equipment:
  • 1457016-00-A KIT, SPRING COMPRESSOR, GEDORE
  • 1137855-00-A TOOL, REAR RIDE HEIGHT TORQUE, MODEL 3
  • 1081765-00-A 3/4 TON UNDERHOIST STAND
  • 1459409-00-A ADAPTER, HUB JACK, MODEL 3

76 Nm (56.0 lbs-ft)

 Reuse
Bolt and nut that attach the RH rear upper fore link to the subframe
76 Nm (56.0 lbs-ft)
  • 18 mm socket
  • 18mm 12pt combination wrench
 Reuse

Remove

  1. Lower the rear subframe assembly, disconnect air supply from the subframe lifting tool, and then move the subframe lifting tool and subframe away from the vehicle. See Subframe Assembly - Rear (Remove and Replace).
    Note
    Do not remove the half shafts. Terminate before removing the LH and RH rear coil springs from the rear lower aft link.
  2. Remove the bolt and nut that attach the RH rear upper fore link to the knuckle.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 18 mm socket
    • 18mm 12pt combination wrench
  3. Remove the bolt and nut that attach the RH rear upper fore link to the subframe.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 18 mm socket
    • 18mm 12pt combination wrench
  4. Remove the RH rear upper fore link from the subframe.

Install

  1. Install the RH rear upper fore link into the subframe.
  2. Hand-tighten the bolt and nut that attach the RH rear upper fore link to the subframe.
    Note
    Torque at later step. While installing the bolt, it may be necessary to turn a few times to align.
  3. With assistance, hand-tighten the bolt and nut that attach the RH rear upper fore link to the knuckle.
    Note
    Torque at later step.
  4. Install the rear subframe assembly. See Subframe Assembly - Rear (Remove and Replace).
    Note
    Do not remove the half shafts. Terminate before removing the LH and RH rear coil springs from the rear lower aft link.
