Striker - Trunk (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1120020022 FRT 0.12 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See for safe and healthy working practices.

  • 2024-01-26: Fixed broken links.
Equipment:
  • 1588903-00-A Body Gap and Flush Tool

Remove

  1. Remove the rear trunk sill trim. See Trim - Trunk Sill (Remove and Replace).
  2. Use masking tape to mark the location of the trunk striker.
  3. Remove the bolts that attach the trunk striker to the body, and then remove the striker from the vehicle.

Install

  1. Align the trunk striker with the masking tape on the body, and then hand-tighten the bolts that attach the trunk striker to the body.
  2. Close the trunk and check gap/flush.
  3. Open the trunk and adjust the striker position as necessary.
  4. Repeat step 2 and step 3 until alignment is satisfactory.
  5. Fully tighten the bolts that attach the trunk striker to the vehicle.
    24 Nm (17.7 lbs-ft)
    Note
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Torx T45 socket
  6. Remove the masking tape and install the rear trunk sill trim. See Trim - Trunk Sill (Remove and Replace).
