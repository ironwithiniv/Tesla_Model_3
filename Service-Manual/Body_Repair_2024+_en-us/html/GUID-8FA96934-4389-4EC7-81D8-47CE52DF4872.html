Rear Quarter Inner (Assembly)

Correction code: 10101906902 10101907002 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Rear Quarter Inner Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • Properly mount the vehicle on a frame bench when performing this procedure.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Wheel Arch Liner - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the Fender Assembly - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
  3. Remove the necessary portion of the Body Side Outer to expose the underlying component being replaced.
  4. Remove the C–Pillar Reinforcement.
  5. Remove the Rear Wheelhouse Reinforcement.
  6. Remove the original component.
    or Factory Spot Welds

    or Factory SPRs

  7. Prepare for installation.
    Note
    A red X indicates a location where a factory-installed fastener is not being replaced. Secure this location using structural adhesive only.

    or Structural Bulb Rivets, 6.5 mm

    or Countersunk Rivets, 4.8 mm Short

    or Countersunk Rivets, 4.8 mm Long

    or Rivets, 4.8 mm
    • A = 8 mm
    • B = 6 mm

    or Structural Rivets, 6.5 mm Short

    or Flow Form Rivets, S08

    or Flow Form Rivets, S18

    or Installation Spot Welds

    or Aluminum Plug Welds
    Note
    For these plug welds, drill a 9 mm hole in each of the indicated locations.
  8. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  9. Install the new component or components.
  10. Perform resistance spot welding.
    or Installation Spot Welds
    Warning
    Failure to follow all welding safety precautions, including the use of personal protective equipment, could result in serious injury or property damage. Only technicians who have completed Tesla’s approved welding training are authorized to weld structural components on Tesla vehicles.
    CAUTION
    Do not weld on a Tesla vehicle before performing the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure (refer to the vehicle-specific Service Manual for more information on the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure). Welding on a Tesla vehicle with an energized high or low voltage system might damage vehicle components.
  11. Perform GMA welding.
    or Aluminum Plug Welds
    Warning
    Failure to follow all welding safety precautions, including the use of personal protective equipment, could result in serious injury or property damage. Only technicians who have completed Tesla’s approved welding training are authorized to weld structural components on Tesla vehicles.
    Warning
    To maintain vehicle crash integrity, use only approved welding wire and an approved GMA welder to perform GMA welding on Tesla vehicles. Refer to Approved Gas Metal Arc (GMA) Welders and Welding Wire for information on approved GMA welders and welding wire.
    CAUTION
    Do not weld on a Tesla vehicle before performing the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure (refer to the vehicle-specific Service Manual for more information on the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure). Welding on a Tesla vehicle with an energized high or low voltage system might damage vehicle components.
    Note
    Before GMA welding, a test weld using material of the same gauge and type should be performed to make sure that the welding equipment settings produce a satisfactory joint.
  12. Install the necessary portion of the Body Side Outer that was removed to expose the component being replaced.
  13. Install the fasteners as indicated.
    or Flow Form Rivets, S28
  14. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  15. Install the Fender Assembly - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
  16. Install the Wheel Arch Liner - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
