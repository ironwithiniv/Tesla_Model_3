Bracket - Tail Lamp - LH (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1001040042 FRT 0.36 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. Torque Specifications

Table 1. Torque Specifications: Bracket - Tail Lamp - LH (Remove and Replace)
Description Torque Value Recommended Tools Reuse/Replace Notes
Bolt that attaches the rear center bracket to the vehicle
4 Nm (2.9 lbs-ft)
  • 10 mm socket
 Reuse
Bolts (x2) that attach the LH taillight bracket to the vehicle
4 Nm (2.9 lbs-ft)
  • 10 mm socket
 Reuse

Remove

  1. Remove the rear fascia. See Fascia Assembly - Rear (Remove and Install).
  2. Remove the bolt that attaches the rear center bracket to the vehicle.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 10 mm socket
  3. Release the clip that attaches the rear center bracket to the LH taillight bracket.
  4. Remove the bolts (x2) that attach the LH taillight bracket to the vehicle.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • Cordless Ratchet/Impact Driver
    • 10 mm socket
  5. Remove the LH taillight bracket.

Install

  1. Install the bolts (x2) that attach the LH taillight bracket to the vehicle.
    4 Nm (2.9 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  2. Install the clip that attaches the rear center bracket to the LH taillight bracket.
  3. Install the bolt that attaches the rear center bracket to the vehicle.
    4 Nm (2.9 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
