Remove

  1. Remove the rear fascia assembly. See Fascia Assembly - Rear (Remove and Install).
  2. Remove the rear left BLE from the rear fascia.
  3. Remove the bolts (x4) that attach the lower rear fascia to the upper rear fascia.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  4. Remove the LH and RH rear fascia lights. See .
  5. Remove the bolts (x2) that attach the rear fascia diffuser to the inside of the rear fascia.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  6. Remove the bolts (x6) that attach the rear fascia diffuser to the outer edge of the rear fascia.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  7. Release the tabs (x21) that attach the rear upper fascia to the rear fascia assembly, and then remove the rear upper fascia.

Install

  1. Install the tabs (x21) that attach the rear upper fascia to the rear fascia assembly.
  2. Install the bolts (x2) that attach the rear fascia diffuser to the inside of the rear fascia.
    3 Nm (2.2 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  3. Install the bolts (x6) that attach the rear fascia diffuser to the outer edge of the rear fascia.
    3 Nm (2.2 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  4. Install the LH and RH rear fascia lights. See .
  5. Install the bolts (x4) that attach the lower rear fascia to the upper rear fascia.
    3 Nm (2.2 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm socket
  6. Install the rear left BLE from the rear fascia.
  7. Install the rear lower fascia. See .
