Parcel Shelf Extension (Front)

Correction code: 10100134302 10100108902 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Rear Quarter Inner Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • This procedure can be completed without using a frame bench.

Parts List

Quantity Description Image / Notes
1 EXTN, PARCEL SHELF FRONT (Parcel Shelf Extension - Front)
1 ASY, PARCEL SELF RETRACTOR REIF (Parcel Shelf Retractor)

When ordering parts, refer to the Parts Catalog and enter the VIN of the vehicle being repaired to find the correct parts (and the part numbers) for the vehicle. Alternatively, use the search function in the Parts Catalog to find a specific part for the vehicle.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Front Carpet - Rear Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the Trim - Side - Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Glass - Backlight (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Amplifier - Audio - External (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the Woofer - Parcel Shelf (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the Retractor - Seatbelt - 2nd Row - Outboard (Single Side) (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the Trunk (Remove and Install).
  8. Remove the Hinge - Power Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace).
  9. Remove the Rear Quarter Inner (Panel).
  10. Remove the Parcel Shelf Extension (Rear).
  11. Remove the original component.

    or Factory Spot Welds

    or Drill through factory spot welds
    Note
    When drilling out spot welds, use a drill bit that creates a hole correctly sized for the fastener that will replace the spot weld.
  12. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the EXTN, PARCEL SHELF FRONT and the ASY, PARCEL SELF RETRACTOR REIF.
  13. Assemble the EXTN, PARCEL SHELF FRONT and the ASY, PARCEL SELF RETRACTOR REIF to create the new component.

    or Countersunk Rivets, 4.8 mm Short

    or Structural Bulb Rivets, 6.5 mm

    or Flow Form Rivets, S18

  14. Prepare for installation.

    or Structural Bulb Rivets, 6.5 mm

    or Rivets, 4.8 mm

    or Flow Form Rivets, S18

  15. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  16. Install the new component or components.
  17. Install the Parcel Shelf Extension (Rear).
  18. Install the Rear Quarter Inner (Panel).
  19. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  20. Install the Hinge - Power Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace).
  21. Install the Trunk (Remove and Install).
  22. Install the Retractor - Seatbelt - 2nd Row - Outboard (Single Side) (Remove and Replace).
  23. Install the Woofer - Parcel Shelf (Remove and Replace).
  24. Install the Amplifier - Audio - External (Remove and Replace).
  25. Install the Glass - Backlight (Remove and Replace).
  26. Install the Trim - Side - Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace).
  27. Install the Front Carpet - Rear Trunk (Remove and Replace).
