A-Pillar Outer Reinforcement

Correction code: 10100100802 10100100102 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Body Side Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • This procedure can be completed without using a frame bench.

Parts List

Quantity Description Image / Notes
1 ASY - DOOR RING OUTER (Door Ring Outer Assembly)
10 Structural Countersunk Rivet, 6.5 mm
5 High Strength Structural Rivet, 6.5 mm

When ordering parts, refer to the Parts Catalog and enter the VIN of the vehicle being repaired to find the correct parts (and the part numbers) for the vehicle. Alternatively, use the search function in the Parts Catalog to find a specific part for the vehicle.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Hood Assembly (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the Carrier Bracket - Cowl Screen - LH (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Cover - Rocker Panel - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Windshield (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the Glass - Backlight (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the Glass - Roof - Fixed (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the Glass - Body - Rear Quarter - LH (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the Fender Assembly - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
  9. Remove the Airbag - Curtain - RH (RHD) (Remove and Replace).
  10. Remove the Steering Column (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  11. Remove the Beam - Cross Car (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  12. Remove the Carpet - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  13. Remove the Module - Body Controller - LH (Remove and Replace).
  14. Remove the Door - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
  15. Remove the Hinge - Door - Front - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  16. Remove the Hinge - Door - Front - Upper - LH (Remove and Replace).
  17. Remove the Door - Rear - LH (Remove and Install).
  18. Remove the necessary portion of the Body Side Outer to expose the underlying component being replaced.
  19. Remove the Shotgun Outer.
  20. Remove the B-Pillar Outer (Upper).
  21. Remove the Roof Rail Extension.
  22. Remove the A-Pillar Outer from the Door Ring Outer service part as indicated below to make the new component.
  23. Remove the original component.
    or Factory Spot Welds
    or Drill through factory spot welds
    Note
    When drilling out spot welds, use a drill bit that creates a hole correctly sized for the fastener that will replace the spot weld.
  24. Prepare for installation.
    Note
    A red X indicates a location where a factory-installed fastener is not being replaced. Secure this location using structural adhesive only.
    or Installation Spot Welds

    or High Strength Structural Rivets, 6.5 mm

    or Structural Countersunk Rivets, 6.5 mm

    or Flow Form Rivets, S28

  25. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  26. Install the new component or components.
  27. Perform resistance spot welding.
    or Installation Spot Welds
    Warning
    Failure to follow all welding safety precautions, including the use of personal protective equipment, could result in serious injury or property damage. Only technicians who have completed Tesla’s approved welding training are authorized to weld structural components on Tesla vehicles.
    CAUTION
    Do not weld on a Tesla vehicle before performing the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure (refer to the vehicle-specific Service Manual for more information on the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure). Welding on a Tesla vehicle with an energized high or low voltage system might damage vehicle components.
  28. Replace the Roof Rail Extension.
  29. Replace the B-Pillar Outer (Upper).
  30. Replace the Shotgun Outer.
  31. Install the portion of the Body Side Outer Assembly that was removed to expose the component being replaced.
  32. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  33. Install the Door - Rear - LH (Remove and Install).
  34. Install the Hinge - Door - Front - Upper - LH (Remove and Replace).
  35. Install the Hinge - Door - Front - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  36. Install the Door - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
  37. Install the Module - Body Controller - LH (Remove and Replace).
  38. Install the Carpet - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  39. Install the Beam - Cross Car (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  40. Install the Steering Column (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  41. Install the Airbag - Curtain - RH (RHD) (Remove and Replace).
  42. Install the Fender Assembly - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
  43. Install the Glass - Body - Rear Quarter - LH (Remove and Replace).
  44. Install the Glass - Roof - Fixed (Remove and Replace).
  45. Install the Glass - Backlight (Remove and Replace).
  46. Install the Windshield (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  47. Install the Cover - Rocker Panel - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  48. Install the Carrier Bracket - Cowl Screen - LH (Remove and Replace).
  49. Install the Hood Assembly (Remove and Replace).
