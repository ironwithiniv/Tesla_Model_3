B-Pillar Inner Assembly (Complete)

Correction code: 10100117002 10100117102 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Body Side Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • Properly mount the vehicle on a frame bench when performing this procedure.

Parts List

Quantity Description Image / Notes

High Strength Structural Rivet, 6.5 mm

When ordering parts, refer to the Parts Catalog and enter the VIN of the vehicle being repaired to find the correct parts (and the part numbers) for the vehicle. Alternatively, use the search function in the Parts Catalog to find a specific part for the vehicle.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Glass - Roof - Fixed (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the Glass - Backlight (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Door - Rear - LH (Remove and Install).
  4. Remove the Hinge - Door - Rear - Upper - LH (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the Hinge - Door - Rear - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the Cover - Rocker Panel - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the Seal - Body - Side - Front - Primary - LH (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the Seal - Body - Side - Rear - Primary - LH (Remove and Replace).
  9. Remove the Airbag - Curtain - RH (RHD) (Remove and Replace).
  10. Remove the Bracket - B-Pillar - LH (Remove and Replace).
  11. Remove the Retractor - Seatbelt - Driver (Remove and Replace).
  12. Remove the Carpet - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  13. Remove the Carpet - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  14. Remove the B-Pillar Outer (Complete).
  15. Trim the A–Pillar Outer as shown to expose the underlying B–Pillar Inner.

    Cut Line

  16. Remove the original component.
    or Factory Spot Welds
  17. Cut the new B-Pillar Inner Assembly as indicated below to make the new component.
    Cut Line
    • A = 5 mm
  18. Prepare for installation.

    or Installation Spot Welds

    or High Strength Structural Rivets, 6.5 mm

  19. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  20. Install the new component or components.
  21. Perform resistance spot welding.
    or Installation Spot Welds
    Warning
    Failure to follow all welding safety precautions, including the use of personal protective equipment, could result in serious injury or property damage. Only technicians who have completed Tesla’s approved welding training are authorized to weld structural components on Tesla vehicles.
    CAUTION
    Do not weld on a Tesla vehicle before performing the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure (refer to the vehicle-specific Service Manual for more information on the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure). Welding on a Tesla vehicle with an energized high or low voltage system might damage vehicle components.
  22. Install the B-Pillar Outer (Complete).
  23. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  24. Install the Carpet - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  25. Install the Carpet - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  26. Install the Retractor - Seatbelt - Driver (Remove and Replace).
  27. Install the Bracket - B-Pillar - LH (Remove and Replace).
  28. Install the Airbag - Curtain - RH (RHD) (Remove and Replace).
  29. Install the Seal - Body - Side - Rear - Primary - LH (Remove and Replace).
  30. Install the Seal - Body - Side - Front - Primary - LH (Remove and Replace).
  31. Install the Cover - Rocker Panel - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  32. Install the Hinge - Door - Rear - Lower - LH (Remove and Replace).
  33. Install the Hinge - Door - Rear - Upper - LH (Remove and Replace).
  34. Install the Door - Rear - LH (Remove and Install).
  35. Install the Glass - Backlight (Remove and Replace).
  36. Install the Glass - Roof - Fixed (Remove and Replace).
