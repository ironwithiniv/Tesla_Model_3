Approved Paint Systems for Tesla Warranty Repairs

Lists the paint systems that meet Tesla warranty repair standards.

Paint systems from the manufacturers listed below are recognized as meeting the Tesla's quality standards for approved Collision Centers:

  • BASF
  • Axalta Coating Systems
  • PPG
  • Akzo Nobel
  • Sherwin-Williams
