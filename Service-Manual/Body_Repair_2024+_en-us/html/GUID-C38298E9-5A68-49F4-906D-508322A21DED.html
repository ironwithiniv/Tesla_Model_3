Roof Rack Brackets

Correction code: 10102700202 10102700102 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Body Side Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • This procedure can be completed without using a frame bench.

Parts List

Quantity Description Image / Notes
1 each, as needed M3, ROOF RACK, FR, LH (Roof Rack Bracket – LH Front)

M3, ROOF RACK, RR, LH (Roof Rack Bracket – LH Rear)

M3, ROOF RACK, FR, RH (Roof Rack Bracket – RH Front)

M3, ROOF RACK, RR, RH (Roof Rack Bracket – RH Rear)
Note
Each roof rack bracket (front LH, front RH, rear LH, and rear RH) has a different part number. Order the correct replacement bracket for each bracket being replaced.
2 rivets per bracket Structural Countersunk Rivet, 6.5 mm

When ordering parts, refer to the Parts Catalog and enter the VIN of the vehicle being repaired to find the correct parts (and the part numbers) for the vehicle. Alternatively, use the search function in the Parts Catalog to find a specific part for the vehicle.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Seal - Body - Side - Rear - Primary - LH (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the Seal - Body - Side - Front - Primary - LH (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Airbag - Curtain - RH (RHD) (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Airbag - Curtain - RH (RHD) (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the Windshield (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the Glass - Roof - Fixed (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the Glass - Backlight (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the original component.

    or Factory Spot Welds

  9. Prepare for installation.
    Note
    A red X indicates a location where a factory-installed fastener is not being replaced. Secure this location using structural adhesive only.

    or Structural Countersunk Rivets, 6.5 mm

  10. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  11. Install the new component or components.
    Note
    Remove a small area of the NVH foam under the new component to allow clearance for the rivets.
  12. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  13. Install the Glass - Backlight (Remove and Replace).
  14. Install the Glass - Roof - Fixed (Remove and Replace).
  15. Install the Windshield (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  16. Install the Airbag - Curtain - RH (RHD) (Remove and Replace).
  17. Install the Airbag - Curtain - RH (RHD) (Remove and Replace).
  18. Install the Seal - Body - Side - Front - Primary - LH (Remove and Replace).
  19. Install the Seal - Body - Side - Rear - Primary - LH (Remove and Replace).
