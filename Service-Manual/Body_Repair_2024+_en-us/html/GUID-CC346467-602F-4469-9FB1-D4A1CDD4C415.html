Torque Box Rear Upper

Correction code: 10101010302, 10101010502 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Front Inner Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • This procedure can be completed without using a frame bench.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Wheel Arch Liner - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the Brake Pedal (Remove and Replace)
  3. Remove the Brake Lines - Front Bundle (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Insulator - Dash (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the original component.
    or Factory Spot Welds
  6. Prepare for installation.
    Note
    A red X indicates a location where a factory-installed fastener is not being replaced. Secure this location using structural adhesive only.
    • or High Strength Structural Rivets, 6.5 mm
  7. Prepare for installation.
    • or Structural Bulb Rivets, 6.5 mm A = 38 mm B = 20 mm
    • or High Strength Structural Rivets, 6.5 mm C = 8 mm D = 14 mm
  8. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  9. Install the new component or components.
  10. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  11. Install the Insulator - Dash (Remove and Replace).
  12. Install the Brake Lines - Front Bundle (Remove and Replace).
  13. Install the Brake Pedal (Remove and Replace)
  14. Install the Wheel Arch Liner - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
