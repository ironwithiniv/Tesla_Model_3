Front Frame Rail Outer

Correction code: 10101002502 10101002702 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Front Inner Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • Properly mount the vehicle on a frame bench when performing this procedure.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the A/C Refrigerant (Recovery and Recharge) (Restore).
  2. Remove the Damper - Front - LH (Dual Motor) (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Drive Unit - Front (3DU) (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Jounce Hose - Front Brake - LH (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the Radiator (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the Assembly - Cooling Fan and Shroud (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the Ankle Catcher (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the Crush Can - Upper - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  9. Remove the Crush Can - Upper - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  10. Remove the Carrier - Bumper - Front (Remove and Replace).
  11. Remove the Fender Assembly - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
  12. Remove the Torque Box Cover Assembly.
  13. Remove the Shotgun Shear Wall.
  14. Remove the original component.

    or Factory Spot Welds

  15. Remove any foam.
  16. Remove any remaining adhesive from the mating surfaces of the vehicle.
  17. Prepare for installation.
    or High Strength Structural Rivets, 6.5 mm
    • A = 8 mm
    • B = 9 mm

    or Installation Spot Welds

    Note
    Make sure the brake pedal is moved out of the way.
  18. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  19. Install the new component or components.
  20. Perform resistance spot welding.
    or Installation Spot Welds
    Warning
    Failure to follow all welding safety precautions, including the use of personal protective equipment, could result in serious injury or property damage. Only technicians who have completed Tesla’s approved welding training are authorized to weld structural components on Tesla vehicles.
    CAUTION
    Do not weld on a Tesla vehicle before performing the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure (refer to the vehicle-specific Service Manual for more information on the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure). Welding on a Tesla vehicle with an energized high or low voltage system might damage vehicle components.
  21. Install the new Shotgun Shear Wall.
  22. Install the new Torque Box Cover Assembly.
  23. Install the fasteners that attach the Torque Box Cover Assembly to the Front Frame Rail Outer.
    High Strength Structural Rivet, 6.5 mm
    • C = 6 mm
    • D = 63 mm
    • E = 40 mm
  24. Prepare to install the weld stud as indicated.
    Stud
    • F = 20 mm.
    • G = 20 mm.
    GMA Weld
  25. Perform GMA welding.
    GMA Weld
    Warning
    Failure to follow all welding safety precautions, including the use of personal protective equipment, could result in serious injury or property damage. Only technicians who have completed Tesla’s approved welding training are authorized to weld structural components on Tesla vehicles.
    Warning
    To maintain vehicle crash integrity, use only approved welding wire and an approved GMA welder to perform GMA welding on Tesla vehicles. Refer to Approved Gas Metal Arc (GMA) Welders and Welding Wire for information on approved GMA welders and welding wire.
    CAUTION
    Do not weld on a Tesla vehicle before performing the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure (refer to the vehicle-specific Service Manual for more information on the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure). Welding on a Tesla vehicle with an energized high or low voltage system might damage vehicle components.
    Note
    Before GMA welding, a test weld using material of the same gauge and type should be performed to make sure that the welding equipment settings produce a satisfactory joint.
  26. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  27. Install the Fender Assembly - Front - LH (Remove and Install).
  28. Install the Carrier - Bumper - Front (Remove and Replace).
  29. Install the Crush Can - Upper - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  30. Install the Crush Can - Upper - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  31. Install the Ankle Catcher (Remove and Replace).
  32. Install the Assembly - Cooling Fan and Shroud (Remove and Replace).
  33. Install the Radiator (Remove and Replace).
  34. Install the Jounce Hose - Front Brake - LH (Remove and Replace).
  35. Install the Drive Unit - Front (3DU) (Remove and Replace).
  36. Install the Damper - Front - LH (Dual Motor) (Remove and Replace).
  37. Install the A/C Refrigerant (Recovery and Recharge) (Restore).
