Assembly - Reservoir - Windshield Washer (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1750020112 FRT 0.30 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See for safe and healthy working practices.

  1. 2025-10-10: Added steps related to the front fascia camera washer hose.
Equipment:
  • Air Operated Fluid Evacuator
Table 1. Torque Specifications: Assembly - Reservoir - Windshield Washer (Remove and Replace)
Description Torque Value Recommended Tools Reuse/Replace Notes
Bolts (x3) that attach the windshield washer reservoir to the body
4 Nm (2.9 lbs-ft)
  • 10 mm deep socket
 Reuse

Remove

  1. Remove the underhood storage unit. See .
  2. Open the washer reservoir cap.
  3. Place a fluid evacuator near the front of the vehicle and connect it with a compressed air.
  4. Open the evacuator valve and position the evacuator hose into a corner of the washer reservoir, and then evacuate as much as washer as possible.
  5. Disconnect the compressed air and remove the fluid evacuator.
  6. Disconnect the front fascia camera washer hose.
    Note
    Unlock the black tab and then disconnect the hose.
    Note
    The hose may not be present on early production vehicles.
  7. Disconnect the windshield washer level sensor connector.
  8. Disconnect the windshield washer pump connector.
  9. Release the clip that attaches the windshield washer hose to the washer pump.
  10. Release the clip that attaches the windshield washer hose to the washer reservoir.
  11. Release the clips (x4) that attach the electrical harness to the windshield washer reservoir.
  12. Release the clips (x2) and guide clips (x4) that attach the hose to the washer bottle.
  13. Remove the bolts (x3) that attach the windshield washer reservoir to the body.
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm deep socket
  14. Release the locator pins (x3), and remove the windshield washer reservoir from the vehicle, and then recover any fluid from the reservoir.

Install

  1. Install the windshield washer reservoir to the body.
  2. Install the bolts (x3) that attach the windshield washer reservoir to the body.
    4 Nm (2.9 lbs-ft)
    Tip
    Use of the following tool(s) is recommended:
    • 10 mm deep socket
  3. Install the clips (x2) and guide clips (x4) that attach the hose to the washer bottle.
  4. Fasten the clips (x4) that attach the electrical harness to the windshield washer reservoir.
  5. Fasten the clip that attaches the windshield washer hose to the washer reservoir.
  6. Fasten the clip that attaches the windshield washer hose to the washer pump.
  7. Connect the windshield washer pump connector.
  8. Connect the front fascia camera washer hose.
    Note
    Lock the black tab to connect the hose.
    Note
    The hose may not be present on early production vehicles.
  9. Connect the windshield washer level sensor connector.
  10. Install the underhood storage unit. See .
  11. Fill the windshield washer reservoir with fluid.
  12. Close the washer reservoir cap.
  13. Close the hood.
  14. Raise the LH front window and close the LH front door.
  15. Verify operation of the windshield wipers and washer.
