Hose - Inlet - Front Drive Unit Inverter (Remove and Replace)

Correction code 1830020162 FRT 0.42 NOTE: Unless otherwise explicitly stated in the procedure, the correction code and its associated FRT reflect all of the work required to perform this procedure, including the linked procedures. Do not stack correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. NOTE: See to learn more about FRTs and how they are created. NOTE: See Personal Protection to make sure you are wearing proper PPE when performing the procedure below. NOTE: See for safe and healthy working practices.

Remove

  1. Raise and support the vehicle. See .
  2. Remove the rear underhood apron. See Underhood Apron - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  3. Disconnect the LV power. See .
  4. Remove the cabin intake duct. See .
  5. Remove the front aero shield panel. See Panel - Aero Shield - Front (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the push clips (x3) and bolt that attaches the RH front wheel arch liner to the skid plate.
  7. Use a bungee strap to pull back the RH front wheel arch liner and secure it to the subframe.
  8. Position a coolant drain container underneath the RH front of the HV battery.
  9. Release the clip, disconnect the powertrain return hose from the front drive unit inverter inlet hose, and then immediately plug both fittings.
  10. Position a coolant drain container underneath the LH rear of the front drive unit.
  11. Release the clip, disconnect the front drive unit inverter inlet hose from the front drive unit, and then immediately plug both fittings.
  12. Use a clip prytool to release the clip that attaches the front drive unit inverter inlet hose to the front subframe.
  13. Use a clip prytool to release the clip that attaches the front drive unit inverter inlet hose to the front drive unit.
  14. Remove the front drive unit inverter inlet hose.

Install

  1. Install the clip that attaches the front drive unit inverter inlet hose to the front drive unit.
  2. Install the clip that attaches the front drive unit inverter inlet hose to the front subframe.
  3. Remove the plugs from the fittings, immediately connect the front drive unit inverter inlet hose to the front drive unit inverter, fasten the clip, and then perform a Push-Pull-Push check of the fitting.
  4. Remove the plugs from the fittings, immediately connect the front drive unit inverter inlet hose to the powertrain return hose, fasten the clip, and then perform a Push-Pull-Push check of the fitting.
  5. Remove the coolant drain collector from under the vehicle.
  6. Remove the bungee strap from the RH front wheel arch liner.
  7. Install the push clips (x3) and bolt that attaches the RH front wheel arch liner to the skid plate.
  8. Install the front aero shield panel. See Panel - Aero Shield - Front (Remove and Replace).
  9. Connect LV power. See .
  10. Perform the cooling system (partial refill and bleed) procedure. See .
  11. Install the rear underhood apron. See Underhood Apron - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  12. Remove the vehicle from the 2 post lift. See .
