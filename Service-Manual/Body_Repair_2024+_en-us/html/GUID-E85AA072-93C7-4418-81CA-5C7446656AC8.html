Door Ring Outer

Correction code: 10100100402 10100100502 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Body Side Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • Properly mount the vehicle on a frame bench when performing this procedure.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Brake Line Assembly - Side Sill (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the Trim - Side - Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Carpet - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Retractor - Seatbelt - Driver (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the Beam - Cross Car (LHD) (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the Module - Body Controller - LH (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the Carpet - Front - LH (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the Airbag - Curtain - RH (RHD) (Remove and Replace).
  9. Remove the Bracket - B-Pillar - LH (Remove and Replace).
  10. Remove the necessary portion of the Body Side Outer to expose the underlying component being replaced.
  11. Remove the Shotgun Outer
  12. Remove the C–Pillar Reinforcement.
  13. Remove the Roof Rail Extension.
  14. Remove the original component.

  15. Remove the Sill Insert.
  16. Mark the center lines for the Sill Insert Mounting Brackets on the new Sill Insert as shown below.
    Note
    The measurements are from the end of the Sill Insert to the center of the Sill Insert Mounting Bracket.
    • A = 399 mm.
    • B = 420 mm.
  17. Mark the new Sill Outer to show the location for the new Sill Insert.
    • C = 177 mm.
  18. Using the bracket center lines drawn earlier, position the Sill Insert Mounting Brackets between the Sill Insert and the Door Ring outer, then adjust the brackets so that they make contact with the Door Ring Outer.
  19. Prepare to install the mounting brackets on the new Sill Insert.
    or High Strength Structural Rivets, 6.5 mm
  20. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the new Sill Insert and the new Sill Insert Mounting Brackets.
  21. Install the new Sill Insert Mounting Brackets on the new Sill Insert.
  22. Prepare to install the new Sill Insert in the new Sill Outer.

    or Rivets, 4.8 mm

  23. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the new Sill Insert, the new Sill Insert Mounting Brackets, and the Door Ring Outer.
  24. Install the new Sill Insert on the new Sill Outer portion of the Door Ring Outer assembly.
  25. Prepare to install the new Door Ring Outer.
    Note
    A red X indicates a location where a factory-installed fastener is not being replaced. Secure this location using structural adhesive only.

    or Bolts

  26. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  27. Install the new component or components.
    Torque the bolts to 19 Nm.
  28. Perform resistance spot welding.
    or Installation Spot Welds
    Warning
    Failure to follow all welding safety precautions, including the use of personal protective equipment, could result in serious injury or property damage. Only technicians who have completed Tesla’s approved welding training are authorized to weld structural components on Tesla vehicles.
    CAUTION
    Do not weld on a Tesla vehicle before performing the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure (refer to the vehicle-specific Service Manual for more information on the Vehicle HV Disablement Procedure). Welding on a Tesla vehicle with an energized high or low voltage system might damage vehicle components.
