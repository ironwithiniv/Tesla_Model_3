Rear Quarter Outer (Lower)

Correction code: 10101013202 10100117802 NOTE: Unless explicitly stated in the procedure, the above correction code includes all Collision Repair and Service repair work required to perform this procedure, including the linked Collision Repair procedures and linked Service procedures. Do not stack Collision Repair correction codes unless explicitly told to do so. Depending on the damage to the vehicle, additional repairs may be required.

Parent topic: Rear End Panels

Repair Information

  • Review all collision repair general practices and safety documentation and wear the appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) before beginning this procedure.
  • This procedure can be completed without using a frame bench.

Repair Procedure

  1. Remove the Wheel Arch Liner - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
  2. Remove the Bracket - Tail Lamp - LH (Remove and Replace).
  3. Remove the Bracket - Rear Wing - LH (Remove and Replace).
  4. Remove the Bumper Beam - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  5. Remove the Air Extractor (Remove and Replace).
  6. Remove the Tub Carpet - Rear Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  7. Remove the Trim - Side - Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace).
  8. Remove the original component.

    or Factory Spot Welds

    or Factory SPRs

  9. Prepare for installation.
    Note
    A red X indicates a location where a factory-installed fastener is not being replaced. Secure this location using structural adhesive only.

    or Structural Bulb Rivets, 6.5 mm

  10. Apply structural adhesive to the mating surfaces on the vehicle and the new component or components.
  11. Install the new component or components.
  12. Perform any necessary post-repair operations.
  13. Install the Trim - Side - Trunk - LH (Remove and Replace).
  14. Install the Tub Carpet - Rear Trunk (Remove and Replace).
  15. Install the Air Extractor (Remove and Replace).
  16. Install the Bumper Beam - Rear (Remove and Replace).
  17. Install the Bracket - Rear Wing - LH (Remove and Replace).
  18. Install the Bracket - Tail Lamp - LH (Remove and Replace).
  19. Install the Wheel Arch Liner - Rear - LH (Remove and Replace).
